# Frozen frequency-symbol assembly and controls

Date: 2026-09-04. These are exact local-symbol regressions, not the finite-time
control proof or a cosmological in/out S matrix.

For the oscillator variable `Y=a^(3/2)Qc`, at a=1 an incoming frequency w
gives `Qc=Y`, `dot Qc=(-i w-3H/2)Y`. Direct jets also retain the earlier
`-D log Z` scalar normalization derivative, tensor dual norms, all spatial
constraints, full geometry and canonical boundaries. The coefficient field
permits separate scalar and tensor frequency parameters.

The cubic symbol is `V3=-H3(P0)`. The quartic is
`V4=-H4(P0)+(1/2)H3_P A^-1 H3_P`, with three unordered partitions and
both tensors in the Legendre correction. The stripped frozen tree is

```
V4 - sum_(three partitions, scalar and two tensors)
          V3_left V3_right/[w_internal^2-Omega_internal^2].
```

The minus sign follows from `(iV3)i/(w^2-Omega^2)(iV3)` relative to `iV4`.
The Legendre correction is not propagating exchange. Spatial and frequency
sums must vanish; proper spatial subset sums must be nonzero. Internal
poles raise an error instead of receiving an invented finite value.

Omega is the exact quadratic oscillator frequency from S5.3a. The report
checks external shells and records every exchange contribution separately.
Examples cover scalar, mixed scalar/tensor and tensor elastic configurations,
plus a scalar case inside the separate proof's hard band. Lower-frequency
examples are only algebraic regressions.

Independent controls include direct frequency seeding versus all S5.2
coordinate/velocity assignments, including H4's Legendre term and an exterior
nonzero-H volume correction. Reversing frequencies/momenta complex-conjugates
a mixed symbol. A four-scalar case has nonzero tensor exchange. Bad frequency
sums, singular charts, zero subsets and changed report scope fail closed.

For a free massive scalar redefined by `chi=phi+g phi^2/2`, actual labelled
Lagrangian jets give cubic on-shell source `g(K^2-m^2)` and quartic contact
`g^2 m^2`. Since `s+t+u=4m^2`, exchange sums to `-g^2 m^2`: the total is
zero although both pieces are nonzero. The test expands the derivative and
mass monomials, not only an inserted cancellation formula.

For context, [Ageeva and Petrov, section 3.3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2206.10646)
exhibit cancellations in a different bounce model. That motivates the
control but is not evidence for our witness.

Freezing and diagonalizing do not commute with arbitrary time-dependent
canonical transformations or integrations by parts. This explicit oscillator-
frame prescription is useful for diagram checks, but neither chart-independent
scattering nor a physical freezing-error bound is demonstrated. The separate
control proof uses exact free evolution and time-dependent Hamiltonians,
so it does not rely on that missing approximation theorem.
