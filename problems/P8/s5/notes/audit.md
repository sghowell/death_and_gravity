## Written no-go review

The earlier exclusion proof uses nonzero-Theta connected components, not a
global isolated-zero assumption. Positive Lambda and the regular endpoint
gradient imply a descending zero. Positive-Theta components therefore
extend to the past; negative-Theta components extend to the future. The
divergent tensor integrals contradict the sign of xi in the corresponding
direction. A component cannot terminate on a touching/accumulating zero
with zero derivative while maintaining the stipulated strictly positive
regular principal gradient. The identically-zero exceptional case uses
the explicit conventional Lambda tail in the frozen formulation.

That last hypothesis is part of the scoped theorem, not a theorem about
arbitrary quantities called "GR asymptotics." The argument has not been
extended to weaker tails, arbitrary matter, or nonlinear stability. No
new defect was found in this targeted reread; this does not constitute an
exhaustive independent audit of every mathematical lemma in S0--S4.
