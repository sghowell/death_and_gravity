# Proper-time operator and the necessary first derivative

The derivative part of A1 is

p L(D,a Gamma_second+a_prime Gamma_prime)/(2048pi^2).

Since the spatial tensor form is time-independent, this is the proper-time divergence operator partial_t(a partial_t Gamma), not a Gamma_second alone.

For real scalar components D,Gamma, the exact identity is

D partial_t(a Gamma_prime)-Gamma partial_t(a D_prime)
=partial_t[a(D Gamma_prime-D_prime Gamma)].

It extends componentwise to the symmetric tensor form L and by sesquilinearity to Fourier tests. Compact support or vanishing appropriate boundary jets removes its boundary term. An independent polynomial test with nontrivial vanishing boundary jets gives zero for the full operator but nonzero when the first derivative is omitted.

The source-value mass, spatial and curvature coefficients are real symmetric tensor multipliers. This establishes the formal symmetry of this local-time endpoint coefficient only. The complete retarded quantum response includes its causal memory term and is not being declared symmetric.

No metric initial data, physical stress switching or nonlinear solution is introduced by these test-function identities.
