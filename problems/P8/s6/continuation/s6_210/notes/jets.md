# Actual low jets and the constrained longitudinal contribution

The frozen full recurrence gives exactly

P1_T(t,z1)=0,
P1_L(t,z1)=-2(7t^2+1)/(1+t^2)^2=-(H_prime+2H^2)/2.

No higher W8 term affects inverse-radius degree two. This is Taylor-jet independence, not replacement of the finite-momentum W8 reference; all four terms remain in the independent physical-mode checks.

For normalized momenta at P0, write Omega=sqrt(a^-2+m^2 x^2), What_T=Omega+O(x^4), and What_L=Omega+cL x^2/Omega+O(x^4). The transverse real momentum correction obeys

What_time/(2What)+H/2=O(x^2),

so Pi_T=-i What_T F_T+O(x^3). The longitudinal rate is H+O(x^2), giving Pi_L=(-i What_L-xH)F_L+O(x^3). These are statements about the complete constrained Proca readouts, not a Maxwell replacement.

For the scalar amplitudes in the complete four-sector factorization, the source jets through x2 are

A=1/(2a)+3a m^2 x^2/4,
B=1/(2a)-a m^2 x^2/4,
TL=LT=m x-i a H m x^2/2,
LL=1/(2a)+a(3m^2/4-cL/2)x^2,
gLL=a/2-a^3(m^2/2+cL)x^2/2.

These expressions are finite jets only, not finite-x modes. The detector has the corresponding Schwarz sign. Independent source and detector scale factors, H values and cL values are retained.

The mixed amplitudes do have an imaginary x2 term. Because their leading amplitude is order x, their complete pair product first acquires an imaginary term at x3. The TT and LL complete pair products are also real through x2. Real geometry and inverse-phase jets preserve this at arbitrary fixed P.

Consequently the current in the j1/d1 slot vanishes: its endpoint prefactor is real and the needed pair coefficient is real. This is an evaluated slot in the actual reference. The j1/d3 coefficient and full finite odd endpoints remain, as the physical checks demonstrate.

For the complete equal-time longitudinal j0 contribution, multiplication of both amplitudes and its own inverse phase gives

1/(8a)+a(5m^2/16-3cL/8)x^2.

The resulting curvature correction is -3a cL(n.D.n)(n.G.n)/8. The source/detector and inverse-phase contributions all matter for the coefficient3/8.
