Every curvature insertion first costs x2. Adding external P through the normalized momenta costs a further x. Thus its second-grade coefficient is obtained at the leading directions n,-n, while all spatial P dependence at this grade is the complete massive flat expression.

The other possible second-grade slots are j1/d1 and j2/d0. The first is evaluated as zero by the complete actual low-jet argument. For the second, keep source and detector times independent. The leading full pair product has the form

Then

partial_s(g F)=C(n) Gamma_prime(s)/(8a(t)),

There is no j2/d0 source-value term. The first source derivative generally does not vanish; at the bounce it vanishes because H0, not because the term may be omitted.
