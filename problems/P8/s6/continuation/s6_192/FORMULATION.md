# S6.192 formulation

## Claim

For the same actual canonical Gaussian Proca sector and S55 all-order
state, take gamma=epsilon Gamma on I=[-1/2,1/2], with real symmetric
tracefree smooth Gamma, ||Gamma^(j)||op<=1 through j12,
|epsilon|<=.01 and a common zero neighborhood of the initial surface.
Let D be a fixed smooth tracefree symmetric shear-coordinate detector
with ||D(t)||op<=1. No bound on its derivatives is used here.

The actual metric current J_D=-omega tr(G_D Sigma)/2 includes the
physical Hamiltonian vertex and every frame/frequency/amplitude
derivative. Define J_ad4 as the orders0,2,4 marker Taylor comparison
of the same tenth-order matrix-reference current, not as a new
renormalization prescription.

The complete mode integral of J_D-J_ad4 is C2 in epsilon into C0(I).
Through derivative order2 its absolute integral bounds are
1e77,1e94,1e111, and the integral Taylor remainder is at most
epsilon^2*1e111/2. It is zero at epsilon0 and strictly below the
display for nonzero epsilon. The physical current's actual-reference
and reference-comparison high tails are separately controlled;
the finite complement is not omitted.

## Fixed inputs and nonclaims

The affine parent, fixed M1 charge, scalar profile, mass1000,
kappa1e800, state, and original mu=m covariant prescription are
unchanged. The proof partition K=1e16 is not a physical cutoff.

Finite local/contact matching of J_ad4 to the original prescription
is still required. No complete physical self-energy/inverse,
finite-coupling background, interacting parent/cutoff or original
V/G/B and P8 closure is claimed. See notes/scope.md.
