# Actual physical leading symbol

Use the literal ten-field stress order (E, B, mA0, mAsp). Contracting the full spatial stress with a tracefree D gives the symmetric matrix diag(-D,-D,0,D). The zero coefficient of mA0 in this contraction does not remove the longitudinal constraint from the physical readouts.

At fixed positive mass and large r = |k|, the unit-W8 modes obey

W/r -> 1/a,
sqrt(r) f -> sqrt(a/2),
p/sqrt(r) -> -i/sqrt(2a).

The leading transverse vector, after division by sqrt(r), is

(-i e, i n cross e, 0, 0)/sqrt(2a).

The leading longitudinal vector is

(0, 0, -1, n)/sqrt(2a).

For the second momentum -k, its direction is -n. The longitudinal temporal and mass-spatial readouts remain; the longitudinal electric and transverse mass readouts are lower order. These limits keep three physical massive modes. They are not a replacement of Proca by Maxwell theory.

Let PT = I-n n^T and C v = n cross v. The complete leading TT amplitude is the restriction of (D-C^T D C)/(2a) to the transverse plane. Summing its four squared/mixed contractions and multiplying the inverse pair-frequency factor a/2 gives the TT part. The LL pair supplies (n.D.n)(n.G.n)/(8a). The four mixed pairs have zero leading amplitude by direct scaling.

For tracefree D and G, the complete result is

h = [4tr(DG)-4 n.(DG+GD)n+3(n.D.n)(n.G.n)]/(8a).

The exact code uses general noncommuting tracefree matrices and all nine pairs in an axis frame. Rotational covariance of the stress and complete projector contraction yields the invariant expression for every n. Independent actual W8 tests use noncollinear momenta, multiple directions and times, and three separated momentum scales.

The current sign is retained: -Im(-i A_D# A_G/(W_k+W_l)) = Re(A_D# A_G/(W_k+W_l)). For D = G it is positive before the negative lost-shell sign is applied.
