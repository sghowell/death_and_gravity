# Actual arbitrarily small backgrounds and order of limits

Let eta(u) be a smooth compactly supported lapse probe that vanishes near
the original initial slice and is a fixed nonzero constant aroundu0.
Choose N=1+epsilon eta, a_phys=(1+u^2)^2, a_hat=R(u,N)^1/4 a_phys,
m=ell(u), Hbar=0 and W0=3(R-1)(Hhat-Hclock). Hclock remains the fixed
coefficient, not the varied Hubble rate. These are admissible smooth
homogeneous OFF-SHELL background arguments for the local Gaussian
operator. They are not asserted to solve the unforced mean equations.

The entire source germs in S253 prove exactly R(u,1)=1 and
R_N(u,1)=-2/(1+u^2)^3; they do not replace R off the clock. Atu0 the
unchanged reference has a=D=Z=Y=Yv=1,

    L2=4 partial_N(N C3)=-1,
    Jc=243/160+rho_total(0)-7 pressure_total(0)/8>0.

The last strict sign uses the existing full current reference bounds,
including both fixed profiles, not a newly chosen pressure or a live
quantum mean. For each sufficiently small fixed nonzero epsilon,
R-1=-2epsilon eta+O(epsilon^2) at0 is nonzero. Continuity of the literal
full coefficient functions and primitive gives a small compact time
interval about0 on which all positive margins hold and r,L2 stay nonzero.
Taking R in(1/2,2) also gives gamma>1/4 by the exact identity in background.py.
All heavy source, mass and lower coefficients are finite and smooth there.

The leading fast quartic coefficient has the clock expansion at0

    Bfast=epsilon^2 eta^2/(2Jc(0))+O(epsilon^3),
    partial_epsilon^2 Bfast at0=eta^2/Jc(0).

This finite jet is checked from the full-germ identities. Positivity for
nonzero epsilon uses continuity of the FULL coefficient functions. It
does not evaluate them by a truncated Taylor polynomial.

The same argument gives bad prescribed backgrounds arbitrarily close
to the reference in every fixed C^k norm, and in the usual smooth
topology: a fixed smooth bump times epsilon tends to zero with all
derivatives. The local operator on each such background lacks the stated
all-momentum estimate on its small interval. Thus no unrestricted smooth
background neighborhood of the reference can have such an estimate for
every homogeneous member. Restricted solution families or restrictions
to a finite Fourier ball are different questions.

Crucially, first FIX epsilon nonzero and its compact interval and then
take P to infinity. The growth constant, chart condition number and
required threshold may deteriorate as epsilon tends to zero. The huge
fixed heavy mass can enlarge the threshold enormously. Nothing here
compares that threshold with a controlled Wilsonian cutoff. No exchange
of these limits, no reference-instability claim, and no conclusion about
the allowed physical finite momentum window is made.
