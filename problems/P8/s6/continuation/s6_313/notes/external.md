# Exhaustive double-external grouping

The S312 fixed-root labeled-tree inventory has20 contact,60 heavy
and60 Einstein graphs with exactly two internal light-scalar lines.
Every such line separates one external massive leg and a nonempty
radiation subset. With two labeled rays and exactly two such lines,
both rays must be attached successively to massive external lines:
12 different-leg assignments and8 same-leg orderings per four-scalar
core. Scalar seagulls and internal metric emissions have fewer such
lines; the pure two-ray graviton subtree belongs to the47 pair class.
Thus this is an exhaustive graph bijection, not soft power counting
used to omit graphs.

Write A=p.q1,B=p.q2,Z=q1.q2, Na=p.eps1.p,Nb=p.eps2.p,
Xa=2p.eps2.q1+q1.eps2.q1,Yb=2p.eps1.q2+q2.eps1.q2.
Transversality gives the exact two-order external factor. Subtracting
its leading product gives the polynomially verified identity
 Na(Nb+Xa)/[A(A+B+Z)]+Nb(Na+Yb)/[B(A+B+Z)]-NaNb/(AB)
 =NaXa/[A(A+B+Z)]+NbYb/[B(A+B+Z)]
  -NaNbZ/[AB(A+B+Z)].
Both shifted numerators and the Z propagator term are essential:
removing either produces a nonzero symbolic negative control.

Different-leg attachments give the exact product of individual
currents, with their momenta shifted in the hard four-scalar core.
The same-leg sum first uses the identity above. For a hard channel
L|R, the resulting leading terms are four distributions LL,LR,RL,RR:
 -N/kappa^2 * [J_L1 J_L2/D_R+J_R1 J_R2/D_L
               +J_L1 J_R2/D(q1)+J_L2 J_R1/D(q2)].
Here J_Lr sums the external currents on L, and D is the actual hard
denominator with the indicated ray assignment. N is the contraction
of the two unshifted recoil stresses, with trace reversal. Each actual
shifted stress core differs from this N by the explicit bilinear
stress-shift identity, checked in all16 components. The contact and
heavy cores have the same20-term attachment structure with their
own contact or single-heavy denominator.

The formula has six attachments with both rays on each side and
four on opposite sides in each order:6+6+4+4=20 per channel.
No ordering factor or scalar seagull is supplied implicitly.

Independent calibration weights every internal light-scalar current
in the frozen canonical TreeEngine by a formal marker. Its entire
degree-two coefficient equals the explicit20-per-core external sum
in the pure198, with-contact236 and complete434 graph inventories,
at a nonopposite rational state. All contact, heavy and Einstein
differences from the leading-only grouping are nonzero. Canonical
vertices and original SymPy are unchanged. These finite calibrations
supplement the exhaustive graph bijection and general identities;
they do not replace the continuous-domain proof.
