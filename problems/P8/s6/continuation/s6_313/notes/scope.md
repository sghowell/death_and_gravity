# Boundary of the result

The result is a finite signed two-real tree measure in a precisely
specified, gauge-consistent overlap subtraction. It covers the whole
original434 tree, both state-correct one-real faces, all emitted
directions and all nonforward hard Born angles on the compact energy
window. The proof does not use a hard-angle or energy-sharing cut.

It is not the complete inclusive detector observable. Its integrated
counterterms must still be matched to the finite virtual terms in
one common convention. S309 uses a different one-residual dressed
decomposition; adding the present result to that expression without
deriving their overlap would double-count terms. No such sum is made.

Higher nonleading radiation multiplicities, radiative hard loops,
finite physical matching coordinates, all-loop unitarity and the
interacting quantum state remain unproved. Absolute complex Regge
control and the original common-parent field/derivative/state/cutoff
bounce estimates remain open as well. The real-energy complex tube
used for a local Cauchy derivative is not an asymptotic complex-s
Regge bound.
