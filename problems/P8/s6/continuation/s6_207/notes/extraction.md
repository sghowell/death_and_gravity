# Finite retained powers, all time jets and the remainder

Each source derivative in the integration-by-parts operator introduces a factor x from g = x ghat, while the complete pair product contributes x^-2 and the exterior inverse phase contributes x. Therefore endpoint j is exactly x^(j-1) times its normalized analytic row H_j(x).

The radial measure is k^2 dk. A term x^(j-1+d) can fail absolute integrability only when j-1+d <= 3. Thus the retained degrees are

j = 0: d = 0,...,4,
j = 1: d = 0,...,3,
j = 2: d = 0,...,2,
j = 3: d = 0,1,
j = 4: d = 0.

There are fifteen endpoint/power slots and sum_(j=0)^4 (5-j)(j+1) = 35 source time-jet entries. These are inverse-radius slots, not a claim that each individual coefficient diverges after the complete current/contact contractions.

Analytic composition preserves the filtration in which each occurrence of P carries at least one x. Consequently the degree-d coefficient has spatial degree at most d. The time derivatives do not alter this count: comoving P and r are held fixed.

The actual third/fourth W8 frequency terms start at x^6/x^8. Analytic multiplication, roots and inversion with nonzero constant terms, and source-time differentiation, do not lower their order. Hence they do not affect the retained degree-at-most-four ultraviolet jets. Full finite-momentum amplitudes still use all four coefficients.

After subtracting the stated exact jets, the first omitted power for every endpoint is x^4. Cauchy's remainder estimate on the fixed-P analytic disc bounds it by C k^-4 for sufficiently large k. Integrating k^2 k^-4 gives a finite tail proportional to 1/K. The constant and threshold can depend on the bounded external-P set; no explicit uniform all-P norm is claimed.

The original sharp mask remains on the integrands. These statements do not finish its conversion artifacts, the complete one-leg contact or the fixed finite covariant matching.
