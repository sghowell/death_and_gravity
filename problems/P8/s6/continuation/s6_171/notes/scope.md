# What changed and what remains open

The new result supplies an absolute free curved one-loop tensor,
not just a difference of states. Its physical geometry and states
are exactly S6.170. Its vacuum/pole-mass reference is S6.168 and its
flat limit is S6.169. The evanescent curvature basis and quadratic
Newton anchor are separately stated new gravitational reference
choices; no older frozen candidate, proof or report is edited.

All previous primitive and matching rows are retained byte-for-byte
in value. One scoped matching row is added. Original V/G/B and P8
remain OPEN; scoped P8(a) and A.20-A.23 are unchanged.

Still required are the full interacting/canonically re-expressed
parent state and stress, heavy/gauge/scalar sectors, cutoff and
higher-loop errors, quantum target matching, and a controlled
background with perturbation response. Positive-kinetic canonical
classical matter plus a small prescribed free stress estimate does
not automatically produce a bounce. An ansatz failure would not
exclude every completion or the whole original DHOST ladder row.

Vacuum decoupling, full contour/cut and truncation estimates, and
the fixed finite-gravity IR/Regge remainder are separate live
obligations. No global vacuum-to-bounce scattering history or
extra all-UV theorem is added to the adopted conditional contract.
Missing estimates are research work, not a new user-choice blocker.
