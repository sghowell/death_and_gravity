# Source ownership and external-theorem boundary

S310 already supplied the selected covariant action and its complete
zero/one/two-real source. Its finite implementation explicitly stops at
Phi^2 h^3,H^2 h^3,H Phi^2 h^2,Phi^4 h^2,EH3,EH4. A third real
graviton requires higher vertices; changing the old cardinality guard
alone would miss diagrams and fail the full Ward identity.

This successor leaves S310 and all its hashes untouched. It derives a
new arbitrary-order coefficient engine and overrides only the vertex
dispatch of the same general root-current recursion. No coupling is
retuned. The derivative Proca/Gaussian and finite hard-loop work in
other checkpoints is not reclassified as part of this bare tree sum.
Heavy and light loop corrections remain separate.

S314's probability matching and state-transport connector also remain
unchanged. A new N3 source does not supply its soft subtraction,
virtual completion or an improved inclusive rate.

The primary [Akhoury-Saotome-Sterman paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1109.0270),
sections IV/V, proves fixed-order infrared collinear cancellation for
wide-angle amplitudes. Its scope does not give the quantitative
multiplicity growth or forward-uniform remainder needed here.
The [Okui-Yunesi gravity SCET paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1710.07685),
sections3.3/3.4, constructs leading-power interactions with hard
matching at a chosen perturbative order. Its discussion of a further
Glauber/Regge extension is not such an estimate either. The relevant
complete PDF pages were visually inspected. These structural results
guide the research but are not invoked as an unproved numerical bound.
