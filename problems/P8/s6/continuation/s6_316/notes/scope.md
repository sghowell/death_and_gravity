# Established result and the remaining quantitative step

Established for the unchanged finite-tree source:
the complete one-off-shell graviton current with free physical leaves
is conserved; each fixed physical pair branch factors through that
current; its explicit pair propagator obeys the conditional S311
angular inequality; and matching-forest inclusion-exclusion accounts
for all overlapping pair classes. Exact complete-source and
counterexample calculations independently calibrate these statements.

The result does not say that a completed remainder has an angle-uniform
norm. Its additional graviton propagators can approach other singular
configurations. Multiple pair intersections have multi-off-shell
remainders, where free-leaf conservation no longer follows.
The isolated-cluster counterexample shows that a local independent
cluster norm bound cannot be obtained solely from conservation of an
outer source, even when that source is the actual original hard current.

Still needed for an all-N nonleading-radiation estimate are a quantitative
gauge-complete treatment of nested and simultaneous collinear regions,
the soft-energy dependence of the completed currents, and multiplicity
growth compatible with the detector phase space and Bose factors.
Separate vertex and graph majorants from S315 do not fill that gap.
The finite source also does not supply radiative hard loops or full
real-virtual/evanescent matching.

Original interacting quantum-state/limit, absolute complex Regge and
common-parent bounce requirements remain. The finite physical-edge
identities do not imply analyticity on an unrestricted complex domain.
No finite matching coefficient is chosen or set to zero.

All nine primitive records, every earlier matching record, all six
historical qualifications and the rejection of S279 are retained.
Original V/G/B/P8 remain OPEN. Scoped P8(a) is unchanged.
