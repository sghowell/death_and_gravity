# Conservation of a complete one-off-shell root

## Finite labeled coefficient proof

Work in the finite commutative coefficient ring generated by separately
labeled nilpotent wave parameters z_i, with z_i^2=0. This is only a
device for extracting multilinear tree coefficients; it is not a
convergence assertion. Every nonempty proper subset labels one current.

Let E_g^{mu nu},E_Phi,E_H be the Euler derivatives of the UNGAUGEFIXED
covariant action, with E_g a symmetric density. Diffeomorphism invariance,
after one integration by parts, gives the off-shell identity

-2*d_mu(g_(nu rho)*E_g^(mu rho))
+E_g^(mu rho)*d_nu g_(mu rho)
+E_Phi*d_nu Phi+E_H*d_nu H=0.

Canonical rescaling does not change the zero identity. Expand about
the same Minkowski vacuum with vanishing Euler derivatives.

At a singleton, physical on-shell scalar/heavy waves and physical
graviton TT waves solve the free Euler equations. Assume that all
proper-subset coefficients have been constructed by the root-current
recursion and solve the full ungaugefixed Euler equations through
those coefficients. At the next subset S, every product on the right
of the Noether identity contains an Euler coefficient on a proper
subset and therefore vanishes. Only the background metric multiplying
the divergence of E_g[S] survives.

Write E_g[S]=L_g h[S]+J[S], where J[S] is the amputated nonlinear source
assembled from the lower currents and all allowed vertices.
The linearized Einstein Euler operator has identically zero divergence,
irrespective of h[S]. Consequently P_mu J[S]^(mu nu)=0.

The linear de Donder inverse maps this conserved source into a field
obeying the linear harmonic constraint and solving L_g h[S]=-J[S].
Indeed h[S]=-trace_reverse(J[S])/P^2 has vanishing divergence of its
trace reverse precisely when P_mu J[S]^(mu nu)=0. Thus the coefficient
solves the ungaugefixed equation, not just a gauge-fixed equation.
The scalar/heavy inverse kinetic operators solve their coefficient
equations as well. This closes the induction. All inverses used here
are required to exist; the theorem is an identity off internal poles.

The same finite root-cut bijection as S315 identifies J[S] with the
COMPLETE amputated tree current. Hence P^T eta R=0. Nothing in the
proof requires the final root P to be on shell.

## Exact scope of the premise

The free leaves must solve their linear Euler equations. If a collapsed
subtree is inserted as a new off-shell leaf, its linear Euler residual
does not vanish. The proper-subset step above then has extra source
terms, so transversality cannot be inferred. A conserved tensor placed
on that leaf does not make it a free on-shell solution.

Likewise, removing some root partitions destroys the cancellation
between nonlinear Euler terms. A single cubic descendant attached to
an off-shell outer source is not a complete current with free leaves.

Ghost lines without external ghosts cannot make a tree contribution
in this sector. Linear de Donder gauge changes only the inverse used
to solve each free coefficient; it supplies no omitted nonlinear
gauge-fixing vertices here. No loop Ward identity is asserted.

## Calibrations, not the general proof

The symbolic-r complete pure-three-soft root has four trees and zero
Ward residual. Removing its Einstein quartic leaves three trees and a
nonzero residual. The complete pure-four-soft root has26 trees and
zero residual. Each completed434-tree hard root in the full three-real
factorization is conserved at both diagnostic and original parameters.
A separately computed two-off-shell434-tree remainder is not conserved.
