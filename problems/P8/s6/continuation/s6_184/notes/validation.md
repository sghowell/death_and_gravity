# Validation boundary

Exact checks connect the literal full canonical source
to its regular all-Y formula, verify the complete
complex coefficient bounds and derivative counts,
specialize the constrained Hamiltonian, retain all
three positive physical polarizations, and check
the full fixed-profile and family normalization.

Independent tests evaluate the original rational switch
and its removable coefficient on complex domains,
differentiate the full multijet source, check forced
longitudinal energy/temporal reconstruction and integrate
positive-frequency covariance fixtures with all
polarizations. Full-profile derivative and exponent
fixtures retain the fixed coefficient rather than
truncate to its first nonzero vacuum jet.

Fixtures supplement the written continuum, Cauchy,
energy, Plancherel and Gaussian arguments. They do not
replace those proofs with a finite time/momentum grid
or certify a quantum background or physical cutoff.
The written analysis is not FORMALIZED.

The report uses an explicit18-file scientific manifest
and20 fields, source-pinned predecessor reconstruction,
full read-only equality, every-field mutation rejection
and unsupported scope controls. Science/tests/proofs
are frozen before lossless native report capture.
Native, direct science, ordinary and CLI use unmodified
SymPy; only full P8 regression uses its audited GCD
adapter. Frozen predecessor bytes are never rewritten.
