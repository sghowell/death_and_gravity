# Established and not established

Established: both unequal-mass Bose spin2 waves; literal stress and
identical-pair normalization; conditional coupled-pole simple-residue
algebra; full generated matter moment and tail; original quarter-share
and low-window bounds; a whole-Legendre-Q order inequality; and the
conditional finite-transfer error map with an explicit arc contribution.

Not established: a UV tower, complex-J continuation, numerical Regge
trajectory/species-coupling/arc bounds, absence of other UV singularities,
analytic trajectory terms, the high-energy complex-energy contour,
full physical massless IR/all-loop control, or original V/G/B/P8 closure.

Numerical integrations independently calibrate exact written identities
and inequalities. They do not prove contour analyticity or uniform UV
control. The known tail is a formal graph tail, not a physical UV cutoff.
