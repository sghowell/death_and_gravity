# Complete unequal-mass partial waves and pole algebra

For the full crossed Bose tree
M(z)=g^2/(a-bz)+g^2/(a+bz), a>b>0,
the even spin-two wave is

f2 = g^2 Q2(a/b)/(8pi b),  a2=80pi f2,

where Q2(v)=(3v^2-1)atanh(1/v)/2-3v/2. The single ordered exchange
has half this value. Constant C and direct H exchange project onto P0.

For PhiPhi, q=T-4mu, a=n+q/2,b=q/2.
For HH, a=T/2-n,b=sqrt[(T-4mu)(T-4n)]/2.
The gaps are n(n+T-4mu) and n^2+mu(T-4n), respectively.
No equal-mass substitution is made.

The full spatial stress tensors are
S_i=q_i nn^T/2-T I/2 and S_a=q_a mm^T/2-T I/2.
Their graviton contraction has P2 coefficient q_i q_a/6.
S290's complete identical-pair tensor sew consequently gives

2 Im f1_a(T) = beta_a (T-4a)/(T-4mu) f2_phi,a(T).

For a conditional factorized pole r_i r_j/(J-alpha), put the exact
identical-pair phase matrix at diag(beta_a/2). The matter cross term
h phase rr^T + rr^T phase h has only a simple J pole, and factorizes with

Disc(delta r_i)=sum_a beta_a h_ia r_a/2.

There is no double pole from this selected cross term; hence no selected
trajectory discontinuity. An independent analytic trajectory term is
NOT fixed. At J=2 the minimal stress ratio is r_a/r_phi=q_a/q_phi;
the two external endpoint variations reproduce both F1 cuts exactly.

At noninteger J this requires an isolated even-signature Regge pole and
the appropriate analytic continuation of coupled-channel unitarity.
Neither follows from checking the integer-spin algebra. The light spin2
projection itself is regular at T=n because the divergent direct scalar
pole is P0; the unprojected physical resonance remains outside this claim.

For comparison only, the primary study
[Caron-Huot and Tokuda](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.07606v2), Eqs30,41-46,
uses a single ordered exchange before including its permutations. Its
light-mass approximation does not fix this model's two-mass spectral weight.
