# Weak source and force spaces and the explicit graph domain

Let X_r be the completion of the original smooth initial-prepared scalar sources in H13_t H^(r+8)_x, and let Y_r=L2_t H^(r-3)_x. Spatial norms are Bessel Sobolev norms on R3, with the tuple Euclidean norm. The initial preparation is that of the original reference response; it is not replaced by an independent reset at an arbitrary time.

S220 bounds the full ordered response by1e117 V03[D]U138[G]. Because the detector norm has no time derivatives, its smooth final-germ test space is dense in L2_t H3_x. Riesz representation thus defines an output inL2_t H^-3_x. All coefficient maps and the response are spatially translation invariant, so scalar Bessel weights commute with them. Applying the zero-index estimate after a weight shift yields
Rhat:X_r -> Y_r, norm<=1e117,
for every real r. Multiplication on the output by a^-3 costs at most1 because a>=1. The normalized operator Qbar therefore has norm<=1e-683 on this pair of different spaces.

Let h be such that C Zh belongs to X_r, with Zh=G0h, and let e belong to Y_r. A sufficient smooth core for h consists of smooth compact-momentum drives with the original initial zero neighborhood: the finite-momentum ODE and fixed smooth coefficients preserve all required time jets and that germ. No numerical bound on high stress jets is inferred.

For this input, define the Schur graph domain
D_T={g in Y_r : Tg in X_r}, T=D+C G0F.
Then the force equation [I-Qbar T]g=e+Qbar C Zh is meaningful. The original formulation instead requires s=CZh+Tg inX_r; the two domain conditions agree because C Zh is already inX_r. The reversible argument in feedback.md takes place on precisely this graph.

No claim is made that D_T is dense, that it is invariant under a proposed iteration, that the equation is onto, or that an inverse exists or is bounded. Defining a graph domain is not a substitute for proving any of these facts. Likewise writing four phase coordinates does not reduce the full quantum evolution to a four-dimensional local ODE; the force histories retain its nonlocal and higher-derivative content.

The scalar P=0 extension is the R3 measure-zero continuation already established in S220/S221. It is not a new global homogeneous constraint or charge statement. The old homogeneous quantum inverse is not imported here.
