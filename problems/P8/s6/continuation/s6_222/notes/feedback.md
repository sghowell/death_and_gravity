# Ordered causal block equation and reversible reconstruction

Let G0 denote the source-pinned S221 zero-initial classical retarded propagator. Introduce explicitly declared mathematical probe drives h in the phase equation and e in the normalized scalar-force equation:
Z'=KZ+Fg+h,
s=CZ+Dg,
g=e+Qbar s.
These drives do not choose new quantum initial data or supply an unproved independent-state source map.

Put Zh=G0h and T=D+C G0F. Classical variation of constants gives Z=Zh+G0Fg. Therefore the metric is s=CZh+Tg and the complete force equation is
[I-Qbar T]g=e+Qbar C Zh.
The ordering of D,C,G0,F and Qbar is fixed. In particular the direct auxiliary block D does not disappear, and Qbar=W_output Rhat cannot be replaced by Rhat W_output.

The same conclusion follows from an exact unit-triangular factorization of the phase/force block:
[[I,-G0F],[-Qbar C,I-Qbar D]]
=
[[I,0],[-Qbar C,I]]
[[I,-G0F],[0,I-Qbar(D+C G0F)]].
This factorization assumes no inverse of Qbar or of the Schur block. Products represent ordered operator composition, not commuting scalar coefficients.

On the common domain stated in the next note, both directions are valid. Any solution of the forced system gives the Schur equation by classical variation of constants. Conversely, a graph-domain force solution reconstructs Z and s, satisfies the full first-order phase equations distributionally, and gives the force-dependent lapse and shift. Reversing the nondegenerate scalar saddle then recovers all original coefficient Euler equations with the full response current. No equation is dropped.

The independent two-time rational fixture contains a nonsymmetric causal response, distinct density weights at its two times, both drives, and distinct C,F,D blocks. Direct inversion of its full phase/force matrix agrees with the Schur reconstruction. Deleting D or commuting the output normalization through the response gives a nonzero discrepancy. This tests algebra and ordering, not existence of the actual continuum inverse.

For formal bookkeeping only, scale Qbar by a marker epsilon and let a prescribed source be S0. Then g1=Qbar S0 and g2=Qbar(D+C G0F)g1. The residual after these two formal terms is exactly the corresponding cubic ordered word. The direct term Qbar D g1 is nonzero in the independent fixture. There is no assertion of convergence, a finite-epsilon error bound or an order-reduced physical theory.
