# Primary-source boundary and unresolved original obligations

Anderson, Molina-Paris and Mottola, [gr-qc/0209075](https://arxiv.org/pdf/gr-qc/0209075), SectionIII, formulate semiclassical linear response with a retarded stress kernel and local renormalized terms. Their explicit stability application is a scalar field around Minkowski space on scales larger than the Planck length. It is not this curved constrained Proca/clock problem.

Flanagan and Wald, [gr-qc/9602052](https://arxiv.org/pdf/gr-qc/9602052), SectionIV.C, describe reduction of order as a declared approximation prescription. They discuss ambiguities for integro-differential equations and note that reducing time order need not remove problematic spatial derivatives. No such prescription, changed equation or pole deletion is introduced here.

Meda and Pinamonti, [Linear Stability of Semiclassical Theories of Gravity](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00023-022-01246-1), construct retarded fundamental solutions for a specified quantum/classical scalar toy model. The paper's restricted model does not supply this full Proca inverse. The references guide the distinction between a causal equation, its inverse and stability, rather than replacing the required calculation.

This checkpoint establishes the complete force interface, an ordered graph-domain equivalence, and bounds for explicitly prescribed first responses. It does not prove a quantum inverse, compatible-space feedback closure, numerical high stress jets, a finite-coupling remainder, nonlinear solution, quantum stability, physical cutoff or heavy-sector control.
