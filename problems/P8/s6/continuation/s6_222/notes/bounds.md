The huge exp(1e29) factor and unevaluated C13 also preclude a smallness conclusion from the explicit density factor alone.

This is a limitation of the current estimates, not a proof of instability or nonexistence. These comparison multipliers are not the actual Proca response; they show why neither implication can be drawn from the norm data alone.
