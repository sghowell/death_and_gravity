# Literal force-dependent constraints and all phase equations

Use the complete S220 scalar coefficient action, including DeltaJ n²+3Tcorr nv+9Av²/2, and put J=Jc in the following algebra. The normalized classical Legendre definitions are
pv=-6vdot+6Theta n-3ell sigma+2b,
ps=sigmadot+wn.
They are auxiliary phase definitions in the full forced system, not a declaration of a four-component quantum Hamiltonian.

The normalized scalar force pairs with s=(n,zeta,b). The first clock map zeta=v+delta n makes its clock components jn=gn+delta gzeta, jv=gzeta, jb=gb. Our convention is Euler_L+j=0; consequently momentum'+3H momentum=L_field+j_field. Both quantum current signs and local contacts are inherited from the complete ordered S220 response.

The shift equation is2(vdot-Theta n)+ell sigma+gb=0. Combining it with the first momentum gives b=(pv-3gb)/2. Substitute both velocities and this full shift in the lapse equation. The gb terms cancel there, leaving2J n-Lc+jn=0, where
Lc=Theta pv-w ps+3Theta ell sigma-(2Eq+3Tcorr)v.
Thus n=(Lc-jn)/(2J). Solving all four saddle equations directly gives the same result; their determinant is-8J, nonzero even at Theta=0.

The full four phase equations retain the force changes:
vdot=vdot_classical-Theta jn/(2J)-gb/2;
sigmadot=sigmadot_classical+w jn/(2J);
pv'=pv'_classical-(2Eq+3Tcorr)jn/(2J)+gzeta;
ps'=ps'_classical+3ell Theta jn/(2J).
In the last row the gb terms cancel between the velocity and shift contributions. All four classical rows include the original weighted momentum damping.

Write C for the exact classical map s=CZ. The full interface is
s=CZ+Dg, Z'=KZ+Fg,
D=-u u^T/(2J)-(3/2)e_b e_b^T,
u=(1,delta,0), e_b=(0,0,1), F=-Jcan C^T.
These identities retain all finite-q, A,Tcorr and current Jc terms. There is no Theta or inverse-q denominator.

D is symmetric negative semidefinite for J>0. Its eigenvalues are0,-(1+delta²)/(2J),-3/2, with null direction(-delta,1,0). This local sign and rank do not give the sign or invertibility of the full retarded operator.

Here b always means the physical shift divergence of S220. The complementary coordinate called b in S221 is instead y_b=-pv/(2q), and must not be confused with it. The full force histories contain all nonlocal and higher-time-derivative content of the quantum equations; no additional quantum mode is removed by this rewriting.
