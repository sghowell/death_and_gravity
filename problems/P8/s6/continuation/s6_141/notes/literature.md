# Provenance and verification boundary

The primitive determinant/Legendre ownership
is frozen in S6.137, and its primary-source
review remains in that checkpoint's literature
note. S6.140 supplies the literal cyclic-word
catalog and the shared reference parameters.
The present twelve-word routing, cycle
enumeration, four-dimensional angular identity
and double radial bound are derived in this
checkpoint's written notes and checked by
independent symbolic and numerical tests.

No external theorem that renormalizability
implies UV completion is used. No pole-mass
or exact gauge spectrum assertion is borrowed.
The MS subtraction is only the degree-zero
overall contact for these specific graphs;
all proper cycles are explicitly UV finite.

The analytic integration, regulator-limit and
symmetry arguments are written proofs, not
proof-assistant formalization or independent
peer review. Exact checks validate their finite
identities, combinatorics, domain constants
and actual parameter inequalities.
