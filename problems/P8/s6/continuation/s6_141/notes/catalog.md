# Twelve complete opposite-chord words

Start with the exact S6.140 six-vertex cyclic
catalog. The internal boson endpoints split
the fermion cycle into arcs of three fermion
propagators and two external Phi vertices each.
There are twelve such words per scalar/gauge
sector. The full permutation group of the
four labelled external legs preserves this
subset. Both fermion-loop orientations remain.

In -Tr log(1+S V), the sixth-order coefficient
is +1/6. Choosing the two boson positions and
differentiating four labelled Phi fields gives
15 times 24 terms; quotienting the six cyclic
rotations gives 60 words, each with weight one.
The Gaussian contraction is (1/2) times the
second boson derivative, so the derivative's
factor two cancels it. The opposite-endpoint
subset is 12 of these 60, not six. The scalar
coupling/flavor factor is N Y^3. The gauge
factor before its Dirac-index sum is N Cf a Y^2,
with N=6 active color/flavor entries and
Cf=4/3. Twelve inert flavors do not couple to Phi.

Enumerate every nonempty subset of the seven
edges of each literal six-vertex graph.
A connected degree-two subset is a simple
cycle. There are exactly three: the two
three-fermion/one-boson cycles and the full
six-fermion cycle. Their superficial degrees
are respectively 4-3-2=-1 and 4-6=-2.

Any proper loop-containing 1PI subgraph of this
two-loop theta graph is one of those cycles:
a proper connected subgraph with two independent
cycles would have to retain all three paths
between the two degree-three endpoints, hence
every edge. Attaching a tree does not create a
new 1PI subgraph. Thus no divergent proper
subgraph or proper counterterm is omitted here.

The full graph has degree 8-6-2=0. Its only UV
subtraction is a local Phi^4 contact. Take this
subtraction in the shared regulated MS parent
before removing the regulator. Finite dimensional
numerator products with that overall pole are
also contacts. They cannot affect b2. This does
not make other two-loop primitive rows, their
proper forests or matching conversions vanish.
