# Connected-sheet obstruction and what the fold does not solve

Along the exact field path, the designated spatial point satisfies

    C(N1,s)=0, C_N(N1,s)=6(s-s*).

At s0 its branch is the established S266 one. Suppose a globally C1 lapse
map extended that branch to every finite input on this path and a
neighborhood of its endpoint. Local uniqueness at eachs<s* forces its
value at the designated point to stayN1. Continuity then givesN1 at s*.
If the full field branch already fails at another point earlier, the
hypothesized global regular extension has already failed.

At the endpoint vary ONLY the nonzero tensor momentum amplitude,
holding the scalar configuration and matter momentum fixed. This is a
smooth transverse variation of shear becauseA*=sqrt(s*/2)>0. The full
spatial momentum constraints continue to hold. A C1 root would obey

    0=C_N N_s+C_s=0*N_s+3,

a contradiction. This excludes a globally C1 extension of THIS local
sheet, not a disconnected remote root chosen by a different prescription.

Because C_s=3, solve the entire C=0 for s as a function ofN near the fold.
The exact solve is s(N)=-C(N,s0)/C_s(N), keeping full source functions.
Its first derivative is zero and its second is-D*/3<0 atN1. Hence it has
a nondegenerate maximum: for transverse s just below s* there are two
nearby positive roots and just above s* there are none nearby.

The original K-first temporal calculation is REGULAR here: R=Gamma=N=1.
Atp=G=B=Href=0 the ENTIRE raw Hamiltonian is

    H_raw=H-NR^(-3/4)T^2/(2Gamma).

AtT0 its mixed lapse/temporal derivative vanishes, H_TT=-1 and the full
auxiliary Hessian isdiag(0,-1). This is not the removable Gamma pole
of notes/auxiliary.md.

On either nearby regular branch the exact envelope gives

    (H_red)_s=H_s=2NR^(-1/4),
    (H_red)_ss=-C_s^2/C_N.

The latter diverges towards the fold. The leading local Taylor form is
H-H*=2b+3ba+D*a^3/6, witha=N-1,b=s-s*,D*=C_NN>28.
Its stationary energy has a nonzero order|b|^(3/2) branch difference.
This cubic is not the full Hamiltonian and its remainder is not discarded.

For a FINITE primary/secondary auxiliary bracket block

    Omega=[[0,-D],[D,J]],

withD symmetric andJ any skew2x2 matrix, detOmega=(detD)^2. At the fold
the displayed finite block has rank2, irrespective ofJ. This algebra is
not a continuum functional determinant or proof of a global operator
kernel. The regular-branch second-class measure cancellation cannot
simply be divided through a zero pivot.

General constrained quantization explicitly requires the second-class
measure as well as gauge fixing; a primary-source example is
[Bellorin and Droguett, sections5.1 and AppendixA](https://arxiv.org/html/1912.06749v3).
Their different gravity model does not supply a quantization of this fold.
Model transitions through internal-time turning points also exist
[Amaral and Bojowald](https://arxiv.org/abs/1601.07477), but no such result
is transferred here: this fold has finite positive lapse and has not
been shown to be a clock-time turning point.

Time preservation of constraints at a rank-changing locus, a compatible
branch measure and state, operator domains and unitary evolution remain
to be analyzed in the actual parent. A vanishing finite determinant does
not establish a new gauge symmetry. A formal Airy integral does not
establish any of those missing properties.
