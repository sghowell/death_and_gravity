# Inverse Ward identity, operator controls and transverse boundary

## Algebraic sign, residue and local-operator controls

For arbitrary endpoint inverse values Kp,Kr a Ward-compatible
unprojected representative is

Gamma^{mu nu}=(p^mu r^nu+r^mu p^nu)(Kr-Kp)/(r^2-p^2)
             -eta^{mu nu}(Kr+Kp)/2.

At k^2=0 it satisfies k_mu Gamma^{mu nu}=p^nu Kr-r^nu Kp.
For K(v)=v-1 it equals the literal free tensor above. At p^2=1
with K(1)=0, its physical TT quotient Gamma/K(r^2) reduces to
the free external factor. A possible extra transverse kk form factor
is not claimed absent from the unprojected tensor.

Independent noncommuting operator checks vary every position in
(-Box)^j, j=1,...,8. The insertion is the complete sum
sum_(a=0)^(j-1) r^(2a) p^(2(j-1-a)), not just one endpoint.
Subtract the fixed value and first derivative:
F_OS(v)=v^j-1-j(v-1).
The same vertex/propagator cancellation follows for each exact local
quadratic action, including all metric insertions and its kinetic
counterterm. Omitting that counterterm leaves a nonzero remainder;
using the wrong Dyson sign also fails.

At real null k,p.k!=0, the general two-direction symmetric
homogeneous tensor A pp+B(pk+kp)+C kk+D eta is transverse only
if A=0,D=-B p.k. Its remaining terms have zero contraction with
both physical TT polarizations. A kk form factor is not declared
absent from the unprojected loop tensor. The positive massive gap
supplies analyticity through the soft limit. This statement does not
apply to a four-hard-direction Weyl operator or an off-shell graviton.
