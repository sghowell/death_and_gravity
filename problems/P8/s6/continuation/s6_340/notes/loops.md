# Both actual massive insertions, counterterms and analytic domain

## Explicit metric insertion, not a guess from the flat inverse

Use the actual two-mass loop of S338 with external momenta p and p+k,
p^2=1,k^2=0,r=p+k,d=r^2-1=2p.k. Put

M=1-z+nz-z(1-z)p^2,
M_L=M-2zya,0<=y<=1-z,
M_H=M-2(1-z)ya,0<=y<=z,a=p.k.

The two literal triangle numerators after transverse isotropic
integration have weights z^2 and(1-z)^2. Their exact-D integrands
are -2z^2 M_L^(-1-epsilon) and
-2(1-z)^2 M_H^(-1-epsilon), with the shared Gamma(1+epsilon).
The full antiderivatives are
-z M_L^(-epsilon)/(a epsilon) and
-(1-z)M_H^(-epsilon)/(a epsilon).
Gamma recurrence gives the divided difference of the complete bubble,
including both the light and heavy insertion before pole subtraction.

In the finite limit their endpoint sums are
[Log M_r-Log M]/a, M_r=M-z(1-z)d.
This is the metric variation of the bilocal inverse kernel.
The physical scalar/graviton vertex has the opposite sign of this
operator variation: the free limit is the frozen S295 vertex
T^{mu nu}(p,r)=p^mu r^nu+r^mu p^nu-eta^{mu nu}(p.r-1).
In TT it is2epsilon(p,p), agreeing with the elementary
Feynman vertex -i T/sqrt(kappa) and the propagator i/(r^2-1).

Let B(v)=-integral Log M(v). Its complete raw loop vertex is
2epsilon(p,p) g^2 [B(r^2)-B(1)]/(16pi^2 d).
The fixed mass counterterm has only a trace metric vertex in TT.
The fixed kinetic counterterm contributes
-2epsilon(p,p) g^2 B'(1)/(16pi^2).
The complete renormalized TT vertex is therefore

V1_TT=2epsilon(p,p) Pi_OS(r^2)/d.

All source-volume vertices are retained before the physical trace
projection; they are zero in that projection, not omitted because
the flat amplitude happens to vanish. Both matter triangles are
needed to obtain this relation.

## Uniform analytic domain and what is not being asserted

For the original compact recoil domain |p.k|<4omega<=1/2,
so0<r^2<2. At v<=2 the mixed denominator is
1+(n-3)z+2z^2>=1 for n>=128.
Both shifted triangle denominators interpolate between positive
endpoints. No threshold, collinear pole or hidden1/k^2 arises.
The limit d->0 is analytic because Pi_OS has a double zero.

The full logarithmic remainder obeys
|Pi_OS|<=g^2 d^2/[96pi^2 n^2(1-1/n)] for|d|<=1,
using alpha<=(1-z)/n and integral(1-z)^2=1/3.
Its relative inverse quotient is therefore bounded by
g^2/[864 n^2(1-1/n)]<1e-400 at the ORIGINAL parameters.
This bound controls the selected coefficient, not omitted loops.
The algebraic reciprocal of the truncated inverse is never promoted
to an exact resummed propagator.

With only p,k available, a homogeneous symmetric tensor is
A pp+B(pk+kp)+C kk+D eta. At null k and p.k!=0, transversality
forces A=0,D=-B p.k; the remaining terms have zero physical TT
projection. Analyticity extends the conclusion through the soft limit.
This argument supplements the explicit two-triangle computation.
It does NOT apply to massless-graviton loops with different singular
structure, or to the S336 four-hard-direction Weyl operator.

The selected quadratic external sector consequently adds zero
