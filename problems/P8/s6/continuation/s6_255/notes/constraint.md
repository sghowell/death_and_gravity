# Exact full-current initial constraint root and strict margins

M1 RATE from the full lapse constraint, not from an imposed mean equation.
The primitive can be removed exactly from this initial constraint using
I_N=3 U R_u R_N/(4RN). Define, before settingu0,

    G=partial_N[N U F+9U R_u^2/(16RN)]
        -partial_u[3 U R_u R_N/(4RN)],
    Z_N=partial_N(U/N),
    mc_positive=sqrt(-2G/Z_N).

Apply the complete current R,F bindings and derivatives, then setu0 and
N=1+epsilon. This expression solves the ENTIRE off-clock initial lapse
constraint G+Z_N mc^2/2=0. No clock polynomial is substituted for a full
function and no integration constant in the primitive is re-chosen.

The source-pinned clock jets give atu0,N1

    Z=1, Z_N=1/2, Z_NN=-1/4, R_N=-2, L2=-1.

and T=rho-3P/2. The exact positive central constraint root satisfies

    mc^2=1/100-4T,
    Jroot=243/160+rho-7P/8+3T/4,
    Cnn_root=38/25+rho-7P/8+T/4.

The prior full profile C5 bounds give |rho|,|P|<=e=2*10^-400. Hence
|T|<=5e/2. Direct rational inequalities show

    mc^2>9/1000, Jroot>3/2, Cnn_root>3/2,
    |mc-1/10|=4|T|/(mc+1/10)<=40|T|<10^-397.

two nearly equal numbers. These are bounds for the actual fixed profiles,
not adjustable fluid functions. The entire full off-clock functions are
smooth on the common strip. Continuity therefore supplies a neighborhood
of N1 in which G<0, Z_N>0 and all these needed strict pivot margins persist.

real, positive and admitted. Since R_N=-2 and L2=-1 at the center, it
also has r=R-1 nonzero and L2 nonzero.
