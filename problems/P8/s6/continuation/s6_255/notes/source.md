order-eight exponential and order1024 finite source switches, both fixed

of the clock scalar and X, not independently retuned time-dependent
means. The same field domain and timelike unitary clock gauge are used.

Write Hphysical=sqrt(kappa) hbar, with rates mc=chidot and mh=hbardot.

    V=-n hbar^2/2+JH(u,N) hbar/sqrt(kappa),

field or source is eliminated by a derivative expansion. In particular,
its complete heavy force density is N U(-n hbar+JH/sqrt(kappa)). The
two momenta are a^3 Z mc and a^3 Z mh; only the M1 momentum is conserved.

Homogeneous aligned W0=3(R-1)(H-Hclock), Wi0 obeys F(W)=0 and W-S=0.
Both terms in its vector Euler equation therefore vanish exactly.
Its first contribution to the remaining background Euler equations
also vanishes because both factors are zero. This is an exact statement
about the homogeneous classical background, not deletion of vector
fluctuations: their full source square remains in the Gaussian block.
The 56 algebraic affine-complement variables are reconstructed by the
unchanged regular parent map; the four projective directions use the
same gauge. No nonlinear quantum determinant is assigned by this step.

The data family changes a classical M1 initial rate within the same
theory. It does not alter any fixed coefficient or re-prepare the old

of this finite local action and are not claimed solved here.
