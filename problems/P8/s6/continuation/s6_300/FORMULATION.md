# Radiative-state coefficient, not a complete inclusive amplitude

Use the unchanged original mass-one scalar source and finite kappa.
The four massive hard legs have incoming COM scalar energy E in[5/4,2].
Allow any finite number N>=0 of outgoing null gravitons with positive
energies and total energy R<=1/8, at arbitrary angles. Generate the
massive outgoing pair by the exact recoil map at fixed pair-rest-frame
direction u. There is no particle-count cutoff.

For that state compute the coefficient of an additional infinitely
soft graviton. Its D4 physical angular norm fixes the universal real
logarithmic coefficient a_sigma=K_sigma/(4pi^2*kappa). The corresponding
virtual rate pole must use the SAME state. The D-dimensional angular
limit exists; its first derivative, hence its finite angular conversion,
is not supplied here.

A finite positive angular-energy measure sigma labels the continuous
extension of this coefficient. This is not the original quantum
functional measure or a constructed interacting dressed state.

Only after state-correct infrared pairing compare the known functions
x^a*exp(-EulerGamma*a)/Gamma(1+a), with nu1 and R<=x<=1/8.
No finite hard term, evanescent angular term, complete inclusive
unitarity/analyticity, high-energy Regge remainder, original state/domain
or bounce matching is inferred. Original V/G/B/P8 remain OPEN.
