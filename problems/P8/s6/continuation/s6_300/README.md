# P8 S6.300: radiative-state soft index and uniform multiplicity bound

The complete soft logarithmic coefficient on an exactly recoiled
four-scalar plus N-graviton state includes all massive/massless and
massless/massless pairs. It is finite after collinear mass-log
cancellation, boost invariant, and continuous as a function of the
finite angular energy distribution.

For total radiated energy R<=1/8 on the original compact hard domain,
|a_radiative-a_elastic|<40R/kappa, independently of N and collinear
splitting. State-correct real/virtual poles must be combined before
regulator removal. The subsequently compared known detector power and
Gamma factor differ by less than3*10^-799 at original parameters.

This does not determine the finite D angular conversion or radiative
hard amplitude. See [formulation](FORMULATION.md), [exact recoil](notes/recoil.md),
[complete index](notes/index.md), and [uniform stability](notes/stability.md).
The original V/G/B/P8 gates remain OPEN; scoped P8(a) is unchanged.
All frozen ancestors are untouched. Ordinary/native/CLI use original
SymPy; the exact-GCD adapter remains FULL-only.
