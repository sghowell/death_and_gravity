# Scope and qualifications

The recoil map and soft coefficient are exact on the declared
kinematic domain. The multiplicity-independent estimates are written
inequalities, not fits to the numerical cases. Independent TT
integrals, boosts, collinear partitions and fictitious-mass limits
calibrate the complete formula.

A finite angular ENERGY measure is a classical label for these
coefficients; it is not the missing quantum functional measure.
A positive soft angular norm is not a proof of full scattering
unitarity, analytic continuation or convergence of every hard loop.
The finite first dimensional derivative is not inferred from bounded
zeroth-order angular convergence.

Original V/G/B/P8 remain OPEN, and scoped P8(a) is unchanged.
Frozen sources and native raw originals are never edited.
