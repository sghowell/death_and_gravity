# Smooth recoil against arbitrary total null radiation

Write Q=sum_r q_r=(R,v), with each q_r future null. Then
0<=Q^2<=R^2 and|v|<=R. Set
h=E-R/2, Ep=sqrt(h^2-|v|^2/4), r=sqrt(Ep^2-1).
For a unit pair-rest-frame direction u, define

p3^0=h-r(u.v)/(2Ep),
vec p3=r*u+[r(u.v)/(4Ep(h+Ep))-1/2]*v.

The fourth scalar reverses every r term. The incoming pair is
(E,0,0,+/-sqrt(E^2-1)). The exact mass shells are p3^2=p4^2=1,
and the outgoing four-momenta sum to the incoming pair minus Q.
This formula remains regular at v0. It follows from a Lorentz boost
using (gamma-1)/|v|^2=1/[4Ep(h+Ep)], not a singular unit direction.
For one null q it agrees exactly with the frozen S295 map.

Since Ep^2=E(E-R)+Q^2/4, the compact domain gives
Ep^2-1>=13/32>1/4 and the incoming radius is>=3/4.
The radial shift is
r0-r=(ER-Q^2/4)/(r0+r)<=2R.
Each final energy changes by at most R, since r/Ep<1 and|u.v|<=R.
The boost shift in the spatial radius is bounded by R/2, and the
explicit recoil shift is<=R/2. Thus|delta vec p|<=3R.
All physical scalar energies remain<=2.

For future unit-mass momenta,1<=pi.pj<=7. A changed final momentum
changes a dot with a fixed initial leg by at most
2*R+2*3R=8R. The four mixed pairs therefore contribute<=32R;
the final/final pair contributes<=16R and the initial pair is fixed.
The sum of all six absolute dot shifts is<=48R, independently of N.
