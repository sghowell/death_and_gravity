# Complete tensor contraction and a bounded t-channel contribution

For two conserved scalar vertices2PP F1+(qq-eta*t)F2 the entire harmonic
tensor contraction is

N=4[(P_L.P_R)^2-(mu-t/4)^2/2]F1^2
  +2t(mu-t/4)F1F2-3t^2 F2^2/2.

Here P_L.P_R=(s-u)/4, s+t+u=4mu. At F1=1,F2=-1/2 it becomes
Ntree=2mu^2-2mu*t-su, in the original A_t=-N/(kappa*t) convention.
Linearizing both corrected endpoints gives
dN/dF1=2Ntree+mu*t+t^2/2 and dN/dF2=t(t+2mu).
For the crossing coordinate v=s-2mu+t/2, only the first has a v^2
coefficient, equal to2. Thus the generated matter contribution is
delta_b2_t(t)=-2 f1(t)/(kappa*t), continued analytically at zero, and
delta_b2_t(0)=-Pi_second(mu)/(3kappa)<0.

This is one finite contribution. It is not the full crossed b20, an
improved low-cut subtraction or the pole/Regge combination. The full
F2, including H-metric mixing, cannot affect this t-channel v^2
projection. A constant R Phi^2 improvement contributes proportionally
to sum_a(a+2mu)=10mu in the whole crossed amplitude and has zero b20,
but this does not eliminate other local operators or an R H heavy pole.
The transverse Ricci-derivative tensor in notes/ward shifts the displayed
t-channel coefficient by-xi/kappa and is not fixed by scalar OS.

The strict pointwise majorant
z^2(1-z)^2/F^2 < (1-z)^2/n^2
on a nonzero-measure subset yields
0<Pi_second<g^2/(48pi^2 n^2).
Using pi>3, established by the eight-term lower geometric polynomial
for4int_0^1 dx/(1+x^2), gives the exact bound g^2/(432n^2).
At the actual original g,n,kappa this is below10^-405.
The forward magnitude is below10^-1205, and the entire complex disk
abs(t)<=2 has magnitude<=2g^2/(1296kappa n^2)<10^-1205.
No huge denominator is rounded and no omitted-loop bound is inferred.
