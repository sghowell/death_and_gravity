# The protected mass argument on the actual kinematic unit clock

For the covariant canonical clock Psi=sqrt(kappa)t
in cosmic time and the fixed light-mass units,
physical X is one. The polynomial gate is
identically zero, so Fhat=Psi for every t,
irrespective of the old cubic map's curved
connection terms.

Consequently the protected SAT8 masses have
exactly the argument used in the earlier direct-
clock calculation:

    m_+/-=mF +/- y f_R(sqrt(kappa)t).

Their pointwise mass floor remains 0.99mF.
The first and second mass-derivative ratios
are bounded respectively by

    3.06091215182124273e-103 <1e-102,
    2.78264741074658430e-202 <1e-201.

This removes the specific argument mismatch
that made the unmodified cubic-map extrapolation
fail its small-derivative screen at t=0.
It does not erase that earlier negative control:
the old and new maps differ outside their
shared low-field jets.

The result remains kinematic. The ordinary
canonical polynomial parent is not thereby
made into a bounce solution. A mass derivative
ratio alone also does not bound the complete
Dirac mode evolution in FLRW, metric derivatives,
particle creation, the Hadamard-state remainder
or the interaction cutoff. Those state and
common-parent requirements remain open.
