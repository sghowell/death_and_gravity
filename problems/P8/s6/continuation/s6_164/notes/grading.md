# Vacuum grading and covariant clock transparency

Define Fhat=Psi+q8(X)R_old. Since R_old has field
degree three and X has degree two, the difference
from Fold=Psi+R_old has exactly the possible field
degrees 19,21,...,33. Its first coefficient is

    -6435 X^8 R_old.

The full action/source vertices needed for the named
through-two-loop vacuum observables therefore remain
the same as before. Use the full transformed action,
all generated interactions, counterterms and Jacobian
with physical source J Fhat. No independently
normalized ordinary-Psi source is substituted.

There is no need to truncate the gate to assert this.
The entire map is finite degree 33; higher generated
action terms remain in its definition even when
they first contribute beyond the named loop order.
The same local regulated Jacobian treatment applies.

For a general field/metric variation near a unit clock,

    X(epsilon)=1+epsilon a+epsilon^2 b+... .

The exact (X-1)^8 factor makes the map difference
from identity start at variation order eight.
Its coefficient is 6435 a^8 R_background, generally
nonzero. First through seventh variations vanish,
including mixed field/metric variations. Coordinate
derivatives do not lower the variation order.

If physical X is identically one along a trajectory,
q8 is identically zero there and Fhat=Psi together
with its derivatives along that trajectory. This
statement is independent of any flat-space formula
for the old cubic R. It is a map identity, not an
assertion that the trajectory solves the parent
background equations.
