# All quadratic graphs and their assigned local insertions

Use a hybrid coordinate system H with physical Phi mass/residue,
minimal total interaction poles and the MS fermion/gauge
representation through the orders needed for this Phi result.
The Gaussian heavy H field retains its fixed convention.
This H label denotes the scheme, not a new heavy field.

The frozen S6.137 half-trace and counterterm functional have
exactly THREE fermionic quadratic rows:

    scalar_Phi2_W0_F2,
    scalar_Phi2_W1_F0,
    gauge_Phi2.

The two direct rows include both self-energy words and the
vertex word. S6.145 retains the complete proper MS fermion
kernel on each marked line and both overlapping MS vertex
subtractions. Its note explicitly distinguishes a proper
zero-momentum anchor from a canonical Yukawa conversion.
S6.146 independently retains the entire proper kinetic,
mass and Yukawa insertion
2(nu-z)Pi1+(eta-z)m partial_m Pi1, differentiating at fixed mu.
There is no remaining separate copy of these counterterms.

The insertion row is paired with its FULL dimensional inner
physical fermion OS mass/residue subtraction. S6.148 includes
its fixed heavy-source cancellation and outer MS local
reference; S6.136 supplies its finite slope and OS remainder.
Together with S6.154's full scalar inner OS contribution,
this is the complete first Phi quadratic insertion, not a
scalar-only inner prescription.

S6.154 retains all 32 scalar refinements, the interaction-MS
proper-reference change and every nested term. The remaining
one-loop quartic counterterm insertions, including the fermion
quartic pole, are tadpoles with zero momentum derivative.
Whole-fermion-cycle contractions and H-source allocations
are also local mass terms. They need their physical mass
references but cannot supply another slope or OS remainder.

Thus the complete pre-outer-OS hybrid inverse slope t_H is
bounded by the sum of S6.154, S6.146 and S6.148. Define its
sign by the full inverse, consistently with k0=r0-fp0.
All three imported bounds are absolute, so no sign convention
is used to cancel an error. Vacuum constants and source
values are not declared solved by these derivative facts.

No finite zero-proper fermion coordinate dictionary is
applied: its finite anchors are already restored inside
MS-renormalized kernels. The necessary Phi-induced first
Yukawa shift is nevertheless retained below. External-fermion
second-order dictionaries can matter for other observables;
they first enter these loop-induced Phi functions at order
three and are not asserted complete here.
