operator and the same Hadamard in/out states. The change
is a stronger mathematical bound, not a new physical
renormalization prescription or a modified mass profile.

two-level mode equation. Their final coupling is bounded,
not discarded. Thus every quadratic transition order is
retained. This is not a statement about all Feynman loops
or the interacting gauge/fermion system.

state-independent local subtraction terms. It is not an
absolute renormalized stress bound. A small difference

energy is small. Finite source/counterterm contributions
and background response remain to be controlled.

Dirac stress observable. This is not the variation of the
complete common-parent action with all metric-dependent

kinematic alignment alone still does not make the
polynomial parent support the target bounce.
