# Exact finite-frame transitions and a uniform local state-energy difference

Retain the specified free flat SAT8 external-mass Dirac
operator and its Hadamard in/out states from S6.165-S6.166.
Perform four exact further frame rotations and bound the
remaining off-diagonal generator rather than discard it.

With m0=.99m, Delta/m0<1/100 and tau*m0>=2^20, prove

    |beta_p|<=2^42 pDelta/[tau^4(p^2+m0^2)^3].

The actual parameters give a uniform amplitude below
1e-390 and out-particle energy below 1e20. They also give

    sup_t |rho_in(t)-rho_out_state(t)|<1e411

for the symmetric Dirac T00 with a prescribed external
scalar mass. Both states use the same local subtraction
and finite counterterms. The latter is a local state
DIFFERENCE including coherence, not an absolute energy
or the asymptotic particle-energy observable.

The calculation uses the quadratic operator on its full
momentum space. It establishes no interacting physical
cutoff, curved common-parent state, bounce backreaction
or physical Feynman-loop error. Original P8 is OPEN.
