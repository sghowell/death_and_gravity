# Regular-side solutions of the full source reach a finite endpoint

Choose the fold datum at u0,N1,alpha0,beta0,eta0,ph0 with p=-1/10,
m=+sqrt(1217/200-2P+3rho) and the positive Pbeta corresponding to the
fold shear. The original fixed profiles imply

    D=C_NN>28, J=K>11/1000.

The full C=0 submanifold is regular because C_s=3 and the shape momentum
is nonzero. In a neighborhood, N,R,Gamma and the spatial metric are
positive. All original coefficient functions and their primitive are smooth
enough for the following finite-dimensional local ODE theorem; the fixed
profiles are C5. No analytic continuation in complex time is assumed.

Let x include ALL homogeneous density and reconstructed cyclic coordinates,
with F their complete original canonical vector field. In particular the
heavy pair is live and the temporal vector is reconstructed at its full root.
Define the desingularized field on the extended (u,N,x) space by

    u_tau=C_N, x_tau=C_N F, N_tau=-K,
    K=C_u+C_x.F.

Its derivative of C is exactly
C_u C_N-C_N K+C_N C_x.F=0, not merely zero to a finite Taylor order.
It is a smooth tangent field on the regular constraint submanifold. The
ordinary local existence theorem therefore gives its unique local curve
through the chosen datum. This is a curve of the FULL source, not of the
cubic normal form of a fold.

At tau0, x_tau=u_tau=0 and N_tau=-J. Differentiating once more gives
u_tau_tau=-DJ. Consequently,

    u=-DJ tau^2/2+O(tau^3),
    N=1-Jtau+O(tau^2),
    x=x0+O(tau^2), C_N=-DJtau+O(tau^2).

For tau>0 sufficiently small, C_N<0 and u<0. Since u_tau is nonzero
there, inversion supplies ordinary functions N(u),x(u). They satisfy

    x_u=F, N_u=-K/C_N, C=0.

Thus they are regular-side solutions of the entire original homogeneous
equations, including every fixed source and heavy force, not merely
algebraically admissible snapshots. C_N stays strictly negative on this
open side, and the full temporal/Legendre reduction is regular there.
All spatial constraints are zero throughout the homogeneous invariant
sector. Cyclic M1 and beta are reconstructed by their full rates.

As u increases to0 on this side, tau decreases to0. The lapse tends to1,
so the remaining physical proper time is finite. Moreover

    N_u=1/(D tau)+O(1),
    (N-1)^2/(-u) tends to2J/D.

There is no finite C1 lapse extension through the endpoint, consistently
with the direct preservation obstruction. The curvature statement is
proved independently from the physical metric in geometry.md.

This theorem asserts some positive local interval, not an evaluated
number, a uniform family lifetime or a route from the original prepared
state. For p=+1/10 the first numerator has the opposite sign and the
orientation is reversed; no global interpolation of these families is
asserted. The endpoint datum itself is NOT a smooth-time initial datum.

The auxiliary parameter tau is useful only to prove existence on the
regular side. At its endpoint du/dtau=0, so it is not a regular physical
coordinate change. The original clock gradient remains timelike with
X=1/N^2 tending to1. This is not a scalar-clock turning point or a proof
of unitary quantum branch crossing.
