# Full spatial Hamiltonian before projection

Let E=exp gamma(t,x), Q=E^-1 and det E=1. The physical volume
is a^3. The affine coefficient factor R(u,X), distinct from a
curvature scalar, is1 on u=t,X=1. Box u=3H and uHu=0 remain
true for spatially varying unimodular shear. The full S174
source therefore vanishes exactly on this restricted external
metric family. This does not make an arbitrary such history
a solution of the other parent field equations.

The actual canonical vector action is ordinary Proca with
mass1000. Its momentum and auxiliary constraint are

    pi=a Q(A'-grad A0),
    div pi+a^3m^2 A0=0.

In the Legendre transform the complete auxiliary part is
-A0 div pi-a^3m^2 A0^2/2. Solving it gives the positive
(div pi)^2/(2a^3m^2) term. Thus

    H=1/2 integral dx [
       pi^t E pi/a+(div pi)^2/(a^3m^2)
       +a m^2 A^t Q A+(curl A)^t E(curl A)/a ].

The magnetic expression follows from the three-dimensional
adjugate identity at det E=1. No spatial derivative of E
has been discarded: varying the quadratic form gives the
full operator curl E curl, including derivatives acting on E.
Curl is self-adjoint under compact or periodic integration
by parts. In operator notation

    K=E/a-grad div/(a^3m^2), V=a m^2 Q+curl E curl/a.

The constraint remains an essential positive part of K;
A0 is not a fourth physical oscillator. All four quadratic
energy pieces are nonnegative. Pointwise
exp(-delta)I<=E,Q<=exp(delta)I also gives the full energy-form
comparison to the isotropic Hamiltonian, for bounded gamma.
No derivative truncation of the exponentials is used.

With Fourier convention f(x)=integral exp(i k.x) fhat(k)
d^3k/(2pi)^3, curl has symbol i C_k, C_k v=k cross v.
At the clock M0(k)=diag(V0(k),K0(k)) with
K0=I/a+kk^t/(a^3m^2), V0=a m^2I+C_k^t C_k/a.
The exact identity K0 V0=omega_k^2 I recovers the three
unchanged physical modes and their actual normalizers.
