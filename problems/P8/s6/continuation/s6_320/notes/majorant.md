## Explicit Cauchy consequence, and its strict limitation

Define the analytic scaled amplitude
 G_N(z)=product_i(zi)*sqrt(rho(z))*M_N(z)/A0.
Since |zi|<2wi and |sqrt(rho)|<2,
 |G_N(z)|<=6n^2*N!*(4e17)^N/kappa^(N/2)=K_N.

Every proper denominator and recoil radicand remains nonzero on
a neighborhood of the closed polydisc. For any nonnegative integer
multiindex alpha, ordinary multivariable Cauchy gives at its center

 |partial^alpha G_N|
 <=product_i(alpha_i!)*K_N/
   product_i[(1e-12 wi)^alpha_i].

The amplitude stays bilinear and holomorphic; conjugation and norms
enter only its bounds. This derivative estimate is uniform in angles
but has shrinking energy radii. The tube DOES NOT reach the soft
faces wi=0, and this estimate alone does not yield an integrable
all-N overlap subtraction or probability sum. The explicit proper
cluster pole explains why a global-W disc is not a valid shortcut.
