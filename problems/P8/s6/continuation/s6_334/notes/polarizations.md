# All polarizations and the canonical rank-two representative

For real physical kinematics, the momenta can be embedded in a
four-dimensional Lorentz subspace. After projecting perpendicular to
the emitted ray, their nonmetric anisotropic parts have rank at most
two. Fix their canonical representative by discarding explicit
eta terms from the invariant contraction, AFTER Ward contraction and
TT projection; retain all momentum dyadics. Do not infer this
representative from D4 helicities alone.

Let M be the stripped original matter core on that two-dimensional
transverse span, and G the canonical G_4^alg core. Define

A=M/sqrt(kappa)+G/kappa^(3/2),
B=2mu^2 S/kappa^(3/2).

The full tree core is A+t B. For complex symmetric cores the complete
D-dimensional polarization sew, analytically continued from integers,
is

Q(t)=||A+tB||_F^2-(1-t)|tr(A+tB)|^2/2
    =Q4(A+tB)+t |tr(A+tB)|^2/2.

At integer D the usual symmetric trace-free projector yields this
formula exactly. In D4,D5,D6 the direct literal graph sum agrees with
explicit 2,5,9 orthonormal polarization sums, with every
matter-gravity interference retained. At real epsilon>=0 this rational
form is nonnegative; this is a property of the continued bilinear,
not a claim that a noninteger-dimensional Hilbert space was constructed.

Write <A,B>_4=tr(A^dagger B)-conj(tr A)tr B/2. Then

Q(t)=Q4(A)
 +t [2Re<A,B>_4+|tr A|^2/2]
 +t^2 [Q4(B)+Re(conj(tr A)tr B)]
 +t^3 |tr B|^2/2.

This is a generic complex-matrix identity; retaining only D4 helicities
would miss the t|tr(A+tB)|^2/2 term. The negative control A=I2,B=0
has Q4(A)=0 but Q(t)=2t, so no D4-helicity reconstruction can generally
supply that contribution.
