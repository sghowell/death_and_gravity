# Exact TT dimensional decomposition

Let G_D be the stripped gravity radiation tensor, so its physical
contribution is kappa^(-3/2) G_D. Let S be the stripped auxiliary
massless-scalar-exchange tensor: use S295's three seven-graph exchange
channels with auxiliary exchange mass squared 0, cubic coefficient 1,
contact 0 and kappa 1, retaining their internal emission and both
metric-volume contacts. This is a mathematical function, NOT a change
of the original heavy scalar or a physical extra state.

For every TT polarization (k.epsilon=0, tr epsilon=0), the exact identity is

G_D:epsilon = G_4^alg:epsilon
             + 2mu^2 (D-4)/(D-2) S:epsilon.

Here G_4^alg is the explicit canonical rank-one polynomial obtained by
setting D4 in the invariant-index contractions; at D>4 it is continued
as THAT polynomial in momentum contractions, not as an arbitrarily
chosen representative reconstructed from only two D4 helicities.

Proof: contract one channel with epsilon=xi xi, impose k.xi=0,xi^2=0,
and subtract the right hand side. The numerator vanishes identically.
The auxiliary channel's surviving TT contraction is, with
L=(p1+p2)^2,R=(p3+p4)^2 and J_i=(p_i.xi)^2/(p_i.k),

S_channel=-(J1+J2)/R-(J3+J4)/L-2[(p1+p2).xi]^2/(L R).

The proof uses no special dimension-four Gram relations. Complex null
rank-one tensors span symmetric trace-free tensors in a transverse
Euclidean space of integer dimension >=2: (e_i+i e_j)^2 and its
conjugate give off-diagonal and diagonal-difference generators.
By gauge invariance this suffices for all physical polarizations.
Then sum all three labelled partitions. Independent D4,D5,D6 component
expansions agree with the actual S295 literal auxiliary tensor.

Set t=(D-4)/(D-2)=epsilon/(1+epsilon). The tree correction is therefore
exactly rational in the regulator, with first derivative at zero
2mu^2 S. This is not a finite hard-loop evanescent matching coefficient.
