# Remaining channels and why this is not a small-feedback theorem

The source marker distinguishes interactions that require the H-linear source from effects present when it is absent. The full metric-dependent H determinant is already of loop order1 and supplies the retained heavy mean, response, noise and higher metric cumulants. The clock source zeros do not remove it.

Quantizing the remaining metric/light fields permits mixed effects without any source insertion. For example, two minimal H²h vertices joined by two H lines and one graviton line form a connected two-loop vacuum graph. Its value is not calculated here, but its topology disproves an inference that all mixed heavy effects first appear at the source-dependent orders. Pure light, metric, M1 and gauge-sector interactions likewise remain.

The full current scalar and Ia functions are held fixed in the source comparison. Their other interactions are not multiplied by the source marker and are not eliminated by its order8 zero. No new covariant renormalization or choice of interacting clock state is supplied.

The exact generic canonical source coefficient t² exp(-A)/(64kappa³) is strongly suppressed, and the separate physical forced-H equation has an explicit energy bound. These do not imply a small complete nonlinear metric response. A compatible estimate must include every source norm, derivative, contraction, omitted-loop term and the actual coupled inverse. The present coupled inverse constants are finite but unevaluated; one cannot assume exp(-A) beats them. In particular A=10^420 is smaller than kappa²=10^1600, which can enter a very conservative exponential inverse weight.

Nor is a fixed external Fourier ball closed under nonlinear multiplication. A projected nonlinear evolution would be a different problem unless its removed modes were controlled. The source and graph work supplies a separation of remaining tasks, not a hidden physical cutoff.
