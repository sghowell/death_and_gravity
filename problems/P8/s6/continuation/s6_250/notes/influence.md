## Source-dependent degree statement

Both density sources are order8 or higher around the reference. Their full contour inverse is a smooth formal variation on the prescribed nonsingular prepared histories; every inverse variation introduces nonnegative additional field degree. The source influence therefore starts degree16. The retarded coherent H response starts8; source-dependent metric/light Euler currents start15 and their first response starts14. This retains all full-metric contacts rather than assuming them absent.

A nonzero fixed coherent homogeneous H mean would add a term linear in J, producing an order8 influence and order7 current. The actual zero-mean preparation is essential. These are smooth Taylor-jet and conditional Gaussian identities, not nonlinear norm estimates or convergence statements.
