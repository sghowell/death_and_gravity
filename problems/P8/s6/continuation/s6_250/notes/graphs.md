# Complete source-marker and loop-degree bookkeeping

Introduce a formal marker zeta multiplying the ENTIRE retained H-linear source, including the fixed finite onepoint extension. It is a bookkeeping device, not a new physical candidate or a changed source. At zero coherent H mean, the specified Gaussian preparation and source-independent H action are even under H->-H. Pure nonheavy amplitudes are therefore even in zeta, while one-external-H amplitudes are odd.

The generic bare source vertex contains H and at least8 light/metric perturbation factors, of total degree at least9. The exact source quadratic after eliminating H begins with degree16. Kinetic inverse and density variations add fields, and do not lower that minimum.

For a connected graph with vertex field degrees d_v, internal edges I, total external legs E and topological loop number L_top=I-V+1,
sum_v(d_v-2)=E+2L_top-2.
A countervertex of formal loop grade r_v gives the complete identity
sum_v(d_v-2+2r_v)=E+2Ltotal-2,
Ltotal=L_top+sum_v r_v.
All fields, including metric and gauge-sector fields when quantized, count; derivative order is not field degree.

A source-dependent pure nonheavy graph has at least two H-linear source insertions. Their contribution to the left side is at least14. Ordinary baseline tree interactions have degree at least3, and correctly graded generated countervertices respect the same filtration. Thus
E+2Ltotal>=16.
For one external H, a single degree9 source already gives E_total+2Ltotal>=9.

The generic first topologically permitted loop orders are:

| Observable external legs | Source-dependent minimum loop order |

These are lower bounds, not estimates of nonzero renormalized coefficients. Individual spatial or tensor/vector projections can have stronger zeros.

For sharp finite graph feasibility, join two degree9 source vertices by their one H edge and k cross-light edges. There are16-2k nonheavy external legs and k loops. This realizes the even-E bounds. The actual next clock source jet H delta_u(deltaX)^8 has degree10; pairing it with degree9 and nine internal edges gives E1,L8. A single degree9 source with four light self-contractions has one external H and four loops. These diagrams need no omitted H interaction.

Finite Gaussian moments expose the allowed contractions, but actual derivative kernels, gauge cancellations and renormalization may still cancel particular continuum amplitudes. Graph feasibility does not prove their values or signs.

At the Minkowski vacuum X0, the same source has the retained cubic gH Phi²/2. Its one-loop heavy-onepoint condition is different. The clock filtration is not a vacuum amplitude or quantum gravitational-limit theorem.
