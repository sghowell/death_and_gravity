# What remains open

The result uses the complete source, including every
switch/exponential term and every covariant Hessian
connection contribution. It bounds the centered
Gaussian scalar-force smearing at fixed CD geometry.

At the exact clock, DS=0 makes this source-induced
force noise vanish. It does not make Proca metric
stress fluctuations vanish. The conditional determinant
is scalar-independent at fixed physical metric, not
metric-independent or a full interacting parent measure.

The bound is state-dependent, all-momentum and smeared.
It is not a QEI lower bound, pointwise/coincident
noise estimate, cutoff certificate or a statement
that arbitrarily high modes belong to a physical EFT.

No small or stable full inverse, stochastic/nonlinear
light or metric solution, finite-coupling remainder,
physical heavy/cutoff/threshold matching or interacting
amplitude is constructed. Complete vacuum cuts/contour/
truncation and finite-gravity IR/Regge remain open.

The primitive frontier and every earlier scoped
matching row are retained. This adds one observable
row without promoting any original V/G/B obligation.
No additional user choice or intervention is needed
to continue investigating those missing estimates.
