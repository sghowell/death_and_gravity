# Validation and boundary of the certificate

Independent exact tests rederive canonical normalization,
physical momentum, temporal constraint and covariance
trace identities. Actual all-order Cauchy fixtures include
both inactive and active higher preparation terms.
Numerical ODE fixtures evolve those data with the actual
time-dependent transverse and longitudinal frequencies,
and compare field, momentum and complete covariance
readouts throughout the observation interval.

Independent high-precision complex tests evaluate the
literal full switch, its derivative and complete covariant
jet source, including stable evaluation of its tiny
complement. Directional Frechet fixtures check exact
reference vanishing and the nearby value/spatial bounds.
Canonical pairings are tested separately from the
clock-normalized display.

These finite fixtures supplement written continuous
mode, complex-polydisc, Cauchy and Sobolev proofs.
They are not the proof of a continuum norm or a
numerical construction of a quantum solution.

The report has an explicit18-file scientific manifest
and20 fields. Full equality, source hashes, per-field
mutation rejection and unsupported scope rejection
are separate tests. Scientific bytes are frozen before
lossless native report transfer.

Native, direct science, ordinary and CLI use unmodified
SymPy. Only full P8 regression uses its audited exact
GCD adapter. Written analytic arguments are not
FORMALIZED and do not close original P8.
