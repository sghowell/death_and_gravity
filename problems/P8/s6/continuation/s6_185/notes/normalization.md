# Scope of the canonical estimate

The field dictionary at the actual fixed anchor is
Phi_pert=sqrt(kappa)psi and eta_Phi=sqrt(kappa)eta_u.
Raw-clock variational pairings obey
F_Phi[eta_Phi]=F_u[eta_Phi/sqrt(kappa)].

If Var(F_u[eta_u]/kappa)<=D delta^2 ||eta_u||^2/kappa,
then Var(F_Phi[eta_Phi])<=D delta^2 ||eta_Phi||^2.
Both factors from the unnormalized force and the
canonical test must be applied; no residual1/kappa
is allowed in the canonical variance bound.

D=108m*2048^2+144*(4e8)^2/m<4e16 at m=1000.
Thus the full delta<=1/100 outer ball alone supplies
no small canonical estimate. A sufficient explicit
smaller ball is delta<=1e-14. There the canonical
source-force standard deviation is below2e-6 per
unit H3 test norm. The previous full-source causal
mean transforms to4e10 delta^2 times the canonical
perturbation/test norm product, below4e-18.

This is a fixed-anchor local statement, not a claim
that the CD background stays fixed while independently
varying kappa in the canonical decoupling family.
The delta bound concerns raw-clock background jets;
canonical perturbation jets are correspondingly
sqrt(kappa) larger. No subcutoff norm or physical
stability assertion is implicit in that dictionary.

The deterministic scalar retuning changes mean currents
but adds no source-sector Gaussian noise. Scalar-source
noise is linear in the centered vector; metric stress
noise is quadratic and requires a different analysis.
