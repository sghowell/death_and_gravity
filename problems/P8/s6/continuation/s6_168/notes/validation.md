# Independent checks and immutability

Exact checks cover the alternating Bloch rotations,
both mixed terms, full first-frame Taylor coefficients,
all radial factors, dimensional gamma residues and
finite parts, the prior two-point slope, paired
potential coefficients, the complete anchor tail
and the high-field counterterm degree/forest identity.

Independent diagnostics use direct two-by-two Pauli
rotations, Taylor integral bounds at finite angles,
full radial quadrature, an exact dimensional integral
at nonzero regulator and its finite limit, and high-
precision paired potentials/anchor integrals with
their cancellation-free expansions. They also test
the source/metric distinction and the unsaturated
reference negative control.

Zero mass variation, both profile signs and explicit
invalid input classes are retained. Every own report
payload is passed through the unchanged exact
serializer before freeze. Decimal diagnostics are
strings; physical bounds remain exact rationals.
SymPy Float remains rejected by the serializer.

Private validation precedes source freeze. The native
report is a lossless output of the original-SymPy
rebuild and pins every source/proof/test byte. Direct
science, ordinary replay and independent CLI use
unmodified SymPy; the separately audited exact GCD
adapter is confined to the full captured regression.

These tests are diagnostics and exact algebra, not
validated numerical integration, formal Lean proofs
or independent peer review. Analytic inequalities
and the chosen finite prescription remain explicitly
visible rather than hidden inside a pass count.
