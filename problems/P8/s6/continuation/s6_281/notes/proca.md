# Literal massive-vector stress and complete angular sewing

Use the original canonical physical action
L=-F_mn F^mn/4+M^2 A_m A^m/2 with signature+--- and M=1000.
For all-incoming q1,q2 and physical polarizations e1,e2, let
f_i=q_i e_i^T-e_i q_i^T. The literal stress insertion is

 B=-[f1 eta f2^T+f2 eta f1^T-eta(f1:f2)/2
     +M^2(e1 e2^T+e2 e1^T-eta e1.e2)].

In the final COM frame q1=(-e,0,0,-k),q2=(-e,0,0,k),
e^2=k^2+M^2. For each leg use the two transverse unit vectors and
longitudinal (k/M,0,0,+-e/M). All inner products, transversality and
the complete sum e e^T=-eta+q q^T/M^2 are checked exactly.
For EVERY one of the nine pairs, B is symmetric, conserved against
q1+q2, and has vanishing temporal rows/columns in this frame.

The complete spatial invariants are
Ctrace=sum_pol(trace3 B)^2=s^2-4M^2s+12M^4,
C2=[sum_pol||B||^2-Ctrace/3]/5
   =(13s^2+56M^2s+48M^4)/30.
No longitudinal high-energy limit is substituted. At threshold these
are12M^4 and16M^4; the high-energy C2/s^2 tends13/30.

Full angular averaging and polarization completeness make the spatial
stress covariance rotationally invariant. Symmetric three-tensors split
orthogonally into the scalar trace and rank-five traceless subspaces.
The trace projection is delta_ij delta_kl/3; the other projector is
(delta_ik delta_jl+delta_il delta_jk)/2-delta_ij delta_kl/3.
Their traces are1 and5. Contracting the full covariance fixes their
coefficients by the two invariants above, without assuming a forward
configuration or sampling directions.

The independently computed conserved initial scalar stress couples to B
through Jij=(mu deltaij+2p_i p_j)/(kappa s), p^2=(s-4mu)/4.
Its trace is(s+2mu)/(2kappa s), and
J1_TF:J2_TF=(s-4mu)^2 P2(z)/(6kappa^2s^2).
Thus the ENTIRE nonforward production sewing is
Ctrace (trJ)^2/9+C2 (s-4mu)^2 P2(z)/(6kappa^2s^2).
Multiplication by sqrt(1-4M^2/s)/(32pi) includes both the identical
intermediate-particle and optical halves. Set the physical cut to zero
below4M^2 and take its continuous zero at threshold.

As an independent normalization check, inserting instead the massless
scalar stress invariants s^2 ands^2/30 reproduces the entire original
S278 M1 polynomial, not merely its forward value.
Each spin weight is nonnegative in its physical domain, but the
nonforward Legendre polynomial can have either sign. Forward optical
positivity is not pointwise positivity of every angular kernel.
