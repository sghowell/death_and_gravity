This scoped continuation retains the original full source and supplies
its entire leading elastic tree and nonforward angular convolution.
It derives the finite first-loop elastic cut in an explicit minimal
dimensional continuation matched to the S278 analytic soft prescription,
including finite phase-space and evanescent graviton-trace terms.
The complete original mass1000 Proca cut follows from all nine physical
polarization pairs and an independent M1 normalization check.

Together with S278's M1 and S280's TT result, all physical two-body
first-loop s cuts below the first heavy threshold are specified.
This is not the complete real amplitude, a forward b20, finite-G
positivity or original V/G/B/P8 closure.
