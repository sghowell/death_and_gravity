Negative controls really fail when either contour variable is omitted.
At x=1/5,v=25/4,z=0 the undeformed light factor is zero. At x=1/2,v=8,
take z=1/(10^6+2),n=(1-z)^2/z: omitting the heavy deformation puts
a pole on the real integration segment inside the stated mass domain.
Reversing both imaginary signs violates the negative Feynman prescription.
These controls are in addition to domain and scope rejection tests.

primitive with the independent exact S235 two-light cuts at original n
and diagnostic n=10^6 for v=25/4,16,-5. It also compares the root result
with direct two-dimensional rational contour quadrature at n=10^6,
