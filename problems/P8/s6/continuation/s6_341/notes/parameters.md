# Complete off-shell denominators and recoil domain

Exact parameter domain

Let n0 be the original heavy mass squared, 10^6 <= n0 < 10^198.
The triangle has two light masses 1 and one heavy mass n:
x1=(1-z)xi, x2=(1-z)(1-xi), x3=z.
Let a1,a2 in [0,2] be the two singleton external virtualities and v the
light-pair invariant. Its measure is (1-z) dz dxi.

For the ordered alternating light/heavy box use
x1=(1-z)xi, x3=(1-z)(1-xi), x2=z eta, x4=z(1-eta), eta in [0,1].
Its measure is z(1-z) dz dxi deta. Four adjacent virtualities aj are in [0,2];
opposite light and heavy pair invariants are v and w respectively.
Write e=xi eL+(1-xi)eR, where for the triangle eL=a1,eR=a2,
and for the box eL=eta a1+(1-eta)a4, eR=eta a2+(1-eta)a3.
Thus eL,eR in [0,2] at real physical parameters. Define c=1-e and
b=0 for the triangle, b=w eta(1-eta) for the box, w in [-12,16].
Both full denominators are exactly

    Delta=(1-z)^2 L + n z + c z(1-z) - b z^2,
    L=1-v xi(1-xi).

Equivalently S236's primitive receives T(n+c,L,b+c), with no expansion of
the heavy line before loop integration. v belongs either to the timelike
interval [45/8,16] or the crossed interval [-12,0]. The timelike threshold 4
is excluded by an explicit gap.


The subthreshold light interval is extended from[-12,0] to[-12,4/3].
Its real L satisfies2/3<=L<=4, so the same subthreshold contour-sign proof
applies. This includes the fixed symmetric subtraction point.

## Uniform original routing domain

Original hard energies are<=2 and spatial norms<=sqrt(3). For0<=omega<=1/8,
|2p.k|<=2omega(2+sqrt(3))<8omega<=1.
Thus every shifted singleton(p+k)^2 is strictly between0 and2;
the unshifted singleton has square1.

Every unshifted mixed incoming/outgoing pair has invariant in[-12,0]:
future unit vectors obey P.Q>=1 and
P.Q<=E_P E_Q+|vec P||vec Q|<=7.
The incoming timelike pair is4E^2 in[25/4,16]; the outgoing pair is
4E(E-omega) in[45/8,16]. If a pair contains a shifted leg p+k,
its complementary pair is unshifted because sum p_i+k=0, so its square
belongs to the same appropriate interval.

For an internal-line insertion, cyclically put the split line first:
Q0=0, Qj=-sum_(i<j)r_i, sum r_i=-k. The effective loop with
Q0 replaced by gamma*k has adjacent vertices
r0+gamma*k and rlast+(1-gamma)*k; every other vertex is unchanged.
Every invariant is affine in gamma because k^2=0. Its two endpoints
are exactly two of the external-shift configurations just bounded.
Consequently the whole gamma interval has the required domain, not merely
the finite calibration points. In a triangle one vertex is a pair of hard
legs; the two singleton virtualities are the other two vertices.
Each Qj is a partial sum of at most four hard vectors and has Euclidean
norm<=12. This also retains both alternating box mass assignments.
