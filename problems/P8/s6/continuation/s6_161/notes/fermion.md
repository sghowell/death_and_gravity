# Full MS local vertex and convergent Dirac difference

For N active Dirac color/flavor copies, the one-loop potential
at mu=m is -N(m+y Phi)^4[log((m+y Phi)^2/m^2)-3/2]/Q,
summed over the opposite Yukawa signs. Its fourth scattering
derivative is +64 N Y^2/Q, Y=y^2. This agrees with S6.132's
complete regulated local vertex after its entire dimensional
MS reference is removed. It is not zero, not discarded because
its b2 derivative vanishes, and not added again through a
potential-quartic tree.

The difference A_F(p)-A_F(0) is absolutely ultraviolet
convergent. Work in four dimensions only after that
difference and the dimensional local subtraction. On real
Euclidean loop momentum q, the free Dirac inverse satisfies

    ||S0(q)||=(q^2+m^2)^(-1/2).

For physical 4<=s<=6, |E|<=sqrt(3/2), |p|<=sqrt(1/2), and
|E|+|p|<2. Hermitian Euclidean gamma matrices give
||i gamma.r||<=|r_time|+|r_spatial| for the Wick-continued
physical routes. A route contains at most three external
momenta, hence its norm is below six. For m>=24 the
resolvent Neumann ratio is below 6/m<=1/4. This also
justifies continuation from a Euclidean domain without
crossing a fermion singularity.

Telescoping four shifted propagators bounds their product
difference by 4*6 ||S0||^5/(1-6/m)^4. There are six cyclic
orders, both orientations, and trace dimension four.
The exact finite radial integral is

    integral d^4q/(2pi)^4 (q^2+m^2)^(-5/2)=4/(3 Q m).

Thus

    |A_F(p)-A_F(0)|
       <=768 N Y^2/[Q m(1-6/m)^4]
       <3072 N Y^2/(Q m),

using (1-1/4)^(-4)=256/81<4. The complete fermion
allowance is N Y^2(64+3072/m)/Q. It includes every
momentum degree, with no expansion of a heavy inverse
inside an unbounded loop. Inert flavors have Y=0; N=6
is the active multiplicity, not the 42-state gauge-vacuum
multiplicity.

The separate first canonical field contribution is
-2 k0 A0. S6.114's actual full-tree bound |A0|<73lambda
therefore gives its allowance 146 |k0| lambda. This
factor is not included again in the Dirac vertex.
