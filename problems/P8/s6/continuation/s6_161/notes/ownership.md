# Complete first-amplitude and intermediate-state ownership

Use physical Phi mass one, canonical external residue and the
same numerical MS interaction boundary as S6.133/S6.157.
The complete first amplitude can be written

    A1=A_scalar,MS+A_fermion,MS-2 k0 A0,
    k0=r0-fp0.

In S6.133 the old scalar amplitude contained its fixed
finite contact -sigma and its zero-momentum bubble reference.
The displayed +sigma and scale conversion undo exactly
those old references. The result here is the direct scalar
MS loop. The one field term -2 k0 A0 already contains both
scalar and fermion first normalization. Do not add a second
LSZ factor or the fermion potential quartic again.

The scalar loop is one half the bubble of full nonlocal
quartic vertices. In each channel v its external piece is
C(v)=-L+g/(M-v). Expanding the two full vertices gives one
C^2 light bubble, four Cg light-light-heavy triangles and
four g^2 light-light-heavy-heavy boxes. There are three
channels and common factor 1/(2Q), Q=16 pi^2. The full
two-field local parent supplies the MS poles of L,G,M.
No internal heavy inverse is truncated. Physical Phi
self-energy effects belong to the fixed external reference;
parity forbids an omitted internal light tree exchange.

The full fermion contribution contains six cyclic boxes,
both orientations, and N=6 active color/flavor copies.
There is no H Yukawa or direct gauge-singlet Phi gauge
vertex. Inert flavors contribute no Phi box. All local
fermion references are fixed, not fitted to b2.

For an n-particle cut, L_total=L_left+L_right+n-1.
Through order two on 4<=s<=6:

- Two Phi states give the tree square and tree/one-loop
  interference.
- Three Phi states are forbidden by exact Phi parity.
- Any state containing H or massive fermions is above
  the window. No stable-H external particle is assumed.
- A two-gauge-boson final state needs a fermion loop on
  each side, so its cut first occurs at parent order
  three, as in S6.129-S6.130.
- Four or more Phi states have at least three phase-space
  loop weights; free decoupled spectators have no vertex.

The statement is perturbative at the named order. It does
not identify a nonperturbative Yang-Mills spectrum.
