# Complete second cut and formal improved coefficient

With canonical physical external states, S=1+iT implies
2 Im A=(1/2!) integral dPhi2 |A|^2 for the identical
two-Phi state. The radial energy delta function gives
dPhi2/dz=beta/(16pi), beta=sqrt(1-4/s). The amplitude
is even in the physical scattering cosine z. Therefore

    rho1=beta/(32pi) integral_0^1 A0^2 dz,
    rho2=beta/(16pi) integral_0^1 A0 Re A1 dz.

The physical pole mass remains one, so no mass-coordinate
variation of phase space is missing. Expanding the full
matched A1 already retains every field/interaction change
that belongs to this order. Its imaginary part drops out
of the tree interference. The |A1|^2 term first belongs to
the order-three cut and is not its complete coefficient:
A0-A2 interference and newly available states would also
have to be included.

Let B1 be the full one-loop amplitude allowance from the
scalar, fermion and first-field bounds. On the window
beta<=3/5, pi>3 and |A0|<73lambda, giving

    |rho2|<=73lambda B1/80.

For b2=[(s-2)^2]A(s,0), not the full second derivative,

    Ij=(2/pi) integral_4^6 rhoj(s)/(s-2)^3 ds,
    integral_4^6 (s-2)^(-3) ds=3/32.

Consequently |I2|<=73lambda B1/1280, while S6.114 gives
lambda^2/20<I1<3lambda^2. The strict first absorptive
weight is nonzero on an open interval and zero at the
threshold. No sign is presumed for rho2 or I2.

For 0<=h<=1 the full fixed-order improved polynomial has

    b2_tree+h b2_1+h^2 b2_2-h I1-h^2 I2
       >=4lambda-E1-E2-3lambda^2-73lambda B1/1280.

All quantities refer to the same MS interaction boundary
and the S6.160 matched physical source. The actual rational
values, approximately, are

    Bscalar          5.40364420857652e-408,
    Bfermion         1.10685580061296e-410,
    B1               5.41471276658265e-408,
    |I2|/(4lambda)   7.72019593672917e-410.

The cut-subtracted total relative allowance remains below
10^-6 and the displayed lower bound is positive. B1 is an
absolute bound, not a proof that A1 is small relative to the
exceptionally canceled tree amplitude. The loop contact
pieces cannot be inferred from a bound on b2 alone.

This finite-order inequality does not bound the exact
higher-loop cut or establish a global dispersive equality.
