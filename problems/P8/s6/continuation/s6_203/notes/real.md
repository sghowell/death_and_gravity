# Complete real endpoint row and low band

For arbitrary real k,l=-k+P, define nu=sqrt(m^2+|k|^2/Amax^2) and mu analogously. The complete unit-W8 pair has complex-time norm at most Cref sqrt(nu mu), Cref=10^9. Both unit amplitudes satisfy the earlier conservative bound because the removed constant-alpha enlargements are unnecessary. The finite alpha correction itself remains elsewhere.

On the fixed time discr=1/20000, |g|=|1/(W_k+W_l)|<=2/(nu+mu). The complete S6.198 product rule, summing all nine pairs, bounds the jth endpoint's qth source time-jet coefficient by

9 Cref^2 nu mu [2/(nu+mu)]^(j+1) c_jq r^(-(j-q)).

Here j0,...,4 and the exact row sums are1,3,14,91,765. Since nu+mu>=2m, mu/(nu+mu)<=1 and r<1, the combined row norm is at most

18 Cref^2 nu sum_(j=0)^4 sum_q c_jq/(r m)^j
=2.216405898e27nu <4e27nu=2E nu.

This is uniform in external P, including |l|=0 and arbitrarily large |l|. It is not a replacement of the complete pair by one internal leg; the second frequency cancels only inside the explicit upper bound.

No complex spatial frame is required for |k|<m. At a zero real momentum one can choose any real orthonormal polarization basis; the full physical sum and bound remain finite. The single zero point is immaterial to integration.
