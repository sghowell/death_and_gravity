# All-momentum weak limit and original cutoff tail

Adding the unexpanded low-band constant, near C4+C5 and S6.202 far coefficient10^54 gives the exact upper bound approximately2.337193e54, below3e54. For |P|>=0 the powers0,1,4,5,6 are all bounded by(1+|P|^2)^3. Explicit positive-square identities certify the odd powers as well.

Cauchy-Schwarz in time and Fourier space gives

|Q_K|<3e54 ||D||L2 X46[Gamma],
X46^2=sum_(j=0)^4||(1-Delta)^3 partial_t^j Gamma||L2^2.

The time interval is the unchanged unit slab. This is an absolute weak estimate with six spatial derivatives, not a global-time or same-space operator claim.

For the original removed union, either |k|>K or |l|>K. In NEAR, |k|<B0|P| with B0=2Amax/delta; therefore max(|k|,|l|)<(B0+1)|P|. A removed pair forces |P|>K/(B0+1). Its indicator is consequently bounded by(B0+1)|P|/K. The near contribution is bounded by

(B0+1)(C4|P|^5+C5|P|^6)/K.

The SAME six-spatial-derivative weight controls this tail, without adding a derivative.

For |k|<m, max(|k|,|l|)<m+|P|. The removed-pair indicator is at most(m+|P|)/K, valid for all K>=m including K=m. This gives(m+1) times the low-band constant divided byK in the same norm.

S6.202 already bounds the far removed union by10^58/K. Adding all three tails gives the exact rational numerator approximately4.188729e60, below5e60. Therefore

|Q-Q_K|<5e60 ||D||L2 X46[Gamma]/K.

The absolute majorants prove dominated convergence and give the stated rate. Both original internal momenta remain; no regulator is replaced by a one-leg band.
