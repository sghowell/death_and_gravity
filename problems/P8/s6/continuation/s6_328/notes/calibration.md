# Exact algebra, boundary approaches and missing-diagonal control

Generic identities verify the pair-sum transfer, mixed and null ratio
rewrites, double-integral diagonal and ambient TT projection.
The exact original five radiative calibration states and real/complex
polarizations reproduce the frozen full F and G away from excluded
points. Collinear atom splitting remains exact at the new complete
coefficient boundary.

Independent tests approach one-, two- and four-ray collinear states
along two azimuthal paths and several angular scales, using the
ORIGINAL rejecting API off the boundary and the new continuous value
at the boundary. Separate tests keep the isolated current's opposite
azimuthal limits. A missing-diagonal check shows that dropping an
atom's self term disagrees with the original complete coefficient.
Moving positive atomic measures approach a limit atom while their
total momentum and induced recoil vary, exercising the measure
extension in addition to trivial exact splitting.

These finite tests calibrate signs, normalization, domains and diagonal
bookkeeping. They do not prove all-path joint continuity or uniform
convergence. Those claims rely on the explicit singular-intersection
proof and compact finite-net measure argument, not sample agreement.
