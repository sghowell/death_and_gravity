# Positive Borel angular energy and uniform weak continuity

The kernels are continuous on the compact domain of their unit angular
arguments, unit TT tensors and physical massive momenta. Replace the
finite sums by int sum_i K_M d sigma and
(1/2)int int K_N d sigma d sigma, for any finite positive Borel sigma
with massR<=1/8. This includes atoms, diffuse parts and mixtures.
The product-measure diagonal is included, with its established
continuous kernel value. Integrals are finite by compact boundedness.

Avoid an unjustified global helicity frame. With P=I-n n^T, use the
ambient symmetric TT projector
T -> P T P-(1/2)P tr(P T).
It is continuous in n, orthogonal in Frobenius norm, and projects
onto the two-dimensional TT space. Applying the coefficient kernels
to a fixed ambient symmetric matrix basis represents them as a
globally continuous TT tensor field. Finite-dimensional component
convergence is equivalent to convergence in the angular TT norm.

Weak sigma_m=>sigma implies convergence of masses and first moments,
hence(R_m,Q_m)->(R,Q). The original recoil is a continuous function
of these moments and(E,u), with its physical gaps bounded away from
zero. Its change therefore perturbs the continuous kernels uniformly
on the compact domain.

For completeness, product-measure convergence need not be assumed.
Cover S2 by finitely many small balls and choose continuous nonnegative
partition functions phi_i subordinate to this cover, summing to1.
Uniform continuity approximates a continuous double kernel by
sum_i,j K(v_i,v_j)phi_i(v)phi_j(v').
After integration each term is a product of two weakly convergent
integrals. The uniform approximation error is bounded by its supremum
times the product of the total masses, uniformly bounded by1/64.
This proves convergence for double kernels as well as single ones.

To make convergence uniform in the soft direction and fixed tensor
basis component, use the compact kernel family in the sup norm.
For any tolerance it has a finite sup-norm net. Weak convergence
controls each of the finitely many net integrals; the bounded total
masses control the net error. Together with the recoil estimate this
proves uniform-angular TT convergence C_sigma_m->C_sigma.

Finite positive atomic measures are weakly dense: replace the mass
of each small partition cell by an atom inside that cell. Their
total mass can be preserved exactly. The continuous-kernel formula
therefore defines the unique extension agreeing with finite states,
independently of approximation. The closed finite-state bounds
<=293 and<=23667R pass to this uniform limit, leaving the explicit
300 and24000R margins. AtR=0 sigma is zero and the difference vanishes.

None of this constructs a probability measure over quantum field
configurations, an interacting state or a sum of hard amplitudes.
It is a continuous functional of positive classical angular energy.
