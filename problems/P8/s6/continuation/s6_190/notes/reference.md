# Finite matrix Riccati reference and exact residual

All matrix products retain their order. Introduce a
formal derivative-order marker epsilon only to organize

    epsilon(r'-[R,r]) =
      2i omega r+epsilon S-epsilon r S r.

No convergent infinite series is asserted. Equating
the finite coefficients yields

    r1=-S/(2i omega),
    r_(n+1)=(r_n'-[R,r_n]
             +sum_(j+l=n, j,l>=1)r_j S r_l)/(2i omega).

Every coefficient is symmetric: differentiation and
[R,r] preserve symmetry when R is skew; the nonlinear
sum contains both reversed j,l products. Derivatives
retain frequency order: r_n and each finite raw time
derivative have order omega^-n. Write
||r_n^(d)||<=b_(n,d) omega^-n for n+d<=11.

The coefficient majorants in riccati.py apply the raw
Leibniz rules to this recurrence. The commutator costs
2; the triple product uses the full multinomial sum.
Every omega inverse derivative is bounded by its
normalized jet constant from jets.py. No derivative
of a rapidly oscillating actual mixing coefficient
is used. The supplied reference_jets function implements
the same ordered matrix recurrence without substituting
commuting eigenvalues.

For rhat=sum_(n=1..10)r_n, all lower-order terms cancel
in the exact residual F=rhat'-2i omega rhat-[R,rhat]
-S+rhat S rhat, leaving

    F=r10'-[R,r10]
      +sum_(1<=j,l<=10, j+l>=10)r_j S r_l.

Thus, at every omega>=m,

    ||rhat||<=sum_n b_(n,0)m^-n <1/100,
    ||F||<=C omega^-10,

where
C=b_(10,1)+2R_0 b_(10,0)
  +S_0 sum_(j+l>=10)b_(j,0)b_(l,0)m^-(j+l-10).
The exact rational C is below3.7e23. Its bound and the
small-reference inequality are independently checked.
This statement holds for every admitted time history,
not just a finite set of mode fixtures.

For the exact pure-state graph, e=r-rhat obeys

    e'=2i omega e+[R,e]-e S r-rhat S e-F.

The fast scalar phase and the time-ordered orthogonal
rotation generated by R preserve the matrix operator
norm: their homogeneous solution is
exp(2i integral omega) O e0 O^T. Since ||r||<1 and
||rhat||<1/100, the remaining coefficient norm is at
most11(1+1/100)<12. Duhamel and Gronwall on the unit
slab therefore give

    ||e(t)|| <1e6 (||e(t0)||+C nu_minus^-10),

for any common positive frequency lower bound nu_minus.
Here exp(12)<3^12<1e6. Unlike the earlier Dyson
majorant, the exponent contains no positive power
of momentum. The initial mismatch is still required;
state.md supplies it for the actual unchanged state.
