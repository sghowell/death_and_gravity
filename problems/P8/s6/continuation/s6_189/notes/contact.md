# What this supplies to the full response

S6.186 controls centered stress covariance. C-number
terms in the mean prescription cancel from that
fixed-background covariance. They do NOT disappear
when the physical metric dependence of the mean
current is varied.

S6.188 separately derived the exact finite-mode
Hamiltonian tangent and its generally nonzero
second-metric contact. To pass to the covariantly
renormalized continuum, the state/subtraction piece
and the fixed finite local matching action must both
be varied in the same convention.

This continuation computes the latter action's
complete compact TT Hessian. Its finite density,
state choice and scalar profile are not adjusted.
The independent lapse/scale calculation reproduces
the source-pinned energy and pressure signs, and
the direct TT curvature calculation supplies the
spatial tensor terms absent from an isotropic
stress readout.

The remaining state/subtraction-dependent continuum
response, including any local terms carried by that
part of the definition, is not computed or bounded
by this local Hessian. The modewise growing bound
from S6.188 is not promoted to an integrable majorant.
Only after the remaining terms are controlled can
they be assembled into the actual full response.

A small H4-to-L2 local norm does not make the
second-order tree inverse a contraction: derivatives
are lost in that naive composition. No extra
higher-derivative solution is discarded, and no
pole, cutoff or stable finite-coupling inverse is
deduced from the local coefficient.
