# Scope and unchanged frontier

canonical vector normalization, mass, kappa,
original scalar profile, M1 and matter frame
are retained. The response is for compact smooth
spatial TT metric variations on the actual CD slab.

This is the complete Hessian of the specified
finite local density, not the entire determinant
or every finite term in the separate mode/subtraction
response. It is not a full tensor inverse, a
finite-coupling or nonlinear feedback bound,
a scalar/mixed response, or a quantum background.

All spatial derivatives and the two TT polarizations
are kept. A homogeneous mode or sampled Fourier
grid is not substituted for the continuous norm.
The fourth-order term is retained rather than
removed by an unstated order reduction.

promoting any primitive or prior matching row.

Scoped P8(a) and A.20-A.23 are unchanged.

remaining contacts, interacting parent measure/loops
and full feedback, physical heavy/cutoff/threshold
