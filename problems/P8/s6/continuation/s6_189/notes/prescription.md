# Same covariant finite action, with independent physical signs

The S6.176 prescription, source-pinned transitively here,
has at mu=m the finite local Lorentzian density

    [5m^4/2+5m^2 R_old/3-4a4_scalar]/(64pi^2),
    a4_scalar=C^2/120-Euler/360+R_old^2/72.

It is the local part matched to fourth-order mode
subtraction, not the entire state-dependent determinant.
The four-dimensional finite density follows the
already specified dimensional continuation; no new
finite renormalization is introduced here.
R_old=-R_P8 and the curvature-square invariants are
unchanged by the overall metric-signature reversal.

For compactly supported variations the Euler integral
and the retained box-R divergence have zero Euler
variation. The equivalent local variational density is

    [5m^4/2+5m^2 R_old/3-C^2/30-R_old^2/18]/(64pi^2).

No noncompact history flux is discarded. No statement
is made that the other state/subtraction-dependent
part of the determinant or all its local response
terms vanish.

As an independent sign and normalization check, vary
N a^3 times each homogeneous term before setting N=1.
For ds^2=N^2dt^2-a^2dx^2,

    R_old=6[a''/(aN^2)+a'^2/(a^2N^2)-a'N'/(aN^3)].

Our physical variation convention gives
rho=-EL_N/a^3 and P=EL_a/(3Na^2). The independent
Euler calculations reproduce all six frozen local
coefficients, before their mass/common factors:

    rho0=-5/2, P0=5/2,
    rho1=-10H^2, P1=(10/3)(2H'+3H^2),
    rho2=12H^2H'+4HH''-2H'^2,
    P2=-12H^2H'-8HH''-6H'^2-(4/3)H'''.

This also checks the sign of the m^2 tensor kinetic
correction. A Euclidean potential sign is not silently
used as a Lorentzian stress sign.
