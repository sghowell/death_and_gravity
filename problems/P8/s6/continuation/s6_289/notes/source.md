# Unchanged original source and exact perturbative order

The packet imports the complete source data through S288, copying its
residual dictionary before adding checks. Thus the frozen source and
its cached residuals cannot acquire new entries. The inherited actual
canonical parameters, entire R/F functions, historical qualifications,
nine original primitive records and144 previous matching records are
retained. A separately named scoped matching record is appended.

The fixed S285 H/Proca metric prescription contains whole volume,
Newton, Weyl-squared, R_old-squared and Euler terms. All Gaussian
volume densities cancel before metric variation. This eliminates
their cosmological double-pole insertion without a new subtraction.
The finite H/Proca Newton correction is delta_kappa=2c_R and is
kept separate from their regular four-point amplitude.

The Phi vacuum-volume condition does not fix its Newton or curvature
finite coefficients. Substituting the H formula at the Phi mass
would be a new prescription, not matching; it is not done here.
The positive exact Phi nonlocal function does not resolve that
freedom or prove the full physical Newton residue positive.

The original interactions not belonging to a Gaussian metric
determinant are neither deleted nor absorbed into it. In particular,
the original cubic, scalar self-interactions, mixed heavy/light
production, graviton vertices and other matter-loop diagrams retain
their own perturbative orders and matching requirements.

A local coefficient counted as order hbar enters once on a TREE.
For its pure metric curvature-squared term the counterterm has
at least two gravitons, no scalar fields, and no flat-background
tadpole. Let M be the number of ordinary minimal two-Phi vertices,
G the ordinary pure-gravity vertices and c>=2 the counterterm
graviton valence. Four external Phi legs imply Iphi=M-2. Tree
Euler counting gives Ih=G+2. Each ordinary matter vertex has at
least one graviton and each pure gravity vertex at least three,
so M+3G+c<=2Ih=2G+4, or M+G+c<=4. With M>=2,c>=2 the only case
is M=2,G=0,c=2. Thus only the quadratic metric kernel of that
one pure curvature counterterm enters this four-Phi tree.
This does not eliminate its higher vertices in other observables,
at higher loop order, or for an insertion with external gravitons.
