# Proper-reference conversion and the nonlocal remainder

Write I0=P0+ell/Q+O(epsilon), P0=1/(Q epsilon). The inherited
momentum-dependent interaction insertion is
Lg I0 B(s)-g^2 I0 B2(s)/2, with B2=-partial_M B.
Replacing this interaction reference by its MS pole changes Pi by

    -Lg ell B(s)/Q + g^2 ell B2(s)/(2Q),

apart from momentum-independent outer references.
The B derivatives are finite, so their finite reference change
contains no additional epsilon-times-derivative-pole term.
Undifferentiated mass poles remain in their assigned mass ledger.

At s=1, D=xM+(1-x)^2, a=x(1-x), b=a/D.
The derivative densities are b/Q and xa/(QD^2).
Since D>=xM, their integrals are bounded by 1/(2QM)
and 1/(2QM^2), respectively. Thus the extra absolute slope is

    Lg ell/(2Q^2 M)+g^2 ell/(4Q^2 M^2).

For v=s-1 and |v|<=1, b<=1/M. The subtracted logarithm's
power series and its exact squared-heavy counterpart give

    |B_R| <= |v|^2/[6QM^2(1-1/M)],
    (B2)_R integrand = x b^2 v^2/[QD(1-bv)],
    |(B2)_R| <= |v|^2/[3QM^3(1-1/M)].

The squared-heavy bound uses
x a^2/D^3 <= (1-x)^2/M^3, not a missing extra M power.
The added two-loop quadratic coefficient is therefore at most

    ell (Lg+g^2/M)/[6Q^2 M^2(1-1/M)].

Keep the entire old S6.126 B2 and add this conversion once.
With its unchanged B1, the total is below 10^-18. After
imposing the same OUTER physical mass/residue conditions,
D_[2](s)=(s-1)[1+(s-1)H(s)] has no additional zero in the
unit disc and its s=1 residue is one. This is a truncated
scalar inverse theorem, not a result for an unnormalized
MS field or a global/all-orders propagator.
