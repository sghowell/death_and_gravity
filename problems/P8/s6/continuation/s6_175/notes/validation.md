# Verification boundary

The immutable certificate records source SHA-256 values and rebuilds its
frozen S6.174 ancestry. Every report field is compared exactly, including
its source manifest, open frontier, control ledger and claim scope.

Exact checks include full flat mixed-index operator products, left/right
inverses, divergence and longitudinal response, Gaussian source contact,
the nonsymmetric-kernel variation, homogeneous curl and conformal-time
identities, literal rational bound margins and the leading source degree.
Written proofs supply the covariant extension, causal support theorem
hypotheses, full-function inequalities, Fourier norms and pole packets.

Independent tests vary the original flat vector action, invert dense
four-vector matrices, compare finite Gaussian integrals, evaluate the
actual analytic target/source at high precision on both X signs and at
null gradients, recover the vacuum germ, and inspect below-pole and
near-pole vectors. Omitting source contact, sourced divergence, regulator
or real-time boundaries is tested explicitly. Parameter and frontier
mutations are rejected.

Direct/private, native, ordinary and CLI replay use unmodified SymPy.
Only the complete repository regression uses the separately audited exact
GCD adapter, with its original tuple controls and captured test/source
guards. Runtime recursion and integer-print allowances alter no input
file. Native report bytes are transferred losslessly and independently
hashed; no scientific source is patched after freezing.

Exact calculations and written analytic arguments are not claimed as
FORMALIZED. Finite high-precision controls supplement the proofs; they do
not replace the uniform bounds or demonstrate a continuum quantum state.
