# Exact sixth and eighth canonical density germs

Grade Phi and each first/second derivative by one, so that
Y has degree two, L3/L4 degree four and L5 degree six.
The rational T begins at degree 2n; even after the X
derivatives/divisions in A3-A5, it affects no displayed
degree at or below eight for even n>=6. This is a finite-jet
fact, not permission to discard T in the full action.

The literal tree's degree-at-most-four germ is

    -28/25-2781 u^2/200-753 X/200
    -9579 u^4/200-813 X u^2/100-78 X^2/25.

Through the required orders one may use

    R=1+(-n X^2+n X^3+n^2 X^4)(1-3u^2+6u^4),
    1-exp(-u^4)S=u^4+n X^2-u^8/2-n u^4 X^2-n^2 X^4.

The inverse replacement 1/R=2-R is used only to extract
these finite germs; the full inverse is bounded separately.
Literal four-dimensional substitution verifies the quartic
density is exactly

    lambda Y^2-n Phi^4/(3 kappa)-2n(L3-L4)/kappa,

the same S6.111 target including its equation-of-motion
and boundary-current convention.

The sixth density is

    +3n(2Phi^2+Y)(L3-L4)/kappa^2.

Its nonzero value is not absorbed into the quartic match.
