# Matching and finite-coefficient ownership

The original primitive and matching records remain unchanged.
H8A457 records this scoped complete minimal quartic-gravity coefficient,
its whole scalar-cut check and explicit error bounds. The predecessor
H8A456 heavy-transition pairing and every historical qualification
remain in force. Original V/G/B/P8 remains OPEN.

The coefficient computed here is linear in the original C and1/kappa.
It does not combine all g^2/kappa diagrams, the independent pure-gravity
sector, M1/Proca loops or higher original/EFT operators. Each such
coefficient retains its own matching and error requirements.

Constant quartic matching changes the four-point amplitude by a
constant. A constant R Phi^2 improvement changes the single-exchange
sum by a multiple of sum_a(a+2mu), also constant on shell. Their
single-insertion b20 values are therefore zero without choosing their
finite coefficients. They remain in the full amplitude and its local
UV matching. Higher-derivative operators can contribute to b20 and
have not been fixed by these two zero results.

The inherited S6.278 soft prescription is used exactly at its linear C
order. The calculation derives its finite remainder and resolution
flow; it neither proves physical factorization nor chooses a detector.
The remaining E dependence must not be optimized to claim positivity.
The C-linear interference cut also need not be positive by itself.

A computed regulator remainder is not a bound on higher-loop,
higher-operator, resonance, detector or Regge errors. Those are further
research requirements under the original conditional contract, not
arbitrary numerical corrections for the user to choose.
