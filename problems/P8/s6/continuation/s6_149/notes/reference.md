# Finite primitive constants and what the vacuum reference still needs

At the unchanged candidate the exact rational bounds, printed
approximately, are

    scalar primitive: 3.54193856196148e592,
    all-flavor gauge primitive: 7.41746690538194e593,
    sum of absolute bounds: 7.77166076157809e593 < 1e595.

The one-loop fermion vacuum at mu=m includes all fourteen
Dirac flavors and equals 63m^4/Q. With Q<256 it is larger
than 63m^4/256. The new absolute bound is at most
3.15800818248253e-206 of that lower bound and, strictly,
less than 1e-203 of the one-loop value.

Both scales matter. These are large dimensionful vacuum
constants, retained with their exact local-reference prescription.
Their small relative size is not a global-potential theorem,
a proof of semiclassical backreaction control, or an all-orders
nonperturbative gauge-vacuum estimate.

The previously fixed physical vacuum-energy-zero condition
eventually determines one complete local reference coefficient.
It must include these two assigned primitive contributions,
the remaining scalar vacuum graphs and counterterms, the
order-one H-source square and any other correctly owned
matching terms. This checkpoint neither changes that condition
nor introduces a new freely adjustable source or counterterm.
It has not yet assembled that complete coefficient.
