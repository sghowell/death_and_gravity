# Primary literature and unchanged original-problem boundary

Henneaux and Slavnov, [A note on the path integral for systems with
primary and secondary second class constraints](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9406161),
especially PDF pages 3-7, explain why higher-generation constraints
require a local configuration measure in a canonical-to-Lagrangian
construction. Their discussion distinguishes the quadratic case from
higher stationary-phase terms, makes regulator dependence explicit,
and separates the path integral from the physical-state problem.
This motivates retaining the derivative constraints and local density
here. Their general argument does not choose the present interacting
state, regulator or operator ordering.

Martins-Filho, [Covariant quantization of the Einstein-Hilbert theory
in first-order form](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.25117v3), version 3,
PDF pages 6-8 and 11, uses a normalized auxiliary insertion, retains
source-dependent inverse contacts and discusses the associated
determinant's cancellation. The original pages and equations were
rendered and inspected. That first-order Einstein-Hilbert theory
does not have the present unrestricted torsionful connection,
source-dependent trace update, dynamical Proca directions or
clock/matter system. Its measure, BRST analysis, effective-action
claims and quantum state are not imported. Every finite matrix,
source and density identity here is derived independently.

directions, exact inertia, finite Gaussian normalization, scale

constraint-density bridge. It can be inserted conditionally into an
already specified retained finite functional. There is no deletion
of the original dynamical vector, matter or metric fields.

flat configuration measure was correct. Likewise a full cotangent
map is not by itself covariance of an unchanged nonlinear Weyl
operator. The exact ordering counterexample is retained for this reason.

state data must be transported, not selected again.

source functional is used to remove a physical term. The existing
spatial gauge and its residual translations, the lapse constraints,
and the original derivative-coordinate constraints remain. A complete
covariant/BRST continuum gauge measure and physical curved subtraction

The fixed quantum preparation is unchanged. The source-centered
connection's average is a composite expectation; no solved or
factorized mean is inferred. Conditional source-functional contact

The S256 classical lifespan and sub-heavy growth band are unchanged
