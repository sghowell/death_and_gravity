# Frontier and excluded conclusions

The actual parent, fixed scalar functions and retuning,
M1 charge, full affine source, all-order CD state,
stress scheme, mass and kappa are unchanged. The exact
pure tensor restriction is specific to the clock; it
does not erase generic scalar or metric responses.

This continuation adds one restricted tensor-response
row while retaining all nine primitive rows and all
previous matching rows without promoting their status.
Original P8(b), V/G/B and P8 remain OPEN. Scoped P8(a)
and A.20-A.23 are unchanged.

The result is not any of the following:

- a norm bound for every finite-initial-time detector,
  an uncorrelated quantum initial state, or a
  Proca/CD initial-time divergence theorem;
- a compact-support theorem for nonlocal TT projection;
- a complete tensor self-energy inverse or its
  finite-coupling remainder;
- a reduced scalar/mixed response, nonlinear/stochastic
  solution or interacting quantum bounce;
- full parent state/measure/loops, physical heavy or
  cutoff/threshold and omitted-order matching;
- complete vacuum cuts/contour/truncation or a fixed
  finite-gravity IR/Regge prescription and remainder.

The detector restriction is a genuine scope limitation,
not a whole-row exclusion. No measured observational
prior, global-time norm or division by a vanishing
bounce energy density is inserted. Missing response
and matching estimates remain research obligations,
not a need for a new user choice.
