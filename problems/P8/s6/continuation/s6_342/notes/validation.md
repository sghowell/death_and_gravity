# Immutable source and read-only replay contract

The cold preflight checks all exported symbols, exact residuals, proof
gates, rejected-input controls, full provenance and science tests under
the original SymPy backend. The independent fresh process first rebuilds
the frozen immediate-parent science packets, then waits for one START
after exact final source copying. Its ancestor cache reuse is explicit.

The native process receives one frozen source-count/twenty-field
specification. Every complete chunk is checked before its continuation ACK;
there is no final ACK. Complete raw bytes, all sources and direct input
manifests, counts, matching history and primitive frontiers are validated.
Ordinary tests, the dedicated CLI, and a covering full-suite run are
separate acceptance gates.

Operational receipts and timing records remain outside the immutable
scientific manifest. Later acceptance cannot revise scientific source
bytes or silently replace a prior failed diagnostic. All assertions use
exact algebra, not floating tolerances or numerical quadrature.
