# Covariant Hessian and all noncommuting variations

Let M_f mean scalar multiplication by f and let G_L,G_H be the full
covariant Feynman inverses. With K=C I+g^2 G_H, the frozen Hessian pieces are

 Wloc=-M_(K Phi^2)/2; Wbi=-g^2 M_Phi G_H M_Phi.

The quartic first-loop functional is-Tr(G_L W G_L W)/4. Keeping the two
selected classes gives

 Gamma_T=-g^2/4 Tr[G_L M_(K Phi^2) G_L M_Phi G_H M_Phi],
 Gamma_D=-g^4/4 Tr[G_L M_Phi G_H M_Phi G_L M_Phi G_H M_Phi].

Differentiate before commuting, using deltaG=-G(deltaK_inverse)G.
The triangle has four separate metric positions: two light loop lines,
one heavy loop line, and the K-dependent outer heavy propagator.
The box has four: two light and two heavy loop lines.
The multiplication at K Phi^2 varies by
delta M_(K Phi^2)=M_(g^2 deltaG_H Phi^2), not by replacing a flat vertex
with its on-shell value.

The covariant trace measures and the minimal potential vertices also vary.
Their first metric variations are proportional to tr(epsilon); they are
retained until this vanishes in real physical TT projection. This statement
does not set the unprojected or off-shell metric response to zero.

Generic noncommuting product/inverse identities check the operator signs.
An independent three-site noncommuting matrix control takes the actual
four separate field derivatives and the metric derivative of the entire
trace functional and compares every labelled contribution. Every required
metric-position class is nonzero in that control. These matrices are
diagnostic kernels, not replacement physical propagators or source masses.
