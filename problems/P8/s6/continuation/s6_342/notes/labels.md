# Complete off-shell labels and unchanged finite scheme

For four conserved, not necessarily on-shell, momenta, let
v_ab=(p_a+p_b)^2 and w_bc=(p_b+p_c)^2. The complete selected hard kernel,
before the common1/(16pi^2), is F=F_T+F_D, with

 F_T=g^2/4 sum_(a,b,c,d permutations) A(v_ab)
     Cbar(v_ab;p_d^2,p_c^2);
 F_D=g^4/4 sum_(a,b,c,d permutations)
     Dbar(v_ab,w_bc;p_a^2,p_b^2,p_c^2,p_d^2);
 A(v)=C+g^2/(n-v).

The off-shell scalar integrals are defined by the exact S341 denominators
and physical contour. Cbar's two light singleton legs are exchangeable;
the two opposite mass assignments in Dbar are not.
On-shell, each of the three triangle channels occurs eight times, giving
2A(v)g^2 Cbar(v). Each of six ORDERED box invariant pairs occurs four times,
giving g^4 Dbar(v,w). This exactly reproduces S235 after removing its
factorized bubbles. No relation Dbar(v,w)=Dbar(w,v) is used.

The triangle/box classes and their TT insertions are UV finite. The three
old UV counterterms belong to the full bubble pole already canceled in
S339. They are not counted twice. The existing OS4 condition is linear in
the loop sectors: subtract F_sym=F at p_i^2=1,s=t=u=4/3 once.
It is a finite loop counterterm, not a new choice of the original Born C.
Its real-TT emission is-F_sym*sum_i J_i, with the potential measure term
pure trace. Selected quadratic external terms are separately zero by S340.
