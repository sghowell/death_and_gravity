# Completing the two quartic primitive word catalogs

The S6.140 cyclic six-vertex catalog contains
24 words whose boson endpoints have short
cyclic distance two. Rotate each word so its
short arc contains one external Phi and two
fermion propagators; the other arc contains
three external Phi vertices and four fermion
propagators. Both orientations are retained.

Replacing any one external vertex in each of
the six first-loop cyclic boxes by B,Phi,B
gives exactly these 24 words. The literal
bijection and a noncommuting matrix variation
check the local-vertex normalization. As in
S6.141, the sixth-order determinant coefficient
and Gaussian contraction leave weight one
per labelled cyclic word, not an extra half.

Enumerating every edge subset gives exactly
three proper one-loop cycles: the short
two-fermion/boson cycle of degree zero, the
long four-fermion/boson cycle of degree -2,
and the six-fermion cycle of degree -2.
Any proper 1PI loop subgraph is one of them:
retaining two cycles would require all three
paths of this theta graph. Each word therefore
has exactly one divergent proper Yukawa vertex.

The proper total Yukawa counterterm belongs
to this vertex group. The proper mass/kinetic
counterterms belong to S6.140's self-energy
group. These assignments are removed from
any separate counterterm insertion list.
The opposite-chord group has no proper
divergent subgraph. The three word sets are
disjoint and contain 24+12+24=60 words.

Both affected quartic primitive rows become
bounded only after adding all three groups.
This says nothing about the five other
wholly unevaluated primitive rows, the other
matching conversions or the complete two-loop
amplitude.
