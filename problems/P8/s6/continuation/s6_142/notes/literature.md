# Provenance and proof boundary

S6.137 owns the full primitive/counterterm
ledger. S6.139 derives the proper MS local
fermion anchors, including the fixed-scale
Yukawa derivative and dimensional finite
gauge terms. S6.140 and S6.141 supply the
disjoint word subsets, Dirac domain and
four-dimensional angular integral.

The present paired vertex decomposition,
region-dependent Cauchy proof and explicit
regional moments are derived in this
checkpoint. No unverified external estimate
is used for their constants. Independent
tests cover the literal graph topology,
matrix cancellation, complex shifts and
convergent radial integrals.

The regulator-limit and Lorentz/permutation
arguments are written proofs, not proof-
assistant formalization or independent peer
review. Renormalizability is not substituted
for a UV completion or a global error bound.
