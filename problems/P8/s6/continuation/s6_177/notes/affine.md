# Generic affine construction and the analytic lower dictionary

For each finite kappa use the unchanged generic-R algebra of S6.174.
The coefficient domain still has R>1/2. Thus the full projective quotient,
source-centered isotropic trace mass and the56 exactly algebraic
complement directions have the same nonsingular algebraic identities.
The trace shift and source-squared contact remain part of the action.

Since R_base-1 has a factor x^2, r=Y^2 a(Phi,Y) with analytic a.
Every apparent Y quotient in the canonical coefficients is removable:
r_Y/Y=2a+Y a_Y and r_Y^2/Y=Y(2a+Y a_Y)^2.
This includes a nonzero null gradient, not only the zero vacuum gradient.

The physical metric chart remains

    g_physical=C g_hat+D du du,
    C=R^(-1/2), D=(1-C)/X, C+DX=1.

The inverse is explicit, the metric determinant ratio is C^3 and the
ten-dimensional fixed-du Jacobian is C^9. The physical matter metric is
not relabeled as the hatted metric. All quotients are regular at X=0.
At kappa0 the chart and the full64-direction connection construction
are exactly the existing parent, not a transfer from the old kinetic EFT.

The lower dictionary solves

    q_X+[1/(2X)-3R_X/(4R)]q=3R_X R_u/(4R).

Multiplying by X^(1/2)R^(-3/4) and imposing regularity gives, after
rescaling the integration variable,

    q=(3X/4) R(u,X)^(3/4)
         integral_0^1 sqrt(t) R_X(u,tX) R_u(u,tX) R(u,tX)^(-7/4) dt.

The last expression is real and analytic from either sign of X;
no square root of negative X is being chosen. The straight segment
from0 toX stays in the coefficient strip and R stays positive.
On each compact subdomain, derivatives under the t integral are
bounded by an integrable multiple of sqrt(t). In a sufficiently small
complex neighborhood of each compact segment, R stays nonzero and
its positive-real fractional-power branch extends analytically.
The parameter integral is therefore locally analytic. Its convergent
power series gives q=O(X^4). Away from zero the analytic ODE continues
q uniquely.
The homogeneous solution
X^(-1/2)R^(3/4) has no nonzero analytic extension through zero, so the
regular solution is unique. Its first three nontrivial ODE coefficients
and both-sided numerical integral controls are checked independently.

Href(Phi)=H_base(Phi/sqrt(kappa0)) defines B=3Href(R-1)+3(q+R_u/2)/2.
The shifted complete source is the generic S6.174 expression and q
cancels from it. A regular source is not a zero source; in particular
its null-gradient value can be nonzero.

These are finite-kappa algebraic/analytic parent identities. Clock
frequency and seven-mode neighborhood estimates from S6.174 apply at
the base clock; they are not silently promoted to all backgrounds or
off-base solutions.
