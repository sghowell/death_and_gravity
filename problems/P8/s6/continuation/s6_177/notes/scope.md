# What this advances, and what remains open

This is an actual CLASSICAL decoupling family through a specified
complete parent, with complete independent canonical functions fixed.
It supplies more than a formal instruction to erase graviton diagrams,
a fixed quartic matching coefficient, or an integer sequence in the
switch order. It is not the regulator-removed quantum statement needed
to finish V.

The established observables are distinct:

1. A fixed-domain analytic family with the same full base action and
   regular finite-kappa affine quotient/chart at kappa0.
2. A full-function classical density and finite-jet limit on compact
   canonical sets, after the exact Einstein divergence.
3. The exact complete four-scalar TREE amplitude of that limit.
4. A finite-gravity tree pole diagnostic which shows why the fixed-t
   decoupling estimate is not uniform as t approaches zero.

There is no new all-UV theorem, all-parameter-family exclusion,
naturalness prior or required global history connecting vacuum and
bounce. In particular an off-base family not preserving the same
bounce is not a failure of this vacuum construction.

The fixed canonical strip includes the base clock configurations,
but that does NOT make the compact-jet remainder constants small there.
The earlier tiny vacuum Fourier-class bounds and the conditional
ordinary-Proca clock stress remain their own observables. Neither is
a full interacting parent or a solved quantum bounce.

## Primary-source comparison and finite-gravity boundary

[Tokuda, Aoki and Hirano, arXiv2007.15009v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2007.15009v2),
sections3.1-3.4 and equation3.12, explicitly assume high-energy behavior
and track finite terms when the graviton pole is combined with the
dispersive integral. Their assumptions are not certified for this
parent and their schematic scale estimate is not a computed P8
remainder. The comparison supports keeping the missing G obligation
separate, not borrowing its conclusion.

[Herrero-Valea, Koshelev and Tokareva, arXiv2205.13332v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2205.13332v1),
sectionsIII-IV and VI, analyze the sensitivity of the simultaneous
forward/high-energy limit and possible infinite-arc contributions.
No uniformity theorem for this full P8 amplitude follows from a
pointwise low-energy decoupling limit. Our explicit shrinking-transfer
tree control is independently derived and does not reproduce or
validate their full dispersion analysis.

## Remaining research obligations

V still needs a controlled quantum decoupling/matching statement for the
complete interacting parent and observable, the full pole/cut/contour
analysis, and physical omitted-order or operator-truncation control.
The positive tree b2 is only a necessary low-order check.

G still needs a specified finite-gravity IR/Regge observable and a
quantitative finite remainder. An arbitrary pole deletion or an
uncomputed order-one constant cannot supply it.

B still needs the common parent/state/field-map domain, heavy and
physical cutoff estimates, all relevant metric/light/auxiliary/mixed
contributions, and controlled background/response errors. S6.176's
conditional Gaussian sector is not silently enlarged to that parent.

Scoped P8(a) and A.20-A.23 are unchanged. Original V/G/B, P8(b) and P8
remain OPEN. Missing research estimates are not user-intervention
blockers.
