# Exact tree amplitude and the finite-gravity forward boundary

The full fixed f and r are even in Phi, so the vacuum has no cubic
scalar vertex. The quartic density of the complete limiting scalar
action is exactly

    lambda Y^2-gamma Phi^4/3+2gamma(L4-L3), mass^2=1.

All higher independent vertices remain in the action. They have at
least six fields and cannot enter a connected tree four-scalar graph
without lower cubic vertices. The dependent Ia corrections start at
eight scalar fields: r_Y=O(field degree2), r_Y^2/Y=O(field degree2).
The complete canonical vector source starts with four scalar legs,
so an A.S vertex has five total legs and the contact starts at eight.
Their presence therefore does not change this decoupled tree amplitude.

For the constant leading coefficient only, the exact flat identity is

    div[Y(grad Phi Box Phi-Hess Phi.grad Phi)]
      =2(L3-L4)+Y[(Box Phi)^2-Hess Phi^2].

It holds after summing the third-derivative terms, and its integrated
use requires the stated compact/Schwartz boundary. It is NOT used to
replace the entire variable-coefficient a3 action by a constant
Galileon. The verifier sums all24 labelled assignments directly for
both the original L3,L4 and the boundary-equivalent quartic. Independent
off-shell momentum/tensor expansion tests the identity before invoking
mass-shell kinematics.

For all incoming p_i with p_i^2=m^2 and sum p_i=0, a channel pair has
dot product (s-2m^2)/2. Retaining i^6=-1 in the six-derivative vertices
gives3gamma stu on shell; no mass term is omitted from the intermediate
Box factors. The potential contributes -24gamma/3=-8gamma at mass1.
Consequently the COMPLETE limiting four-scalar tree amplitude is

    A_tree(s,t,u)=2lambda[(s-2)^2+(t-2)^2+(u-2)^2]
                   +3gamma stu-8gamma,  s+t+u=4.

It is crossing symmetric. At t=0 and s=2+v,u=2-v it is
4lambda v^2+8lambda-8gamma, hence b2=(1/2)partial_v^2 A=4lambda.
Neither gamma nor the nonzero higher scalar interactions was removed.

The full metric variation of the canonical mass1 quadratic scalar
density gives iV_mn/sqrt(kappa), with
V_mn=p_m q_n+p_n q_m-eta_mn(p.q+m^2), all momenta incoming.
It satisfies the on-shell Ward identity. Dense stress contractions
with the normalized de Donder propagator give the t-channel exchange

    A_grav,t=[su+2m^2 t-2m^4]/(kappa t).

At fixed nonzero transfer this vanishes as1/kappa. Its v^2 coefficient
is -1/(kappa t), and along t=-tau/kappa its limit is generally nonzero:
(s^2-4m^2 s+2m^4)/tau. This proves nonuniformity of this tree observable,
not an obstruction to the fixed-transfer decoupling construction.

No graviton pole is subtracted to claim finite-gravity positivity.
All full scattering cuts, regulator-removed amplitudes and omitted-order
errors remain separate requirements. The positive tree coefficient is
not a UV completion or full dispersion certificate.
