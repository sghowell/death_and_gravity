# Complete endpoint, spatial and time-jet partition

For each endpoint j and source time jet r<=j, S6.202 bounds the complete pre-current coefficient on the joint spatial disc by

9 Cref^2 c_jr Rtime^(-(j-r)) nu^(1-j).

Cauchy on the spatial radius delta nu gives the nth homogeneous Taylor polynomial the additional factor |P|^n/(delta nu)^n. This is a coefficient estimate before the current imaginary part; no test Fourier transform or moving sharp domain is differentiated.

Summing all source time jets and using Rtime<1 gives

D_jn=9 Cref^2 sum_r c_jr Rtime^-j delta^-n.

The radial measure turns nu^(1-j-n) into asymptotic power k^(3-j-n). Its positive majorant is integrable at infinity precisely when j+n>=5.

Here F means an absolutely integrable cell and U means a remaining candidate UV cell:

| endpoint j / spatial n | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | U | U | U | U | U |
| 1 | U | U | U | U | F |
| 2 | U | U | U | F | F |
| 3 | U | U | F | F | F |
| 4 | U | F | F | F | F |

There are10 F cells and15 U cells. Each cell includes all j+1 source time jets. Thus40 time-jet entries are restored as finite and35 remain, out of75 total. Every F cell has n>=1, so the finite restoration vanishes at zero spatial transfer.

The borderline positive majorant has radial behavior1/k. That fact alone does NOT prove that an actual current term diverges: phase, tensor, angular and contact cancellations have not yet been computed for the U sector. All fifteen are retained as candidates, without asserting such cancellations either.
