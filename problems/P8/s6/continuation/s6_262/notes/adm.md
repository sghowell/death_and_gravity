# Full ADM metric, matter contractions and coordinate Jacobians

Let gamma be the complete positive symmetric 3x3 hat spatial metric,
b the complete three-component shift, N>0, and C=R**(-1/2)>0.
With E=[[1,0],[b,I]], det(E)=1,
g=E^T diag(N^2,-C gamma) E and
g^{-1}=E^{-1} diag(N^{-2},-gamma^{-1}/C) E^{-T}.
Thus g00=N^2-C b^T gamma b, g0i=-C gamma_ij b^j,
gij=-C gamma_ij. The determinant is -N^2 C^3 det(gamma).
Its positive volume is N U sqrt(det(gamma)); the spatial volume
is U sqrt(det(gamma)). All shifts are retained.

For a full scalar covector d, the contraction is
(d0-b^i di)^2/N^2 - gamma^{ij}di dj/C. Multiplication by half
the full volume yields exactly the original temporal U/N and
spatial N Cchi coefficients. For an arbitrary antisymmetric F,
the coframe electric covector is E_i=F0i-b^j Fji. The Maxwell
density divided by zeta sqrt(det gamma) is
Cchi E_i gamma^{ij} E_j/(2N)
- N M gamma^{ik}gamma^{jl}Fij Fkl/4.
All six F components and their shift contributions survive.
Independent tests evaluate the full four-index metric contraction,
not just these already-separated coefficients.

The covariant metric-field map has ten-dimensional Jacobian C^9.
This is the ratio of the physical and hat ADM-to-metric Jacobians;
lapse derivatives of C do not alter it. Independent differences
use nonconstant C(N) and all ten independent metric coordinates.
The distinct original extended position map also includes W0=N T+b.W
and retains all primary momenta before its cotangent lift. Its
sixteen-dimensional position determinant is N C^6=N R^{-3}, as
the unchanged S257 full point map states. These two Jacobians are
different maps, not competing measures. The extended phase determinant
remains one under its complete cotangent lift. None of these classical
coordinate identities chooses a nonlinear quantum ordering.
