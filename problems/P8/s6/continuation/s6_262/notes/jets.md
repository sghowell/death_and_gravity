# Whole-source finite clock jets, not an off-clock substitution

The existing S253 source factorization licenses R clock jets through
four lapse derivatives from
R_germ=1+(N^{-2}-1)/h, h=(1+u^2)^3.
The unchanged entire R differs by an order-eight clock-distance factor.
Only the licensed finite jets are used. Source.py differentiates the
full abstract R first, then inserts the source-pinned R jets.

The six coefficient families are C, U, N U, U/N, N Cchi, N M.
At u=0 their zeroth through fourth jets agree respectively with the
jets of N, N^{3/2}, N^{5/2}, N^{1/2}, N^{3/2}, N^{1/2} at N=1.
This statement does not identify their whole off-clock functions with
these powers. Independent tests add a nonzero order-eight comparison
remainder and verify both the finite-jet agreement and the off-clock
difference. That comparison is only a diagnostic.

On the reference clock U_N/U=3/(2h). At the bounce it is +3/2,
and U_NN/(2U)=3/8. The spacetime volume N U instead has first
lapse slope +5/2. Confusing spatial and spacetime densities would
produce a second error, so both are retained and distinguished.
The old expression R-1/2 gives a slope -6/h, not the physical
3/(2h); at the bounce its discrepancy is -15/2.
