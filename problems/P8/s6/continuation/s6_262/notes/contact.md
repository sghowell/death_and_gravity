# Correct full physical source contact and its cancellation

For F=a^3 exp(3v) U(N), differentiating the whole parent gives
F_v=3F, F_N=F c1, and
F_vv=9F, F_vN=3F c1, F_NN=F c2, where
c1=-3 R_N/(4R),
c2=21 R_N^2/(16R^2)-3 R_NN/(4R).
No derivative of the complete R is deleted. At the bounce c1=3/2
and c2=3/4. Divide by the homogeneous reference density.

For the unchanged coupled reference and the same finite gauge-weight
Weyl jet as S261, the coordinate-mean derivative is
delta mean=(-9 Cvv/4,-9 Cvn/4).
The covariance derivative is
[[3 Cvv/2,3 Cvn/4],[3 Cvn/4,0]].
Contracting the full source gradient gives
one=-27 Cvv/4-27 Cvn/8.
One half of the full source Hessian contracted with the covariance
derivative gives two=+27 Cvv/4+27 Cvn/8. Both contacts must be kept;
neither a vanishing cross covariance nor deletion of the nonlinear
mean is licensed. Independent three-direction Fourier sampling,
coupled Gaussian cubature and periodic inverse-map quadrature test
these identities with the correct source multiplier R^{-3/4}.

The old claimed onepoint used c1=-6. Thus old_one-one=135 Cvn/8.
Crucially, the sum of onepoint and two-point variations vanishes for
an arbitrary supplied c1 (and an arbitrary NN Hessian coefficient,
because delta Cnn=0 at this order). The same algebra therefore
passes even with the incorrect physical multiplier. This explains
the previous test gap and is recorded as a negative control.

The retained bounce coordinate cross covariance has radial quadratic
coefficient Cvn_coeff=-cs/(16 pi^2 E kappa a^2), equal at the
bounce to cs/(8 pi^2 kappa). The corrected volume onepoint lapse
contribution has coefficient -27 Cvn_coeff/8; its two-point
contribution is the negative. These are unrenormalized reference
asymptotics, not an interacting physical onepoint or cutoff estimate.
