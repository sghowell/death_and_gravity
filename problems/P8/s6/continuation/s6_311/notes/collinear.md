# Uniform physical pair-vertex bound

Rotate q1=a(1,0,0,1), q2=b(1,sin(theta),0,cos(theta)), a,b>0.
Put r=tan(theta/2), W=a+b. Any symmetric conserved hard tensor is
parameterized by its six independent spatial entries:
U0i=Qj Uji/W, U00=Qi Qj Uij/W^2.

Use the plus/cross spatial TT basis for each null direction. The full
cubic quotient -Vhhh/Q^2 is linear in those six entries. Exact algebra
cancels every angular pole in all four polarization pairs. Its normalized
coefficients, multiplied by ab/W^2, become P(z,r)/(1+r^2)^m with
z=a/W in[0,1] and every r monomial degree at most2m.
Thus |z^i r^j/(1+r^2)^m|<=1 for all r>=0. Absolute polynomial coefficient
sums give respective six-coefficient budgets530,128,88,268.

Each plus/cross basis tensor has squared Frobenius norm2. A unit-norm
physical polarization has basis coefficient l1 norm at most1, including
complex polarizations. Spatial rotations preserve ||U_spatial||F, and
every rotated component is bounded by that norm. Therefore

|M_pair| <= 530 W^2/(sqrt(kappa)*a*b) ||U_spatial||F,

uniformly in the relative emitted direction. The r->infinity endpoint
is covered by the degree inequality too. This is a bound on the
specified47-graph current/vertex class, not the entire434-tree amplitude.
