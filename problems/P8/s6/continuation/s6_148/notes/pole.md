# Actual mass reference and the unchanged nonlocal bound

At the inherited candidate, the exact rational upper bounds,
printed approximately, are

    |L T_insert,MS/2| <= 8.96020706191107e-13,
    |g F_MS(M;0)| <= 2.22227357686286e-216.

The complete mass reference is bounded by their sum. Its variation
from s=0 to s=1 is bounded by the uniform derivative constant
4Cg/(QT), about 5.55568394215716e-617. Therefore the complete
row mass reference at one is strictly below 1e-10.

The assigned H-source absolute upper bound is about
4.77877709968590e188. Dividing by the actual M gives an induced
heavy-field shift below 2.44673387503918e-9 approximately and,
strictly, below 1e-6. Both figures are retained: a large dimensionful
coefficient is not automatically a large physical field shift,
nor is a small shift alone a proof of the full vacuum's health.

The positive finite slope and on-shell divided-remainder bounds
already established in S6.136 are unchanged. This local calculation
does not require repeating their on-shell subtractions, and those
subtractions are not used to assign their finite mass anchor zero.

No complete two-loop canonical inverse is constructed here. Other
matching insertions, z1^2 and z2 normalization, parameter changes
inside lower-loop functions, and the vacuum references still need
their correctly owned contributions. No whole light-pole or
higher-order error claim follows from the present row alone.
