# Exact boundary of the new result

The new quantities are the exact free in/out states on the actual
CD geometry and their local energy and pressure DIFFERENCES.
The all-order comparison identifies both as Hadamard; it is not
an inference from a finite energy integral.

The prescribed mass and geometry are the same for both states.
Local counterterms cancel only in their difference. Curved absolute
stress still needs its explicit subtraction, finite gravitational
reference dictionary, vacuum polarization and anomaly terms.

This checkpoint does not bound the full canonical scalar, heavy,
gauge or interacting parent stress. In particular, a positive-kinetic
polynomial parent does not become a bounce because a test field's
state difference is small. No physical cutoff for the nonpolynomial
SAT8 EFT is inferred from a full-momentum free quadratic calculation.

controlled parent background and perturbation response, finite
gravity IR/Regge errors and vacuum contour/truncation estimates
remain research obligations. The small bound relative to kappa
does not by itself control derivatives or response across tau.
