# Entire ordered sixth-time remainder

Use the physical annihilation convention \(-i\omega\). The detector sharp branch has positive phase and the source annihilation branch negative phase. For spatial directions the current is the positive imaginary part of this ordered product, equivalently its sharp difference divided by \(2i\). External tensor coefficients remain bilinear.

Let \(\Theta'=W_k+W_\ell,\ g=1/\Theta'\), and \(LU=(gU)'\). Direct differentiation proves the complete six-step identity
\[
 \int_{-1/2}^{t}e^{-i\Theta(s)}U(s)\,ds
 =e^{-i\Theta(t)}\sum_{j=0}^{5}i(-i)^jg(t)L^jU(t)
       +(-i)^6\int_{-1/2}^{t}e^{-i\Theta(s)}L^6U(s)\,ds.
\]
All lower endpoints vanish by the common prepared source germ. There is no imposed upper-time cutoff. The \(j=5\) coefficient is \(+1\), while the sixth bulk coefficient is \(-1\), before taking the physical ordered current. In particular the two signs must not be guessed from the sign of the imaginary-part convention. The earlier correctly ordered S6.217 sixth-bulk notation includes this \((-i)^6\) factor.

## Complete source-jet majorants

For a complete analytic pair amplitude \(A\), apply the product rule to every factor in \(L^j(AG)\). The uniform Cauchy bounds of the domain note imply
\[
 |L^j(AG)|\le C\sqrt{\nu\mu}
 \left(\frac2{\nu+\mu}\right)^j
 \sum_{r=0}^{j}c_{jr}R^{-(j-r)}|\partial_t^rG|.
\]
Here \(R=1/20000,\ C=10^9\). The coefficients are generated by
\[
 c_{00}=1,\qquad c_{j+1,r}=(2j+2-r)c_{jr}+c_{j,r-1},
\]
with absent entries zero. Literal symbolic product-rule expansion, replacing the \(l\)-th analytic derivative bounds by \(l!\), independently gives all rows:
\[
\begin{aligned}
 &(1),\quad(2,1),\quad(8,5,1),\quad(48,33,9,1),\\
 &(384,279,87,14,1),\\
 &(3840,2895,975,185,20,1),\\
 &(46080,35685,12645,2640,345,27,1).
\end{aligned}
\]
Their fifth and sixth sums are 7916 and 97423. These are whole rows, not selected principal jets.

Let \(F\) consist of the complete fifth endpoint plus the complete sixth bulk. After multiplying the full detector amplitude, both terms have internal weight \(\nu\mu/(\nu+\mu)^6\). Their positive numerators are

 N_5=C^2\,64\,7916\,R^{-5},\qquad

No scalar polarization multiplier is inserted. The unit retarded triangle is bounded by Cauchy-Schwarz in detector/source time; the endpoint is bounded separately. All source jets through six are included in the final graph.

## Full radial integral and two-leg tail

The harmonic-mean inequality gives
\[
 \frac{\nu\mu}{(\nu+\mu)^6}\le\frac1{4\nu^4},\qquad
 \int\frac{d^3k}{(2\pi)^3}\nu^{-4}
 =\frac{64}{8\pi m}<\frac{64}{24m}.
\]
Thus the entire \(F\) numerator over \(m\) is
\[
 C_F=(N_5+N_6)\frac{64}{96}
 =\frac{798092458393600000000000000000000000000000000000000}{3}
 <10^{51}.
\]
This majorant is independent of the external transfer. At equal frequencies the analogous fifth-bulk absolute majorant behaves as \(r^{-1}\) after radial measure; it is not integrable. This is a statement about that absolute majorant, not a claim that the exact fifth-order physical current diverges.

For the original frequency cutoff, the comoving ball has radius
\[
 R_K=4\sqrt{K^2-m^2}>K\quad(K\ge2m).
\]
On the removed union use the preceding bound for the first removed leg and its symmetric counterpart for the second. Their sum is at most one half of the one-leg fourth-power radial tail. Since \(\nu\ge r/4\), the complete numerator over \(R_K\) is
\[
 C_{F,K}=(N_5+N_6)\frac{4^4}{36}
 =\frac{25538958668595200000000000000000000000000000000000000}{9}
 <10^{53}.
\]
Using \(R_K>K\) only enlarges the displayed \(1/K\) estimate. Overlaps are allowed in this upper bound; no removed leg or large-small pair is excluded.
