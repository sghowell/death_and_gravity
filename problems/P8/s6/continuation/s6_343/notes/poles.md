## Conclusion and limitation

These classes exhaust the stated selected scalar valence inventory.
Their existing literal counterfunctionals leave zero ADDITIONAL
curvature-only physical-TT UV pole in this fixed representative.
This does not mean every counterterm vanishes. In particular, it neither
fixes finite chi nor computes unretained internal-graviton contributions,
a full beta function, or Ricci terms relevant to the curved bounce.
