# Original source and graph scope

All original n,g,C,kappa,lambda, the physical metric and named OS4
subtractions are unchanged. S342 is the immediate parent. S336 supplies
the literal curvature direction and its nonzero original amplitudes.
S337--S340 supply the complete local, mixed-source, bubble and quadratic
radiative sectors. S239 supplies the literal scalar valence inventory.

For connected matter graphs, sum_v(degree_v-2)=E+2L-2.
At E4,L1, the allowed scalar patterns are four cubics; two cubics and one
quartic; two quartics; a cubic and the degree-five source; or one degree-six
source. They comprise the polynomial/heavy bubble, triangle and ordered-box
classes, the three mixed-source contractions and the six local tadpoles.
The E2,L1 and E1,L1 external corrections have only the old two-cubic or
quartic graphs and the cubic onepoint graph respectively.

One external metric variation does not change scalar valence: insert it
on every scalar propagator and vertex and retain all external emissions.
Higher scalar degree cannot enter this selected order. Scalar-times-R
source vertices have zero linear real-TT response; that does not remove
them from the curved background. Internal metric propagators and unknown
loop-counted independent curvature vertices are outside this selected
minimal matter calculation. No interchange of a finite-gravity quantum
limit and a regulator removal is made.
