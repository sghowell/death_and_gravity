# The specified complete homogeneous flat Dirac tensor

Keep the S6.168 MS/vacuum/saturated-mass reference and
the same free in/out Hadamard states. Extend its
absolute local energy calculation to pressure.
Fix additional finite curvature terms to zero in
the common MS prescription; the earlier flat T00
did not by itself fix this improvement choice.

The physical pressure matrix has both diagonal and
off-diagonal components in the mass eigenbasis.
Four exact rotating frames control both components
without replacing the exact state. Restore the
full dimensionally regulated pressure finite parts,
including the epsilon dependence of the isotropic
factor1/(3-2epsilon).

The complete subtracted pressure remainder is below
1e410. The restored derivative pressure is below
1e595. The same entire fixed-reference potential
then gives |P(t)|<1e789 for every real time.
Together with the unchanged energy estimate,
every component of diag(rho,P,P,P) is below1e789
in the fixed Minkowski orthonormal frame.

This is the complete homogeneous flat tensor of
the specified quadratic one-loop contribution,
not the full interacting parent stress, curved
state, cutoff, higher-loop error or P8 closure.
