# Complete momentum and uniform stress bounds

The state and z-component pressure remainder is at
most one third of the sum of the three S6.168 mode
energy remainder allowances. For the x-component,
use |pM/omega|<=M<2m. Both occupied helicities and
the radial measure1/(2pi^2) give

    R_x1=Nm1024^3Delta/(3pi^2 tau^4 m0^2),
    R_x3=2Nm1024Delta^3/(3pi^2 tau^4 m0^4).

These use the complete convergent integrals

    integral p^3/E^6=1/(4m0^2),
    integral p^5/E^10=1/(24m0^4).

At the actual parameters, with pi^2>9, they are
approximately7.30362088222e405 and1.27920581307e395.
The complete pressure remainder is approximately
7.89845699163e409, below1e410. All exact half-line
state transitions and both projector components
are included.

For L=2Delta/(m-Delta), the profile gives

    |ell|<=L, |M|<=m+Delta,
    |Mdot|<=Delta/tau, |Mddot|<=9Delta/tau^2.

Thus the entire restored derivative pressure has
the uniform allowance

    N/(Q tau^2)[6(m+Delta)Delta L
                    +Delta^2(L/3+2/3)].

Using Q>144, its actual value is approximately
4.77783350050e594, below1e595. No finite improvement
term is dropped.

Every potential term contributes pressure equal
to minus its energy. The same fixed vacuum and
saturated mass-reference counterterm and the
entire paired potential tail from S6.168 therefore
give the identical potential allowance. Adding
it to the derivative and state remainder yields

    |P(t)|<1e789, every real t.

The in/out states remain rotationally invariant.
Momentum density and off-diagonal stresses vanish
under the angular integral. The homogeneous tensor
in the fixed Minkowski orthonormal frame is
diag(rho,P,P,P); every component is below1e789
in absolute value, or1e-11 of the named kappa scale.

The same bound is not asserted for every Lorentz-
boosted component. Nor is the kappa ratio a relative
error against the zero density at a bounce.
