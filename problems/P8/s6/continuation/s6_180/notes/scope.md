# What this inverse does not solve

The inverse belongs to the actual current-parent retained
HOMOGENEOUS LINEAR RESPONSE operator. Only the specified
conditional Gaussian vector sector is quantized. The complete
classical CD/M1 action is kept and rederived; no old scalar
stress-canceling profile, different matter coupling or parent
coefficient is imported.

The reference CD background is still off shell by its conditional
quantum one-point stress. That stress is already nonzero near the
original Cauchy slice. Its residual is not a smooth prepared
two-force with zero initial neighborhood, and this inverse is
NOT applied to it to assert a corrected background.

A compatible quantum preparation/background construction and
a controlled nonlinear remainder are separate obligations.
Existence of this linear inverse does not establish a small
background correction, numerical full inverse norm, nonlinear
stability, causal cone, arbitrary spatial or independent-state
response, or a symmetric-noise estimate.

The all-frequency massive kernel is exact for the declared
retained Gaussian approximation. It is not evidence that a
finite EFT truncation is physically valid at every frequency.
No pole, high-frequency response or homogeneous solution is
deleted to select a favorable inverse.

The explicit C1<15000 bound addresses local physical-lapse
reconstruction. The weak-log kernel constant C and full scalar
L1 norm K remain finite but unevaluated. The resulting
exponential inverse estimate may be enormous; it is not a
small-error bound merely because kappa is large.

Full affine/light/tensor/auxiliary/mixed quantum contributions,
an interacting measure/state, the physical cutoff and omitted
terms, a self-consistent and globally controlled background,
vacuum matching/cuts/contour/truncation and finite-gravity
IR/Regge estimates remain open. This scoped theorem neither
excludes the full row nor closes original P8 or V/G/B.
Scoped P8(a) and A.20-A.23 are unchanged.
