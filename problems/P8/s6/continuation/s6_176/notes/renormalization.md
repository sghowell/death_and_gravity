# Canonical vector measure and a regular ordinary covariant prescription

This is an explicit choice for the CONDITIONAL retained vector sector.
Use canonical A=sqrt(kappa*zeta)W and its constrained three-polarization
phase measure, exactly as in the ordinary Proca calculation. The
positive finite constant normalization is not hidden in the covariance:
the W connected two-point function is divided by kappa*zeta.

A finite regulator change from W to A has a source-independent constant
Jacobian. We fix the canonical measure, including its vacuum normalization,
rather than treating an infinite product of constants as a computed
cosmological counterterm. This does not specify the quantum measure for
the other56 algebraic affine directions, metric chart, light constraints,
projective/diffeomorphism fixing or mixed loops. They are not covered by
a three-mode Gaussian determinant.

The source-independent UV subtraction is the ordinary constant-mass
specialization of the frozen scalar-coefficient continuation. All mass
deviations delta_a,delta_b vanish IDENTICALLY here, at every metric and
clock configuration. Therefore the additional clock-normal/projector
terms vanish as functions, before variation; this differs from merely
setting their clock values to zero in an old nonminimal action.

Write R for the old -+++ scalar curvature, R=-R_P8, and retain the
fixed four-dimensional covariant heat polynomials

    a4V=-R^2/8+29 Ricci^2/60-Riemann^2/15,
    a4s=R^2/72-Ricci^2/180+Riemann^2/180.

Their curvature-basis checks give

    a4V=13 C^2/120-7 Euler/40+R^2/72,
    a4s=C^2/120-Euler/360+R^2/72.

Before the common 1/(64 pi^2 epsilon_DR) factor, the pole density is

    3m^4+m^2 R+2a4V.

Its coefficients are kept as these four-dimensional scalar coefficients,
while the invariant contractions and metric volume are evaluated in
D+1 dimensions, D=3-2epsilon_DR, before variation and the dimension limit.
This is the named prescription, not an assertion of unique UV matching.
The retained box-R divergence has zero compactly supported local Euler
variation; no infinite-history flux is discarded.

With ell=log(m^2/mu^2), the finite local density matched to the physical
fourth-order mode subtraction, before 1/(64 pi^2), is

    -(3ell-5/2)m^4 -(ell-5/3)m^2 R -2ell*a4V-4a4s.

At mu=m, set ell=0 only after the specified continuation. The remaining
local action is 5m^4/2+5m^2R/3-4a4s. It is not the whole finite,
state-dependent determinant: the convergent exact-mode/subtraction
integral remains essential. The full dimension-limit and integrable
reference/state arguments are inherited only after the new canonical,
operator and physical-readout bridge has been verified.

These ordinary covariant densities contain no u or X denominator.
They therefore extend across X=0, including nonzero null gradients,
unlike the discarded nonminimal projector extension. No new freely
adjusted finite counterterm or older scalar stress-canceling profile
is inserted here. In particular this step does not modify the
classical analytic target to make its quantum residual vanish.

The chosen common prescription permits the structural comparison of
the same ordinary vector determinant and its metric variations on the
same metric/state family. No numerical functional response norm, full
renormalized parent effective action, or all-orders EFT matching follows.
