# What the conditional vector result does and does not establish

The classical analytic target and original M1 remain exactly unchanged.
The allowed mass specialization, canonical vector measure, explicit
all-order Cauchy state and ordinary finite prescription are stated.
They define a CONDITIONAL Gaussian sector, not the full quantized
affine/metric/light parent.

A source-aware Gaussian family exists on the admissible compact history
class. Its connected covariance is Hadamard and its mean solves the
actual sourced equation. On the reference clock its physical ordinary
stress has a full-continuum absolute C5 bound. The ordinary covariant
counterterms contain no clock-gradient singularity. The first
source-induced mean force is cubic, with the contact terms retained.

The state-family construction and its quantitative claims are distinct.
Hadamard smoothness on compact intervals does not give an all-time
uniform stress bound. Qualitative causal solvability does not bound
the nonlinear response, noise, induced scalar vertices or a physical
cutoff. A small vector residual does not demonstrate a corrected
background. No ratio to the zero bounce density is used.

The older scalar profiles which canceled the ordinary one-point stress
are not installed. A one-point cancellation would itself not prove
the corrected constraints or perturbations. The full affine auxiliary
measure, metric/disformal and constrained light measures, light/tensor
states and composites, mixed and higher loops and omitted operators
are not fixed by the canonical three-vector-polarization prescription.

No GY14 matching is transferred. A full common-parent vacuum, contour
and truncation estimate, finite-gravity IR/Regge remainder, physical
cutoff and controlled interacting background/response remain required.
Original P8(b), V/G/B and P8 are OPEN. Scoped P8(a) and A.20-A.23 are
unchanged. The missing research estimates are not a user-choice blocker.
