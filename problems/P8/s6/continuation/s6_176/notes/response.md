# Exact conditional stress split and the first source-dependent response

The vector is Gaussian only CONDITIONAL on the specified light and
metric histories. Its canonical Hessian is ordinary constant-mass Proca
on every such metric, independent of u. All remaining source dependence
is linear in A or a c-number, including the explicit S^2 contact.
Eliminating the temporal constraint retains its source-dependent local
contact as well; it is not replaced by a naive unconstrained oscillator
source. The sourced inverse of S6.175 keeps these terms.

Write A=A_fluct+Abar with zero connected mean and the retarded classical
mean of state.md. In every quadratic physical readout the expectation
of a cross term linear in A_fluct is zero. The renormalized conditional
stress therefore splits into the ordinary connected Proca stress plus
the classical FULL sourced action's stress evaluated at Abar. The latter
includes variations through S, not just the free positive-energy tensor
of Abar. Smooth mean products have no new UV singularity. The finite
Gaussian polynomial control checks this split without assigning a value
to a divergent coincident covariance.

In-in causal mean response and the symmetric covariance/noise kernel
remain distinct. The Gaussian cumulants terminate at second order, but
the explicit source and constraint contacts still have to be retained.
An ordinary variation of a single-branch retarded quadratic functional
would symmetrize its kernel, as shown in S6.175. No such replacement
is used to define the sourced state or physical expectation values.

For the actual covariant source, its bracket multiplying R-1 vanishes
on the reference clock: X=R=1, R_u=0, Box u=3H and uHu=0.
The full source value and first variation therefore vanish for arbitrary
covariant first jets, not only a lapse variation in unitary coordinates.
The new symbolic check varies those jets independently around their
actual clock values.

For a smooth compact one-parameter history family with fixed preparation,

    S_epsilon=epsilon^2 S2+epsilon^3 S3+...,
    Wbar_epsilon=epsilon^2 W2+epsilon^3 W3+....

The second expansion follows by the sourced causal equation and smooth
parameter dependence on every compact time strip. Differentiating the
hyperbolic Cauchy problem gives the usual retarded equation with the
differentiated operator as a smooth forcing; iterate for every finite
parameter order. This is a qualitative argument, not a uniform
quantitative norm for the nonlinear solution map.

The source-dependent effective functional starts at fourth history
degree. Equivalently, physical variations of the mean/source action
contain delta S=O(epsilon) times Wbar-S=O(epsilon^2), so the first
added mean force is cubic; the purely free quadratic mean stress
starts at fourth order. The cubic coefficient is retained in the
certificate, not declared zero.

A second metric derivative of the unintegrated source coupling can
contain a nonzero term linear in the vector. Its expectation and its
odd Gaussian cross contractions vanish at the zero-mean clock. The
first three variations of the conditional Gaussian functional, or the
reference one-point function and its first two history-response orders,
therefore have the same ordinary connected-Proca contribution when the
SAME canonical measure, covariant finite prescription and initial-state
family are used. At fourth order the source contacts and exchange
terms are generally nonzero.

This is an identity of the specified conditional sector. The C5
time-derivative stress bound does not become a quantitative metric
response bound through this identity. No old full-parent light inverse,
self-consistent solution, global pole exclusion or mixed-loop bound
is transferred.
