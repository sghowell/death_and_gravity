# Exact local three-channel reference change

The direct amplitude difference is F_alpha sum C^2,
C=-L+g/(M-z). Its equivalent LINEAR order-two coordinate
shift is fixed by the bare reference equality:

    delta L=-3L^2 F_alpha, delta g=-2Lg F_alpha,
    delta M=-g F_alpha, delta G=-LG F_alpha.

Literal variation of -L+g sum 1/(M-z) gives precisely the
same amplitude. The G1^2 disjoint product is not absorbed
into this linear G2 definition or added again here.

Put D0=M-2, s=2+nu, u=2-nu, t=0. The exact forward identity is

    b2[sum C^2]/b2_tree=-2L+3g/D0.

The t-channel constant is retained and differentiated to zero.
For the actual g/D0<L/2, this factor is negative and has
absolute value below 2L. The direct local term and the
coordinate representation are one contribution, not two.
No finite local parameter is fitted to the forward coefficient,
and no additional LSZ multiplier is applied.
