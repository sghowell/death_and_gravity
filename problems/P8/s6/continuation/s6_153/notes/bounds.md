# Positive finite reference and actual numerical bounds

For M>=32 and 0<=x<=1, D=xM+(1-x)^2 increases from 1 to M.
Also D>=xM and

    (1-x)/M-b=(1-x)^3/(MD)>=0,

so integral b dx<=1/(2M). At the fixed actual scale
mu^2=10^400>=M, ell<=2ell-logD<=2ell. Consequently

    0<F_alpha<=g ell/(Q^2 M)<1200g/(144^2 M).

The exact heavy-tree factor gives the relative conversion
bound 2L F_alpha. At the actual parameters,

    alpha_0<2.64909532335069e-208,
    F_alpha<4.41515887225116e-207,
    E_conversion/b2_tree<2.02109933727317e-411,
    E_conversion<8.08439734909270e-1011.

The complete original 64-refinement insertion bound is kept,
including its constant and both decaying pieces. Adding the
finite conversion leaves the complete family below 1e-614;
its diagnostic upper value is 6.24500451351651e-615.
All decisive comparisons are exact rational inequalities.
The large-momentum estimate is over the whole loop integral,
not an imposed cutoff.
