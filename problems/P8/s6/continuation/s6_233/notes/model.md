# Local model, full kinetic matrix and coercive potential

The separately named V2S-T1 action is written in FORMULATION. Both fields have positive canonical kinetic terms. Direct differentiation with respect to both velocities gives the full identity kinetic matrix, including both diagonal entries and the off-diagonal zero. The potential Hessian at the origin is diag(1,D+2), so the two classical vacuum masses are positive.

The complete potential is

V=phi²/2+(D+2)H²/2-gH phi²/2-Cphi4/24,
C=-g²(3/D-2/D²).

Complete its heavy square:

V=phi²/2+(D+2)[H-gphi²/(2(D+2))]²/2
  +g²(D-1)phi4/[6D²(D+2)].

For D>1 all displayed coefficients are positive. Therefore the only zero is phi=H=0. On a bounded potential sublevel phi is bounded, and then the heavy square bounds H. This proves coercivity and uniqueness of the global classical minimum, not just a sampled Hessian test.

At the actual D>2 there is a sharper exact identity:

V-(phi²+H²)/6
 =phi²/3+(3D+5)[H-3gphi²/(2(3D+5))]²/6
   +g²(9D-10)phi4/[24D²(3D+5)].

Every term is nonnegative, giving the stated global quadratic bound. The field units use light mass1. No gravity or bounce evolution is hidden in this flat-space statement.

The actual coupling is g²=1/2^26, with0<-C<10^-204 and0<g²/M_H²<10^-204. These are exact parameter inequalities, not a proof that delicate quantum matching errors are small.

The negative contact is essential to the potential statement. With C omitted, completing the same heavy square leaves a negative quartic along its valley, and the potential is unbounded below. A separate D<1 control also loses the quartic positivity despite positive vacuum masses. Thus a positive exchange pole alone did not supply this classical potential result.

This local polynomial Lagrangian is at most quartic in its fields. Classical power counting and a coercive potential are not a construction of a full four-dimensional quantum theory, a high-energy amplitude theorem or an exact unitary S matrix. All loop, physical mass/residue and resonance statements require further work. Nothing in this construction supplies the original full affine parent or a cosmological solution.
