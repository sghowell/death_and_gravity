# Complete rational angular integrals and the first elastic coefficient

For S>4 set

k=(S-4)/2,
a=M_H²+k,
d=C+g²/(M_H²-S).

The complete new amplitude is
A(x)=d+2g²a/(a²-k²x²).
Its denominators are positive on the full angular interval because a-k=M_H²>0. Let L=log[(a+k)/(a-k)], on the real logarithm branch. Direct primitives give

I1=integral A dx=2d+(2g²/k)L,

The independent tests differentiate both primitives and compare complete numerical angular quadratures. They do not infer I2 from only two partial waves.

The k0 limits are I1=2d+4g²/a and I2=2(d+2g²/a)². In the physical threshold limit substitute a=M_H² and d=C+g²/(M_H²-4). The normalized identical conventions are unchanged:

t0_tree=beta I1/(64pi),
rho_first=beta I2/(64pi),
beta=sqrt(1-4/S).

Both absorptive coefficients vanish at S4; their angular integrals remain finite. No massless approximation or additional identical-particle factorial is inserted.

For k>0, the exchange term has the uniformly convergent even angular expansion

(2g²/a) sum_n>=0 (kx/a)^(2n).

Rodrigues integration by parts gives a positive projection of x^(2n) on P_(2ell) whenever n>=ell, and zero for n<ell. Boundary terms vanish; the even-order derivative leaves a nonnegative integrand proportional to x^(2n-2ell)(1-x²)^(2ell). Thus every even ell has a nonzero exchange contribution. The contact and s-channel term affect only ell0. The full rational model cannot be truncated to the original ell0,ell2 pair.
