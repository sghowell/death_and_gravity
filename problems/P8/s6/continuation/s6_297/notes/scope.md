# Limits of the result

Established: complete actual matter-vertex equality through the degree
needed for the no-internal-graviton first loop; reuse of its complete
same-OS4 value; the missing matter-loop/gravity-Born interference bound;
the formal first-Newton inclusive assembly; positivity of its full Born
reference; and an explicit forward crossover.

Not established: finite values of independent gravitational hard matching,
a uniform Newton expansion in the forward cone, a finite total or forward
cross section, higher-Newton or omitted-loop errors, an exact stable heavy
particle, an all-order infrared construction, a crossing-analytic positive
dispersion amplitude, or a quantitative original Regge remainder.

The finite-contour estimate is conditional. Relative Regge dominance is
not substituted for the required finite absolute or transfer-derivative
bound. The illustrative norm counterexample is not an admissible UV model
and excludes no witness. A finite inclusive rate is not silently identified
with an analytic elastic amplitude.

Original quantum state/domain/measure, nonlinear bounce, common-parent
matching and V/G/B/P8 remain open. Missing estimates remain research
obligations; they are not a request that the user choose a convenient
finite coefficient. Passing this finite-order calculation is not a UV
completion or a universal positivity/no-go verdict.

All formula tests retain exact arithmetic. Independent numerical or
stress-tensor tests calibrate the formulas; the written degree counting,
positivity and Cauchy arguments supply the continuum reasoning. These
written proofs are not Lean-formalized.
