# Full first-Newton perturbative inclusive numerator

Let the formal amplitude be

A=A_m+h A_G+ell[L_m+h L_g]+higher orders,

with real Born amplitudes, Newton marker h and loop marker ell.
The coefficient of ell*h in|A|^2 is exactly

2Re(A_m L_g+A_G L_m).

S296 computed and paired the first term with the full square of the
26-graph real-emission tree. The second term is now supplied by the
complete finite-kappa no-internal-graviton matter loop in the existing
OS4 prescription. It has no graviton dimensional-IR pole at fixed
nonforward angle. Higher source contractions are not omitted from L_m.

After local UV subtraction, use the same hard reference
H=2Re(L_g,hard/A_m) and the physical S296 finite conversion/recoil terms.
The full coefficient at these formal orders is

N_1=A_m^2(H+Delta_soft+R_real)+2 A_G Re L_m.

The virtual principal part
2 A_m^2 Re Bsoft/(8pi^2 kappa epsilon)
cancels the leading real part
A_m^2 K0/(8pi^2 kappa epsilon), with K0=-2ReBsoft.
The added matter-loop term is finite and changes no such pole.

For the explicitly unexpanded positive normalization B=(A_m+A_G)^2,
write w_m=A_m^2/B. It obeys0<w_m<1. Then

N_1/B=w_m H+w_m(Delta_soft+R_real)+2 A_G Re L_m/B.

The S296 compact error and the new complete matter-loop bound give

|N_1/B-w_m H| <=10^-792+eta/2 <2*10^-199.

Retaining L_m explicitly leaves the tighter conversion/recoil error
w_m*10^-792. The bounds apply only on their common physical energy and
detector domain, after removal of the dimensional regulator at fixed
resolution and nonforward angle.

No finite gravitational matching value in H has been determined, and its
sign is not fixed. The expression is a formal perturbative coefficient,
not a full nonnegative exact rate. The denominator's inclusion of the
gravity Born square is a definition of the comparison, not computation
of missing higher-Newton loops or real radiation. Forward gravity
dominance makes that distinction essential.
