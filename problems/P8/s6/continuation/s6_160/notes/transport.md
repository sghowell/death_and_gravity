# Transfer of the named fixed-order references

The regulated physical-source generating functional equals
the canonical parent's as a formal perturbative series.
Consequently its connected Phi and H correlators and its
zero-source vacuum functional agree through two loops after
the inherited renormalization conditions. Their analytic
continuations near the named physical Phi pole are the same.
No additional external normalization is applied to the
already physical source.

For comparison only, let an ordinary coordinate have unknown
formal overlap o=1+h o1+h^2 o2 with the physical one-particle
state. Its pole residue is o^2 and its four-pole numerator is
o^4 A. Amputating with four full propagators gives A/o^4;
multiplying the four LSZ square roots gives A again. Explicitly,

    Aordinary_1=A1-4 o1 A0,
    Aordinary_2=A2-4 o1 A1+(10 o1^2-4 o2)A0.

This algebra is independent of a numerical estimate for o2.
It is not proof that an independently renormalized ordinary
Psi has a controlled physical perturbation series. Separate
ordinary composite mixing/normalization remains uncomputed.
