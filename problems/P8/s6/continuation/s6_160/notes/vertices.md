# Full scalar, Yukawa and counterterm substitution

After exact Gaussian H elimination the bosonic parent is
S2[Phi]+S4[Phi], with S4 containing the full nonlocal heavy
inverse. In the directional expansion D_R, R is an independent
fixed placeholder: do not differentiate its field dependence
while forming the Taylor coefficients of S[Psi+R]. Then

    degree 2:  S2
    degree 4:  S4 + D_R S2
    degree 6:  D_R S4 + D_R^2 S2/2
    degree 8:  D_R^2 S4/2
    degree 10: D_R^3 S4/6
    degree 12: D_R^4 S4/24.

All six terms are retained. In particular the octic term already
enters two-loop four-point functions. Before H elimination the
coupling H F^2 contains H Psi^2, 2 H Psi R and H R^2.
F does not depend on H, so elimination, its field-independent
determinant and the H-source/vacuum square terms commute with
the map. No stable-H external LSZ assumption is introduced.

Yukawa y Phi bar(psi) psi becomes
y Psi bar(psi) psi + y R bar(psi) psi. The second vertex has
three scalar and two fermion legs. It matters already in
one-loop four-point functions with an ordinary Yukawa vertex.
Masses, gauge vertices and fermion kinetic terms are unchanged.

Every parent counterterm is transformed too. For four physical
Phi sources at total order two this includes the first-order
quadratic counterterms' generated quartic and sextic terms,
the first quartic counterterm's sextic term, the second
quadratic counterterm's quartic term, and the first Yukawa
counterterm's generated three-Phi vertex. The full transformed
counterterm functional, not this illustrative list, fixes
ownership and includes regulator-dependent local terms.

The two-site symbolic calculation uses arbitrary free and
full-quartic kernels and even fermion-bilinear spurions only
for this substitution identity. It does not replace Grassmann
loop signs or fermion numerators by commuting loop fields.
Generated higher derivatives are perturbative interactions;
none is resummed into an additional free propagator.
