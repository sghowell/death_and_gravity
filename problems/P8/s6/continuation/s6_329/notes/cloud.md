# Infinite count, finite angular energy and coefficient convergence

Partition0<w<=1 into dyadic bands(2^(-j-1),2^(-j)],j>=0.
Each has finite intensity a ln2 and defines an ordinary independent
Poisson point process with the fixed angular measure. Their countable
union defines the leading random measure.

The expected energy in band j is a*2^(-j-1), so the total expected
energy is a. Since energy is nonnegative, the total energy is finite
almost surely. For a>0, independent bands have the same strictly
positive probability of being nonempty. The probability that every
band after any fixed index is empty is the limit of products of
exp(-a ln2), hence zero. A countable union proves that infinitely many
bands are nonempty almost surely. At a=0 the cloud is empty.

The positive angular-energy measure sigma=sum_j w_j delta_nj thus
has finite mass although its counting measure is infinite.
For the finite restriction sigma_eta, the omitted mass decreases
to zero almost surely. Its mean is a*eta and its variance a*eta^2/2;
its second raw moment includes the additional a^2*eta^2 term.
The total-variation distance between the ENERGY measures is exactly
the omitted energy. This is not convergence of a divergent particle
count or of a Fock-space vector.

On R<=x<=1/8, S328 weak-to-uniform continuity therefore yields
||C_sigma_eta-C_sigma||sup_TT->0 almost surely. The mark is a
measurable continuous TT field, as a uniform limit of finite-state
fields. The ambient tensor representation gives a separable space
of continuous fields on S2, so its bounded Bochner moments exist.
The limiting object remains a mark on the leading reference.
