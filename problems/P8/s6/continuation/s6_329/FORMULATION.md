# Fixed leading reference and conditioned complete coefficient

Keep the original action and parameters, physical Born hard domain,
nu=mu=1, kappa=10^800, n=10^200/512+2 and g=1/8192.
The D4 physical leading measure is Lambda(dn)*dw/w,0<w<=1,
with positive angular measure Lambda and total index
a=int Lambda=K0/(4pi^2*kappa)>=0. It is fixed at the Born state.

Define sigma=sum_j w_j delta_nj by the Poisson random measure with
this intensity. Let R be its total energy and sigma_eta its finite
restriction w>=eta. For each fixed0<x<=1/8, use the S328 complete
logarithmic TT coefficient on the event R<=x, with the original
massive recoil induced by sigma. The mark does not change Lambda.

The normalized D4 reference has
P(R<=x)=F(a)*x^a, F(a)=exp(-EulerGamma*a)/Gamma(1+a).
The cutoff-conditioned complete coefficient converges in all finite
Lp of the uniform angular TT norm. For p>0,
E[R^p|R<=x]=a*x^p/(a+p), including the zero value at a=0.
Hence the full complex coefficient obeys
||E[C_sigma|R<=x]-C_Born||sup<=23667*a*x/(1+a)
and E[||C_sigma-C_Born||sup^2|R<=x]<=23667^2*a*x^2/(2+a).
Original a<4/(5kappa) gives physical bounds
19000*x/kappa^(5/2) and230000000*x^2/kappa^4.

constructed. The full hard amplitude, marked dimensional conversion,
interacting state and original V/G/B/P8 remain open.
