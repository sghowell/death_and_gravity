# Conditional response evidence, not completion

This step stays at the same finite-kappa base action and specified
conditional Gaussian vector measure/state as S6.176. S6.177's
auxiliary decoupling family is not varied here. The metric is fixed
to the CD geometry; only the scalar clock history is perturbed.

The new results are:

1. A finite-time causal Proca energy estimate with the actual temporal
   constraint and nonconserved external source, at all spatial momenta.
2. Full real analytic-source quadratic Sobolev and arbitrary Frechet
   derivative bounds on a stated compact clock-perturbation class.
3. Spatially integrated free coherent mean-energy bounds and an exact
   causal cubic bound for the source/contact fixed-metric light force.

The mean force is a source-driven classical/coherent response.
At fixed g the centered conditional Gaussian determinant is independent
of u. No new connected metric-determinant loop bound is inferred.

The initial all-order Hadamard covariance, canonical normalization,
physical Proca operator and covariant prescription are unchanged.
The coherent free stress difference is not the total source-dependent
metric tensor, and it cannot simply be added to the old absolute
reference-clock tensor as a complete backreaction calculation.
The exact homogeneous cancellation makes this distinction explicit.

The function class is C-infinity with compact spacetime support and
small coordinate jets through3. It is not a Fourier-band class, a
field-amplitude replacement for a physical momentum cutoff, a
statement about every external source image, or a global-time norm.
The all-momentum result is a theorem for the retained classical/
conditional Gaussian equations. It does NOT certify the physical
EFT at unlimited momentum.

S6.175's unbounded generic whole-time inverse near the Proca mass
shell is compatible with this finite-time energy theorem: the
domain, range and time interval of the norms are different.
Likewise S6.177's finite-gravity forward-limit issue is not resolved
by this fixed-metric causal estimate.

The full-source-force constant need not be small at the outer
delta=1/100 boundary. The separate delta<=1e-8 corollary makes the
specified source-force dual norm small, not the inverse of the
coupled scalar/metric equations.

Still open are variable-metric and symmetric-noise response norms,
the full interacting state and measure, light/tensor/auxiliary/mixed
quantum effects, a physical cutoff and omitted-order bounds, and a
self-consistent controlled background. Small stress/kappa on a
compact slab does not establish a relative error on the eternal
tails where the classical curvature tends to zero.

Vacuum quantum matching, full contours/cuts/truncation and a specified
finite-gravity IR/Regge remainder remain required. No all-UV exclusion,
new naturalness prior or global vacuum-to-bounce history is added.
Scoped P8(a) and A.20-A.23 are unchanged. Original V/G/B, P8(b) and P8
remain OPEN. Missing research estimates are not user-choice blockers.
