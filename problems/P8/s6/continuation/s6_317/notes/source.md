# Source ownership and why the representation changes

S315 owns the all-finite-multiplicity metric jets and complete rooted
tree source. S316 owns the complete single-off-shell-root conservation
argument, fixed-pair factorization and matching-forest accounting. Both
are direct or transitive read-only inputs. Their bytes and native reports
remain frozen; no correction is written into an old certificate.

The starting obstruction is substantive. A complete pure-three-soft
cluster in the original inverse gauge can have a pair-collinear pole,
even when contracted with the actual conserved original47-tree hard
current and even though the total cluster root stays timelike. Its
nested cubic remainder is not conserved. Treating a collapsed off-shell
cluster as a new physical free leaf also invalidates the single-root
Noether premise. S316 retains exact examples of both failures.

This successor does not delete those diagrams or declare their old
isolated values bounded. It changes the gauge representative at every
eligible intermediate pure-soft subset. Since all higher vertices then
receive the new lower coefficients, the compensation terms necessarily
propagate into the rest of the complete tree.

The same original inverse-kinetic policy is used on mixed and hard
subsets. This means the same linear de Donder or scalar inverse, NOT
that those coefficients keep their old numerical values. Retaining an
old hard remainder while changing only a cluster would miss the
induced terms and would not be the claimed complete reorganization.

There is no new interaction, matching constant, mass, regulator or
admissibility assumption. The bare selected tree source does not absorb
the derivative Proca/Gaussian or hard-loop corrections owned elsewhere.
The S313/S314 probability matching and state connector are unchanged.
A better current representation alone supplies no missing virtual
partner or new all-N detector measure.

The symbolic formal routines accept exact rational functions in
explicitly real symbols. They reject floats, unspecified-real symbols
and nonfinite entries. They are not a replacement for the frozen
numeric physical point API: formal identities are read off poles,
and norm statements additionally use the explicit future-ray domain.

The original 5116-tree physical three-real configuration is compared
at unchanged original parameters and at an exact diagnostic parameter
point. Both complete amplitudes and inventories agree exactly. These
calibrations catch normalization or missing-vertex mistakes; the
finite-degree gauge proof, not two samples, owns general invariance.
