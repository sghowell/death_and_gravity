# Complete recursive gauge policy and finite tree invariance

## The algorithm changes a representative, not the source

Use exactly the all-order action vertices and root partitions of S315.
First evaluate each amputated current from all proper subsets. Apply
the original inverse kinetic operator. If the result is a graviton
current on a non-singleton subset consisting entirely of future physical
graviton leaves, apply Pi_Q to that propagated tensor. All other
coefficients retain their original inverse-kinetic policy.

Higher coefficients use the changed lower tensors. In particular,
hard/mixed coefficient VALUES change; merely replacing one isolated
cluster in an otherwise old amplitude is not this algorithm.
Temporal division is never requested on a zero-energy hard channel.

The finite root-cut graph bijection and all graph inventories remain.
Projection is linear at a fixed subset momentum; it distributes over
that subset's complete sum. No graph class or vertex is dropped.
The reorganized weights are gauge representatives of the same complete
source, not a claim that an isolated diagram is gauge invariant.

## Ungaugefixed Euler/Noether induction

Work in the finite labeled ring of notes/chart.md. The free leaves are
the same physical on-shell scalar and TT graviton waves. They solve
their free Euler equations. Assume every proper-subset coefficient has
been solved for the UNGAUGEFIXED action.

The exact diffeomorphism Noether identity for the metric and matter
Euler derivatives contains, at the next subset S, only the background
metric times the divergence of E_g[S]: every other product contains a
proper-subset Euler coefficient and vanishes. Writing
E_g[S]=L_g H[S]+J[S], the linear Einstein identity div(L_g H)=0
therefore implies Q_mu J[S]^(mu nu)=0.

The original linear de Donder inverse of this conserved source solves
L_g H=-J. The difference Pi_Q H-H is of the form
q_mu xi_nu+q_nu xi_mu and lies in the kernel of L_g. Thus the projected
field also solves exactly the same coefficient equation. Scalar and
heavy inverses solve their unchanged equations as before. This closes
the induction for ANY finite number of labels.

All internal inverses are assumed nonsingular. This induction does not
apply to an arbitrarily truncated set of vertices or to off-shell
collapsed clusters treated as physical free leaves. The new complete
currents satisfy the premise; S316's failed isolated shortcuts do not.

## Relation to the literal coordinate chart

Away from Q_S^2=0, the kernel of the free Einstein operator consists of
linearized gauge directions, while the matter inverses have no kernel
away from their mass shells. Compare two formal solutions with the same
free waves, after making all lower coefficients coincide by lower-degree
diffeomorphisms. Their next metric difference is in that linear kernel
and can therefore be removed by a new degree-S coordinate shift; matter
differences are fixed by the unchanged equations. This is a finite
coefficient construction, requiring no infinite transformation.

On the all-positive-energy pure-gravity subring, imposing zero temporal
components fixes that last gauge shift uniquely, by notes/chart.md.
The literal nonlinear pullback and the recursively projected source
must therefore agree at every finite degree. Generic-r N3 and complete
N4 coefficient comparisons are implementation checks of this argument.

The mixed/hard gauge may require additional mixed-subset shifts to
restore its original inverse policy. It is not asserted that applying
only the pure-soft shifts to the entire old hard solution already
leaves its mixed coefficients in that policy.

## Invariance of the complete on-shell scalar-root tree

Construct all proper coefficients with the same incoming/outgoing
physical free waves and amputate the remaining scalar root. Before the
external root is put on shell, its inverse kinetic factor is
P_root^2-mu^2. The two complete coefficient solutions just described
differ by a higher-degree diffeomorphism with no linear action on the
physical free data.

Covariance of the scalar Euler equation transforms its nonlinear root
source into itself plus products involving proper-subset Euler
coefficients and a change of the root field multiplied by the root
inverse kinetic factor. The former terms vanish because all proper
coefficients are solved. The latter vanishes on the physical external
mass shell. The higher-degree coordinate products involved are finite
off proper internal poles; no external-root inverse is used to define
them. This is the finite-tree version of gauge/field-redefinition
invariance, not a loop or massless-LSZ construction.

Hence the complete four-Phi/N-h on-shell tree coefficient is unchanged
for every finite N on the stated generic domain. Its continuation
toward singular configurations requires separate bounds. Exact
5116-tree comparisons pass at both original and diagnostic parameters,
with every vertex and all lower transformations retained.

Nothing in this proof bounds the growth of higher derivatives, graph
sums or detector probabilities. Nor does finite formal covariance prove
the existence of an interacting Minkowski state or complex Regge control.
