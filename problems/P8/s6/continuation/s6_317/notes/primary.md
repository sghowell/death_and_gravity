# Primary-source audit and what is not imported

The [Beneke-Kirilin soft-collinear gravity paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1207.4926)
was consulted in its original PDF, printed pages8-11, equations28-50.
Text extraction and visual page inspection confirmed the structural
role of a coordinate/field redefinition applied consistently to
propagation and hard sources. This motivated retaining nonlinear
compensation rather than projecting one isolated cluster.

The [Beneke-Hager-Szafron soft-collinear gravity review](https://arxiv.org/html/2112.04983)
was read in sections4.3-4.4, particularly the distinction between
linear and nonlinear field combinations around equations4.30-4.31.
That displayed version is the2022v2 text, not a silently updated
substitute for the publisher's later corrections.

The official [2024 erratum](https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP04(2024)141)
and its [publisher-supplied SCOAP3 record](https://repo.scoap3.org/records/84989)
were checked; all three PDF pages were text-extracted and visually
inspected. Corrections concern the cubic Einstein-Hilbert expansion,
a scalar-matter contribution to the trace constraint and
soft-covariant-derivative terms.

No expanded action identity or numerical bound from those formulas
is imported. The metric vertices remain the independently checked
literal-action coefficients of the frozen source. The new temporal
chart follows directly from J^T g(x+iZ)J, and its finite-tree relation
follows from the action's own Noether identity.

The papers are structural guides, not a quantitative detector theorem
for this source. They supply neither this explicit coefficient budget
nor the still-missing arbitrary-energy, all-multiplicity inclusive
remainder estimate. Those statements are not attributed to them.

The first attempted text extraction preceded completion of one PDF
download and failed on the partial file. After the download completed,
extraction and full-page visual inspection succeeded. No input identity
was accepted from an incomplete document.
