# What has and has not been matched

This continuation retains the same canonical mass1000 Proca sector,
full S6.199 flat tensor cut, parent normalization and fixed CD
prescription. It computes a full spatial nonlocal-representative
conversion in the flat reference vacuum. The actual all-order CD
state is not changed or replaced by finite-WKB or flat initial data.

The covariant nonlocal numerator and time-derivative subtraction are
different at nonzero spatial transfer. Their finite difference is
derived, including all three equal-time coefficients; its spatial
nonlocality is proved by a TT diagnostic with infinitely many
nonzero positive spectral moments. This diagnostic does not replace
the full tensor derivation or either spin sector.

The complete specified flat nonlocal representative has a continuum
weak bound and an explicit spectral-tail estimate. Its physical local
tensor/contact polynomial, including tadpole and metric-parametrization
contacts, is still separate. The result does not assert that this
polynomial is zero or redo S6.193's dimensionally continued matching.

In S6.198's actual curved problem the full reference contact and
first five equal-time kernels still require fixed covariant spatial
matching. A flat benchmark cannot transfer that result, identify
different finite regulators, or discard curved odd endpoints.
The constant-alpha W8 readout remains a comparison device.

All nine primitive rows and every earlier matching row are unchanged;
only the new scoped conversion row is added. Tests reject promoted
or missing rows and changes of the fixed parent parameters.
Full spatial/mixed inverse, finite-coupling interacting background,
stability, parent state/measure/loops, threshold/heavy/cutoff and
omitted-order matching, vacuum cuts/contour/truncation and finite-gravity
IR/Regge remain open. Scoped P8(a) and A.20-A.23 are unchanged.
