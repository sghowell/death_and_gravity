# S6.145: paired nonlocal quadratic primitive remainders

Continue S6.144's unchanged GY14 reference model. Establish the
nonlocal physical-on-shell remainders of scalar_Phi2_W0_F2 and
gauge_Phi2, with all relevant proper MS subtractions retained.

For mF>=720, nonnegative rational Y_hi,a_hi and 0<Q_lo<=144,
bound the complete soft Taylor tail of degree four and above on
|s-1|<=2. Deduce a bound for |Pi_R(s)|/(s-1)^2 on |s-1|<=1/2,
where Pi_R=Pi(s)-Pi(1)-(s-1)Pi'(1). Both scalar and gauge sectors,
every cyclic word, both overlapping Yukawa vertex subtractions,
and the local whole-fermion-cycle term must be accounted for.

The finite MS values Pi(1),Pi'(1) are NOT established by this target.
It is not the full matched two-loop pole, vacuum potential or
amplitude. Other finite references, counterterm/matching insertions,
canonical factors, two vacuum rows, truncation, V/G/B and original
P8 remain open. The adopted finite-EFT/necessary-positivity contract
does not acquire an all-orders UV requirement.
