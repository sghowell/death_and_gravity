# Three words and every proper cycle

The fourth-order fermion determinant term has cyclic factor 1/4.
Four labelled derivatives give six cyclic orderings; contracting
the two internal boson labels contributes one half. Hence there
are three words per quadratic primitive, not six or sixty.
Fix external P1 first, choose two internal B positions among the
remaining three, and put P2 in the last position. Adjacent B
positions give two self-energy words. Opposite positions give
one vertex word with two-fermion arcs on both sides.

The graph has four fermion edges around a cycle and one boson chord.
Enumerating every connected degree-two edge subset gives exactly
three proper one-loop cycles. For a self-energy word their UV
degrees are 1,-1,0. For the vertex word they are 0,0,0.
The degree-zero whole-fermion cycle is present in both cases:
it must not be omitted from the forest inventory.

Every pair of these cycles shares edges. Thus none of their products
is a compatible proper forest. There is no other proper two-loop
one-particle-irreducible subgraph; keeping two independent cycles
requires all three paths of the theta graph, hence the full graph.

Closing the chord after the whole-fermion-cycle local subtraction
produces a Phi^2 local tadpole term. It has no external momentum
and is killed by the on-shell affine subtraction used here.
For the gauge row one may organize intermediate local subtractions
before the gauge-invariant sum; the nonlocal result does not
depend on such an auxiliary local allocation. No finite mass
value or new gauge-invariant mass counterterm is inferred.

The full graph has UV degree two. Its overall subtraction is
a mass/kinetic polynomial, likewise killed by the projection.
The surviving nonlocal self-energy and vertex subtractions are
treated before either unbounded loop integration.
