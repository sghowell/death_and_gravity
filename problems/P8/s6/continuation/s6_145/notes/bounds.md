# Exact constants and the actual enclosure

For each self-energy word the three fermion propagators plus trace
give 32/S_q^(3/2). The complete proper kernel obeys the inherited bound

 ||Sigma_MS|| <=sqrt(S_q)(3Y+16a)/Q
                  [log(S_q/m^2)+6].

The soft tail multiplies by 2*360^4/S_q^2. The exact radial moment is
integral x[log(S_q/m^2)+6]/S_q^3 dx=15/(4m^2).
Both marks yield prefactor 8062156800000<1e13. Thus

 E_self <=1e13 N Y_hi(3Y_hi+16a_hi)/(Q_lo^2 m^2).

For the single vertex word, set c=Y_hi+4a_hi C_F=Y_hi+16a_hi/3.
The region proof gives B*(95/2)=25530163200000<3e13, hence

 E_vertex <=3e13 N Y_hi c/(Q_lo^2 m^2).

The two conservative bounds are equal after the coupling dictionary.
Their sum is <=6e13 N Y_hi c/(Q_lo^2 m^2).
The divided on-shell remainder is at most one third of that sum.

All parameters are inherited exactly. N=6 is the active color/flavor
multiplicity; C_F=4/3 is the open-line gauge Casimir. The twelve inert
fermions have no Phi Yukawa vertex and do not enter these rows.

At m=10^200 and the unchanged rational coupling enclosures,

 E_self <=4.91152090801051e-800,
 E_vertex <=4.91152090801051e-800,
 E_total <=9.82304181602103e-800,
 |Pi_R|/(s-1)^2 <=3.27434727200701e-800 <1e-799.

These are absolute error bounds, not signs of the amplitudes and
not a ratio to the four-point tree coefficient. They cover only
the two primitive nonlocal quadratic contributions.
