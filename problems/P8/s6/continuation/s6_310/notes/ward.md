# Ward decoupling and independent physical checks

The general tree Ward statement follows from the original covariant
action, not from a claim that finitely many samples imply a polynomial
identity. Under an infinitesimal diffeomorphism,
delta g_mn=partial_m xi_n+partial_n xi_m+Lie_xi(g-eta)_mn and
delta Phi=xi.partial Phi, delta H=xi.partial H.
Invariance of the action gives the off-shell functional identity

2 nabla_m[(1/sqrt(-g)) delta S/delta g_mn]
-[(1/sqrt(-g)) delta S/delta Phi] partial^n Phi
-[(1/sqrt(-g)) delta S/delta H] partial^n H=0,

up to a simultaneous sign convention for the active transformation.
Its content is the same in either convention: the divergence of a
metric vertex equals adjacent inverse-kinetic insertions and lower
metric/matter variations.

Expand this identity at the field degree required for a six-point
tree. Contracting one external metric leg by q_m xi_n+xi_m q_n
telescopes along the complete root-cut sum: each internal inverse
kinetic factor cancels its propagator, and the two ways to place the
remaining variation at an adjacent vertex cancel by the next Taylor
coefficient of this same functional identity. There is no missing
boundary vertex because the complete metric jets and all tree
partitions through this arity are retained.

At the external ends, inverse scalar kinetic factors vanish on shell.
The other external metric leg is on shell and transverse traceless;
longitudinal gauge-fixing contributions vanish there. Linear de Donder
gauge changes only the free inverse metric operator, not the higher
covariant vertices. With no external ghosts, a closed ghost species
line would be a loop and is absent in this tree argument. This proves
on-shell pure-gauge decoupling for either metric leg of the selected
complete tree. It is the classical covariant-action tree identity,
not an all-loop BRST or quantum-unitarity construction.

Separately, an exact rational non-opposite two-real state checks both
metric Ward replacements for all four independent gauge vectors.
Three scalar transpositions and the graviton exchange check Bose
symmetry. All conservation and mass/null shells are tested directly.
The two newly needed vertices are indispensable: independently
reversing either the Einstein quartic sign or the Phi^2 h^3 sign
makes a Ward contraction nonzero.

At two other rational configurations, the recursion reproduces all7
four-point trees and all47 one-graviton trees from the separately
frozen implementations, including the pure-gravity3/21 subcounts.
These checks test normalization and complete amplitudes, not just
their longitudinal projections. None is used as a uniform bound.
