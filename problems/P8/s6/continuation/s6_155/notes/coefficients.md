# Minimal-pole coefficient map and its finite commutator

Consider a total bare interaction coefficient written in
the MS renormalized Phi field,

    X+h p1/epsilon+h^2(p22/epsilon^2+p21/epsilon).

This statement concerns total vertex coefficients, not
bare parameters with their external field factors omitted.
For Phi weight w, multiply it by K_D^(-w), where

    K_D=1+h(k0+epsilon k1+...)+h^2(t0+epsilon t1+...).

At fixed MS INPUT coordinates, its finite coefficient is

    Xc = X-h w k0 X
      +h^2{[w(w+1)k0^2/2-w t0]X-w k1 p1}.

The remaining double and simple poles are
p22/epsilon^2+(p21-w k0 p1)/epsilon. If they are subsequently
expressed as functions of Xc, their parameter re-expansion
must also be performed; that is not silently included here.

The weights are w_L=2, w_G=1, w_y=1/2 and w_M=0.
The actual first total residues, checked against S6.133
and S6.139, are

    p1L=(3L^2/2-24NY^2)/Q,
    p1G=LG/(2Q), p1M=g/(2Q),
    p1y=(Y-4a Cf)y/Q.

The term -w k1 p1 is the finite failure of pole projection
to commute with a full dimensional field change. Setting
k_D=k0 before multiplication would lose it.

Use FUNDAMENTAL G and y before squaring their series:

    g_c^(2)=g[3k0^2-2t0-k1 L/Q],
    Y_c^(2)=Y[k0^2-t0-k1(Y-4a Cf)/Q].

Both first-order squares are present. Inert y=0 remains
zero without division by y. H, m, a and M are unchanged
in the Phi-only direction.

The G commutator induces b2/tree=-L k1/Q; its quartic
counterpart is a forward constant. This is an equivalent
finite coefficient-map representation. It is not added
again to the already field-transformed FULL renormalized
amplitude, whose raw/counterterm products cancel as shown
in the preceding note. No new finite parameter is fitted.
