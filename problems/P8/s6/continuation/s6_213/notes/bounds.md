# Complementary region and dominated Fourier pairing

The far estimate alone is insufficient. For all real k and ell=P-k, the continued massive frequencies have uniform lower bounds and the phase-stripped amplitudes have the same polynomial energy scaling as the physical modes. General-d contractions are a finite sum with bounded rational multiplicities after transverse averaging. A complete pair is therefore bounded by C sqrt(nu mu), with a constant uniform on the dimension disk; each inverse summed phase is bounded by2/(nu+mu). Source-clock Cauchy differentiation preserves these scalings. In particular,

    2nu mu/(nu+mu) <=2nu

retains suppression of an arbitrarily large second leg. The full j0,...,4 endpoint row is at most C nu, uniformly in external P. This is the same explicit all-real argument as S203; the continued W8 nonzero bounds in dimension.md supply its previously missing complex-d hypotheses. Massive denominators prevent an infrared singularity, including k0 and ell0.

For 0<=r<m the integrand is unexpanded. Its dimensional radial measure has worst exponent r^(2-1/4) at the origin, so the whole low band is uniformly integrable, independent of P. For m<=r<L=200U, retain both the entire raw row and every UV coefficient. The raw integral is bounded by a constant times U^(4+1/4). A slot of degree j+b=q<4 has coefficient at most C_j U^b and radial integral bounded by C L^(4-q+1/4), hence by C U^(4-j+1/4). For q4, keep its logarithm before estimating: r^Re(eta)<=L^(1/4), and

    integral_m^L dr/r =log(L/m)<=L/m.

Thus the worst near power is U^(5-j+1/4). This deliberately loose envelope includes all fifteen slots and all thirty-five source-time entries. In particular it does not delete an endpoint merely because its physical dimension value vanishes.

Because m>=1 and U>=1, every power q<=21/4 is bounded by U^6, and

    U^6 <=8m^6(1+|P|^2)^3.

For Schwartz spatial sources and detectors, Cauchy-Schwarz in Fourier space with the X46 source weight makes the product integrable. Cauchy-Schwarz over the unit time slab retains all source jets. Combining low, near and far radial majorants with the angular envelope gives one integrable majorant independent of d on |d-3|<=1/4.

Dominated convergence therefore puts the dimension limit inside the complete UV-subtracted comparison integral and Fourier/time pairing. Its value is exactly the physical Qnew of S208. Adding the unchanged convergent physical state/time pieces is legitimate separately. The dimensionally integrated singular spatial difference and its original fixed counterterm have the S212 finite part, including all evanescent terms. This establishes the required matching identity for the tracefree spatial response, without asserting a complex-d physical state or a full interacting determinant.
