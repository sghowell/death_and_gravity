# Complete compact conformal-time Hessians

The original finite local action is used after its dimensional finite matching. In physical dimension four,

-4a4_scalar=-C^2/30-R_old^2/18+Euler/90.

The Euler variation is removed only for compact metric variations at this stage. The finite Weyl coefficient-1/30 is not replaced by the Proca pole/cut weight13/120. The fixed Einstein coefficient remains(5/3)m^2, and the unimodular volume term vanishes identically under variation.

The complete quadratic actions, modulo compact spatial and time divergences, are

S_R^(2)=integral a^2[||gamma'||^2-||grad gamma||^2+2||div gamma||^2]/4,

S_R2^(2)=integral[(partial_i partial_j gamma_ij)^2
                  +3(a''/a)(||gamma'||^2-||grad gamma||^2+2||div gamma||^2)].

Varying twice produces

H_R=-(a^2 partial_eta^2+2aa' partial_eta+a^2(qI-2B))/2,
H_R2=-6[r partial_eta^2+r' partial_eta+r(qI-2B)]+2C, r=a''/a.

The full compact Weyl Hessian is

H_C=partial_eta^4+(2qI-2B)partial_eta^2+q^2I-2qB+2C/3.

The literal plane-averaged curvature density differs from its integrated-by-parts quadratic representative by the time derivative of

F=tr[M0(-q M1/2+B_P(M1))].

The compact representative is

||M2||^2/4-tr[M1(2qM1-2B_P(M1))]/4
+tr[M0(q^2M0-2qB_P(M0)+2C_P(M0)/3)]/4.

All terms in F are retained in the independent pointwise comparison. Only after compact integration is its derivative zero. The noncommuting matrix time jets and generic P checks recover this full expression; no odd or nontransverse component is discarded pointwise.

The resulting fixed local Hessian is

H_fixed=[(5/3)m^2 H_R-H_C/30-H_R2/18]/(64pi^2).

It is a compact action second variation with the stated metric parametrization. It is not, by itself, equality of the remaining quantum reference-contact sector to this local action. The latter still requires its original finite/divergent and regulator matching.
