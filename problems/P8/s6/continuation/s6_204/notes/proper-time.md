# Full clock conversion and the action measure

The coordinate change obeys deta=dt/a and partial_eta=a partial_t. Consequently the proper-time density operator in the same compact action pairing is

H_t=a^-1 H_eta.

Keeping only the derivative substitution would lose the density factor. Independent negative controls at a nonconstant CD time detect that mistake for each curvature and each spatial helicity.

For a normalized helicity let(c,b,d,e) be

tensor:(1,2,1,0),
vector:(0,1,0,0),
scalar:(-1/3,2/3,1/9,2/3).

The two tensor and two vector components share their respective coefficients. Put S=Hdot+2H^2. The complete proper-time operators are

H_R x=-[(a^3 xdot)dot+a c q x]/2,

H_R2 x=-6[(a^3 S xdot)dot+a S c q x]+2e q^2 x/a,

H_C x=a^3[x^(4)+6H x^(3)+(4Hdot+11H^2)x''
              +(Hddot+7H Hdot+6H^3)x']
        +bqa(x''+H x')+dq^2x/a.

The fourth conformal derivative is expanded exactly. No time-friction or Hdot/Hddot term is dropped, and no constant-a approximation is taken.

Self-adjointness is with respect to the proper dt measure. It follows directly from the conformal compact bilinear and its a^-1 density factor. Independent actual-CD numerical pairings use compact profiles with enough vanishing boundary jets to check all three curvature contributions in their fixed combination, including very large external spatial momentum.

These are tracefree spatial action operators. Their helicity names do not solve lapse/shift constraints, identify canonical physical scalar/vector modes or supply a complete mixed response norm.
