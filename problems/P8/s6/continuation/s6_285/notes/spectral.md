# Complete conserved Gaussian metric cuts

In the COM frame, put E^2=k^2+nu. The scalar stress pair in the Gaussian
quadratic-form convention has T_0a=0 and

T_ij=k_i k_j-E^2 delta_ij.

It is half the two-scalar Feynman stress insertion. Its spatial trace is
-(s+2nu)/2. On the three-dimensional transverse symmetric tensor space,
the spin0 and spin2 projectors have ranks1 and5. The trace-square/3
eigenvalue is(s+2nu)^2/12; the traceless norm divided by5 is(s-4nu)^2/120.
The Wick pair factor2 is in the measure, not this tensor.

The two-body phase space is beta/(8pi). For the actual current
i theta<[T,T]>/4, the positive density is Wightman/(8pi), giving

rho_Phi,2=beta(s-4nu)^2/(3840pi^2),
rho_Phi,0=beta(s+2nu)^2/(384pi^2).

S199's all-nine-pair Proca calculation gives

rho_A,2=beta(13s^2+56nu s+48nu^2)/(3840pi^2),
rho_A,0=beta(s^2-4nu s+12nu^2)/(384pi^2).

All vanish below4nu and are positive above. The projectors describe
conserved off-shell metric sources, not additional massless graviton
polarizations. The full cut is fixed by rotations and Lorentz covariance,
not a forward-only scalar replacement.

An independent literal scalar exchange calculation gives

A_s=[s^2+8mu nu-(s-4mu)(s-4nu)x^2]/(4kappa s)
=a0+a2 P2(x),

a0=(s+2mu)(s+2nu)/(6kappa s),
a2=-(s-4mu)(s-4nu)/(6kappa s).

The complete angular sew is a0^2+a2^2 P2(z)/5, multiplied by beta/(32pi).
The identical-pair and optical halves are retained. This agrees with
contracting the conserved current projectors with the original scalar
gravitational source. For nu1 this is the metric self-energy SUBSET of
the full PhiPhi cut, never a new cut to add twice.

For I_i=integral rho_i/sigma^4 and J_i=integral rho_i/sigma^5,
the change sigma=4nu/(1-v^2) gives exact polynomial integrals:

scalar: I2=1/(53760pi^2 nu), J2=1/(967680pi^2 nu^2),
I0=17/(26880pi^2 nu), J0=1/(13824pi^2 nu^2);

Proca: I2=3/(3584pi^2 nu), J2=31/(322560pi^2 nu^2),
I0=3/(8960pi^2 nu), J0=1/(32256pi^2 nu^2).

The heavy determinant's metric cut is not the whole heavy-production
scattering amplitude, which has other source and mixed contributions.
