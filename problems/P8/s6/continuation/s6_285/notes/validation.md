# Validation boundary

Exact checks use the whole inherited source and both fixed covariant
prescriptions, unrestricted Gaussian metric volume jets, literal scalar
stress and the original all-polarization Proca density, complete
spin0/spin2 projectors, arbitrary scalar covariance and instantaneous
second vertex, full finite metric variations, exact convergent moments,
radial normalizations and explicit finite-light-coefficient dependence.

Independent science tests reconstruct the literal scalar gravitational
exchange, angular projection and stress norms; solve covariance entries
with independent inputs; integrate moments at additional orders; vary
full metric determinants and local actions; and check the unmatched
light polynomial is neither zeroed nor covered by the H/Proca bounds.
Written covariance, analyticity, finite matching, projector locality
and low-disk arguments are not FORMALIZED. A numerical calculation alone
is not a continuum proof.

A source-scope audit rejected an initially broader private interpretation
before acceptance: H's curved finite prescription cannot simply be
transferred to Phi. The final packet retains that missing Phi matching
explicitly. Successful algebra does not certify an unsupported scope.

Cold private and fresh repository preflights must agree on every exact
source hash, module export and AST-derived report field. Parent-first
warmup must not change the source dictionary or packet contract.
Only after both pass can the source manifest be frozen and the native
helper receive its single report specification.

Native, direct, ordinary and CLI execution retain original SymPy GCD.
Only a captured complete FULL regression may use the exact adapter.
Every report field, source hash, residual, gate and retained qualification
is checked on read-only replay. Actual cold/fresh/native/independent/FULL
outcomes belong in the external assessment, never in edited frozen inputs.
