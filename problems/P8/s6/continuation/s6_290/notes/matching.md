# Matching consequences and still-independent coefficients

S286 determined the graph-generated F1 but explicitly left F2,
H-metric mixing and curved finite terms open. This packet supplies
the complete minimal C/g matter endpoint in the original variables,
including both F2 triangles, quartic and H-mixing bubbles, and the
already fixed covariant OS/onepoint terms.

The remaining minimal curved coordinates are displayed as
ell+h/(n-t). The constant ell contributes only the crossed
constant proportional to10mu and has zero b20. The finite
H-curvature residue h contributes exactly

-2h(n+2mu)/[kappa(n-2mu)^3].

Flat light OS and heavy-onepoint conditions cannot determine it:
R_old H vanishes on the flat background used to impose them.
The exact coefficient of h is useful matching information,
but no bound on h itself is invented. Higher-derivative
curvature/EFT coefficients remain separate matching data.
This is not a new finite prescription for the original action.

The full known endpoint graph contribution has the finite-mass
bound in notes/forward.md. It is not the full mixed matter/gravity
four-point coefficient. For example, a one-loop graph with a
quartic vertex and two scalar-graviton vertices can have one
external Phi at each gravitational vertex and two at the quartic.
It is not the factorized endpoint topology computed here.
Such irreducible graphs, other coupling/species sectors and
their counterterms remain in the full amplitude.

The Phi Gaussian metric contribution identified in S289 and the
minimal-gravity cut completion of S288 are not added again as
new endpoint graphs. Nor does this matter-only calculation
remove S288's massive Coulomb phase or establish a finite
physical detector/dressing/IR observable.

The original finite-gravity contract still requires an explicit
fixed-negative-transfer Regge/high-energy remainder and the
pole/contour combination before the forward limit. The common
parent/state/bounce construction and all-loop error control
also remain necessary. No incomplete quantity is assigned a
positivity sign and no selected ansatz failure closes all P8.
Original V/G/B/P8 stay OPEN; scoped P8(a) is unchanged.
