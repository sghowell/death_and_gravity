# Provenance and verification boundary

The common parent and subtraction are frozen
in S6.133 and S6.138. The scalar insertion
spectral density is frozen in S6.135.
The present dimensional continuation,
gamma-function integral, finite remainder
and local-parent conversion are derived
in this checkpoint.

The dimensional normalization independently
reproduces the old one-loop MS mass finite
term. Tests also compare the spectral
gamma result to a direct radial integral
at epsilon=1/2, and extract both poles and
finite parts with a small complex regulator.

The Gamma shift and half-integer polygamma
recurrences are applied explicitly. Real
logarithm endpoint integrals use one-sided
real-domain primitives. These are exact
analytic identities, not changes to SymPy
or precision-dependent fitted constants.

Analytic continuation, spectral identities
and regulator-limit arguments are written
proofs, not proof-assistant formalization
or independent peer review. No full-theory
UV completion or exact high-energy contour
property is inferred from them.
