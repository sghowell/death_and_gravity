# Finite common-parent interaction conversion

The frozen S6.138 family subtracts the entire
regulated F_D sum C(z)^2. The common MS
interaction scheme subtracts only its pole
part. Their amplitude difference at the same
leading reference parameters is therefore

 A_MS-A_sub=+F_MS sum_z C(z)^2,
 C(z)=-L+g/(M-z).

This sign also follows by equating the two
bare parent parameter representations.
The matched subtracted-scheme parameters
have finite order-two shifts

 delta L_star=-3L^2 F_MS,
 delta g_star=-2Lg F_MS,
 delta M_star=-g F_MS,
 delta G_star=-L G F_MS.

Literal variation of -L+sum_z g/(M-z)
gives +F_MS sum C(z)^2. The squared-cubic
shift is 2G delta G_star=delta g_star.
The lower-order square G1^2 belongs to the
existing full conversion ledger; it is not
generated or counted again here.

For D=M-2 and tree b2=2g/D^3,

 delta b2=F_MS[-4Lg/D^3+6g^2/D^4],
 delta b2/b2_tree=F_MS[-2L+3g/D].

The finite reference is calculated from
the common parent, not chosen to tune b2.
A safe converted family bound is

 E_MS=E_S6.138
       +|b2_tree(-2L+3g/D)| F_abs_upper.

The new conversion is about
3.41341221406136e-413 relative to the tree.
The original full-family bound still
dominates, so the converted b2 bound remains
below 1e-622 and below 1e-22 relative to
the tree. This is a b2 bound; no new claim
about the whole-disc amplitude bound after
conversion is needed.

The new parameter shift is order two.
Its variation of a one-loop graph starts
at order three. Separate order-one shifts
inside one-loop graphs, the full order-two
canonical Phi residue, other counterterm
insertions and other primitive rows remain
in their existing ledger entries.

Only the W2-W2-F0 family's outer interaction
reference advances to the common MS scheme.
This does not complete the full enlarged-model
two-loop amplitude, pole or matching error.
