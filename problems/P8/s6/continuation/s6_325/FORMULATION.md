# Uniform radiative-state continuity and a named paired soft insertion

Keep the original action, parameters and S300 recoil, with incoming
E in[5/4,2] and total positive null energy0<=R<=1/8. Radiation may
have any finite multiplicity, arbitrary real directions and collinear
splitting. State0 is the Born state with the same E and outgoing
pair-rest-frame direction.

The unchanged S301 angular convention is
 a_sigma,e=p(e)*K_sigma,e/(4*pi^2*kappa)
          =a_sigma+2e*Delta_sigma+O(e^2).
The full fixed-ball trace term, radial derivative and phase p(e)
are retained. Delta is this specified universal soft conversion,
not an unknown finite hard-matching coordinate.

The proven differences, for0<R<=1/8, are
 |a_sigma-a0|<1400R/kappa,
 |Delta_sigma-Delta0|<10000R*(1+ln(1/R))/kappa,
 |[(a_sigma,e-a0,e)-(a_sigma-a0)]/(2e)|
       <15000R*(1+ln(1/R))/kappa, 0<e<=1/8.
They vanish at the exact R=0 Born boundary. The last bound is
energy-dependent uniformly in e; a state-uniform O(e^2) estimate
alone could not be integrated against dR/R.

At fixed radiative state, the named universal leading-soft real
term up to energy y is a_sigma,e*y^(2e)/(2e), paired with the
same-state virtual probability pole-a_sigma/(2e). Relative to
Born, set
 Z_e(sigma;y)=[(a_sigma,e-a0,e)*y^(2e)-(a_sigma-a0)]/(2e).
Its limit is delta Delta+delta a*ln y, not a pole left by an
elastic virtual replacement. The amplitude Coulomb phase remains.

Let dLambda0(b,Omega) be the physical D4 leading one-real Born
measure, with radial db/b and angular coefficient a0<8/kappa.
For the exact one-real marked state sigma_b define only the measure
 dnu_e,x=Z_e(sigma_b;x-b)dLambda0 on0<b<x,
with total mass C_e(x)=int dnu_e,x.
Then at every fixed0<x<=1/8 the regulator limit exists in total
variation and
 TV(nu_x)<200000*x*(1+ln(1/x))/kappa^2
      =2*10^-1595*x*(1+ln(1/x)).
Both the b=0 boundary and the remaining-energy b=x endpoint are
integrably dominated. The marked seed is physical; only the
additional universal soft factor is dimensionally continued.

This named leading-soft correction does not reconstruct the finite
radiative hard loop or whole NNLO rate. Matching it to every term
of the full regulated source, retaining outer-state evanescent
terms and hard coefficients, is a separate obligation. No such
term is set to zero. Original V/G/B/P8 remain OPEN.
