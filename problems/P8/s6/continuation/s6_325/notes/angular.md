## State continuity of the finite angular conversion

S301 also gives, for tau=1-t, the state-uniform radial bounds
 |H_sigma(t)-H_sigma(1)|<32000*tau^(1/4),
 |U_sigma(t)-U_sigma(1)|<21000*tau^(1/4),
and the same bounds at Born. Therefore
 |delta H(t)-delta H(1)|
   <=min(100000R,64000*tau^(1/4)),
 |delta U(t)-delta U(1)|
   <=min(60000R,42000*tau^(1/4)).

For AR<B the elementary split at tau0=(AR/B)^4 yields
 int_0^1 min(AR,B*tau^(1/4))/tau dtau
 =4AR*[1+ln(B/(AR))].
Both required crossovers lie below one for R<=1/8, and B/A<1.
Writing L_R=1+ln(1/R), obtain
 I_H<=400000R*L_R, I_U<=240000R*L_R.
The factor sqrt(t) only decreases these absolute majorants.

It follows that
 |delta K1|<=15000R+400000R*L_R,
 |delta K0|<=50000R,
 |delta a|<1400R/kappa,
 |delta Delta|<10000R*L_R/kappa.
For the last inequality use |p'(0)|<4, pi>3 and
(15000+400000+200000)/72=615000/72<10000.
In particular the finite conversion is continuous at zero radiation,
uniformly over arbitrary finite null multiplicity and all angles.
The R=0 values are defined by the exact Born state and R*L_R->0.
