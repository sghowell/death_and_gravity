## Exact unchanged convention

Use S300's arbitrary finite radiative state sigma, total null energy
0<=R<=1/8, same incoming E in[5/4,2] and rest-frame outgoing direction
u as its Born state0. S301 defines the fixed-ball projected current T,
H(t)=mean[tr(T^2)-tr(T)^2/2], U(t)=mean[tr(T)^2], K0=H(1), and
 K1=U(1)/2+int_0^1 sqrt(t)[H(t)-H(1)]/(1-t) dt.
With the unchanged phase p(e),
 a_e=p(e)*K_e/(4*pi^2*kappa)
     =a+2e*Delta+O(e^2),
 a=K0/(4*pi^2*kappa),
 Delta=[K1+p'(0)*K0]/(8*pi^2*kappa).
The full additional-soft projector and its finite trace derivative
are retained. Fractional-dimensional contractions need not be positive.

Background sources distinguish universal leading soft factorization
from corrections involving the hard process. Naculich and Schnitzer's
eikonal construction gives one-loop soft exponentiation under stated
factorization assumptions, not this model's complete hard remainder
[arXiv:1101.1524v3](https://arxiv.org/html/1101.1524v3).
White treats massive emitters and subleading internal emissions
[arXiv:1103.2981](https://arxiv.org/html/1103.2981).
The recent double-soft Wilson-line derivation retains internal
emissions and does not supply a quantified all-multiplicity remainder
[arXiv:2511.05455v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.05455v2).
The estimates below are a direct derivation from the frozen recoil
and fixed-ball formulas, not imported numerical constants.
