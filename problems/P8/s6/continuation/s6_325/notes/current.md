## A uniform state difference at every radial point

For one future massive spatial momentum p, set
 f(p)=P*p*p^T*P/[sqrt(1+|p|^2)-r*n.p], r=sqrt(t).
The relevant spatial convex path has |p|<=sqrt(3)<2, energy<=2
and denominator>1/4. P is an orthogonal projector. In nuclear norm,
the numerator has norm<4 and derivative norm<4 per unit spatial
displacement. The denominator derivative has norm<2. Therefore
 ||df||<4*4+4*2*16=144.
The two outgoing massive momenta each shift by at most3R; incoming
ones do not change. Every null term has nuclear norm<=2omega at
every radial point. Thus, with delta denoting sigma minus Born,
 ||delta T(t,n)||<=2*144*3R+2R=866R
uniformly in direction, radial point, multiplicity and collinear
splitting (using bounded a.e. representatives on exceptional points).

Both currents have nuclear norm and absolute trace<=B=65/4. The
quadratic and trace-square differences consequently obey
 |delta H(t)|<=3B*866R<50000R,
 |delta U(t)|<=2B*866R<30000R.
These estimates include the complete conserved massive-plus-null
current. A recoiled massive subset would be a different kernel.
