## Same-state real and virtual pole, with phase retained

At a fixed generic hard state the universal leading soft real
factor integrated up to remaining energy y is a_e*y^(2e)/(2e).
The inherited same-state virtual PROBABILITY pole is-a/(2e),
equivalently real part-a/(4e) in the amplitude. A virtual Coulomb
phase i*phi/e remains in that amplitude; it drops out of this
first interference only, not from amplitudes or the Regge problem.

The leading external residues are fixed by the original scalar and
Einstein vertices, not an elastic replacement. At one loop the
soft exchange between two external eikonal denominators has radial
power-1+2e. An internal hard attachment without one such denominator
has power0+2e and cannot supply this soft logarithmic pole at fixed
nonexceptional hard state. Summing the conserved current before
angular integration removes the spurious collinear problem. The
real part of its Gaussian soft vacuum contraction has the factor
minus one half of the real emission norm, giving the stated
probability pole. This identifies the universal leading-soft
contribution only; a full radiative hard-loop reconstruction,
UV subtraction and its finite remainder have NOT been supplied.

For a radiative state compared with Born, the paired difference is
 Z_e(sigma;y)=[delta a_e*y^(2e)-delta a]/(2e).
Its fixed-y limit is
 Z(sigma;y)=delta Delta+delta a*ln y.
Using the elastic virtual pole in place of the radiative one leaves
delta a/(2e); no small coefficient can make that pole finite.

## Calorimetric Born control

For a_e=a+2e*Delta+e^2*q, put I_e=a_e*x^(2e)/(2e).
The independent leading Born second-order real/virtual combination is
 RR=I_e^2*Gamma(1+2e)^2/[2*Gamma(1+4e)],
 RV=-a*I_e/(2e), VV=a^2/(8e^2).
Its poles cancel and its finite limit is
 (Delta+a*ln x)^2/2-pi^2*a^2/12.
The Gamma correction is necessary; separate independent-energy
cuts would miss it. This agrees with the second coefficient of
the same fixed-state leading-soft calorimetric reference, not an
all-order hard-loop assertion.
