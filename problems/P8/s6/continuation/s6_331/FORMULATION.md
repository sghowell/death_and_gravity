# Same-prescription soft conversion on the conditioned leading cloud

Keep original kappa=10^800,n=10^200/512+2,g=1/8192,mass1,nu=mu=1.
Fix hard E in[5/4,2] and unit outgoing rest-frame direction u.
Use the exact original recoil and the fixed-ball additional-soft
prescription of S301, including its full-D trace coefficient and phase.
For a positive Borel angular-energy measure sigma of total mass<=1/8,
a_sigma,Delta_sigma are the inherited continuous soft index and finite
conversion. Their basic measure extension was already established by S301.

For sigma=tau+rho positive and t=massrho, this successor proves
|a_sigma-a_tau|<1700t/kappa,
|Delta_sigma-Delta_tau|<11000t(1+ln(1/t))/kappa,
and the regulated state-difference quotient is bounded by
18000t(1+ln(1/t))/kappa for0<epsilon<=1/8.
All differences vanish at t=0. The stronger S325 Born-specific
1400/10000/15000 constants are retained.

Use only the fixed-Born D4 leading probability with intensity
Lambda(dn)*dw/w,0<w<=1, fixed index a=intLambda in[0,1],
including original a<4/(5kappa). This index is not dynamically
replaced by the radiative a_sigma. Condition on total R<=x<=1/8.
The exact CDF exp(-EulerGamma*a)*x^a/Gamma(1+a) remains unexpanded.
For y=x-R and deltas relative to the SAME Born hard state,
Z_e(sigma;y)=[deltaa_e(sigma)*y^(2e)-deltaa(sigma)]/(2e)
converges in the signed measure sense of total variation to

The limiting TV is<23000a*x(1+ln(1/x))/kappa and hence
<19000x(1+ln(1/x))/kappa^2 at original parameters.

Let sigma_eta retain emissions w>=eta,0<eta<=x.
The means of a_sigma_eta conditioned byR_eta<=x and of a_sigma
conditioned byR<=x differ by<=4500a*eta/kappa.

34000eta*(1+ln(1/eta))/kappa^2.

No claim is made that the noninteger-D current is a positive cloud
intensity, that the conditioned emissions remain independent, or that
this prescription supplies dimensionally continued outer hard amplitudes.
The Z regulator limit holds at each fixed x; a quantitative cutoff rate
after changing its remaining-energy logarithm is not established here.
