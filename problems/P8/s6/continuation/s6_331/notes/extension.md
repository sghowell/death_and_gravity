# Inherited measure extension and strengthened L2 control

S301 already established continuous positive angular-energy-measure
extension by almost-everywhere angular convergence and the common
radial Holder majorant. The following argument retains that theorem
and records uniform-radial L2 convergence needed for the quantitative
state comparison; it does not claim a first Borel extension.

## Compact kernel argument

For a null angular ray v, the fixed-ball projected rank-one kernel
has nuclear norm<=2 per unit energy. Choosing zero on its collinear
diagonal gives a bounded Borel kernel. That diagonal is null for
ordinary observing-sphere area times any finite source measure:
integrate its zero observing area for each fixed v. Thus the angular
integrals are independent of the chosen diagonal representative.

The map from(radial,v) into L2 of the observing sphere is continuous
by bounded dominated convergence, including the radial boundary.
It is uniformly continuous on the compact parameter domain.
Finite continuous partitions of unity in v approximate this
Banach-valued kernel uniformly; weak convergence of positive energy
measures consequently gives L2 convergence of currents uniformly
in the radial parameter. The massive currents converge uniformly
through the original continuous recoil and uniform Doppler gap.

The common nuclear bound gives uniform radial convergence of the
angular averages H and U. The inherited quarter-Hoelder radial
difference provides an integrable bound after division by1-s,
so the finite K1 and Delta integrals converge as well.
The S301 regulated expansion and its uniform remainder pass to
these limits. In particular a_sigma,Delta_sigma and the regulated
divided-difference are well-defined continuous functionals of every
positive Borel angular-energy measure of total mass<=1/8.
The interior noninteger-D contraction need not be positive; it
is a regulated mark, not the cloud's probability intensity.

Approximate tau and rho separately by positive atomic measures to
pass the positive-increment bounds to arbitrary positive additions.
This preserves the energy path and does not claim arbitrary signed
perturbation estimates.
