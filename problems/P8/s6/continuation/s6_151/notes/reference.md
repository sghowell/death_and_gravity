# Local heavy-tree variation and ownership

The local conversion is +L^2 F sum_channels C(P).
It is represented by the fixed second-order coordinate shift

    delta L=+3L^3 F, delta g=+L^2 g F, delta M=0,
    delta G=+L^2 G F/2.

These signs are opposite to the old recursive reference
counterterms because the new amplitude restores the finite
part removed by the old subtraction. The map follows from
the literal tree amplitude -L+g sum 1/(M-z); it is not fitted
to b2. At fixed M its exact relative tree b2 shift is L^2 F.
The quartic constant's b2 vanishes.

This is a LINEAR new-order cubic reference. The already-owned
first-order G1^2 term is not included a second time. A second
fixed potential contact is momentum independent and cannot
tune the result. The alternative coordinate representation
and the direct local amplitude term are the SAME contribution,
not two items to be added.

The family is already in canonical fields. There is no extra
external LSZ multiplication. Other first- and second-order
field/parameter-map directions and their mixed terms have not
been eliminated by this graph-family identity.
