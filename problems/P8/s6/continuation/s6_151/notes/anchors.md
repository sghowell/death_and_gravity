# Regulated recursive and proper-MS references are different

Let ell=log(mu^2), since the light squared mass is one.
Expanding the complete Gamma/scale factors only after the
endpoint decomposition yields

    Q^2 J_bare(0)=1/(2e^2)+(ell+1/2)/e
                  +ell^2+ell+j+pi^2/6+O(e).

The zero-momentum one-loop bubble is

    Q I0=1/e+ell+e[ell^2/2+pi^2/12]+O(e^2).

The old recursive wineglass reference is J0=J_bare(0)-I0^2:

    Q^2 J0=-1/(2e^2)+(1/2-ell)/e+j+ell-ell^2+O(e).

For proper MS subtractions the inner counterterm is instead
I_MS=1/(Qe), so J_MS,proper(0)=J_bare(0)-I_MS I0:

    Q^2 J_MS,proper(0)=-1/(2e^2)+1/(2e)
                        +j+ell+ell^2/2+pi^2/12+O(e).

The remaining overall MS operation removes only its displayed
poles. Its finite anchor F is

    F=[j+ell+ell^2/2+pi^2/12]/Q^2.

The scale independence of the proper-MS pole part is an
independent consistency check. The difference between the
finite proper-MS and old recursive anchors is
[3ell^2/2+pi^2/12]/Q^2. Replacing either by the other, or
truncating I0 before multiplying by its pole, loses finite
terms. All displayed products share the same dimensional
regulator and MS measure convention.
