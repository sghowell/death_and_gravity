# Massive analytic factor, its positive radiation origin and its limitation

For future-directed equal-mass momenta p,q, write c=p.q>=m^2.
Feynman parametrization and a direct polar integral give

    integral dOmega/[(p.n)(q.n)]
      =4pi integral_0^1 da/[m^2+2(c-m^2)a(1-a)]
      =2pi log((1+beta)/(1-beta))/(c beta),

where beta=sqrt(1-m^4/c^2); the diagonal limit is4pi/m^2.
The graviton stress projector contributes c^2-m^4/2.
Multiplying this factor gives precisely the pair dependence in the
massive gravitational soft exponential. The soft radiation current
J_mn=sum eta_i p_i,m p_i,n/(p_i.n) is conserved by momentum conservation.
In a frame with n=(1,0,0,1), its norm is
(Jxx-Jyy)^2/2+2Jxy^2>=0. The arbitrary longitudinal components cancel.
This independently fixes the real attenuation sign.

The soft real-emission measure is
d^3k/[2|k|(2pi)^3], and virtual amplitude attenuation is minus one-half
of the real probability. The radial integral in4+2epsilon dimensions
is E^(2epsilon)/(2epsilon). In the original metric normalization this
gives -integral dOmega J:P:J/(64pi^3 kappa) times
(E/mu_ren)^(2epsilon)/epsilon for the real logarithm. The analytic
continuation also retains its Coulomb phase.

For the analytic prescription define

    F(z)=((z-2m^2)^2-2m^4)
          atan(sqrt(z/(4m^2-z)))/sqrt(z(4m^2-z)).

The parameter-integral representation in FORMULATION fixes its sheet on
C minus[4m^2,infinity). It removes the apparent branch at zero. Its
kernel coefficients are (j!)^2/[4(m^2)^(j+1)(2j+1)!].
Their direct polynomial integrals are checked through j8; analyticity
follows from the uniform nonvanishing denominator on compact subsets,
not from this finite set of coefficients. F(0)=m^2/2,
F'(0)=-11/12 and F''(0)/2=1/(10m^2).

There are four ordered off-diagonal pairs in EACH of s,t,u and four
diagonal terms. Together these give B=2(F(s)+F(t)+F(u))-m^2 and
log W_E=G B (E/mu_ren)^(2epsilon)/(pi epsilon).
It is crossing symmetric and includes the self terms.

The construction follows the analytic prescription of
[Bellazzini et al.,2512.13780v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.13780v2),
sections2--4. Its full-amplitude factorization is an explicit premise;
exact finite-coupling unitarity of stripped amplitudes is not established
by the resolution formula. Their gravitational numerical example uses
massless shift-symmetric external scalars, not the present mass1 field.
We do not import its numerical positivity constants.

At two resolutions, dividing the two soft factors BEFORE epsilon removal
gives log(A_E1/A_E0)=-B log(E1/E0)/(4pi^2 kappa).
The independent scale mu_ren cancels from this ratio. This is a dictionary
conditional on the prescribed amplitude, not a construction of it.
