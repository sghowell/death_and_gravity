# Full angular sewing and the complete crossing logarithm

Put Q=s-4m^2 and use unit initial/final directions a,b, with a.b=z.
For the massless M1 direction n, the two tree factors are
[s-Q(a.n)^2]/(4kappa) and [s-Q(b.n)^2]/(4kappa).
The independent Cartesian sphere moments give

    average (a.n)^2=1/3,
    average (a.n)^2(b.n)^2=(1+2z^2)/15.

The identical intermediate pair supplies1/2! and the optical theorem
supplies another1/2: dPhi2=dOmega/(32pi^2), hence

    Im A=average(A_left A_right*)/(32pi).

No incoming velocity is inserted into this invariant amplitude formula.
The normalized elastic partial waves used elsewhere have an additional
incoming beta; using that beta as the massless FINAL phase space would
be wrong. The complete sewn coefficient is

    [s^2-2sQ/3+Q^2(1+2z^2)/15]/(512pi kappa^2).

A second derivation uses A_pair=d0+d2 P2, with
d0=(s-Q/3)/(4kappa), d2=-Q/(6kappa). Spherical convolution gives
[d0^2+d2^2 P2(z)/5]/(32pi). Both positive spin weights and the entire
mass dependence remain. For massive external scattering,
z=1+2t/Q and s+t+u=4m^2, giving P_s/(960pi kappa^2).

The massless internal pair has zero threshold. Analytic continuation of
the polynomial across the unphysical external interval is not an optical
theorem there. On the upper physical s rim, log(-s)=log s-i*pi, so
-c P_s log(-s/nu^2) has precisely the positive absorptive coefficient.

At this order the entire nonanalytic M1 loop is a massless stress bubble
between the two conserved scalar currents. A one-vertex massless tadpole
is scaleless in the stated dimensional prescription and supplies no
cut. No single-M1 channel exists because the M1 action is quadratic and
even in Psi. Higher scalar vertices cannot enter this one-loop M1
nonanalytic topology with four external Phi at this coupling order.
The bubble has only the external channel as a scale. Its two derivatives
per stress give channel^2, canceled by the two graviton propagators;
conservation removes the longitudinal projector denominators. Thus its
nonanalytic part is exactly a polynomial times the massless logarithm.
Remaining subtraction and dimension-dependent finite terms are real local
crossing polynomials, not fixed by the cut. Crossing adds the t and u
bubbles with the same normalization.

At forward transfer P_t=v^2+2m^4, so an explicit log(-t) persists.
This diagram has no virtual soft-graviton line: its one-loop species
coefficient cannot be canceled by the universal one-loop graviton
exponential. Other massless cuts, including pure graviton contributions,
are not included in or excluded by this result.
