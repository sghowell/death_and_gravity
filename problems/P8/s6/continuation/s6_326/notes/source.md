# Source bridge and conventions

Use the frozen S300 exact arbitrary-radiation recoil, S304 scalar/EH
normalization, and the convention audit in logarithmic-soft-loop-convention-audit.md.
The primary theorem is the FIRST line of Krishna--Sahoo (2023), S1_loop_gr,
and the derived eq:oneloop-soft_gr-thm in section 4.3:
https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.16807v2 .
Its section 3 explicitly permits finite-energy massless particles, including
hard gravitons, using the tree-level gauge-fixed EFT action rather than a
nonlocal massless 1PI action. Only the proved ln(omega) term is imported;
the displayed omega*ln(omega) and two-loop terms are not promoted from
their stated conjectural status. Sahoo--Sen's signed massive formulas,
eqgrsoft and e1.17int, are at https://arxiv.org/abs/1808.03288v3 .

The coefficient acts on the tree amplitude and is spin diagonal. A finite
collection of previously emitted gravitons can be the fixed "hard" legs
for ONE additional soft limit. No uniform theorem for the omitted
remainder in simultaneous soft limits follows.

Use all-outgoing k_i, eta=(+---), q=(1,n), |n|=1, spatial TT A with
Frobenius norm one (complex allowed). Four massive legs have k_i^2=1;
the finite set of outgoing null legs is k_j=w_j(1,n_j), w_j>0,
R=sum w_j<=1/8. Massive energies E in[5/4,2] and rest-frame direction u
are held fixed when comparing to Born. Set
D_i=k_i.q, P_ij=A(k_i,k_j), B_ij=P_ii*D_j/D_i-P_ij,
S_ij=B_ij+B_ji, Sbar=sum P_ii/D_i.
Initially exclude null D_j=0; uniform bounds below hold on approaches.
Do not assign a unique TT value to exactly soft-collinear atoms.
