# Mixed-null and null-null contractions

For massive legs |pvec|<=sqrt3 and1/4<|D|<4. A unit-Frobenius complex
spatial TT tensor has operator norm<=1. For a null leg w(1,nj), let
d=1-nj.n and t=P_perp(n)nj, so |t|^2=d(2-d).

In a massive/null S_ij, the four contributions have bounds24w,2w,8w,2w.
Their sum is below36w, uniformly as d tends to zero. For a null/null
pair set v=sqrt(dj/di)*ti-sqrt(di/dj)*tj.
The Gram identities ti.tj=di+dj-di*dj-delta and |ti|^2=di(2-di)
give |v|^2=2delta exactly. Thus |Sij|<=2wiwj delta and the coefficient
delta*(ln(2delta)+1) extends by zero at relative collinearity.

For0<delta<=1/2 the absolute logarithmic product is below1 using
sup_(0<y<=1)y|ln y|=1/e. For1/2<=delta<=2 it is below
2*(ln4+1)<5. This yields the null/null bound without an angular cut.
Similarly sup_(0<d<=2)|d ln d|<3/2.

The finite coefficient contains only direction logarithms, not ln wi.
Splitting an atom into positive collinear pieces leaves all mixed
terms, phases and pairs with other atoms unchanged by linearity.
Split self-pairs vanish. For every finite splitting, no count factor
or artificial energy-entropy sum appears.

Do not infer a unique point at d=0. With n along y and
A=diag(0,1,0,-1)/sqrt2, approaching along the x-y plane gives
A(nj,nj)/(1-nj.n)->sqrt2; the z-y plane gives -sqrt2.
The uniform bounds on approaches are valid despite these different limits.
