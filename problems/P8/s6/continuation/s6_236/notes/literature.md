# Primary-source context and what is derived here

[Henning, Lu and Murayama, One-loop Matching and Running with Covariant Derivative Expansion](https://arxiv.org/abs/1604.01019) provides the functional context for retaining mixed heavy/light effects in a one-light-particle-irreducible calculation. We use the entire nonlocal quartic Hessian and loop representation already derived in S234/S235. A field-independent heavy Gaussian determinant does not remove the mixed diagrams.

[Denner, Techniques for the calculation of electroweak radiative corrections at the one-loop level and results for W-physics at LEP200](https://arxiv.org/abs/0709.1075) gives the conventional scalar-integral notation and Feynman prescription used in the predecessor. The present Cbar=-C0 convention and ordered box signs remain unchanged. The closed quadratic-parameter primitive, analytic coefficient disk, integrated logarithmic majorant and all numerical constants in this checkpoint are independently derived here.

[de Rham, Melville, Tolley and Zhou, Massive Galileon Positivity Bounds](https://arxiv.org/abs/1702.08577) supplies context for distinguishing matching, loop errors and higher operators from an exact UV conclusion. The present real-window first-loop estimate neither proves a physical dispersion relation nor supplies its higher coefficient derivatives or gravitational remainder.

No external theorem is invoked to turn a finite number of samples into a uniform bound. The needed geometric-series and Cauchy estimates, physical branch choices and parameter domains are stated in full in the proof notes. No reference certifies our exact S matrix, omitted loops, common-parent bounce or original P8 closure.
