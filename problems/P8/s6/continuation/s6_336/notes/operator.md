# Covariant contact, complete label sum and physical Ward identity

Use the established metric convention g=eta+2h/sqrt(kappa), signature
+---, and all-outgoing scalar p_i with sum p_i+k=0. Write

v_ij=(k.p_i)p_j-(k.p_j)p_i,
T=sum_(i<j) v_ij v_ij^T.

For the curvature convention implemented by the literal two derivatives,
sqrt(kappa) R^(1)_abcd =
k_b k_d epsilon_ac+k_a k_c epsilon_bd
-k_b k_c epsilon_ad-k_a k_d epsilon_bc.
Consequently its contraction against p_i^a p_j^b p_i^c p_j^d is
epsilon(v_ij,v_ij). This identity is proved before mass shells, nullness
or TT conditions. All 256 components vanish for a pure-gauge metric
perturbation. On a physical null TT wave its Ricci tensor and scalar
vanish, so its Weyl tensor equals this Riemann tensor.

In Phi^2*C_abcd*(nabla^a nabla^c Phi)*(nabla^b nabla^d Phi),
all 24 assignments of the four labelled scalars give four copies of
each of the six unordered pairs. The action coefficient1/4 cancels
this multiplicity. The four Fourier derivatives on the scalars have
sign i^4=1. Extra variations of inverse metrics, determinant or scalar
connections multiply the already-linear curvature and enter only at
two gravitons. The complete one-graviton contact is thus

Delta M5^(1)=chi*T:epsilon/sqrt(kappa).

Since k.v_ij=0 identically, the tensor is conserved. It is polynomial,
local and Bose symmetric. At fixed hard momenta it is homogeneous
degree two in k; bounded physical recoil momenta imply O(omega^2)
along the actual recoil path. The contact changes none of the
omega^-1, omega^0 or omega^1 soft coefficients.

Three original rational recoil configurations check all shells,
conservation, the complete label sum, all Ricci components and both
physical polarizations. Each has strictly positive contact TT norm
and nonzero interference with the full original 47-graph tree.
The signs are -,+,+. These are unit matching-direction derivatives,
not three choices of a physical coefficient. On-shell nonzero
amplitudes exclude a pure total derivative or equation-of-motion
redundancy for this direction. By continuity the differential effect
persists on an open physical neighborhood; no all-angle integral
noncancellation is inferred from these three samples.
