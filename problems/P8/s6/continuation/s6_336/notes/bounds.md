# Uniform contact bound and conditional rate estimates

Every scalar on the original compact recoil domain has absolute energy
at most2 and spatial norm at most2. Thus |k.p_i|<=4omega and
|v_ij,spatial|<=16omega. Transverse projection cannot increase a norm.
The sum of six dyads satisfies the full canonical Frobenius bound

||T||<=1536omega^2.

The D4 TT polarization sew is at most the squared Frobenius norm.
This proof is uniform in physical emission directions, not inferred
from the finite calibration states.

Fix nonforward Born data, delta=min(1,-t,-u)>0, original full A0>0,
and 0<=x<=delta/192. S335's complete tree estimate at D4 is

||Mtree||/A0 <= (64/omega+B/delta)/sqrt(kappa), B=4e9.

Keep both polarizations, all complex interference, actual J<=1 and
the original angular-normalized radial weight omega/(4pi^2). With
C=1536, Cauchy-Schwarz and exact radial integration give the absolute
first-order Born-normalized interference bound

|chi|*[128*C*x^3/3+C*B*x^4/(2delta)]/(4pi^2*kappa*A0).

The square of this contact ALONE is bounded, for all x<=1/8, by

chi^2*C^2*x^6/(24pi^2*kappa*A0^2).

The square is not a complete second-order quantum rate: other
two-loop interference and matching terms have not been calculated.
For the first-order bound x must stay in the low recoil window;
the API rejects use beyond it. Zero-resolution values are continuous
bound extensions, not definitions of a zero-energy external state.

Both expressions are conditional on an explicitly supplied comparison
coefficient. Their tails vanish as at least x^3 and x^6, respectively.
Infrared finiteness therefore cannot set the physical coefficient.
No universal rate bound independent of unknown matching is inferred.
