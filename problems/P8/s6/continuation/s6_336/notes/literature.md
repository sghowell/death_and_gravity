# Prior result and limits of its use

[Laddha and Sen, arXiv:1706.00759v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.00759),
section2.3 point3, explain that independent linearized-Riemann
contractions with finite external momenta prevent extension of their
soft factorization argument beyond sub-subleading order.

The explicit Bose-symmetric original-source contact here is a concrete
instance of that known obstruction; the general obstruction is not
claimed as new. The paper's general loop theorem is restricted to
D>=5 because of infrared issues. No such theorem is imported into a
four-dimensional loop calculation here. The contact derivation and
formal quantum-order comparison are direct local algebra independent
of that theorem.

The exact physical normalization, recoil states, original tree and
retained matching conventions come from the frozen repository inputs,
not from a different massless or high-energy model.
