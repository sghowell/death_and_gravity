# Quantum-order counting and the missing physical datum

The comparison has coefficient hbar*chi, with chi real and of mass
dimension -6 in D4. Its curvature vanishes at exactly flat metric,
so every no-external-metric vertex vanishes. Its first flat-vacuum
vertex has four scalars and one graviton, and there is no lower-field
vertex. Phi parity, locality and reality are retained.

To enter a no-external-graviton four-scalar amplitude, a metric leg
must be contracted. Joining the five-leg contact to minimal hPhiPhi
gives two internal edges and two vertices, hence one topological
loop. The two-graviton contact's self-contraction also has one loop.
More generally every remaining interaction has degree at least three,
so a tree with this degree-five-or-higher contact cannot have only four
external scalars. Quadratic insertions do not change that inequality;
the fixed flat vacuum has no onepoint insertion. Thus an additional
loop is required. With the explicit hbar, the first possible change
to the flat four-scalar amplitude is of order hbar^2.

In contrast, its five-point contact already contributes at order hbar.
The retained one-loop flat matching, discontinuity and lower-point
conditions are identical along this comparison direction, yet its
physical radiative amplitude differs. The known soft coefficients
also stay fixed because the contact begins at omega^2.

This proves insufficiency of THOSE retained data. It does not assert
that a specified microscopic quantum parent has a freely adjustable
physical coefficient, or that equality persists at higher loop order.
The family is a formal local EFT comparison, not a proven UV-complete
family. No sign or magnitude bound on chi is supplied by these data.

A finite minimal representative can still be calculated, but it must
be separated from physical matching. The constructive next requirement
is the complete covariant radiative one-loop functional, its curved
counterfunctional and matching from the quantum parent. Choosing chi=0
or assigning a naturalness scale would add an unsupported physical
condition. This is a research obligation, not a user-choice blocker.
