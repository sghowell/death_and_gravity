# Ten products and fixed six-invariant continuation

The old four TT products,TL,LT,LL are augmented by LC,CL,CC. Let k be unit, ell=-k+y e, B_D=D-tr(D)I/2 and B_G=G-tr(G)I/2. The new LL cross geometries are

    LC=tr(G) k^t B_D ell,
    CL=tr(D) k^t B_G ell,
    CC=tr(D)tr(G) ell^2.

The ordinary LL geometry is(k^t B_D ell)(k^t B_G ell)/ell^2. The other sectors use the complete transverse projectors and magnetic matrices. The LC/CL order is physical; exchanging their names without exchanging the amplitude factors is incorrect.

Use the S212 active block(X,Y,Z,u), with Z^2 the grouped transverse radius, and d-4 passive diagonal components in each matrix trace. Exact normalized sphere moments analytically continue the full two-form contractions; d3 removes the auxiliary fourth component. Independent integer-dimensional field-strength contractions check all ten expressions in every scalar channel at d3,4,5,6.

The basis is

    T=tr(DG), V=e^t DG e, W=(eDe)(eGe),
    S=tr(D)tr(G), UD=tr(D)eGe, UG=tr(G)eDe.

Use the three old normalized tracefree channels plus unnormalized I_d/I_d,I_d/S_d,S_d/I_d, S_d=diag(-1,...,-1,d-1). Their six by six coefficient matrix has determinant d^3(d-1)^3/2. This is an exact analytic reconstruction, not a fit to integer dimensions.

Reconstruct invariant coefficients before differentiating d. Then contract with fixed physical rows(3,1,1,9,3,3),(0,2,2,0,6,0),(0,2,2,0,0,6). Differentiating the dimension-varying test matrices directly would also differentiate their traces, normalization and contractions and yield a different quantity.
