# Full trace Proca pairs and the constraint

For general spatial d let h=a^2 exp(Q), v=a^d exp(tr(Q)/2) and B_Q=Q-tr(Q)I/2. Differentiating the S214 constrained Hamiltonian gives a kinetic first variation a^(2-d) B_Q and the trace variation of(div Pi)^2/(2m^2v). With creation momentum routing q=-ell the latter contributes +tr(Q) k ell^t/(2a^d m^2) to the kinetic matrix.

The current has the negative Hamiltonian-variation sign. Its mass form is +m^2 a^(d-2) B_Q. The magnetic form is derived from the full two-form:

    Mfull(Q)=Mold(Q)+tr(Q)[(k.ell)I-ell k^t]/2.

At d3 this equals Ck^t B_Q Cell for arbitrary trace. Using a three-dimensional magnetic dual as a general-dimensional polarization formula would miss passive-dimension contributions.

Set x=1/r, khat=k/r, ellhat=ell/r, Obar=x omega, Wbar=x a W, and Pbar=-iWbar minus the normalized amplitude derivative. With norm=1/(2sqrt(Wbar_k Wbar_l)), the old A,B,TL,LT,LL scalar amplitudes contract B_Q instead of Q. The LL pair has the additional coefficient

    C=-norm Pbar_k Pbar_l/(2 Obar_k Obar_l),
    R_LL=(r/a)[LL (e_k^t B_Q e_l)
               +C tr(Q)(e_k.khat)(ellhat.e_l)].

It is the differentiated temporal constraint, not an optional new polarization. Both LC and CL and their CC square must be retained. The exact general-dimensional powers of a from the vertex and canonical modes cancel to r/a; the actual d-dependent WKB and dilution rates do not disappear.
