# Actual MS projections of overlapping subgraphs

Let P=1/(Q epsilon) be the inner MS pole and
I=P+f0+epsilon f1+... the entire regulated light bubble.
The finite coefficient is f0=I_R+ell/Q. Write K for projection
onto negative powers of epsilon. In particular,

    K(I^2)=P^2+2P f0,
    K(P I)=P^2+P f0,
    K(I^2-2P I)=-P^2.

The last identity is not permission to ignore the distinct
finite-bubble terms in the first two projections.

For an overlapping/shared-vertex pair the actual six forests are

    I^2-P I-P I-K(I^2)+K(P I)+K(P I).

Their sum is (I-P)^2. The last two terms are the allowed
nested small/whole forests. The pair of the two small shared
cores is forbidden and is checked absent in each actual
restricted forest list. The simple poles containing f0 cancel
only after these terms are combined.

A disjoint pair has the four true forests
I^2-P I-P I+P^2=(I-P)^2. A single divergent bubble paired
with a finite heavy triangle has I T-P T=(I-P)T, retaining
the whole dimensional T in both terms. No evanescent factor
is discarded in an unpaired pole product.

The code enumerates all 24 actual parent refinements and their
restricted forests. Every type is checked independently.
Once the poles cancel, the limit is f0^2 or f0 T_0; no
epsilon-times-pole term is left to assign. This conclusion
follows from the full Laurent identity, not an early d=4 rule.
