# Full family factorization and finite scale shift

Write C=-L+g/(M-z), and let T_A,T_B be the sums of BOTH
full heavy exchange terms on the two sides of the central
vertex. S6.123's complete selected channel is

    [C^3 I_R^2+C^2 I_R(T_A+T_B)]/4.

It follows by expanding
C[(C I_R+T_A)(C I_R+T_B)-T_A T_B]/4.
The eight finite separable triangle-triangle refinements, and
the 32 other finite double bubbles, are already in S6.121;
they are not counted again here.

After the complete MS forests, replace I_R by I_R+d with
d=ell/Q, ell=log(mu^2)=400log10. The exact MS-minus-old
difference per channel is

    [2d C^3 I_R+d C^2(T_A+T_B)]/4 + d^2 C^3/4.

Both powers of d matter. All three channels retain the full
external heavy poles and complete heavy triangles. At this
formal order, shifting the coordinates of an already order-two
finite graph would first change it at order three. The
remaining global lower-order parameter/field re-expansion
is still separate from this assigned interaction-family result.
