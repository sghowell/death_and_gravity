# Scope of the MS interaction-family conversion

The local pole subtractions are applied to their full-model
Phi4, H Phi2 and H2 cores before neighboring heavy propagators
are attached. The selected reduced factors therefore preserve
their ordinary local full-model counterterm interpretation.

The shared-vertex overall counterterm is +P^2 after the actual
nested sum. The disjoint product is also +P^2 but has a
different forest origin. They may have the same algebraic
value without being interchangeable graph owners. In
particular no forbidden shared pair is inserted into a forest.

Replacing the full old zero reference by the MS pole inside
all of these actual forests gives the finite factorization
in the factors note. Its quadratic scale term contains C^3,
including inverse heavy propagators up to the third power.
Those higher inverse powers are produced by repeated ordinary
heavy counterterm insertions, not by a new nonlocal vertex.

The direct finite scale difference already includes the
assigned change of references and their products. Do not add
an equivalent coupling shift again. No finite local reference
is fitted to the forward coefficient, and no extra LSZ factor
is applied to this canonical graph family. Other graph
families and the global field/coupling map remain open.
