# Repeated heavy references and the quadratic scale term

The factor C^3 includes g^3/(M-z)^3. An ordinary heavy
propagator with a one-loop mass reference already gives

    g/(M+h deltaM-z)
      =g/(M-z)-h g deltaM/(M-z)^2
         +h^2 g deltaM^2/(M-z)^3+O(h^3).

The third inverse power therefore has a standard disjoint
counterterm owner. It is not a reason to add a new nonlocal
interaction or to erase the quadratic scale term.

The full C^3 forward coefficient is checked by differentiating
both s and u channels, not by using only one heavy pole.
Keeping the t-channel constant makes explicit why its
derivative vanishes. Its signed relative factor is the exact
expression in the bounds note; the magnitude enclosure is
used only after that identity.

The finite scale-shift amplitude and any equivalent bare
parameter reorganization are the same assigned terms. They
must not be added twice. The fundamental-G convention still
keeps G1^2 separate from the linear 2G G2 reference. The
global map and its remaining cross terms are not completed
by this graph-family calculation.
