# Scope, historical corrections and remaining original problem

This continuation proves an explicit finite-dimensional Weyl operator
comparison for the SAME S6.270 full local source and its two named smooth
regulators. It retains 48 canonical pairs, all 96 phase coordinates,
the actual pure covariance, nonzero prepared modes, nonlinear spatial
reconstruction, matter and vector modes, implicit auxiliary fields, time
and primitive contacts, scalar phases and the original residual charges.

The volume is the full normalized physical-volume symbol, not its Hessian
or a redefined background. Its finite Weyl operator is positive because it
is close in operator norm to the already positive near-identity calibrated
coherent volume. Arbitrary positive Weyl symbols need not give positive
operators, and the tests retain explicit counterexamples.

The complete Dyson propagators are compared, not just finite-order
perturbation theory. Nevertheless they are propagators of the declared
finite smooth regulators. They are not an identified original interacting
state or a construction of the unregularized singular Hamiltonian.
