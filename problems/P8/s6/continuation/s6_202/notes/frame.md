# An analytic full physical frame

For each fixed real k choose any real orthonormal triad(e1,e2,n0), n0=-k/|k|. Since nu<=2|k| on |k|>=m, write b=2delta. Then ||p||/|k|<=b and

|l.l/|k|^2-1|<=eta=2b+b^2<1e-5.

Choose rho=sqrt(l.l) with rho(0)=|k|. Its positive branch has |rho|>.99|k| and |rho/|k|-1|<=eta. The latter follows by factoring the difference of squares; the denominator has modulus at least1 because the square root has positive real part.

Set n=l/rho. Its displacement from n0 is bounded by(b+eta)/.99<1e-5. Define

K=n n0^T-n0 n^T,  R=I+K+K^2/(1+n.n0).

The denominator has modulus greater than1.99. On n.n=1, exact symbolic identities give R^T R=I, det R=1 and Rn0=n. The two transverse vectors are Re1,Re2; the longitudinal vector is n. This is a local analytic frame for each fixed real k, not a global smooth frame over a sphere.

Complex bilinear orthogonality does NOT imply unit Euclidean norm. Instead ||K||op<=2e-5 and ||R-I||op<=2e-5+4e-10/(2-1e-5)<1e-4. Thus all three vectors have genuine complex Euclidean norm below2. The independent fixture n=(i sinh t,0,cosh t) explicitly rejects the incorrect inference from n.n=1 to ||n||=1.

The two transverse frequencies are degenerate. Summing both transverse modes and the longitudinal mode removes dependence on the initial real transverse basis. No polarization is omitted.
