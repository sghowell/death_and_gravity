# Pole-plus-cut representation and total moments

Let B=1/A2. The first-sheet argument gives exactly one simple pole p=-r m^2 with residue R>0. Its cut has Im B(-tau+i0)=-pi rho(tau). At large first-sheet p, the closed expression gives A2=(13/60)log(p/m^2)+O(1) uniformly on the large slit circle. Thus B=O(1/log|p|); it has no instantaneous constant at infinity.

Apply the Cauchy formula to B(z)/(z-p), with the cut removed and a small circle around the real pole. The outer circle contribution is O(1/log Rcircle) and tends to zero. The threshold arc tends to zero because A2 has the nonzero continuous threshold value-172/225. The small pole circle supplies its residue. The two banks combine to the positive cut measure. Since rho=O(log(tau)^-2), the resulting integral converges absolutely for every fixed p off the cut and pole. This proves

1/A2(p)=R/(p+r m^2)+integral_(4m^2)^infinity rho(tau)/(p+tau)dtau,
1/F2(p)=-R/(p+r m^2)-integral rho(tau)/(p+tau)dtau.

No cut-only formula is possible: the pole coefficient is nonzero. The physical residue sign of this isolated F2 reciprocal is negative; positivity here refers to R and to the Stieltjes measure of1/A2, not spectral positivity of the full gravity propagator.

Set p0 and differentiate there. Dominated convergence follows from the threshold square-root bound, the logarithmic tail and tau>=4m^2. From A2(0)=1/30 and m^2 A2'(0)=3/56,

R/(r m^2)+integral rho(tau)/tau dtau=30,
R/(r^2 m^4)+integral rho(tau)/tau^2 dtau=675/(14m^2).

These are TOTAL moments. The continuum static moment alone is strictly positive, but less than30. Even the coarse exact root and weight bounds give R/(r m^2)<17/rlo<30. Numerical illustrations of the continuum moments are about2.09368 and0.0834374/m^2; they are not the proof of either exact identity.

Independent65-digit radial quadrature agrees with the closed formula at four complex/real p values. Four further pole-plus-cut reconstructions agree within1e-50, including p0 and a negative p strictly above the pole. Dropping the pole, or reversing its relative sign in1/A2, produces an error greater than one in those fixtures. These comparisons diagnose normalization or branch errors; only the analytic sign/contour argument establishes completeness of the representation.
