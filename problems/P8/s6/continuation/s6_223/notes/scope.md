# What this isolated inverse does and does not settle

The S222 full scalar force system remains

Z'=KZ+Fg, s=CZ+Dg,
[I-Qbar(D+C G0F)]g=e+Qbar C G0h.

Its direct auxiliary block, output density order, both ADM and clock contacts and original quantum preparation are unchanged. The available weak norms do not yet supply a source-domain self-map or a coupled quantum inverse. An isolated shear inverse does not remove that gap.

The S86 homogeneous trace factor remains a separate zero-free reference input. S223 independently derives the shear weight and finite coefficient. The two have different finite threshold signs, so their zero sets and half-line kernel properties differ. Importing the old trace first-sheet gap or its totalL1 property would be incorrect.

The zero p=-r m^2 is a zero of a specified flat reference fourth-order factor. The original curved reference response, tree terms, lower derivatives, scalar/vector constraints, state corrections and interactions are not all contained in it. The term 'subthreshold pole' always refers to its reciprocal, not a proved physical pole, ghost, instability or mode of the full gravity theory. No pole deletion, order reduction, final-state selection, physical cutoff or finite-counterterm retuning is performed.

The next use of this result requires deriving a complete matrix normal form from the already matched response, retaining its nonlocal auxiliary equations, and proving compatible-space bounds on the remainder. A finite-window isolated inverse can be one ingredient in that task; it is not the task's conclusion. A complete coupled inverse would still not by itself prove a small finite-coupling/nonlinear sourced-parent remainder or a controlled stable bounce.

Scoped P8(a), A.20-A.23, all nine original primitive statuses and all prior matching rows remain unchanged. Canonical full-parent UV matching/cuts, finite-gravity IR/Regge and a common controlled parent/domain/state/map/heavy/cutoff/background package remain open. Failure of one estimate or of a trace-based ansatz is not a primitive exclusion. Original P8 remains OPEN, with no user-intervention blocker inferred from the new isolated spectral structure.

All analytic arguments are written proofs, not proof-assistant formalization. No external theorem about a scalar toy model is used to assert a full Proca/gravity matrix result.
