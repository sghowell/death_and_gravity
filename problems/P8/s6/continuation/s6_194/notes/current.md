# Original-prescription current and its full remainder

S192 bounds the convergent actual-mode current minus its complete
fourth-order adiabatic current, including the finite complement and
the entire infinite momentum tail. Its first three amplitude
derivative displays are (1e77,1e94,1e111). That checkpoint alone
did not equate this comparison to the fixed physical subtraction.

S193 supplies precisely that homogeneous fixed-prescription
identity, with the same physical three-mode state and all finite
local contacts. The original current is the convergent comparison
plus the variation of the unchanged finite local action.
The present local estimate is C2 on the same admitted family.

Adding1e86 for each local derivative gives strict complete-current
displays (1e87,1e95,2e111). Dominated amplitude differentiation of
the S192 comparison and ordinary differentiation of the finite
local expression yield C2 dependence into C0(I). This also applies
to the proper density: divide by a^3>=1, which is independent of
epsilon.

Taylor's integral identity, valid for either sign of epsilon, is

    J(epsilon)-J(0)-epsilon J'(0)
      =epsilon^2 integral_0^1 (1-x) J''(x epsilon) dx.

Its norm is at most epsilon^2*1e111. It is zero when epsilon=0;
strictness of the uniform derivative bound gives strict inequality
for nonzero epsilon. No absolute-value error at the zero endpoint
is hidden. The complete current divided by the actual kappa has
derivative displays (1e-713,1e-705,2e-689).

The momentum partition from S192 remains a proof partition, not
a physical cutoff. The prescription, state and local coefficients
have not been adjusted to make the current small.
