# Complete scalar and fermionic vacuum forest ownership

regulated source/reducible cancellation. The two pure-scalar
raw vacuum graphs have weights

    V2,raw=L T_D(1)^2/8-g S_D(1,1,M)/4.

The two cubic vertices have expansion factor -1/2 and
vertices G/2: two cross-pairings give -g/4 times the

The scalar one-loop Euclidean inverse correction, after
the stationary-source cancellation, is

    Pi1(pE^2)=L T_D(1)/2-g B_HL,D(pE^2).

At the physical reference pE^2=-1, let
r_D=-g partial_(pE^2) B_HL,D(-1). The fixed OS counterterms
are deltaZ=-r_D and
deltaMass=-L T_D(1)/2+g B_HL,D(-1)-r_D.
Their determinant insertion is

    (1/2) Tr D[deltaZ pE^2+deltaMass]
       =-L T_D(1)^2/4+g B_HL,D(-1)T_D(1)/2.

The correlated r_D(pE^2+1) term closes into a constant
trace. It vanishes by the dimensional scaleless identity,
not by dropping a finite kinetic coefficient in isolation.

mass counterterm is deltaM=g/(2Q epsilon), giving the
heavy determinant insertion g T_D(M)/(4Q epsilon).
No independent heavy kinetic or OS condition is introduced.
Thus the full scalar reference before its overall
vacuum subtraction is

    -L T_D(1)^2/8-g S_D(1,1,M)/4
      +g B_HL,D(-1)T_D(1)/2+g T_D(M)/(4Q epsilon).
