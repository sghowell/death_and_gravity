# Literal homogeneous tracefree pair and the original shear logarithm

The zero-transfer object is the actual homogeneous per-comoving-volume current defined by the constrained modes, not point evaluation of an arbitrary weak spatial multiplier. The original state, rotational regulator and local prescription are O(3)-invariant. Consequently the five tracefree symmetric components carry the same time operator and have no linear mixing with trace, lapse, clock or scalar-matter components at zero transfer. This statement does not extend to the scalar representation at nonzero transfer.

Use proper time and a unit-Frobenius tracefree metric direction D in the original exponential chart. At high comoving k, the S216 full two-form vertex and constrained physical polarization amplitudes give, in analytic spatial dimensiond,

C(d)=(2d^2+d-8)/(2d(d+2)).

The code derives this separately from the tensor, vector and scalar tracefree contraction rows, with their full passive-dimension terms. It does not use a three-dimensional magnetic dual as a dimensionally continued vertex.

An independent invariant calculation hasP=I-nn^T. The leading TT readout matrix isPDP+(nDn)P/2. Squaring and summing transverse polarizations gives

tr(D^2)-2nD^2n+(d-1)(nDn)^2/4.

The longitudinal square adds(nDn)^2/4; it is not a removable massless polarization. The exact sphere moments are
average nD^2n=tr(D^2)/d and
average(nDn)^2=2tr(D^2)/(d(d+2))
for tracefreeD. They give the sameC(d). Atd3, TT contributes2/5 and LL1/30, soC(3)=13/30. The first dimension jet is91/450 and is retained. The mixed TL/LT vertices contain one mass factor and have lower radial degree.

For the original annihilation readouts, the S217 canonical covariance identity gives+Im(conjugate(b_D)b_G). The leading pair amplitudes are real after removing their phases; the orientation therefore gives positive sin(2k Delta_sigma), with sigma'=1/a. This is not the earlier mismatched negative-phase annihilation template.

With output normalization64pi^2/a(t)^3 and the original three-dimensional radial measure, the leading kernel is

(208/15) k^4 sin(2k Delta_sigma)/[a(t)^4 a(s)].

The factor is32C(3). The Abel-regulated radial integral is the imaginary part of24/(epsilon-2i Delta_sigma)^5 and tends to3/(4 Delta_sigma^5). The off-diagonal leading singularity is therefore

(52/5)/[a(t)^4 a(s) Delta_sigma^5].

For tau=t-s, four derivatives of13/(30tau) give52/(5tau^5). The original S223 factor has high logarithmF2(p)=-(13/60)log(p/m^2)+O(1); its causal time logarithm has the matching coefficient13/30 before four derivatives. Its finite coefficient remains-1/30. The trace channel's coefficient is different and cannot be copied into this shear problem.

## Full-dimensional frozen matching in proper time

At frozen scalea0, the dimensional radial measure times amplitude is
a0^(-d-2) k^(d+1)dk. Settingk=a0p makes it exactlyp^(d+1)dp for every nearby complexd. The equality includes the first dimension jet of both the measure andC(d). Thus the frozen proper-time fourth-order distribution, including its dimensional finite contact, is the physical flat reference in the same prescription.

This is a proper-time statement. A conformal-time reference with a fixed numerical mass has a different frozen spectral argument; no absence of conformal mass scaling or logarithmic scale terms is asserted.

For the varying actual scale, the smooth ratio

tau^5/[a(t)^4 a(s) Delta_sigma^5]

equals1 on the diagonal and has first lag derivative-3H(s)/2. Its difference from1 thus has a factor tau. The next note treats the resulting lower singularities and their fixed extensions without deleting contacts.
