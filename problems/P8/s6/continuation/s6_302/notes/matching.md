# Finite matching and original frontier preservation

The immediate parent's157 matching records are retained in order;
one scoped record is appended. All nine original primitive records,
six historical qualifications and the archived rejected S279 record
remain unchanged. The current source is not replaced by any historical
dictionary that was later rejected.

The known finite amplitude coordinates are fixed by the literal raw
masters, whole-D cut coefficients, Gram choice, physical pole completion,
on-shell scalar legs, a pure local UV pole subtraction, and the analytic
soft reference atresolution/nu=1. This fixes an explicit representative.

Physical matching still requires alpha,beta,delta_kappa. The API takes
all three explicitly and supplies no zero defaults. No positivity sign,
naturalness estimate or arbitrarily selected reference observable supplies
their missing physical values. Full-current-action hard and radiative
terms likewise cannot be inferred from the minimal sector alone.

The source/frontier and malformed-input controls reject promotion of
a known compact finite coefficient to a complete amplitude, a uniform
forward result, UV completion or original V/G/B/P8 closure.
