- A complete explicit known finite one-loop amplitude representative.
- Correct full-D derivative terms in boxes, triangles, bubbles and legs.
- Both finite Gram contributions and the physical-pole finite completion.
- Tagged ultraviolet subtraction before the analytic infrared division.
- Three unassigned independent physical finite matching coordinates.

Not established: values or bounds of the three physical anchors,
the entire current-action or detector-inclusive amplitude, gravity-Born
radiation, higher loops/operators, exact forward or high-energy/Regge
estimates, full quantum unitarity/analyticity, or original state/domain/
measure/common-parent bounce and V/G/B/P8 closure.

The known representative's bound depends on its declared subtraction
coordinates. A finite perturbative coefficient is not a regulator-removed
quantum theory. The physical comparison window is not a UV cutoff.
