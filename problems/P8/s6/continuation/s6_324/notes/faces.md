# State-correct full three-to-two-real faces

Fix generic positive a,b and real hard directions. As c tends to zero,
the leading1/c poles of the complete original tree amplitude arise
from attaching the new graviton to an external leg. Nonzero internal
cuts remain finite at this fixed hard state; contact insertions and
terms with an explicit new soft momentum do not produce this pole.
The finite tree inventory includes all such terms before the limit.
The scalar and Einstein identities in source.md give
 lim_{c->0} c*M3=S_c(sigma_ab)*M2/sqrt(kappa).

The state sigma_ab contains four signed on-shell recoiled massive
momenta AND both remaining hard null momenta q_a,q_b. Thus
 S_c=sum_{p in sigma_ab} p.eps_c.p/(p.n_c).
Momentum conservation makes the full tensor current transverse:
 [sum p*p^T/(p.n_c)]*eta*n_c=sum p=0.
Keeping only the four massive legs instead gives-q_a-q_b; keeping
only one marked null leg still leaves the other negative null momentum.
Neither truncated current is conserved at this radiative state.

The exact recoil map at c=0 is the two-real recoil map, including
rho3->rho2 and the same elastic A0. Therefore
 G3(a,b,0)=S_c(sigma_ab)*G2(a,b)/sqrt(kappa).
The other two faces follow by permutation. Generic fixed-state
factorization identifies the face but is not claimed to give a
uniform bound on the unscaled O(1) soft remainder. The next note
bounds the explicit scaled face uniformly. S323 supplies compatible
intersections of the full scaled amplitude.

For a literal calibration, use E=5/4, alpha0=19/10, b=1/64 and
c=164539/2869950, orthogonal null rays, unit plus/2 tensors and
u=(3/5,0,4/5). Set alpha=alpha0-h,
 Eprime=(alpha+1/alpha)/2, rprime=(alpha-1/alpha)/2,
 a=(Eprime^2-Eprime0^2)/[-E+(b+c)/2].
This rational parameterization obeys the exact original recoil,
all four mass shells, full conservation and positive phase.
At h=1/1000,1/10000,1/100000 the complete original5116 amplitude
has normalized soft ratio a*sqrt(kappa)*M3/[S_a(sigma_bc)*M2]
within3h of one, with strictly decreasing errors. The massive-only
ratio has over ten times the correct error at the smallest h.
These inequalities apply only to the three recorded samples; they
calibrate the residue argument and do not replace the uniform proof.

Exactly coincident angular poles are not assigned bare values.
The angular estimates hold uniformly on the permitted domain;
its bounded a.e. limits suffice for the measure integration.
