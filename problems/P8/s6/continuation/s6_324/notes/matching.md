# Radiative-state changes are not completed virtual matching

S309 controls a singly marked finite one-real residual dressed with
arbitrary leading-soft emissions at that FIXED marked state, and
its associated same-state leading virtual pole. It does not equate
every exact higher-real probability subtraction to that reference.
The state-correct faces proved here retain the radiative state.

Already for two real emissions, write L_a(sigma_b) for the scaled
leading probability factor for emission a at the one-real state
sigma_b, and L_a0 for its Born-state value. The probability baseline is
 L_a(sigma_b)*P1(b)+L_b(sigma_a)*P1(a)-L_a0*L_b0.
With P1(b)=R1(b)+L_b0 and P1(a)=R1(a)+L_a0, this equals the two
singly marked residual terms plus the Born product AND
 [L_a(sigma_b)-L_a0]*L_b0+[L_b(sigma_a)-L_b0]*L_a0.
These are genuine state-change matching terms, not identically zero.
The module verifies the exact algebra independently of their size.

For the logical negative control L_a=1+b,L_b=1+a,L_a0=L_b0=1,
the extra numerator is a+b. Dividing by the radial ab gives1/a+1/b;
its regulated cube integral is2*(x-epsilon)*ln(x/epsilon), divergent
as epsilon tends to zero. This toy does not model the original
virtual amplitude; it proves that a small nonzero coefficient or
one vanishing energy factor cannot alone remove the other soft log.

Completing the physical rate requires compatible same-state real
and virtual contributions, the associated integrated counterterms
and finite hard/evanescent matching, all with the original action,
regulator and state. No unidentified virtual graph is assigned a
value here, and no free finite term is set to zero by definition.
The finite signed three-real theorem is useful input to that work,
not its completion. An all-N theorem and summable estimates, quantum
state/unitarity, absolute complex Regge with the required limit order
and the common-parent bounce remain independent original obligations.
