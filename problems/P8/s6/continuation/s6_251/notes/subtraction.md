# A reduced free state is not a curved counterfunctional

The new state supplies an exact positive normalized covariance and a common short-distance singularity class for the specified reduced physical quadratic system. Normal ordering against that reference makes sense for its free algebra. It does not calculate the expectation of the original covariant metric/light observables or their response.

To obtain those observables one still needs the complete gauge/constraint or ghost measure, background-dependent field-map Jacobians, full composite-observable definitions and consistent curved subtraction. A collection of reduced mode energies is not automatically the variation of a covariant effective action. The positive selection norm used to define this state is expressly not its physical stress tensor.

The known QG2 coefficient profiles carry their original formal loop grades. A resummed choice of free operator must be accompanied by consistent countervertices or re-expansion when assigning perturbative order. It cannot be combined with the same profile corrections a second time. Likewise, the existing conditional H/Proca determinants and their finite contacts remain in the complete target.

Adding the scalar/tensor loops may change the reference mean equations. No such mean is calculated or cancelled here, and no new QG3 coefficient function is silently chosen. Quantitative omitted-loop and nonlinear estimates remain research work. The original quantum gravitational limit and physical scattering/IR/Regge conditions do not follow from the existence of this finite-slab free state.
