jet for the same parent, rather than another unrelated Gaussian orbit
example. It also obtains the actual central-chart ultraviolet powers.

must be compared before using an off-reference stability calculation.

  finite periodic diagnostic.

  Fourier cutoff, or residual translations can be discarded.

  state/endpoint Ward invariance has been established.

  as-yet uncomputed interacting quantum mean, or is removed by a gauge
  transformation.

and endpoint defects, still supplies the correct quantum comparison
