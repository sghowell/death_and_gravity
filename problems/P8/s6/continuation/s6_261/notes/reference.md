# Entire fixed covariance, finite jets and operator ordering

The scalar reference is exactly S251. In original normalized phase
Z=(v,sigma,pv,ps), physical phase is
z=sqrt(kappa)diag(1,1,a^3,a^3)Z.
Its covariance at time t and radial momentum P is

V_t(P)=S(t,t*;P)V(M(P))S(t,t*;P)^T.

The preparation Gramian, compact sampling bump, all current coefficient
functions and t* are unchanged. The basis-independent full two-mode
covariance V(M) keeps every off-diagonal entry; it is not two unrelated
instantaneous oscillators. Existing tensor, Proca and H blocks and the
M1 mean remain in the full product reference.

The entire S253 reference lapse row is

n=[Theta(pv+3ell sigma)+ell E ps-(2E q+3Tcorr)v]/(2Jc).

Its derivation keeps the source-square/auxiliary structure before the
reference restriction. Converting BOTH row endpoints with C^-1 gives
Cvv=rv V_t rv^T and Cvn=rv V_t rn^T. The latter retains the canonical
momentum cross entries and the full fixed Tcorr. No factorization of
composite expectations is used.

For a finite periodic diagnostic of volume Vol, take one representative
of each nonzero pair +/-k and two real canonical copies with exactly
these full Cauchy covariances. The real field convention is
sqrt(2/Vol)[q_c cos(kx)+q_s sin(kx)]. Consequently each pair contributes
2/Vol times its covariance row contraction, not 1/Vol or 4/Vol.
A full Gaussian cubature checks the quadratic moments with correlated
four-phase variables, independently of the symbolic mode formulas.

This is an explicitly named IR/UV diagnostic. Sampling the original
radial covariance on a finite lattice is not a claim that the original
R3 state has been replaced. A compact bounded momentum band on R3
instead directly restricts its existing two-point distribution. The
original homogeneous lapse/shift constraint sector is still not specified
by the measure-zero Fourier extension.

## Weyl coefficients, not a new quantum prescription

On the reduced canonical phase,

{v,n}=Theta/(2Jc kappa a^3)

with the corresponding equal-time delta distribution/mode factor.
Generic lapse and volume coordinates therefore do NOT commute after
constraint elimination. At the reference bounce Theta=0, their linear
commutator vanishes; the code verifies this independently with Fock
vacuum matrix elements. This does not establish a nonlinear ordering
prescription for the original physical sources.

We use symmetric covariance contractions, equivalently expectations of
the specified finite Weyl polynomials in the already fixed Gaussian
state. The second-order means are -9 Cvv_perp/4 and -9 Cvn_perp/4.
At finite mode number Cvv is strictly positive for any nonempty selection,
because the original full preparation covariance is strictly positive
and the physical row is nonzero.

A finite Gaussian has unbounded support. The calculation is a formal
Taylor/Wick coefficient at the reference, not an integral claimed to
remain inside the nonlinear positive-lapse/regular-auxiliary domain.
Exact classical periodic profiles are used only while they remain in
that domain. No hard amplitude truncation, new state preparation or
unrecorded quantum ordering is introduced. Equating this orbit average
with the interacting Nielsen vector still requires the complete S260
measure, regulator, endpoint and state Ward hypotheses.
