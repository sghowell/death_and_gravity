# Scope of the new candidate

CD-REG-AFFINE-ISO-QG1 is a new physical action with an explicit
coefficient error budget, as allowed by the S6 contract.
It does not rewrite CD-REG-AFFINE-ISO or import an old
profile into an unchanged certificate.

The established stationary mean history and vacuum belong
only to the classical affine/CD/M1 action plus its
specified canonical Gaussian vector contribution. They
are not a full interacting quantum state or a sum of
all light, tensor, auxiliary and mixed loops.

The corrected coefficient is fixed before variation.
The reference state's radial integral is never evaluated
as a new subtraction prescription on a perturbed metric.
No arbitrary source response is cancelled.

Global coefficient smoothness and exact retained mean
identities are distinguished from the small coefficient
bounds, which apply only for |u|<=1/2 on the stated X
domains. They give neither global-time stability nor
nonlinear perturbation/error control.

The prepared retained response exists uniquely, and its
local lapse-recovery constant is quantified. A useful
full inverse norm, a stable spectrum/cone and nonlinear
or noisy/spatial quantum response remain open.

The common parent now has both specified retained mean
backgrounds and bounded coefficient corrections, with
low vacuum germs and the complete fixed-canonical family
accounted for. This is still not the original B gate:
full interacting measure/state/loops, physical heavy and
cutoff matching and omitted-order control remain.
Vacuum cuts/contour/truncation and finite-gravity IR/Regge
also remain open. Scoped P8(a) and A.20-A.23 are unchanged.

No naturalness prior is imposed. Choosing a fixed small
coefficient is not, by itself, evidence that a UV theory
produces it. Original V/G/B and P8 remain OPEN.
