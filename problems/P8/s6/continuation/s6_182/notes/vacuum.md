# Common vacuum and fixed canonical interaction family

The nonconstant part of the new correction has the exact
factor X^1024 times a smooth coefficient near (u,X)=(0,0).
In canonical variables Phi=sqrt(kappa0)u and
Y=(partial Phi)^2=kappa0 X,

    Deltaf(Phi,Y)+P_vac=Y^1024 G(Phi,Y),

with G smooth near the vacuum and P_vac=kappa0*p_v.
Because Y has field degree2, every nonconstant field jet
below degree2048 vanishes. This is a finite-order Taylor
statement for smooth coefficients, not an unsupported
claim of a convergent analytic field expansion.

The scalar positive quadratic residue, mass-one quadratic
term, no cubic vertex and quartic lambda/gamma amplitude
germ from S6.177 are unchanged. R, its regular affine
dictionary and all vector/light source vertices are also
unchanged. The new constant is retained and cancels only
the specified same-scheme Gaussian vacuum energy in the
retained effective mean equations.

The new complete scalar coefficient need NOT be even in u:
the fixed all-order state was selected at t0=-1/2, and its
stress need not have time-reflection symmetry. No old
global parity claim is silently transferred. Lower vacuum
jets are preserved by the X factor, not by assumed parity.

An actual anchored decoupling family is available without
changing its complete independent interactions. Define once

    Deltaf(Phi,Y)=kappa0 DeltaF(Phi/sqrt(kappa0),Y/kappa0).

For every kappa>=kappa0 add
Deltaf(sqrt(kappa)u,kappa X)/kappa to the previously
constructed physical family. Pulling back to canonical
variables gives EXACTLY the same full Deltaf for every
kappa. The R family, physical chart and vector mass are
unchanged. This proves a fixed-interaction family, not
that the same CD background solves its off-anchor members.

No complete vacuum scattering analyticity, absorptive
weight, loop/truncation bound, Regge remainder or UV
completion is obtained from unchanged finite scalar
germs. High-order interactions in the full new coefficient
remain matching obligations.
