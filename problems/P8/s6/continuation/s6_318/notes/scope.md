# Strict scope and unchanged original frontiers

The new H8A482 record owns the uniform weighted PURE-SOFT current bound
at every finite multiplicity, with the stated explicit energy factors.
All173 prior matching records,9 primitive obligations,6 historical
qualifications and the rejected S279 record are preserved.

The result covers arbitrary directions and energy hierarchies on the
generic finite-tree domain. It does not assert energy-independent
boundedness, extend the old point evaluator to exact propagator poles,
or construct a unique all-parallel limiting value.

The auxiliary majorant series is a counting bound, not the physical
inclusive probability series. Factorial current control does not by
itself prove convergence of a Bose-weighted detector sum. All-N hard
amplitude estimates, hierarchical/overlapping soft subtractions and
complete real-virtual matching remain separate research obligations.

Nothing here supplies omitted hard loops, evanescent matching, an
interacting quantum Minkowski state, unitarity or a regulator-independent
massless S-matrix. The fixed-negative-transfer absolute complex Regge
contour, controlled forward limit and common-parent bounce stay open.

Original V/G/B/P8 remain OPEN; scoped P8(a) is unchanged.
