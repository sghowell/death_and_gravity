The original covariant prescription is essential; the sharp computational band has no arbitrary-diffeomorphism Ward guarantee. Neither the actual state nor its one-point current is replaced, and nonlinear chart contacts remain. The full clock/scalar parent is not globally source-free.

Older ordinary-Hadamard-form terminology is not adopted as a denial of modern microlocal Proca Hadamard states. No scalar-field perturbative-agreement theorem is substituted for a Proca theorem.
