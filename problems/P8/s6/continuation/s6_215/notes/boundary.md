# Initial-only source domain and detector endpoints

S213 states its bound first for sources compact in the open slab. The synchronous source need not vanish near the right endpoint. The extension requires checking the proof; one cannot insert a rapidly varying inside-I cutoff and assume its time derivatives are free.

S194's homogeneous current and C12 bound require a common zero initial neighborhood and direction jets on I, not a final zero neighborhood. Its tangent starts from the unchanged initial covariance. S198 integrates by parts only in the source variable over [left,t]. All omitted lower endpoints vanish by the source germ; every upper endpoint is at the current time and is retained. No discarded term uses a final source germ.

S197/S201 state-memory comparisons use the causal triangle and time L2 Cauchy. Bulk and endpoint estimates use the stated source jets on I. S208's low/near/far bounds, S212's local coefficients and S213's dimensional majorant impose no final-source condition. The homogeneous Hilbert lift uses Gamma^(j)(t)=integral_left^t Gamma^(j+1), again only the initial germ. The same proof and constants therefore give the tracefree current and anchored regulator bound for smooth initial-only sources on I. This successor adds that domain statement without editing frozen S213.

Extend the source smoothly beyond the final endpoint and cut it off outside I for compact metric-variation arguments. No norm of this outside extension enters the causal integrals or local jets on I; the detector vanishes near the final endpoint. Causality makes the result independent of the extension. This is not application of the old bound to an uncontrolled inside-I cutoff.

The advanced synchronous detector can reach the initial endpoint. Source jets and retarded output vanish on an initial neighborhood. A smooth time cutoff bounded by1 inside that zero-output neighborhood leaves the pairing unchanged and cannot increase M, which has spatial but no time derivatives. Its final zero neighborhood remains.

The differentiated Ward flux vanishes initially because hG and deltaE vanish, even if xi_D does not. It vanishes finally because xi_D does. A retarded detector primitive generally leaves a nonzero final flux.

Begin with compact spatial variations; Schwartz pairings and completions follow from the explicit bounds, with decaying spatial flux and compact approximation as needed. No strong differentiability of an unbounded stress operator is inferred.
