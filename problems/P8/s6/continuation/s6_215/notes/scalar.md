# Three ordered scalar kernels, not two

For P!=0 put e=P/|P|, Etr=I/sqrt3 and E0=(3ee^T-I)/sqrt6. Both have Frobenius norm1, are orthogonal, and E0 is tracefree. Write Q=gamma+u Etr and v=E0:gamma.

The background, prepared covariance and fixed geometric prescription are rotation covariant. A trace/tracefree pairing is invariant under rotations fixing P, so it depends only on the axial average v E0. Four quarter-turns already remove vector and transverse tensor components on symmetric three-tensors; this projection is checked including off-diagonal components.

S213 controls all gamma/gamma entries. The missing scalar part is exactly

    d_u Rtt g_u + d_u Rt0 g_v + d_v R0t g_u.

Both cross responses are needed. Rotation invariance does not equate Rt0(t,s) and R0t(t,s). Retarded/advanced reciprocity, if established, transposes and reverses time arguments; it does not identify these two forward-time entries. Any two-by-two scalar matrix is compatible with axial invariance.

At P0 the full rotation group forces both trace/tracefree cross entries to vanish, but leaves the trace/trace homogeneous anchor undetermined. The unit projectors are bounded on the spatial Sobolev norms; their direction dependence at the origin neither implies nor requires Borel-state momentum analyticity.

The three kernels still need full state/time/UV/contact calculation, original dimensional finite matching and norm bounds. Symmetry and Ward identities do not substitute for that work.
