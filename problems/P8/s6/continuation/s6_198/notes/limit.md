# Equal time is not yet covariant spatial locality

The first five B_j contain only source time jets at
the readout time. Their amplitudes still depend on
both internal momenta and on P=k+l through the full
physical readouts and inverse summed frequency.

Even the elementary frozen kernel
1/(m+sqrt(m^2+p^2)) is not a finite spatial polynomial.
Its sixth p derivative at p0 is-225/(8m^7), and its
Taylor series has nonzero coefficients at arbitrarily
high even order. Thus a finite time-jet order does
not itself make these boundary kernels spatially local.

The required next step is to extract the FULL
high-internal-momentum spatial expansion, retain all
tensor/magnetic/longitudinal/contact terms and match
the combined local terms to the unchanged covariant
retarded prescription. Possible odd-term cancellations
must be proved for that complete combination.

The constant-alpha W8 reference remains a comparison
device. Its possible CCR defect and nonzero equation
residual do not disappear under integration by parts.
No independent reference Ward identity is assumed.
No finite counterterm is adjusted to simplify the
remaining sector or force a small final response.
