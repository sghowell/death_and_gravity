# Independent original massive-state and frozen-kernel checks

Three original massive recoil states and both physical polarizations
are retained. A separate4-vector implementation reconstructs the
full24 assignments and four cyclic line insertions, integrates their
gamma and angular polynomials exactly, and compares the whole
z-polynomial with the derived curvature weight times the actual T.
All six original T values are nonzero. Underlying scalar mass shells
and total radiative conservation are checked.

For each state/polarization, three external orderings and all four
line positions are also checked against the ACTUAL frozen S342
ordered-box line implementation:72 full kernels in total.
Use the unchanged original heavy mass and a stated common1/16
momentum rescaling into the analytic-origin domain. This is an
off-shell calibration, not a change in the physical source mass.

Both complete denominators and weighted TT densities are compared.
The complex z contour, its Jacobian, the Gamma factor2, the
original split-line weight and both angular coordinates are retained.
When the cyclic order swaps which light/heavy line is first,
xi and eta are both changed accordingly; the physical masses are
not exchanged.

These calibrations are supplementary. The2430-coefficient generic
polynomial proof is what excludes dependence on untested kinematic
configurations. No floating fit, numerical quadrature or physical
Taylor-truncation inference is used.
