# What is, and is not, compared

The theorem compares signed marked densities on one specified full
leading-cloud probability space. The full and retained states use their
own total cuts and their own remaining energy; no mark is evaluated
outside its physical domain. The total variation norm is E|f_eta-f|.

For a>0 the retained emission configuration has finite count almost
surely, while the full configuration has infinite count. Conditioning
on the positive-probability total-energy cut preserves those events.
Their configuration laws are therefore mutually singular. The theorem
does NOT claim their TV convergence. This count argument is not
automatically a statement about angular-energy measure pushforwards,
since coincident source directions can coalesce emissions.

The result completes S331's specifically open cutoff comparison for
the LIMITING additional-soft insertion. It supplies neither a
simultaneous quantitative epsilon/eta estimate for the regulated
insertion nor outer D-dimensional hard/evanescent matching. The
reference intensity remains fixed Born D4; it is not radiative-state
branching or a full interacting detector probability.

Original V/G/B/P8 remain OPEN. Scoped P8(a) is unchanged. All-N hard
summation, finite radiative matching, quantum state/unitarity, absolute
complex Regge, common-parent bounce and UV obligations remain separate.
