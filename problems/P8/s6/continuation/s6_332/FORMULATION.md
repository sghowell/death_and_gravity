# Exact observable and comparison

Fix the original action, physical hard E,u and D4 Born radiation
intensity Lambda(dn) dw/w on 0<w<1, with mass 0<=a<=1.
For the original parameters a<4/(5*kappa), kappa=10^800.
Let sigma be the full positive angular-energy measure, sigma_eta its
restriction to w>=eta, R and R_eta their masses and T=R-R_eta.
Take 0<eta<=x<=1/8. The unconditioned cloud may have R>1/8;
no physical state mark is evaluated there unless its own cut holds.

Put A={R<=x}, A_eta={R_eta<=x}, p=P(A), p_eta=P(A_eta).
The limiting insertion is Z(sigma;y)=deltaDelta(sigma)+deltaa(sigma)*ln y,
where both deltas subtract the same Born state and use the unchanged
additional-soft fixed-ball dimensional convention. In particular the
Born subtraction uses the SAME y as the radiative term.

On the SAME underlying full Poisson probability space compare
f=1_A*Z(sigma;x-R)/p and
f_eta=1_A_eta*Z(sigma_eta;x-R_eta)/p_eta.
Outside the applicable event define its density to be zero without
evaluating its mark. Positive endpoint equalities have probability zero;
at a=0 all states and marks vanish, so define both densities as zero.

The written proofs establish E|f_eta-f|<=63000*a*eta*L_eta^2/kappa,
L_eta=1+ln(1/eta). At original parameters it is strictly below
51000*eta*L_eta^2/kappa^2. This is the total variation NORM of a signed
density difference, without the probability-distance factor 1/2.
It implies convergence of the associated conditioned means. It does
not compare the configuration pushforwards of those reference laws.
