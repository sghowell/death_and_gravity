# Physical two-endpoint radiation and independent normalizations

## Explicit physical TT insertions and ordered product rule

For any scalar endpoint kernel F(v), with a channel partition L,R,
P=sum_L p_i, v=P^2, u=(P+k)^2, the physical TT amplitude is

sum_L J_i F(u)+sum_R J_i F(v)
 -2epsilon(P,P) [F(u)-F(v)]/(u-v),

where J_i=epsilon(p_i,p_i)/(p_i.k).
At equal invariants the divided difference is F'(v), not a pole.
A Ward-compatible extension contains epsilon(k,P) and trace terms
as detailed in the initial kernel derivation. Additional transverse
k_mu k_nu terms in the unprojected loop tensor are not determined
or claimed here; they vanish in the actual physical TT projection.

For the inner G_phi^2 kernel, both explicit massive-line triangles
in exact D give the divided difference of B. For the outer minimal
heavy resolvent, the literal heavy stress insertion gives the divided
difference of A. Write A_v,A_u and B_v,B_u. The ordered three-factor
variation is exactly

D_A(v,u) B_u A_u + A_v D_B(v,u) A_u + A_v B_v D_A(v,u)
 =D_(A^2 B)(v,u).

This retains both heavy insertions and both scalar-loop insertions.
It establishes the full physical TT F=A^2 B graph class directly,
not by guessing a general curved completion from its flat value.
The coincidence limit is2AA'B+A^2B'.

As independent controls, F=1,v,v^2 agree with complete literal
covariant quartic actions from S337. The resolvent test kernel agrees
with all ten symmetric tensor components of the frozen complete heavy
exchange engine at three physical recoil configurations. These
diagnostic tests use a specified test mass17 and unit coefficients;
the final bounds and absorptive checks use ORIGINAL n,g,C.

## Complete physical TT kernel, and a Ward-compatible completion

For a partition L,R of the four hard legs let
P=sum_(i in L) p_i, v=P^2, u=(P+k)^2,
a_i=p_i.k, H_ij=epsilon(p_i,p_j),
J_i=H_ii/a_i+epsilon(k,p_i)/a_i-tr(epsilon)/2,
and D_B(v,u)=[B(u)-B(v)]/(u-v), continuously B'(v) at u=v.

One convenient Ward-compatible channel representative is

sum_(i in L) J_i B(u) + sum_(i in R) J_i B(v)
 -(2epsilon(P,P)+2epsilon(k,P)) D_B(v,u)
 +tr(epsilon)[B(v)+B(u)]/2.

This satisfies the full pure-gauge Ward identity for arbitrary endpoint
values of the kernel. In physical TT gauge the k and trace terms
vanish, leaving the explicitly computed complete physical amplitude.
The full unprojected loop tensor can additionally contain a transverse
k_mu k_nu form factor invisible to real TT emission; the representative
is NOT claimed to determine every curved/unphysical tensor component.

For the full class replace the diagnostic kernel B by A^2 B and
subtract3A(4/3)^2 B(4/3)[sum_i J_i+tr(epsilon)], restoring
1/(32pi^2 sqrt(kappa)). All three UV counterterms are retained as
shown in loops.md; the C^2 diagnostic alone is not the full class.

As independent normalization checks, kernels F(v)=1,v,v^2 reproduce
the complete literal covariant quartic radiation of
Phi4/8, Phi4/6 and Y2/2+Phi4/6 respectively. The resolvent test kernel
F(v)=1/(n-v) reproduces the full frozen massive-exchange tensor at all
ten arbitrary symmetric components of each of three recoil states.
That comparison uses a diagnostic n=17 and unit coefficients to test
the general kernel identity; it does not replace original parameters.
