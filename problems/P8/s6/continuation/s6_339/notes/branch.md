# Actual physical complex branch and continuous quotient

## Actual physical invariant ranges and branch bounds

For5/4<=E<=2 and0<omega<=1/8, each outgoing scalar has energy
E-omega/2 plus or minus omega*r'*cos/(2E'), strictly less than E.
The sum-of-two-incoming and sum-of-two-outgoing timelike invariants
therefore obey

45/8<=4E(E-omega)<=v,u<=4E^2<=16.

A crossed pair is a difference of two future-directed mass-one
vectors whose energies are at most E. Consequently
-4(E^2-1)<=v,u<=0, hence v,u in[-12,0].
Each channel's two endpoints and its segment lie entirely on one
of these separate intervals; they do not cross the threshold4.
The Born endpoint lies on the same interval.

On v in[5,16], beta=sqrt(1-4/v) and the Feynman boundary is

B(v)=2-beta[L(v)-i pi], L=log((1+beta)/(1-beta)).
B'(v)=-2[L-i pi]/(v^2 beta)-1/v.

Here beta>2/5, beta<=sqrt3/2, L<3 and pi<4. The split logarithm
integral proves the real part and the positive imaginary cut pi*beta,
rather than discarding absorption. Since0<=beta L<3,
|2-beta L|<=2 and hence |B|<6. The derivative formula gives|B'|<2.
With beta''=-4/(v^3 beta)-4/(v^4 beta^3), differentiation also gives
|B''|<2 using the same endpoint margins. These are complex-modulus
bounds on the physical boundary, not positive Euclidean integrals.

On[-12,0], the positive denominator gives
|B|<=log4<2, |B'|<=1/6 and|B''|<=1/30.
At the symmetric subtraction point B(4/3) is real, positive and<1.
The divided difference is the segment integral of B', so it extends
continuously through v=u and has modulus<2 in every physical channel.

## Branch and original recoil bounds

The actual compact recoil domain gives timelike channel endpoints
in[45/8,16] and crossed endpoints in[-12,0]. Each channel segment
stays in its own interval; the endpoints never straddle threshold4.
The Born endpoint belongs to the same interval. Equal invariants
occur in genuine nonforward recoil configurations and are allowed.

For v in[5,16], beta=sqrt(1-4/v) and
B=2-beta[log((1+beta)/(1-beta))-i pi].
The split logarithm integral gives the positive cut Im B=pi beta.
Here beta>2/5, log((1+beta)/(1-beta))<3 and pi<4. Exact differentiation
and endpoint margins prove |B|<6,|B'|<2,|B''|<2.
On[-12,0], the positive parameter denominator gives
|B|<2,|B'|<=1/6,|B''|<=1/30.
At v=4/3,0<B(v)<1.
These physical complex-boundary estimates follow from the explicit
derivation above; the branch module checks the derivative and cut identities.
In particular the imaginary part is not discarded.
