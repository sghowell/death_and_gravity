# Independent components and exact original absorptive states

The general resolvent kernel is compared with the frozen S295 complete
massive-exchange tensor at three original rational recoil states and
all10 symmetric tensor components each. This is a diagnostic n=17,
unit-cubic,zero-contact normalization test, not a replacement for the
original mass/couplings in any physical estimate.

For the physical absorptive calculation use the three S304 sample
states and the additional exact state E=5/4,Eprime=461/380,
omega=E-Eprime^2/E, emission direction(1,0,0), outgoing rest direction
(0,3/5,4/5). The last state has genuine coincident crossed invariants:
four channel/polarization coincidences, evaluated continuously.

The timelike incoming/outgoing beta=sqrt(1-4/s) are rational in all
four states. In the center-of-mass frame epsilon(P_in,P_in)=0.
Write b(v)=nC/g^2+n/(n-v). After removing the common pi, the complete
absorptive kernel in each physical polarization is exactly

b(s_out)^2 beta_out (J0+J1)+b(s_in)^2 beta_in (J2+J3).

Compute all three channels first, then check this reduction; do not
assume vanishing crossed-channel imaginary parts fixes the real part.
Subtract b(s_in)^2 beta_in times the complete Born soft current.
Each remaining polarization is nonzero rational. The sewn sum of
squares divided by the actual polarization norm is exact and positive.

Restore the prefactor g^4/(32pi n^2 sqrt(kappa));pi>3 gives the exact
squared original-normalized comparison
norm*g^8/(96^2 n^4 A0^2)<10^388.
The omitted common1/kappa is explicit. Original n,g,C and full A0
are used here. Samples calibrate but do not prove the uniform bound.

Earlier90-digit numerical evaluations of the C^2-only diagnostic
complex kernel were exploratory, not interval certificates, and
