# Exact-D insertions, operator ordering and fixed subtractions

## Both internal scalar insertions in exact dimension

Specialize the actual two-mass triangle derivation of S338 to equal
mass one; it is an algebraic specialization, not an import of its
large-heavy-mass gap to this cut kinematics. Put a=P.k and

M(z;v)=1-z(1-z)v,
M_L=M-2zya, 0<=y<=1-z,
M_R=M-2(1-z)ya, 0<=y<=z.

After isotropic loop integration the two TT weights are z^2 and
(1-z)^2. Relative to Gamma(1+epsilon), the integrands are
-2z^2 M_L^(-1-epsilon) and
-2(1-z)^2 M_R^(-1-epsilon).
Their parameter antiderivatives and gamma recurrence give the complete
insertion -2epsilon(P,P) D_BD(v,u), before subtracting the dimensional
pole. At a=0 the two parameter widths combine by

z^2(1-z)+(1-z)^2z=z(1-z),

which gives the exact-D derivative B_D'(v), not a pole. The
momentum-independent UV pole cancels from the divided difference.

Establish these equalities first in a subthreshold complex domain
where the Feynman integrals are analytic. They continue as identities
on the same Feynman sheet to the physical cut. The physical boundary
values and their derivatives below are finite away from threshold.
Do not replace a Feynman integral across a zero by an ordinary
absolute-value parameter integral.

## Existing UV and finite subtractions

Only the inner bubble has a constant dimensional pole Delta.
The old fixed UV counterterms are

deltaC=-3C^2 Delta/(32pi^2),
deltag=-C g Delta/(32pi^2),
deltan=g^2 Delta/(32pi^2).

For each channel their contact/trilinear/heavy-mass combination is

deltaC/3+2g*deltag/(n-v)-g^2*deltan/(n-v)^2
 =-A(v)^2 Delta/(32pi^2).

Taking the full covariant metric variation, including all propagator
and source insertions, gives precisely the opposite TT divided
difference. Thus all three UV counterterms cancel the complete
selected radiative pole, not only the contact piece.
The remaining constant -3A(4/3)^2 B(4/3)/(32pi^2) is the linear
bubble contribution to the already fixed OS4 finite value condition.
Its full five radiation graphs are retained. No new finite derivative
or curvature matching choice is introduced.

The two equal-mass triangle identities are initially proved in a
subthreshold complex domain and then analytically continued to the
same Feynman boundary. The pole cancels before the finite limit.
No absolute-value integral across a Feynman denominator zero is used.
