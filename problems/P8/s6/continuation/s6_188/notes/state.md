# Same Cauchy state on the explicitly extended shear family

The metric family here is spatially homogeneous rather
than compactly supported in space. This extension of
the external-metric domain is explicit; it does not
silently claim the older compact-spacetime family has
unchanged hypotheses. The parent and its coefficient
functions, vector mass and initial state are unchanged.

A smooth tracefree gamma(t) with compact time support
has a uniform finite norm bound. Its spatial metric
a^2 exp gamma is positive, and coordinate causal speed
is at most exp(||gamma||/2)/a. Since a>=1 and the bound
is finite, a causal curve with a finite limiting t
has a finite spatial endpoint and extends. Therefore
the t slices remain Cauchy and the metric is globally
hyperbolic. Near t0 it agrees with the actual CD metric.

[Moretti, Murro and Volpe,2210.09278v3,Proposition4.7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2210.09278v3)
extends the common-neighborhood quasifree state uniquely
and preserves its Hadamard property. The required
Proca wavefront-set gap in that argument is repaired by
[Fewster,2503.12544v2,Theorem5.1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.12544v2).
The fixed positive mass, global hyperbolicity and common
Cauchy neighborhood have been checked here. No spatial
compactness premise or global Fock implementation is added.

At the common surface use exactly the S6.55 all-order
cutoff-summed Cauchy preparation, not W8 reset data.
For each polarization the coordinate mode data are

    A_r=f_r/g_r, pi_r=g_r p_r,
    gT^2=a, gL^2=a m^2/omega^2,
    p=f'-d f, dT=H/2,dL=(1/2+z)H.

The symmetrized covariance is the real part of the
positive mode Gram matrix, whose antisymmetric part
is the required iJ. Propagation by the exact symplectic
Hamiltonian preserves positivity and the CCR.
The S6.185 actual-state inequalities give
|f|^2<9/omega and |p|^2<27 omega. Thus each real
three-oscillator Fourier sector has initial energy
trace below3*(9+27)nu=108nu.

The covariance derivative holds these Cauchy data
fixed while varying the prescribed external metric.
It is neither a change of the mean-stress scheme
nor a postulate about quantum metric initial data.
Hadamard regularity does not supply the missing
quantitative UV-subtracted response remainder.
