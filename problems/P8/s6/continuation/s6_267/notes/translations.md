# Residual translations are retained and solved only as stated

The slice div Q=0,det Q=1 is invariant under constant coordinate
translations. The Fourier projectors and unique nonlinear shape solution
commute with those translations. Where the full S266 auxiliary branch
applies, its uniqueness and the scalar source/constraint transformation
law give translation equivariance too.

Consequently the full canonical one-form on the slice yields

J_i=integral[Pi_v partial_i v+Pi_tau:partial_i tau
 +Pi_W dot partial_i W+Pi_M partial_i M1+Pi_H partial_i H].

These are the three remaining global momentum constraints, not terms to
drop from the reduction. Their canonical Poisson brackets vanish,
including every vector/matter channel. On the unreduced slice the
cotangent correction leaves their values unchanged, since
A V(constant)=0. They can thus be imposed after the nonzero second-class
pairs have been eliminated.

The mean-zero descriptors are NOT a Lie algebra:
[sin(x)partial_x,cos(x)partial_x]=-partial_x.
No restricted diffeomorphism bracket or finite-difference Leibniz identity
is inferred. The nonzero constraint-constraint block was retained in the
full second-class inverse. The quotient at J=0 is still by the residual
compact translation group; fixed points, such as homogeneous fields,
can give strata. We do not claim a globally regular reduced quotient.

For a nonzero real Fourier pair write
q=a cos(k.x)+b sin(k.x). With normalized coordinate volume, its canonical
momentum is2pc cos(k.x)+2ps sin(k.x), so
J_i=k_i(b.pc-a.ps). This fixes the factor2 from the real Fourier norm.
Different mode pairs add their complete charges. The exact diagnostics
check three non-collinear wavevectors and all three commuting generators.

A symmetric finite set of physical modes has an exact translation action:
products in a translation-invariant integrated symbol conserve momentum.
This small residual group can be represented exactly on the finite
canonical space. It does not follow that a finite collocation of the
unreduced full diffeomorphism algebra closes or that the complete
regulated interacting symbol/domain has already been constructed.
