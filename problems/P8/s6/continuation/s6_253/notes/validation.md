# Validation, freezing and evidence boundaries

The publication manifest has17 scientific sources: six modules, two test
files and nine Markdown proof/formulation files. The native report has20
top-level fields and pins the entire S252 parent report by raw SHA256.
Native replay rebuilds the full unchanged ancestry. Source hashes and
report fields are checked independently before publication.

The exact checks cover the complete parent clock germs and fixed profile
bindings, all coefficient lapse jets and reference identities, full joint
Legendre/constraint reduction and physical measure, held-W square and
normal vertex, every physical background chain, all remaining mode maps,
and the entire6/16-dimensional fixed-state response. Scope tests reject
changes to physical normalization, state preparation, covariance, phase
dimension, original obligations and earlier matching records.

Independent diagnostics re-enter the full raw scalar ADM action, complete
held-vector Fourier square, three-mode physical-density Hamiltonian and
twelve-dimensional Poisson brackets. All18 physical coefficient rows
are separately differentiated at three rational background times through
second order. Jet power rules, complete eight-mode product ordering and
nonzero mixed source noise are checked separately.

Whole three-mode sparse Fock calculations check the means, ordered
quartic moments, commutator, noise and full contact. Those quartic vacuum
moments fit exactly within the stated occupation support. Evolved-state
CTP overlaps are instead finite numerical diagnostics at two cutoffs;
they are not infinite-Fock convergence bounds. Direct16-dimensional
four-index contractions check full-mode ordering. Time-dependent phase
evolution, its first/second parameter equations, central differences and
the independent Duhamel integral check the response and nonzero contact.

The private source/field preflight precedes freezing; it verifies the pinned
parent report and its source hashes without re-running the ancestry. The
native, ordinary and CLI routes rebuild that ancestry. The fresh repository
and native/source field audits must agree before publication. The raw
native JSON bytes are copied without parse/reserialization,
including huge exact integers. Ordinary and standalone CLI replays are
read-only. A complete captured P8 regression then checks all captured
test files were present and unchanged at collection. The final counts,
durations, snapshot hashes, native transfer and publication evidence are
recorded in the external dated assessment, not by editing frozen sources.

Native, direct science, ordinary and CLI use original SymPy. Only full
regression uses the separately audited exact-GCD adapter. No older
scientific file, test, proof or native report is changed. Publication uses
an explicit21-file staging manifest, validates staged native/source hashes,
preserves unrelated P4/P9 changes and independently checks the remote hash.

Exact and finite numerical diagnostics support these written scoped
arguments. They do not FORMALIZE the theory, construct the unclaimed
nonlinear covariant functional, choose physical subtraction, compute a
physical stress or establish original P8 closure.
