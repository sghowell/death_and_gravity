# Original shell, hemisphere and positive grazing strip

For K>p+m, the second created momentum reaches its cutoff at

r*=p u+sqrt(K^2-p^2(1-u^2)).

The lost shell is r in(r*,K], and it exists precisely for u<p/(2K). The negative of its integral is the original pair-band minus one-ball comparison. The low endpoint m does not intervene under this condition.

Write e=p/K and M=4-q. For M=1,...,4, A_M=(1-R^M)/M with R=e u+sqrt(1-e^2(1-u^2)). The first four hemisphere coefficients are

a1=-u,
a2=(1-M u^2)/2,
a3=(M-1)u/2-(M^2-1)u^3/6,
a4=(2-M)/8+M(M-2)u^2/4-M(M^2-4)u^4/24.

Only h<=M is retained. The exact current conversion is minus K^M times the angular integral.

The moving positive strip is not replaced by a hemisphere. Put u=e v, 0<=v<=1/2. Then A_M(e v,e)=e^2(1/2-v)+O(e^4). Taylor expansion of the azimuthal average Fbar gives

integral_0^(e/2) Fbar(u) A_M(u,e) du
=e^3 Fbar(0)/8+e^4 Fbar_prime(0)/48+O(e^5).

Thus the complete h-th angular shape is

C_qh[Fbar]=2pi integral_(-1)^0 Fbar(u) a_h(u)du
+2pi delta_(h,3) Fbar(0)/8
+2pi delta_(h,4) Fbar_prime(0)/48.

The universal formula is valid before imposing actual coefficient symmetries. For the actual leading even coefficient the quartic strip derivative vanishes; its cubic strip value does not. General polynomial fixtures test both functionals without being called physical counterexamples.

Ten universal q/h shapes instantiate twenty original j/d/h branches. Their thirty-five source-time-jet shapes retain all original endpoint contributions. The q4 radial primitive is logarithmic. Its shell vanishes as O(p/K); its one-ball logarithmic UV coefficient is not deleted.
