# Leading target data, not completion

This checkpoint reads the unchanged S6.109 analytic family, its
source-pinned original CD_matter witness and the retuned margin.
It rederives the exact leading clock metric equations and keeps
the original M1 matter normalization. No earlier coefficient,
state, proof, test or report is changed.

The result explains why the S6.172 minimal canonical diagnostic
does not exclude the actual target: the target has leading
nonminimal/derivative structure and a reconstructed scalar kernel.
It also explains why a coordinate map and a small vacuum-unit
action bound alone do not establish a common-parent realization.

The physical parent must reproduce the leading clock source and
constraint data on a real clock-neighborhood domain, in addition
to its vacuum matching. Its propagating spectrum, state, cutoff,
higher-loop and higher-operator errors remain obligations.
No physical interacting remainder or healthy UV completion is
established here. The fixed free S6.171 quantum tensor is still an
added source requiring state-aware background and response control;
the exact classical cancellation does not cancel it automatically.

Individual action pieces in the table are not separately conserved
matter sectors. No positive/negative energy conclusion about a
physical propagating mode is drawn from one piece's background
Euler source. In particular A4,A5 cannot be deleted on that basis.

Original V/G/B and P8 remain OPEN. Scoped P8(a) and A.20-A.23
are unchanged. Neither a global scattering history nor an extra
all-UV completion theorem is added to the adopted contract.
Missing leading-parent estimates remain research, not a request
for the user to choose an arbitrary correction.
