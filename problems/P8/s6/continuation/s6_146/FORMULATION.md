# S6.146: finite MS slopes of both quadratic fermion primitives

Continue the unchanged GY14 reference and proper MS scheme of S6.145.
Determine the finite zero-momentum MS slopes of the complete scalar
and gauge quadratic primitives with a massless exchanged boson.
Restore the actual scalar boson mass one by a uniform bound on the
full difference, not by setting that mass to zero.

Use the prior nonlocal remainder to bound the shift from the zero-
momentum slope to the on-shell slope at s=1. All proper counterterms,
the dimension-dependent numerator and finite pole products must remain
through this calculation. Trace of the Dirac identity is four.

The actual scalar and gauge primitive slopes together must be bounded
at the shared mF=10^200, N=6, C_F=4/3 and rational coupling enclosures.
The domain for the bound is m>=720, Y,a,E>=0 and 0<Q_lo<=144, where
E is a supplied bound on these primitives' soft tail on |s-1|<=2.

This is not the finite mass reference, the full matched canonical
two-loop pole or amplitude, a vacuum potential or an all-loop estimate.
Other matching/parameter/field contributions, two vacuum rows,
truncation, V/G/B and original P8 remain open. The adopted finite-EFT
and necessary-positivity contract is unchanged.
