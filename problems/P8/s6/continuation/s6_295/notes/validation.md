ASCII newline-terminated source files. The verifier has20 AST-derived
fields and is read-only. Exact packet payloads contain no floating values.
All immediate parent sources and native raw hash are checked before reuse.

Cold and fresh forced-parent-warm private checks must agree before source
freeze and native capture. The persistent original-SymPy native helper
receives the SPEC exactly once; chunks are size/hash checked and separately
validated against source, AST, count and frontier contracts. Scientific
sources and native raw originals are not rewritten afterward.

Required acceptance includes the original-SymPy ordinary test and CLI
replays plus a complete captured P8 FULL snapshot. Only FULL may use the
separately audited guarded exact-GCD adapter. No subset or earlier FULL
is presented as coverage of this packet. Run-specific evidence belongs
in an external assessment, not a retroactively modified frozen source.

majorant, independent literal physical tensors, full TT sew, on-shell
recoil and12 finite rate quadrature cases at two orders. The first research

canonical zero comparison; this did not change the formula or any frozen
