# Matching and physical infrared boundary

The exact original tuned C and heavy-exchange amplitudes are retained.
No finite R H,R Phi^2,heavy-residue or higher-operator coefficient is chosen.
No old matching record, rejected S279 status or historical qualification
is replaced by this selected real-emission result.

The new physical comparison concerns the squared complete matter tree,
including C^2,Cg^2,g^4 interference at one emitted graviton. Its hard-angle
convention and finite-resolution domain are explicit. The finite difference
does not make either Fock real rate finite. A virtual/dressed pairing and
the conversion to the S278 dimensional analytic representative remain
necessary, together with gravity-exchange radiation and hard-loop errors.

Bellazzini et al., https://arxiv.org/html/2512.13780v2, sections3.9-4.2,
motivate distinguishing finite-resolution physical rates from analytic
soft division. Their comparison is not imported as a proof for this
massive tuned source; the tensor, recoil and error calculations here
are independent. In particular no massless formula closes our Regge or
forward-limit obligation.
