# Noise input is not a quantum metric solution

This result concerns the specified zero-mean conditional
canonical Proca state on the actual unperturbed CD
geometry, including arbitrary compact spacetime tensor
tests. It is not restricted to homogeneous stress,
zero external momentum or a flat vacuum.

The original all-order state, source, physical metric,
fixed scalar coefficient and renormalization convention
are unchanged. At the reference clock the source's
first metric variation is zero; deterministic mean
retuning does not cancel quadratic stress fluctuations.

The exact quantum quadratic stress need not have
Gaussian higher statistics. Its covariance can be
used as an input to a separately justified stochastic
approximation, but no such approximation or solution
is constructed by this bound.

Einstein normalization of a metric test is an explicit
field convention, not a solution of the lapse/shift
constraints or a diagonalization of the coupled
scalar-metric modes. In particular the noise norm
cannot simply be multiplied by a homogeneous C0
inverse to infer full spatial metric fluctuations.

The actual spatial/nonlinear retarded response, useful
finite-coupling inverse and remainder bounds, complete
interacting parent state/measure/loops, physical heavy/
cutoff/threshold and omitted-order matching remain
open. So do full vacuum cuts/contour/truncation and
finite-gravity IR/Regge.

Bounds use the fixed reference kappa, never the
classical energy density vanishing at the bounce.
They apply only on the unit CD slab, not globally
in time. All momentum integrations belong to the
specified conditional free sector and do not certify
an all-frequency physical EFT.

Original P8(b), V/G/B and P8 remain OPEN. The closed
scoped P8(a) and A.20-A.23 are unaffected.
