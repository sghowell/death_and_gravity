# Full complex reference amplitudes at every momentum

Let nu=sqrt(m^2+|k|^2/Amax^2), m=1000 and Amax=25/16.
On the real CD slab, nu<=omega<=Amax nu. Fix each real
time and extend to the complex time disc radius1/10000.

There |u|<.51 and |1+u^2|>.7, so |H|<3. Integrating
the analytic logarithmic derivative of a^-2 gives a
relative defect bounded by
6r/(1-6r)<1/1000. It follows that
|omega^2/omega_real^2-1|<1/1000.
The positive-real square-root branch is analytic, with
|omega|>=.99nu, Re omega>=.99nu and |omega|<2nu.
The same rational map gives |z|<1.01.

Use the full four coefficient polynomials in W8:
W=omega[1+sum_(n=1)^4 P_(2n)(u,z)/omega^(2n)].
Each P has a positive constant times(1+u^2)^j as its
denominator. The exact polynomial coefficient sum at
|u|=.51,|z|=1.01, with denominator modulus>.7^j,
bounds it throughout the complex domain. These are
continuous coefficient estimates, not sampled discs.
The full relative W defect is below1/1000 in both modes.

Therefore Re W>.5nu and |W|<3nu. On the inner time
disc radius r0=1/20000, Cauchy gives
|W'|<=3nu/r0=60000nu. The actual canonical rate is
dT=H/2 or dL=(1/2+z)H, hence |d|<5.
The phase-stripped reference field and momentum are

    f_amp=1/sqrt(2W),
    p_amp=(-iW-W'/(2W)-d)/sqrt(2W).

Their moduli are bounded by nu^-1/2 and64sqrt(nu).
Also |m/omega|<2 and |(k/a)/omega|<3. Every component
of the ten-field readout is consequently below
1000sqrt(nu); its Euclidean norm is below4000sqrt(nu).
All square roots use continuous analytic branches
on the disc. The conjugate-on-real-axis reference
readouts have the same bounds on their analytic
Schwarz continuations.

For a pair k,l, the summed reference phase derivative
has real part above(nu+mu)/2 throughout the same disc.
Its reciprocal is analytic and bounded by2/(nu+mu).
This is an actual curved-background phase bound, not
a Minkowski mass-shell prescription.
