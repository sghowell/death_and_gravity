# Four-metric parameterization and first-current contact chain

The independent chart used here is N=1+n, contravariant beta^i, and h=a^2 exp(Q). The physical +--- four-metric is

    g00=N^2-h(beta,beta), g0i=-(h beta)i, gij=-hij.

For a direction D=(n_D,b_D,D_Q) at the reference,

    Dg00=2n_D, Dg0i=-a^2 b_D,i, Dgij=-a^2 D_Q,ij.

For two directions D,G,

    D_G g00=2n_D n_G-2a^2 b_D.b_G,
    D_G g0i=-a^2(D_Q b_G+G_Q b_D)i,
    D_G gij=-a^2(D_Q G_Q+G_Q D_Q)ij/2.

All noncommuting spatial words are retained. Consequently the same renormalized metric functional obeys the chain rule

    W_ADM''[D,G]=W_g''[Dg,Gg]+W_g'[D_G g].

For example, an ADM shift-shift Hamiltonian contact can vanish while the four-metric g00 chart term is nonzero. Lapse-lapse has the analogous N^2 contact. Zero ADM contact is not a license to delete a covariant stress contact or its tadpole term.

The actual Gaussian one-point stress is not set to zero in this formula. The fixed scalar retuning in the full parent is a separate action term and must itself be varied when eventually forming the complete coupled equations. This checkpoint does not replace the nonlinear metric map by its first derivative alone.

Literal expansion of the full ADM metric and an independent quadratic four-metric functional verify both first and mixed second chart identities, including a nonzero one-point derivative. They supplement the elementary functional chain-rule proof; the toy functional is only a chart test, not a substituted quantum action.
