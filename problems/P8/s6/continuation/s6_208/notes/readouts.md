# Full normalized physical fields and genuine complex frame norms

For the inner time radius R = 1/20000, Cauchy from the outer disc gives |What_time| <= 3/R = 60000. Since Re What > 1/2, the normalized F has modulus at most one. The complete canonical amplitude obeys

|Pi| <= 3+(epsilon/m)(60000+5) < 4,

for either analytic Schwarz sign.

For lscaled = -n+xP, let rho_l = sqrt(lscaled.lscaled), n0 = -n and n_l = lscaled/rho_l. With eg = 2epsilon+epsilon^2,

|rho_l| >= sqrt(1-eg),
|rho_l-1| <= eg/(1+sqrt(1-eg)).

It follows that ||n_l-n0|| < 1/40 in the genuine complex Euclidean norm. The exact bilinear rotation Rrot = I+K+K^2/(1+n_l.n0), K = n_l n0^T-n0 n_l^T, differs from I by at most 2eta+4eta^2/(2-eta) < 1. Thus all continued polarization vectors have Euclidean norm below two. Bilinear unit norm alone would not prove this.

The transverse field norms satisfy

||E|| < 8, ||B|| < 4, ||mxA|| < 0.02.

For the magnetic estimate use ||C_v|| <= sqrt(2)||v||, |a^-1| < 1.01 and the complete continued frame. The longitudinal fields satisfy

||E|| < 1, |mA0| < 8, ||mAsp|| < 4.

The temporal multiplier |rho_l/(a Omega)| is below two; the longitudinal constraint has not been removed. Both full ten-field norms are below 20. The literal sixteen-component stress-pair bound is then 2*20^2 = 800 < 1000.

These improved bounds apply only to the normalized analytic domain. The low band and the complementary real-momentum estimate continue to use their independently established unscaled bounds.
