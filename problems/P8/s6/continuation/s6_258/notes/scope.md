## What the literature review does and does not import

Bufalo, Oksanen and Tureanu, [How unimodular gravity theories differ
from general relativity at quantum level](https://arxiv.org/abs/1505.04978),
PDF pages 16-17, equations 3.72, 3.77 and 3.80-3.83, distinguish a
conformal Dirac spatial density from the harmonic density in canonical
gauge fixing. This motivates checking the weight and full ghost
variation directly. Their time-gauge, gravitational action, unimodular
constraints and path-integral determinant are not transferred to the
present source-dependent parent. The entire operator here is rederived
from this parent's spatial generator.

Andersson and Moncrief, [Elliptic-hyperbolic systems and the Einstein
equations](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0110111), version 3, define their
spatial-harmonic condition near the start; Lemma 5.2 on PDF pages 27-28
uses negative curvature in its spatial gauge isomorphism argument.
That is not a theorem for this flat reference or this matter/clock
system. The present flat-torus argument instead isolates translations,
uses the displayed exact Fourier inverse and proves a small-shape
weak-coercivity estimate. Neither their CMC time gauge nor their
physical evolution theorem is asserted here.

The relevant original PDF pages were rendered and inspected during
research. These sources guide distinctions, not substitute assumptions
for the explicit current-parent calculation.
