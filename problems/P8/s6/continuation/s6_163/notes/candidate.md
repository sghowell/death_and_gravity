# The named EFT variation and the simple crossing it avoids

Original GY14 has two active opposite real Yukawas with
masses mF+y Phi and mF-y Phi. There are twelve additional
Yukawa-inert fundamental Dirac flavors. The active
color/flavor multiplicity is six; there are fourteen
flavors in total, not six active flavors.

Straight extrapolation of the original linear mass
functions to arbitrary real Phi reaches a zero at
Phi=+/-mF/y. This observation alone does not prove that
such a scalar value lies in an already matched domain.
It does show why the vacuum mass cannot be assumed
uniformly nonzero on an unrestricted continuation.

GY14-SAT8 replaces the two mass functions by

    mF +/- y f_R(Phi),
    f_R(Phi)=Phi/(1+(Phi/R)^8)^(1/8), R=10^300.

The scalar potential and full heavy H kernel, gauge
representations/interactions, inert masses and fixed
lower-field reference values are unchanged. Real
Phi parity remains exchange of the two active flavors.

This is local in the original canonical fields and adds
no new field or kinetic derivative. Its nonpolynomial
Yukawa interaction is a named EFT variation. It is not
the original globally polynomial renormalizable theory;
the old marginal flow does not by itself establish the
full new theory's high-energy behavior.

original statements. The new item is appended to the
matching ledger rather than used to close original B.
