# What the new branch does and does not establish

GY14-SAT8 avoids a simple unrestricted real mass-zero
crossing while preserving the named lower-field
through-two-loop vacuum coefficients and the entire
zero-fermion classical full-target match.

The new branch does not close original GY14, the
analytic target's quantum matching, physical
truncation, global V contours/cuts, finite-gravity G
or common-parent B. A local mass floor is not a
uniform spectral or state theorem on a changing
background. The conditional diagnostic clock is
not a supplied rolling coordinate dictionary.

Higher-point functions differ already at one loop;
later lower-point coefficients can differ as well.
The new action is nonpolynomial and its full
high-energy behavior is not inherited from the
old marginal-running screen. It is not declared
globally renormalizable or UV-complete.

The naive flat linear-clock cubic-map screen is a
failure of that small-derivative extrapolation,
not an exclusion of SAT8, other matching choices
or any original operator row.

All previous primitive owners and matching rows
remain unchanged. A single named-candidate item
is appended. Scientific source/report files are
frozen before native and independent replay.

Written analytic estimates and exact/numerical
tests are not formalization or independent peer
review. The adopted finite-EFT and necessary-
positivity contract is unchanged. Scoped P8(a)
and A.20-A.23 are unchanged; original P8(b)
and original P8 remain OPEN.
