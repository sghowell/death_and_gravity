# GY14-SAT8: protected masses and named two-loop invariance

Define a separately named EFT branch, not a rewrite of original
GY14. Replace only its two active opposite Yukawa arguments by

    f_R(Phi)=Phi/(1+(Phi/R)^8)^(1/8), R=10^300,

with the positive real eighth root. Keep the scalar/gauge
sector, twelve inert flavors and the named lower-field
regulated reference prescription unchanged.

Both active real pointwise masses stay strictly between
0.99mF and 1.01mF for every real argument. The first new
interaction has nine scalar and two fermion legs. Connected
loop grading, including counterterm forests, preserves all
named physical Phi pole/four-point, vacuum/H and low-energy
cut coefficients through two loops. The classical zero-
fermion full-target match is unchanged identically.

This does not identify the whole generating functional or
all higher loop orders. It does not transfer the original
global marginal-running argument to a nonpolynomial EFT.

Conditional mass-derivative bounds distinguish a direct
linear argument from the extrapolated literal cubic map.
The latter already fails the small-derivative mass screen
on a flat linear-clock test at zero. Neither this diagnostic
nor a pointwise mass floor establishes a transported bounce
state, physical remainder, common-parent B or P8 closure.
