# What this calculation does and does not close

The exact same reference Hamiltonian, physical force map,
nonzero M1 charge, conditional Gaussian state, full
massive response and fixed renormalization prescription
are used. The new scalar coefficient belongs to the
separately named S6.182 action. Its profile and the
Gaussian response remain fixed under variation.

The numerical norms apply only to the finite homogeneous
slab I and the stated physical normalization. They do
not imply global-time stability, arbitrary initial-state
or spatial response, small noise, a nonlinear quantum
background, controlled heavy thresholds or a physical
cutoff. Mathematical all-momentum conditional response
is not an all-frequency claim of EFT validity.

The small formal coefficient is useful perturbative
information but cannot be promoted to a finite-coupling
truncation error. Quantum derivative loss prevents
iterating the displayed norm as a C0 contraction.
Such an error estimate still requires sufficient higher
response bounds and an appropriate closed function
class, or an independently justified matched cutoff/
UV prescription and its error control. No causal poles
are removed and no all-alpha branch is assumed.

The full interacting affine/light/tensor/mixed sectors,
state/measure/loops, omitted-order matching, complete
vacuum cuts/contour/truncation and finite-gravity IR/Regge
remain original V/G/B research obligations. A failed
ansatz is not a whole-row exclusion. Scoped P8(a) and
A.20-A.23 stay unchanged; original P8 stays OPEN.
