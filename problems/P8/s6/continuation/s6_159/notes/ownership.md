# Complete stationary-H one-point ownership

The bare Gaussian equation at H=Phi=0 fixes

    J_B+G_B <Phi_B^2>/2=0.

In canonical coefficient language the order-two one-point
function is G/2 times the first corrected light tadpole
plus deltaG1/2 times the free tadpole. The latter is the
proper total cubic MS coefficient deltaG1=LG/(2Q epsilon);
it is not contained in the covariance insertion.

The first light covariance correction is the sum of
the scalar and active-fermion OS corrections. The
complete fermion tadpole and its G/2 source factor
are inherited from S6.148. The scalar covariance has
no local L remainder after physical OS subtraction.
There is no one-loop pure-gauge Phi inverse: Phi is
a singlet. A direct gauge-fermion vacuum row has no
H dependence at this order.

The heavy-MS-mass determinant reference is independent
of H at Phi=0, as are the proper fermion MS counterterm
vacuum traces. Their H derivatives vanish; the latter
are already assigned to S6.149, not missing source
graphs. The H-reducible occurrences cancel by the
same exact stationary Gaussian identity used in S6.158.

Second Phi mass, field or cubic counterterms placed
inside a one-loop source graph first contribute at
order three. A source has no tree constant J0, so a
second coordinate variation supplies no order-two
tree-source term. The only new coordinate contribution
at this order is the first full regulated G shift
inside J1,D, as shown in matching.md.

These observations exhaust the one-point occurrences
at the stated order. They do not redefine the heavy
field normalization or create an additional source
choice. The physical H expectation remains fixed at
zero after the source counterterm is included.
