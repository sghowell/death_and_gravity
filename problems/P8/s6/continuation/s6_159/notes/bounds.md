# Complete actual-parameter source enclosure

Take exact rational caps mF<=U^8 and 3M<=B^16 with
U=10^25, B=10^13 and Q_lower=144 at the named boundary.
For the scalar OS tadpole and proper cubic insertion,
the four source allowances are

    differentiated sunset: 7000 Gg U^2 B^3/Q_lower^2,
    mixed B0 I2:           2048 Gg U^2 B/Q_lower^2,
    correlated r T:        70 Gg U^2/(3Q_lower^2),
    proper cubic MS:       280 L G U/Q_lower^2.

The sunset uses the factor -g/2 for the two light
lines and then -G/2 for the fixed source. For the
other terms, the full regulator-circle bounds are
|I2|<64U/Q, |B0|<64UB/Q, |r_D|<(2/3)gU/Q and
|T_D(1)|<70U/Q. The explicit proper cubic pole
supplies 1/|epsilon|=16. No separately truncated
finite numerator is substituted.

The four allowances sum to approximately
6.14049277817371e76, below 1e80. The finite first
Phi-coordinate source map has allowance

    G[(ell_upper+1)|k0|_upper+|k1|_upper]/(2Q_lower),

approximately 1.64161230192542e-210, below 1e-209.
The already complete S6.148 fermion source dominates
the total:

    |J2| upper       4.77877709968590e188 < 1e189,
    |J2|/M upper     2.44673387503918e-9 < 1e-8,
    G|J2|/M upper    2.98673568730369e-13 < 1e-12.

All comparisons are exact rational inequalities.
The displayed decimals are orientation only.
The last two quantities are reference allowances,
not a prediction that physical H or the physical
light mass changes after its fixed condition is
imposed.

The complete two-loop vacuum reference from S6.158
is unchanged: J2 cannot contribute a vacuum constant
at order two. No finite part of a higher-order source
square is inferred by squaring these finite bounds;
at such orders its regulated pole products would
need their own calculation.
