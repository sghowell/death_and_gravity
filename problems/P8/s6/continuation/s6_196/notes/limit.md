# Why this does not settle the coincident retarded response

The finite-band current is not bounded uniformly in K by
S195's raw absolute majorants. Here smooth time integration is
performed first; S186's oscillatory pair estimate and a new
both-leg tail bound then remove K. This works because strict
temporal order eliminates the retarded step on the actual
product of test supports.

For overlapping supports that step is not constant. A bounded
full commutator bilinear cannot just replace a time-ordered
triangle. Already for a single unit-frequency oscillator in its
vacuum, T=q^2 has i<[T(t),T(s)]>/4=sin(2(t-s))/4.
Pair this with the same nonnegative smooth bump in0<t<1/4.
The full square integral is zero by antisymmetry. The retarded
triangle integral is strictly positive because 0<2(t-s)<pi.
The independent numerical fixture supplements this sign proof.

Hence the present argument is not a prescription for multiplying
a singular continuum stress distribution by theta at coincidence.
The fixed spatial retarded extension, its local matching and
a quantitative response norm on overlapping supports remain
required. Neither a derivative-loss test norm nor a small
canonical coefficient is a same-space inverse estimate.
A finite-amplitude remainder, coupled constraints and background
stability are separate missing parts, not removed poles.
