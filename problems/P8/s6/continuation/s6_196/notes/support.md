# Strict order, contacts and causal support

Require smooth compact spatial tracefree Gamma and D with
strictly ordered support intervals inside (-1/2,1/2).
The source is zero near the unchanged initial Cauchy surface.
On this product of supports theta(t-s)=1 identically.
Thus S195's actual finite-regulator retarded commutator reduces
to the full smeared commutator before taking the continuum limit.

The full exponential metric contact contains the pointwise
product of Gamma and D and is zero here. Every jet of Gamma
also vanishes on a neighborhood of supp D, so the response of
each unchanged finite local curvature term pairs to zero.
No new subtraction constant or metric-dependent state is chosen.

For locality retain the complete massive vector Green operator.
In de Rham conventions P=delta d+m^2, Q=I+d delta/m^2 satisfy

    PQ=QP=delta d+d delta+m^2.

The normally hyperbolic one-form Green operators composed with
the local differential Q give the Proca Green operators.
Differentiation preserves causal support. Consequently all local
Proca fields and their Wick stress commute on mutually spacelike
supports. The matrix fixture checks only this operator algebra;
the causal support statement uses the globally hyperbolic Green
construction, not a conclusion from finite matrices.

On the actual clock a>=1, any causal curve obeys
|dx/dt|<=1/a<=1. If spatial centers have a verified separation
lower bound d, the support radii sum is r, and T bounds every
time difference, d-r>T suffices for mutual spacelike separation.
The full response then vanishes. A finite momentum projection is
spatially nonlocal and need not itself satisfy this zero.

The Proca stress generator is also discussed in Benini,
[Relative Cauchy Evolution for Spin 1 Fields](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1111.6471),
Theorem3.2.6. Its statement is a weak quadratic-form identity
for represented Weyl observables in a quasifree Hadamard
representation. It is not a stress operator-norm or finite-amplitude
differentiability theorem; our stress-stress weak limit is
established by the explicit two-particle estimates above.
