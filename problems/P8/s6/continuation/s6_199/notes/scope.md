# Exact scope of the flat tensor benchmark

The object is the same canonical mass1000 Proca Gaussian sector,
examined in a flat reference vacuum as a matching benchmark. This is
not replacement of the actual CD state by flat initial data, a change
of the common prepared state, or a reset of the covariant prescription.

S6.198 split the actual spatial current into the controlled state
remainder, a finite sixth-order bulk plus last endpoint, and the
unresolved full reference contact plus first five equal-time kernels.
This continuation computes a flat full-tensor cut that any successful
flat limit of that matching must reproduce. It does not prove that
matching on the curved CD slab. Time-local kernels can be spatially
nonpolynomial, and the contact's one-leg and memory's two-leg regulators
cannot be silently interchanged.

All nine primitive V/G/B rows and every previous matching row retain
their exact status. One new scoped benchmark row is appended. Tests
reject promoted or missing rows and changes of the parent mass,
normalization and compact slab; no row exclusion follows from failure
of an ansatz.

The local finite polynomial, full curved spatial/mixed response and
inverse, finite-coupling background and stability, remaining parent
state/measure/loops, threshold/heavy/cutoff and omitted-order matching,
vacuum contour/cuts/truncation and finite-gravity IR/Regge requirements
remain open. Formal coefficients, spectral positivity and a small
external-metric factor are not substitutes for these estimates.
Scoped P8(a) and A.20-A.23 are unchanged. Original P8 is OPEN.
