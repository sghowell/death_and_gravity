# Fixed preparation, CTP endpoints and the full original source

The S251 light/tensor, S55/S176 Proca and S240 heavy preparations
remain fixed. They are not re-minimized after a gauge change,
coefficient probe, chart change or auxiliary reduction. The
entire current source, both stress profiles, matching terms,
canonical primitive and physical boundaries stay in place.

On a finite CTP interval the initial density is a kernel in the
two initial configurations, not just a covariance number. A
canonical coordinate change transports this kernel with the
same endpoint phases, Jacobian convention and operator ordering.
The two branches have opposite action orientations and the
appropriate final sewing; a gauge change must preserve or
transport the sewing too.

There are two distinct endpoint situations.

1. If the gauge variation and all required ghost boundary jets
   vanish in neighborhoods of both temporal endpoints, it acts
   trivially on the fixed initial density and endpoint phases.
   This can remove their contribution to the INTERIOR Ward
   variation without postulating an entirely new state.
   It does not prove measure/regulator invariance or construct
   the nonlinear interacting evolution.
2. A gauge transformation acting at an endpoint must transport
   the state and boundary data, or prove the corresponding
   invariance. Holding an incompatible coordinate kernel fixed
   is a state change and produces a Ward defect. A Gaussian
   defined on the linear physical reference modes is not by
   itself a full nonlinear BRST-state construction.

Spatial surface fluxes, residual translations and the finite
torus infrared prescription are also explicit. Local slice
regularity does not provide a global group quotient.

## Auxiliary sources survive the reduction

The original source couples to the physical variables before
their nonlinear map and the regular auxiliary equations are
used. If F(q,a) is that map, the source is J.F(q,a), not a
new linear J.q. The source changes the auxiliary equations
and their Hessian. Its resulting contacts must be differentiated
along with the retained source.

Specifically, the S259 complement integration retains

J.Gamma_bar - (1/(2 lambda)) J^T K A^-1 K^T J,

where K here includes the original full connection embedding.
This K is a complement map, NOT the Nielsen vector. The entire
coefficient-dependent inverse, cell scale and nonlinear center
are retained. Projective invariance removes only the explicit
compensating projective shift from the source pairing.

At zero source the connection mean is the expectation of the
composite center, not that center evaluated at the mean of
the retained fields. Its second source-functional contact is
also not an additional propagating oscillator. The conditional
Ward identity operates on this whole source functional and
cannot be used to normalize these terms away.

The actual P8 interacting state, Nielsen vector, physical
subtraction, omitted-loop estimate and fixed mean are not
computed here. In particular the S254-S256 nonreference
classical principal obstruction is not removed by a formal
gauge identity, and finite reference perturbation theory is
not an unrestricted off-reference quantum construction.
