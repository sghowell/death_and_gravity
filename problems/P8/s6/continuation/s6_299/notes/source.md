For positive eikonal denominators x_i=p.q_i, the sum over ordered
attachments is product_i(1/x_i). Induct on N by grouping on the last
partial sum: each permutation has the same final denominator sum_i x_i;

Summing attachments to different legs then factors the leading soft
current. This argument identifies the leading coefficient at each N.
It does not bound the all-N remainder of the full amplitudes.
