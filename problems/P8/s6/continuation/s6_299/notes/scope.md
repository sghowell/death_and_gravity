# Scope and non-closure

Established: all-N leading eikonal combinatorics, exact total-energy
simplex, a positive whole-sum regulator limit, the S296 finite angular
normalization, the entire Gamma-function inequality, original uniform
conversion error and an explicit noncommuting-limit check.

Not established: uniform full-amplitude soft remainder for allN,
complete gravitational Born recoil, all finite multi-soft/hard loops,
a globally analytic unitary amplitude for the physical inclusive rate,
high-energy complex-energy Regge/remainder matching, original quantum
state/domain/measure, bounce matching or V/G/B/P8 closure.

A positive auxiliary Poisson measure is not by itself a proof of
scattering unitarity or crossing for the complete gravitational theory.
The normalized rate need not inherit amplitude analyticity. Likewise
the absence of leading radiation at zero scattering angle does not
remove the Coulomb pole, elastic singularity or amplitude phase.

No frozen ancestor is modified, no primitive or original closure status
is upgraded, and no external numerical bound is borrowed from a
massless or equal-mass model.
