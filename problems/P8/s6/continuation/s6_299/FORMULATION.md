Hold the four massive scalar hard momenta fixed. Use D=4+2e with e>0,
the S296 complete conserved D-dimensional current, all ordered pairs,
moving diagonal terms, and its phase normalization. For each N retain
the leading product of soft factors, sum over physical polarizations,
divide by N!, and integrate with total COM energy at most E.

The reference scale is nu, with0<E<=nu. The analytic virtual rate factor
is exp[-a/(2e)], where a=K0/(4pi^2*kappa) and K0=-2ReBsoft on the
frozen physical sheet. Finite matching is not chosen or absorbed silently.
Take e->0 FIRST at fixed E,nu,kappa. A subsequent correlated
E/nu=exp(-kappa*chi) limit is a separate calculation.

This is an exact sum of the defined leading-soft approximation, not an
exchange of that approximation with the full infinite-multiplicity
physical rate. No non-leading radiation, gravitational Born recoil,
hard-loop, complex-energy Regge or original bounce/state gate is closed.

scalar energy[5/4,2],nu1,E<=1/8,kappa10^800 domain. The general Gamma
inequality itself holds for every nonnegative a.
