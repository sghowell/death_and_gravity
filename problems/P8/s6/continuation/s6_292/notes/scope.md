# What this successor establishes and leaves open

Established at the stated perturbative and graph scope:

- Complete minimal proper H Phi Phi graph ownership, including every
  pair numerator, metric-cubic contact and external minimal-GR factor.
- Exact D-dimensional master reduction with UV and IR origins kept
  separate; no replacement of a raw combined pole by an IR pole.
- Full Feynman normal-sheet light-root moments and their convergent
  first derivatives, plus unexpanded positive heavy-light moments.
- Exact real soft-pole cancellation between the whole Hh production
  cut and the known formal heavy delta coefficient.
- A complete known finite compact-window pairing, loop-scale and
  mass-unit consistency, and an explicit conditional O(e) remainder.

Not established:

- Full H Phi Phi matching, non-1PI metric response, higher-EFT or
  finite local/curved coefficients outside the selected graph sum.
- A stable heavy asymptotic state, a width prescription or a uniform
  approximation arbitrarily near the physical resonance.
- Cancellation of the imaginary Coulomb principal value or completion
  of every cut and all-angle physical infrared contribution.
- A positive inclusive/dressed physical spectral measure or a
  complete full-source b20 coefficient/sign.
- Fixed-negative-transfer Regge/contour remainder control, exchanges
  of infrared/forward limits, all-loop or omitted-operator bounds.
- The original state/domain/canonical-map and bounce requirements.

The finite threshold functional is a formal named combination. Its
remainder assumes a fixed C1 weight and compact massive domain; it
does not quantify a physical detector resolution or a limit with
shrinking mass gap. No leading-pole or finite-subset result is promoted
to physical S-matrix, positivity or P8 closure. Original V/G/B/P8
remains OPEN. Scoped P8(a) and all historical qualifications remain.
