## Original forest and eight signed algebraic contributions

The literal3767 hard-core tree excludes only nonsingleton pure-soft
graviton currents. Removing its four external scalar chains leaves
a central source H_R with no internal Phi propagator and no pure-soft
graviton branch. It retains every contact, heavy and Einstein vertex.
For a partition of the three labeled leaves among four external
chains S_l and a central set R, the term is
 H_R(p_l+Q_(S_l); q_R) * product_l j_(S_l)(p_l).
All shifted momenta, including the amputated root, conserve momentum.

An independent original-parameter125-assignment calculation equals
the direct3767 evaluator. Central counts at r=0,1,2,3 are7,19,67,307;
the graph totals by central-leaf count are1288,1368,804,307.
The line counts are1,1,3,13 for multiplicities0..3.

Strip the canonical power in each normalized scalar-line current:
f_S=kappa^(|S|/2)*product_(i in S)wi*j_S, S_i=f_i,
t_ij=f_ij-S_i*S_j and
U_123=f_123-sum_pairs(f_ij*S_k)+2*S_1*S_2*S_3.
Thus f_123=product S_i+sum_pairs(t_ij*S_k)+U_123.

Split t_ij=L_ij+R_ij as below. The full forest then has eight
EXACT algebraic contributions, with canonical factor kappa^(-3/2):
1. three central leaves;
2. two central leaves, one singleton chain;
3. one central leaf, one connected pair chain;
4. one central leaf, two independent singleton factors;
5. no central leaf, a connected triple chain;
6. no central leaf, a pair remainder and singleton;
7. no central leaf, a pair leading term and singleton;
8. no central leaf, three independent singleton factors.
This is not a disjoint partition into positive graphs: connected
currents include explicit signed lower products. No term is dropped.

## Independent count oracle and source equality

The scalar-chain exponential generating function is
L(x)=1/(2-exp(x)), with counts(1,1,3,13). The central function is
C(x)=exp(x)+6*exp(2x)/(2-exp(x)), with counts(7,19,67,307).
The third derivative of C(x)*L(x)^4 at zero is3767. The count oracle
is separate from the exact original amplitude equality.

The production evaluator reconstructs each of the125 assignments,
its shifted central source and scalar chains, and checks the original
3767 amplitude. It then checks each central-leaf group and the sum of
all eight signed contributions. All eight fixture contributions are
nonzero; no zero fixture is used to conceal an omitted class.

The generic cumulant relations are identities in arbitrary line
currents and hard-kernel values for every assignment. Numerical
calibration does not justify deleting their shifts or the root.
The implementation also checks all12 pair-leading parity identities.
