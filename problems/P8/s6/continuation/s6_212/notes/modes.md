# Actual dimensional modes, complete source derivatives and analytic pairing

The transverse and longitudinal eigenvalues of the continued kinetic matrix are a^(2-d) and a^(2-d) omega^2/m^2. Their canonical mode dilutions are

    b_T=(d-2)H/2,
    b_L=[(d-2)/2+z]H.

In Obar=a*x*omega and Wbar=a*x*W variables, the normalized momentum is pbar=-i Wbar-x a b, where the transverse normalized b contains a*Wbar_time/(2Wbar)+(d-3)aH/2, and the longitudinal one adds a*z*H. Dropping the(d-3) term before differentiation would lose finite data.

Use the existing general-dimensional clock-matching WKB coefficients. Through inverse-radius degree4 only P1(t,1), its first z derivative and P2(t,1) enter. The exact leading values are

    P1_T(t,1)=-(d-3)H'/4-(d-3)(d-1)H^2/8,
    P1_L(t,1)=-(d-1)H'/4-(d^2-1)H^2/8.

The five complete scalar amplitudes, genuine dimensional magnetic matrix, four distinct inverse phases, all endpoint labels0..4 and all source derivatives are retained. Write each amplitude as Abar/a, keep the detector Abar_sharp(t) fixed, and apply partial_s[a(s)gbar(s)*(...)] only to the source. Coincidence follows differentiation. The exact finite series may discard degrees above those needed by the next endpoint because time differentiation does not lower inverse-radius order.

Schwarz pairing is imposed first for real d; the resulting coefficient expressions are then analytically continued. At complex d one continues both opposite-phase branches without conjugating d. It is not a literal Hermitian conjugation in a noninteger-dimensional physical state space.

The independent mode tests construct the full general-dimensional recurrence through all four WKB orders and check every normalized endpoint/source coefficient at dimensions2.9,3.1,4, multiple clocks and widely separated transfers. Each of those four coefficients separately reduces to the unchanged physical W8 coefficient atd3.
