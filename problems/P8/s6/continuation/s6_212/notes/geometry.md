# Genuine general-dimensional field-strength vertex and sphere geometry

The canonical kinetic matrix is K=a^(2-d)[I+kk^t/(a^2m^2)]. The full mass and field-strength terms, rather than a three-dimensional magnetic dual, must be varied in d spatial dimensions.

For creation-pair momenta k,ell and tracefree D, direct contraction of the varied F_ij F_kl action gives the magnetic amplitude matrix

    Mmag=-(k.D.ell)I+ell k^t D+D ell k^t-(k.ell)D.

The full term proportional to the identity has a dimension-dependent trace. Atd3 the matrix equals Ck^t D Cell exactly for arbitrary momenta and tensors; that identity is a check, not the definition of the continued vertex.

To evaluate the complete normalized azimuthal contractions analytically, use an active four-component representation k=(X,Y,Z,u), ell=-k+y e4. Here X,Y are Cartesian transverse components and Z^2 collects the remaining d-3 transverse squared components. Full traces add d-4 copies of each passive scalar block. For tensor/vector channels the passive D block is zero, but the passive magnetic block need not be; for the scalar channel it is retained in every contraction.

The normalized transverse moments are

    <X^(2A)Y^(2B)Z^(2C)>
    =(1-u^2)^(A+B+C)
      (1/2)_A(1/2)_B((d-3)/2)_C/((d-1)/2)_(A+B+C).

This follows from the Gaussian/sphere moment formula with a grouped radial subspace. Odd X/Y moments vanish. Odd powers of the grouped radius are not signed Cartesian moments and are rejected. The remaining normalized sphere moment is<u^(2n)>=(1/2)_n/(d/2)_n.

The active/passive representation is an algebraic identity for full traces and vector contractions before the analytic dimension limit. Atd3 its negative passive count removes the auxiliary fourth direction. Literal field-strength matrices in dimensions3,4,5,6 independently reproduce all seven contractions in every tensor/vector/scalar channel; these integer checks do not define a fitted continuation.

Rotational covariance and parity leave the three bilinear invariants T,V,W. Normalized tensor,vector,scalar probes have(T,V,W)=(1,0,0),(1,1/2,0),(1,(d-1)/d,(d-1)/d). Their dimension-dependent basis must be accounted for when fixing the physical source.
