# Unchanged source, gauge and graph completeness

All S286 source identities and original parameters are retained by copy,
not by mutating a cached predecessor check dictionary. The original
R_full, F_full, retained heavy source, fixed vacuum constants, scalar
mass and potential couplings are not altered. No new curved finite
counterterm is selected.

## The precisely selected one-loop coefficient

The new sector has internal harmonic gravitons and canonical light Phi
lines, two external Phi and one external metric. The literal minimally
coupled scalar stress is
T_mn(p,r)=p_m r_n+p_n r_m-eta_mn(p.r-mu).
Its vertex is -i*T/sqrt(kappa). The ordinary gauge-fixing functional is
quadratic in h; thus no ordinary cubic gauge-fixing vertex is silently
added to the entire EH cubic.

A graph with one loop and two external scalar legs has one scalar path.
At this order the allowed minimal vertices are Phi^2 h^j and pure
gravitational vertices. Place the external h either on the scalar path,
on a scalar contact, or on a gravitational junction. The resulting
proper graphs are exactly:

- A: two internal h and one internal Phi, with the EH cubic.
- B: one internal h and two internal Phi, with the external h on Phi.
- C: two internal h, scalar Phi^2 h^2 seagull and EH cubic.
- D: one internal h and one Phi, with the external h and one external
  Phi at a seagull; retain both orientations.
- E: the internal-h tadpole at the complete Phi^2 h^3 contact.

A ghost loop with a single graviton attachment to the scalar path is
one-particle reducible in the metric channel, not this proper vertex.
Two attachments to that path would create a second loop. Thus no ghost
proper graph is deleted at this order. Metric self-energies, vacuum
tadpoles and counterterms remain separate four-point matching work.

The original curvature/derivative extras start at four Phi. They cannot
enter the selected one-internal-h-loop proper vertex without additional
loops or scalar legs. This does NOT say that every scalar-only
higher-arity tadpole vanishes: for example a Phi^4 R contraction can
supply a local curvature-improvement tensor. Such scalar-only curvature
matching is outside this selected internal-graviton sector and is not
declared fixed. S286 already distinguishes generated matter F1 from the
complete F2 and curved counterfunctional.

## Background comparison

Use the same linear metric coordinate with
g_total=g_bar+2gamma/sqrt(kappa). At flat background the propagators
coincide. Variation of the original matter and EH actions with respect
to a constant background metric gives exactly the same vertices as an
ordinary external h, with the common canonical normalization.

Choose background harmonic
F_nu=bar_g^ab(bar_nabla_a gamma_bn-bar_nabla_n gamma_ab/2),
with no background scalar term. At zero background momentum all
connection-variation terms proportional to that momentum vanish.
The remaining difference is the COMPLETE constant-metric variation of
-sqrt(-g)F_m g^mn F_n/2. Its volume, inverse metric and both F variations
are treated together in gauge.md. No background/ordinary identification
is assumed before that calculation.

The dimensional measure and scalar source are background covariant;
there is no bosonic diffeomorphism anomaly at this perturbative order.
Covariant local UV counterterms obey the same scalar Ward relation.
Their unspecified finite transverse additions do not supply a
zero-transfer F1 charge shift.
