# New candidate and remaining common-parent obligations

CD-REG-AFFINE-ISO is a new local classical EFT, not an edit or
automatic continuation of the older trace kinetic model.
It retains the full analytic S6.109 light action, physical M1
coupling and regular lower dictionary. It changes Xi and B
explicitly. This gives one action on a connected coefficient
domain with the specified vacuum, exact clock and local clock
constraint neighborhoods.

It does NOT transfer the GY14 scalar/gauge/fermion parent, its
source maps or finite references, any old retarded/Hadamard/QEI
construction, or the S6.171 free fermion tensor to this action.
Those results remain valid only for their original named objects.
In particular a classical source-alignment identity does not
bound the vector determinant, higher loops or background response.
The four-field source permits new interactions; they cannot be
ignored because the quadratic blocks decouple.

The completed scoped linear classification, scoped P8(a), and
A.20-A.23 gates are unchanged. Vacuum V contour/truncation,
finite-gravity G IR/Regge and the full quantum/cutoff/response B
obligations remain open. No global scattering history or new
naturalness prior is imposed.

## Focused primary-source review

Aoki--Shimada's projectively invariant metric-affine construction
is the primary comparison for the original auxiliary dictionary.
Its application here uses the repository's independently checked
coefficient/sign correction and unrestricted full quotient, not
a propagating-spectrum result imported from that paper.
[arXiv1806.02589v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1806.02589v2).

The June2026 two-field DHOST work analyzes a refined interacting
model using cubic-action and three-point-function estimates.
Its matter sector and model differ from the fixed M1 target.
Those estimates do not supply this candidate's physical cutoff,
UV parent, loop remainder or finite-gravity contour; no such
result is imported.
[arXiv2606.03302v1, sections2--3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.03302v1).

Barker--Marzo--Santoni's symmetry-first analysis restricts
parity-preserving models with totally symmetric distortion.
It is useful motivation to audit radiative constraints, but is
not a no-go for our unrestricted, scalar-dependent connection.
We neither adopt its no-further-tuning criterion as a new prior
nor claim our new mass tensor is protected by its symmetries.
[arXiv2505.23894v3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2505.23894v3).
