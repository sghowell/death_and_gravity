# All six local tadpoles off shell

The generic polynomial uses independent hard Gram entries and k.p_i,
with only sum p_i+k=0,k^2=0, epsilon.k=trace epsilon=0.
Do NOT impose p_i^2=1. For every one of the six operators, sum all720
labelings, retain exactD isotropic tensors, and add the source seagull
and massive loop graviton insertion. The entire expression equals
the TT variation of the full off-shell raw quartic contraction.

Only Gal and Y_Hdiff carry six external derivatives in that quartic
functional. Their full flat coefficients in the S345 basis are
(0,1/2,0,0,-1/2,0) and(0,-1,0,0,1,0). Their literal metric/connection
responses equal those lifts as whole generic polynomials, so both
extra curvature coefficients are zero. The exactD equality also
controls the finite F(4)-2Fprime(4) term, not just its pole.
No on-shell EOM reduction or earlyD4 substitution is used.
