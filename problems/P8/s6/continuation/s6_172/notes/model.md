# Named minimal diagnostic and physical-frame Einstein equation

This is the separately stated CD-MIN-EQ diagnostic, not a claim that
the full nongravitational GY14 reference already specifies a gravitational
completion. Take the physical metric Einstein term -kappa R/2, a
tree-canonical clock Phi=sqrt(kappa)t, any additional homogeneous
classical scalars with positive kinetic metric and healthy isotropic
Yang-Mills, and an arbitrary derivative-free potential. Add exactly the
S6.171 quadratic/reference curved free Dirac tensor in its fixed
EC-N0 prescription.

All other quantum, finite-reference, scalar/heavy/gauge/gravitational
terms and leading nonminimal or derivative operators are a separately
signed additional null contribution. No small bound on that complete
remainder is assumed to have been established. In particular, an
interacting physical field/Newton dictionary is not silently supplied
by its free-sector reference.

In the P8 (+---) curvature convention R=-6(Hdot+2H^2). For lapse N,
v=partial_t log a, the reduced Einstein density is
-3kappa a^3 v^2/N. Direct lapse/scale variation gives gravitational
Euler-source components -3kappa H^2 and kappa(2Hdot+3H^2).
Thus the matter equations require

    rho=3kappa H^2, P=-kappa(2Hdot+3H^2),
    rho+P=-2kappa Hdot.

A canonical scalar has rho+P=Phidot^2, independently of its potential.
A positive kinetic metric contributes a nonnegative quadratic form;
healthy isotropic Yang-Mills contributes2(E^2+B^2)/3>=0. Extra M1
canonical matter, when present, strengthens rather than repairs the
inequality. The unit clock alone contributes kappa.

The calculation evaluates the exact proposed CD trajectory and its
specified free states. It does not assume that the scalar or Einstein
equations are already solved: their incompatibility in this diagnostic
is the point of the test.
