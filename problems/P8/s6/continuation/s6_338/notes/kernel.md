# Exact-D massive two-endpoint kernel boundary

The explicit light and heavy triangles, their literal three-denominator
completion of the square, exact-D radial factors, endpoint integrations
and finite logarithm identity are derived in cross.md and triangles.py.
The two positive-mass propagators and original recoil domain give the
uniform Feynman-parameter gap at least1. This excludes a hidden
1/k^2 term in the actual null-graviton response.

A homogeneous symmetric two-endpoint rank-two response has the form
A pp+B(pk+kp)+C kk+D eta. At k^2=0, p.k nonzero, its homogeneous Ward
identity sets A=0 and B(p.k)+D=0. Its TT projection therefore vanishes.
This is a useful uniqueness check for THIS two-endpoint massive kernel;
the actual result is also computed from both loop insertions.

It is not a general claim that a four-point flat amplitude determines
five-point curvature matching. The S336 operator has four independent
hard directions and is not this two-endpoint kernel. Its formal
one-loop coefficient remains unspecified. Nor is the scalar-loop
argument applicable to an uncomputed massless internal-graviton loop.
