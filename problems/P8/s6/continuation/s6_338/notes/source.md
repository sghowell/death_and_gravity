## Quantity and exhaustive selected classes

Let the light mass be one, n=M_H^2, J2=g Phi^2/2 and
J4=c Phi^2 Y, c=-4g/kappa, Y=(nabla Phi)^2. At first loop order,
the cubic-plus-five-field contribution is the one-light-pair contraction
of J2 G_H J4. The pair lies within J2, within J4, or across the factors.
At one real external graviton include the metric variations of both
sources and every internal propagator, all four external emissions,
the full heavy-onepoint counterterm, the fixed pole-only counterterms,
and the linear portion of the already imposed symmetric four-point
value subtraction. No expansion in 1/n is made inside an integral.

These are selected known matter loops with one external graviton.
They do not include the old polynomial/heavy loop sector, loops with
internal gravitons, unknown curvature matching, or a complete curved
counterfunctional. The new local degree-six sector is independently
computed in frozen S337 and may be added by linearity.
