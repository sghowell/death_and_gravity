# Source and literature boundary

The full original source, mass spectrum, couplings, state/domain
qualifications and primitive/matching records are unchanged.
S300 fixes the actual radiative recoil and full state-specific soft
index. S296 fixes the dimensional phase and physical/analytic soft
convention. Both raw reports and their complete ancestry are checked.

The new argument is the explicit fixed-three-ball formula and uniform
collinear modulus in notes/angular.md and notes/bounds.md. It is not
an assumption that bounded angular convergence can be differentiated.

Akhoury, Saotome and Sterman discuss the Ward-identity cancellation of
collinear singularities and the perturbative soft structure at fixed
wide angles. Their conclusion explicitly does not give a quantitative
uniform forward/Regge bound for this problem:
https://arxiv.org/html/1109.0270v1 (sections III-V and appendix A).

Bini et al. show that dimensional-state choices and evanescent terms
affect finite one-loop radiation observables. Their classical waveform
is not this scalar observable; the relevant lesson is to state the
scheme and retain hard evanescent obligations:
https://arxiv.org/html/2402.06604v2 (section IV.3 and equations 74-79).

Neither source is used as a substitute for the present inequalities,
full finite hard matching or original P8 closure.
