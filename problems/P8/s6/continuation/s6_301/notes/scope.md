# Established and unestablished

Established within the stated radiative compact domain:

- A fixed-ball D-dimensional representation of the complete soft current.
- The retained radial and trace terms in its first dimensional derivative.
- An integrable collinear bound uniform in positive finite multiplicity.
- An explicit second-order regulator remainder and finite soft conversion.
- A continuous finite-energy-measure extension of those coefficients.
- Original-parameter bounds for the known unexpanded detector reference.

Not established: finite or evanescent hard radiative amplitudes,
all nonleading soft contributions, all-loop control, complete
physical unitarity/analyticity, uniform forward/Regge estimates,
or original state/domain/measure/bounce and V/G/B/P8 closure.

Positive physical D4 polarization norms do not imply that the interior
fixed-ball contraction is positive for a noninteger D. No such claim
is needed. Numerical calibrations do not certify quadrature errors.
All frozen scientific sources and reports remain untouched.
