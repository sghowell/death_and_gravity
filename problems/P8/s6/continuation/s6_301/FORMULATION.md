# Finite angular derivative for the same radiative state

Retain the original finite-kappa source and every S300 radiative state:
four mass-one scalars of incoming COM energy in [5/4,2], any finite
number of outgoing positive-energy null gravitons with total energy
R<=1/8, and all angles. Use the exact smooth S300 recoil map.

Continue only the universal additional-soft angular/projector factor
to D=4+2e with the inherited S296 phase convention, then evaluate at
physical four-dimensional external momenta. Prove its first derivative,
an explicit uniform e^2 remainder for e in [0,1/8], and the finite
same-state analytic-reference conversion. All null directions and
arbitrary collinear splitting are included without a multiplicity cutoff.

The continuous extension to finite angular-energy measures is a
kinematic coefficient construction, not an interacting quantum state.

At original parameters, compare only the known functions
exp(Delta_sigma)*F(a_sigma)*x^a_sigma with their elastic counterparts,
after correct same-state infrared pairing and with R<=x<=1/8, nu=1.
Keep x^a unexpanded and remove e at fixed nonzero resolution first.

Do not set finite or evanescent hard terms, infer a full physical rate,
bound every nonleading soft contribution, exchange forward or Regge
limits, or promote this statement to V/G/B/P8 closure.
