# Whole source and loop convention

S282 and its S281/S280 ancestry pin the whole current R,F, heavy source,
all three vacuum constants, formal loop marker and physical frame.
The exact whole source jets exclude additional lower-arity vacuum
gravitational couplings. This successor copies their check dictionary
without mutation and identifies the complete invariant pure-gravity tree
with S281's gravity sector, keeping the external mass nonzero.

With g=eta+2h/sqrt(kappa), the linear scalar coupling is-h:T/sqrt(kappa).
For one incoming scalar p and outgoing scalar r, its rule is
-i*T/sqrt(kappa), where
T_mn=p_m r_n+p_n r_m-eta_mn(p.r-mu).
The harmonic propagator uses the full D-dimensional1/(D-2) trace.
The two-point graph is i*Sigma; Dyson resummation therefore gives inverse
p^2-mu+Sigma and residue Z=1/(1+Sigma') in this convention.

This is not a new choice of vacuum, mass, coupling or quantum state.
The pure-gravity sector is separated from heavy/contact and other-species
loops. In particular its zero on-shell mass shift is not a statement that
all contributions to the original interacting mass vanish.
