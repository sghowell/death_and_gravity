Am>4g^2/n^3, AG>8/(kappa*delta), both positive.
At n=1e200/512+2,kappa=1e800 each regular sector divided by
its own positive Born part is below1e-395/(ab). Positive Born
fractions combine them with no factor2:
 |M_regular|/(Am+AG)<1e-395/(ab).
S311 adds |M_pair|/(Am+AG)<9W/(sqrt(kappa)*ab),
whose coefficient is below1e-395. Thus
 |M_full434|/(Am+AG)<2e-395/(ab).
This applies to all physical emitted directions and all nonforward
hard Born directions; collinear endpoint limits are bounded without
asserting a unique exactly-collinear helicity value.
Only the full434 amplitude is the physical Ward-invariant tree.
Its two computational graph classes are not separately claimed
as gauge-invariant detector observables.
Squaring this envelope against a da b db has logarithmic soft
divergences. A tiny coefficient does not make it IR-finite.
