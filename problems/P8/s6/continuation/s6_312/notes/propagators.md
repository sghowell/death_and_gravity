# A common gap for every hard propagator
For each mixed cut assign its outgoing radiation to Qa and the
complement to Qb, with Qa+Qb=Q and both future causal (zero allowed).
A=p3+Qa, B=p4+Qb are two future clusters with A+B=k1+k2.
Write A2=1+u, B2=1+v, s=4E2, U=u+v, r=|Avec|=|Bvec|.
Since external outgoing massive energies<=2, their Doppler gap>1/4,
so U>=W/2 and U<=8W+W2<=65/64.
The exact Kallen identity is
 r0^2-r^2=U/2-(u-v)^2/(4s)>=147U/320>U/4>=W/8.
Here s>=25/4. As r0+r<4, r0-r>W/32.
The smaller cluster energy is at least sqrt(1+r^2), whence
 |A0-E|<=E-sqrt(1+r^2)<=sqrt(3)/2*(r0-r).
Consequently -(A-k1)^2>=|Avec-k1vec|^2/4>W^2/4096.

S300 gives |p3vec-p3Bornvec|<=3W. Adding Qa changes it by<=W.
Writing tau for the associated positive Born transfer, the spatial
difference is at least sqrt(tau)-4W. For W<=sqrt(tau)/8 the
mixed denominator has magnitude>=tau/16; otherwise tau<64W^2.
Both regimes therefore give the single conservative strict gap
 -K^2>(tau+W^2)/300000.
This uses the total radiation, including the complement, not merely
the energy assigned to this particular hard propagator.
It works for all four assignments of two real gravitons.
Timelike hard cuts have45/8<=K^2<=16. Heavy propagators obey
1/(n-K^2)<2/n, n>=128. Internal light-scalar cuts are one
external scalar plus a nonempty radiation subset (or its complement);
their denominators have magnitude>=3w_subset/8.
