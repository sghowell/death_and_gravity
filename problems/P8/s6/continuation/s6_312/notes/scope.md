Established: a whole434 bare-tree upper bound at the original
parameters, uniform in the stated emitted and nonforward hard angular
domain. It includes all contact, heavy-scalar, scalar seagull,
internal Einstein cubic and quartic contributions.

Not established: an overlap-subtracted two-real remainder, real/virtual
IR cancellation, an integrated finite detector correction, all-N
radiative errors, hard loops/finite matching, a UV-complete amplitude,
absolute complex Regge/IR-contour control, or the common-parent
quantum state and bounce. Original V/G/B/P8 remain OPEN.

computation. Neither is claimed as a separately physical detector
observable. Their exact sum is the full Ward-invariant S310 tree.

needed to remove its leading soft terms before physical integration.
