# Canonical vertex bounds
For entrywise tensor bounds A,B,C and momenta with components<3:
density coefficients d0..d3 are bounded by1,4,48,960;
inverse metric coefficients by1,2,32,768; density times inverse
by1,6,96,2400. Thus each mass-one Phi2 h^r multilinear vertex is
bounded by(144 K_r+d_r)*product(fields)<1024^r*product(fields).
Heavy momenta have components<5, so H2 h^r <=n*1024^r
for r<=2,n>=128. Potential metric densities also obey1024^r.
For the original tuned contact,4/n-[3/(n-2)-2/(n-2)^2]
=(n-4)^2/[n*(n-2)^2]>0, proving |C|<4g^2/n.
The whole H path contributes at most8/n after its metric mass
numerators cancel all but one heavy denominator.

For general tensors and maximum momentum component L:
 Gamma1<=3LA/2, Gamma2<=6LAB, Gamma3<=24LABC;

The bilinear Gamma-Gamma contraction has at most512 terms for
general M and128 for eta. Including all ordered polarizations yields
 C3=138240, C4=10616832 multiplying L^2 times field bounds.
No TT assumption is made about internal or open-root tensors.
