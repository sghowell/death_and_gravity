## Ultraviolet and value conditions

The new prescription is H8A420-VAC-OS4. At mu1 retain d=4-2epsilon through tensor averaging and subtract only the Laurent pole of each **complete generated off-shell local contraction functional**. The local degree-six pole counterfunctional is the negative of its full one-pair contraction, before imposing external mass shells; its on-shell projection alone is not used to define the counterterm.

In addition to S235's old polynomial UV terms, the J4 tadpole requires +c Delta H(Y+Phi²)/(16pi²). The full mixed3+1 graph has off-shell pole 6cg Delta sum_i p_i²/(16pi²). The literal counterterm -3cg Delta Phi²Y/(16pi²), whose four-light vertex is twice its coefficient times sum_i p_i², cancels it. The full heavy source onepoint cancellation is retained independently. These statements fix the flat jet counterfunctional needed here, not a complete curved counterfunctional at all field orders.
