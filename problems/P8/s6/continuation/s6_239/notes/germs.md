For a connected graph with E external lines and L loops, sum_v(degree_v-2)=E+2L-2. At E4,L1 the only patterns (v3,v4,v5,v6) are (4,0,0,0), (2,1,0,0), (0,2,0,0), (1,0,1,0), (0,0,0,1). The first three are exactly the old polynomial calculation. The fourth is the cubic/five-field source graph; the fifth is one of the six literal degree-six tadpoles. A vertex of degree at least seven cannot enter.

At E2,L1 only a cubic pair or one quartic enters; at E1,L1 only a cubic tadpole enters. Thus the old first-order light on-shell and heavy-onepoint conditions receive no new higher-vertex contribution. Countervertices already of order hbar enter at tree order and are treated separately. This finite graph identity is exact at the stated order, not a statement about higher loops.
