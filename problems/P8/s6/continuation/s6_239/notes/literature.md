# Primary-source context

[Henning, Lu and Murayama, One-loop Matching and Running with Covariant Derivative Expansion](https://arxiv.org/abs/1604.01019) explains why mixed heavy/light loops remain after Gaussian heavy-field elimination. Here this is checked directly through the three J2 G_H J4 contraction classes and literal new higher jets; the paper is not taken as a theorem for this model.

[Denner, Techniques for calculation of electroweak radiative corrections at the one-loop level](https://arxiv.org/abs/0709.1075) provides the standard scalar-integral and dimensional-regularization context. Signs here are fixed by the inherited S234 iPi convention, the explicit coincident contraction I=-(Delta+1)/(16pi²), and independent numerator/parameter calculations. Setting d4 before subtracting a pole is not justified.

[de Rham, Melville, Tolley and Zhou, Massive Galileon Positivity Bounds](https://arxiv.org/abs/1702.08577) and [de Rham, Melville and Tolley, Improved Positivity Bounds and Massive Gravity](https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.09611) motivate tracking higher forward coefficients and low-energy errors rather than relying on one finite positivity test. They do not establish a physical UV completion for the new full action.

These references were inspected as primary context during the continuation. The actual new coefficients, exhaustive contractions, prescribed subtractions and bounds are derived in the adjacent notes and executable checks. No external theorem is used to interchange quantum and gravitational limits.
