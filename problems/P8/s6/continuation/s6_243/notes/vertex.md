# Entire scalar spatial Hamiltonian before UV extraction

Write the minimally coupled Hamiltonian in physical ADM variables,

H[N,beta,h]=N[pi²/sqrt(h)+sqrt(h)(h^ij partial_i Phi partial_j Phi+n Phi²)]/2
+beta^i pi partial_i Phi.

For h=a² exp Q, the canonically fixed first spatial direction has the complete five-feature matrix
M_Q=diag(-tr Q/2,(tr Q)I/2-Q,tr Q/2)
in F=(S',i k S/a,m S), m²=n. The physical current is -a^d F^t M_Q F/2. The matrix derivative retains the inverse-metric, volume, kinetic and mass terms. No tracefree restriction is used.

For k=r nvec, ellvec=P e-k and x=1/r, put ellbar=-nvec+Px e and u=nvec.e. The full annihilation mode comparison is a^(-d/2)(2W)^(-1/2) exp(-i theta), theta'=W. Set Wbar=a x W and Pbar=a x[-iW-dH/2-W'/(2W)]. Then the phase-stripped pair is

a^d V_Q=(r/a)[A tr Q+B nvec^t Q ellbar],

B=1/[2 sqrt(Wbar_k Wbar_l)],
A=B[-Pbar_k Pbar_l-nvec.ellbar+m²a²x²]/2.

This identity is checked from the literal five-feature matrix for all six independent entries of Q, before any asymptotic expansion. It includes the trace/time term and its imaginary part. In the leading flat limit A=B=1/2; the trace term is not optional.

The full second spatial Hamiltonian feature has kinetic and mass entries (tr D)(tr G)/4 and gradient block (B_D B_G+B_G B_D)/2, where B_D=(tr D)I/2-D. There is no external metric derivative. Its equal-point expectation is therefore independent of transfer P and cancels in the spatial P-minus0 difference. It is nonzero in the full response and must be retained in the homogeneous anchor.

For the complete local UV grades, write Wbar=O+a²x² P2/O+a^4 x^4 P4/O³ modulo x^5, with O²=1+m²a²x² for k and1-2Pux+(P²+m²a²)x² for ell. The FULL minimal-scalar Riccati coefficients obey

P2=-U/2-lambda'/4+lambda²/8,
U=dH'/2+d²H²/4, lambda=-H z,
z=1-m²a²x²/O².

Through the displayed radial degree, use P2(1)+P2_z(1)(z-1) and P4(1). This is exact coefficient extraction, not an approximation substituted for the full response. P2(1)=-(d-1)H'/4-(d²-1)H²/8. The derivative of the full normalized frequency gives
Pbar=-iWbar-a x[(Wbar'/Wbar)+(d-1)H]/2.
No longitudinal-vector rate or polarization multiplicity is borrowed.

S240's full six-iterate defect and exact variation-of-constants bounds show that exact comparison minus full W6 mode readouts are of strictly lower radial order than these UV grades. For fixed transfer, both large internal frequencies are comparable; each exact-to-W6 normalized mode error is O(r^-13), and its j<=5 derivatives are O(r^(j-13)). Pair-product errors are integrable at large r before the present finite extraction. This identifies the local high-frequency coefficients, but is not the missing all-transfer remainder bound or the dimensional dominated-limit proof.
