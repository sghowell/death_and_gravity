# Original source and two selected signed measures

Use the unchanged P8 action and parameters, mu=nu=1, kappa=10^800,
n=10^200/512+2 and g=1/8192. In the compact physical domain
5/4<=E<=2 and every nonforward hard angle, let A0=Am+AG>0 be the
associated Born amplitude. Let F_lambda=M5_lambda/A0 include all26
matter and21 Einstein diagrams with the physical recoil density rho.

Let S_lambda be the associated Born leading-soft factor, and let
h=H4_known/A0 be the fixed S303 known hard coefficient in its original
analytic reference, evaluated at that Born state. Its extension over
the radiation interval is DEFINED to be S_lambda*h; it is not identified
with the complete five-point one-loop amplitude.

The first signed density, with the D4 one-particle measure and both
physical polarizations, is
2Re[rho sum conj(F_lambda)S_lambda h]-2Re(h)sum|S_lambda|^2.
Its total variation is less than min(1e30,2e32*x)/kappa^2.
The second is the named S326 difference
rho sum(|F_lambda+L_lambda|^2-|F_lambda|^2), where
L_lambda=C_Born,lambda ln(1/w)/kappa^(3/2), |C_Born,lambda|<320.
Its total variation is less than
1e18*x*(1+ln(1/x))/kappa^2
+3000*x^2*(ln(1/x)^2+ln(1/x)+1/2)/kappa^3.

Both claims hold for0<x<=1/8, all emitted directions and both unit
physical TT polarizations, in the stated normalized nonforward domain.
They are independent selected contributions, not the full square of
F+S*h+L and not a complete real-virtual detector observable. The
logarithmic square is retained but not called one-loop perturbative order.
