# Why the phase cancels only in the stated interference

In a real unit TT basis the full tree F and Born soft factor S are real.
The original scalar and Einstein tree couplings and stripped vertices
are real. Physical timelike recoil and mixed-transfer gaps keep every
internal denominator away from zero for positive radiation energy
and nonforward hard angle; the original heavy pole lies above the
compact physical channel energies. There is therefore no absorptive
tree contribution on this domain. Endpoint singularities are treated
by limits, not by assigning a value at the hard forward pole.

For any unitary polarization change U,
sum conj((UF)_lambda)(US)_lambda=F^T S is real. Accordingly
2Re[rho sum conj(F_lambda)S_lambda h]-2Re(h)sum|S_lambda|^2
=I_known[rho sum Re(conj(F_lambda)S_lambda)-sum|S_lambda|^2].
The entire imaginary hard phase remains in h. Only this fully summed
linear interference is independent of it.

For an individual helicity Fh=(Fp+iFc)/sqrt2, Sh=(Sp+iSc)/sqrt2,
Im(conj(Fh)Sh)=(Fp Sc-Fc Sp)/2 can be nonzero. Actual original
47-diagram calibrations at two independent kinematic points show this
nonzero value. No per-helicity phase deletion is valid in general.
A real-part bound does not bound |h|, |h|^2, off-diagonal density
entries or the combined square |F+S*h+L|^2.
