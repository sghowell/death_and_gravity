# Independent checks and the proof boundary

The generic helicity algebra checks unitarity, the real summed
tree-soft inner product and a one-helicity imaginary counterexample.
Two original-parameter examples use the actual S295 whole26 matter
tensor plus S304 full21 Einstein amplitude and the full Am+AG Born
denominator. Their real plus/cross and complex helicity values are
preserved in the report, including nonzero individual-helicity
imaginary inner products. No reduced matter-only or gravity-only
example is substituted for these calibrations.

Exact arithmetic checks the low/high budgets, a change of integration
variable, elementary primitives, stationary point and inherited
original constants. Tests independently exercise rational unitary
frames, original-parameter API values, invalid inputs, elementary
integrals and numerical low/high integrated majorants at multiple
scales including narrow forward gaps and tiny resolution.

These finite tests do not prove angular uniformity or exhaust all
physical kinematics. The written physical denominator, TT reality,
unitary-basis and low/high integration arguments supply those steps.
The result is not proof-assistant kernel formalization.
