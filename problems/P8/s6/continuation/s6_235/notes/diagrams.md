# Complete one-light-loop four-point representation

The S234 exact heavy integration gives the full nonlocal quartic light action, not merely a local derivative truncation. Its light Hessian is K_phi+W[phi], with W quadratic in phi. The degree4 first-loop functional is -Tr(G_phi W G_phi W)/4, with the separately specified counterterms. It is one-LIGHT-particle irreducible and may be reducible through an internal heavy line.

Taking four labelled external derivatives groups the24 permutations into three pairings, with eight assignments each. Including the two Hessian1/2 factors and the trace-log1/4 gives the bubble coefficient1/2 for each pairing. An independent finite tensor calculation checks every distinct fourth-field component against this pairing formula.

For a channel s, the full nonlocal vertex at either end of the light bubble is

A_s+g²(G_1+G_2),
A_s=C+g²/(n-s),
G_i=1/[n-(loop+external_i)²].

Multiplying the two COMPLETE vertices before integration gives one A_s²/2 bubble term, four equal A_s g²/2 triangle terms, and four g4/2 box terms. The boxes form two equal pairs within this channel. The full result is

A1=(16pi²)^-1 sum_channels[
 A_s²B_MS(s)/2+2A_sg²Cbar(s)+g4(Dbar(s,t)+Dbar(s,u))].

The three channels produce SIX ordered boxes. Alternating light/heavy masses have two assignments for each of three external cyclic orderings. The first box variable has a light-pair cut, while the second has a heavy-pair cut. Interchanging them is not a symmetry. Even below cuts their derivatives differ: at s=t=0, the difference is proportional to integral z(1-z)(1-2z)/[(1-z)²+nz]^3, positive for n>1 after pairing z with1-z. Thus this distinction is not only a threshold label.

For the triangle group the Feynman parameters as x_light1=(1-z)xi, x_light2=(1-z)(1-xi), x_heavy=z give Jacobian1-z. For the alternating box, use light parameters(1-z)xi,(1-z)(1-xi) and heavy parameters z eta,z(1-eta). Its absolute Jacobian is z(1-z).

With all external virtualities e, the resulting denominators are

Q_C=(1-z)+nz-ez(1-z)-s(1-z)²xi(1-xi),
Q_D=Q_C-tz²eta(1-eta).

The Euclidean radial integral contributes Gamma(N-2)/(16pi²) after the Feynman parameter factor; for N3 and N4 this is exactly1/(16pi²). Each internal heavy denominator is1/(n-q²), so Cbar=-C0 in conventional Minkowski scalar-integral notation; Dbar=D0. Direct vertex/propagator signs and the first-cut check verify this convention.

Set e=1 for the light on-shell amplitude. The S234 finite quadratic conditions make its light pole residue1 at this order, and phi parity leaves no light cubic exchange to attach. The base finite four-field contact is zero. The one-point condition remains; its source counterterm is quadratic after eliminating H and gives no extra degree4 term at this order.

The parameter formulas are ordinary convergent integrals where all denominators are positive, including the symmetric point used below. Elsewhere they denote the Feynman analytic continuation with i0 kept through the integration. In particular Q^-2 across a zero is not interpreted as an ordinary positive integral. No uncontrolled expansion over arbitrarily large loop momentum is used.
