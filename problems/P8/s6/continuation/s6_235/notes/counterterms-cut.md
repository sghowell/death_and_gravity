# Complete UV pole cancellation and the independent first elastic cut

Only the channel bubble integrals have a UV pole. The triangle and box parameter integrals are UV finite. The complete divergent four-light amplitude is

A1_div=Delta/(32pi²) sum_channels[C+g²/(n-s)]².

It contains three kinds of terms, not only C². The separate-model MSbar counterterms are

deltaC=-3C²Delta/(32pi²),
deltag=-CgDelta/(32pi²),
deltan=g²Delta/(32pi²).

Their full tree insertion is

deltaC+sum_channels[2g deltag/(n-s)-g² deltan/(n-s)²].

Expansion shows exact cancellation of A1_div. The sign of the heavy mass insertion agrees with the S234 inverse convention. Omitting any of the three terms fails independent controls. These are not counterterms for the original affine parent. The S234 heavy one-point and light on-shell finite quadratic conditions are retained; at this order they do not add a direct four-field term.

For a forward configuration4<s<n, set beta=sqrt(1-4/s), k=(s-4)/2, a=n+k and L=ln[(a+k)/(a-k)]. The full scalar-integral cuts are

Im B_MS(s)=pi beta,
Im Cbar(s)=pi beta L/(2k),
Im Dbar(s,0)=pi beta/(a²-k²),
Im Dbar(s,4-s)=pi beta L/(2ak).

Only the first/light-channel argument s contributes here. A box with s in its second argument instead crosses the heavy pair, whose threshold4n is outside this window. The heavy simple pole at n is also outside the open interval.

An independent triangle derivation integrates the Feynman delta function. Between the two light roots in xi, let h=s xi(1-xi)-1>0. The unique z root is

zstar=2h/[2h+n+sqrt(n²+4nh)].

The delta-function integral for Im Cbar/pi is integral(1-zstar)/[n+2h(1-zstar)]d xi. It agrees with the displayed complete angular cut. The identity Dbar(s,0)=-partial_n Cbar(s) independently gives the first box cut; the opposite on-shell heavy angular denominators give the second.

Insert all four cuts in the COMPLETE one-loop formula. With A_s=C+g²/(n-s), the result is

Im A1(s,0)=beta/(64pi)[
 2A_s²+(4A_sg²/k+2g4/(ak))L+4g4/(a²-k²)].

This is exactly the S233 integral of the full rational tree squared, with its labelled-identical factor and infinitely many even angular channels. Numerical delta-function and angular integrations are independent diagnostics of the same normalization. No l0/l2-only replacement is made.

The threshold limits are removable: L/k tends to2/n as k tends to0 and both cuts carry beta. These are perturbative discontinuities. They neither construct an exact unitary S matrix nor control a resonance or omitted higher-loop cuts.
