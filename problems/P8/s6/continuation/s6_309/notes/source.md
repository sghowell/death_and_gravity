## External boundary check

Chakrabarti, Kashyap, Sahoo, Sen and Verma,
[Subleading soft theorem for multiple soft gravitons](https://arxiv.org/html/1707.06803v2), JHEP 12 (2017) 150,
eqs. (1.1)-(1.4), explicitly includes a two-soft contact contribution
besides products of one-soft factors. The introductory dimension
qualification restricts the result to tree amplitudes in D<=5; its
all-loop statement applies in D>=6. It treats simultaneous soft scaling.

Accordingly that theorem is not imported as a four-dimensional all-loop
remainder estimate, nor as uniform control of all unequal soft-energy
hierarchies. Our independently proved marked convolution supplies no
missing two-soft contact or multiple-residual bound. The primary source
is used to check this boundary, not as proof of a complete rate.
