Established: exactly one finite signed one-real residual, built from the
unchanged complete selected 47 trees, can be dressed with arbitrarily
many additional leading-soft emissions. Their same-state virtual pairing
and total-energy sum have a controlled analytic-regulator limit. The
resulting remaining-energy convolution has a uniformly tiny relative
bound against the unexpanded elastic soft reference, and that relative
bound vanishes as the detector threshold tends to zero.

Not established: equality of this reference to a complete all-N physical
rate, or a bound on their difference. The additional emissions are leading
soft with the marked state held fixed. Correlated exact recoil, simultaneous
two-soft contact terms, multiple finite residuals, finite radiative hard
loops, evanescent hard terms and physical matching require further work.
Their coefficients are neither chosen nor set to zero.

The signed-series argument does not identify fractional-dimensional
contractions with physical probabilities. Positive values of P0+D[R]
do not establish a probability measure, a monotone detector CDF or
unitarity. A leading-soft sum also does not remove the Coulomb amplitude
branch, prove a finite forward cross section or furnish a complex-energy
Regge remainder.

qualifications and the rejection of S279 are retained. The original action,
canonical parameters, state/bounce and quantum-construction obligations
remain. Original V/G/B/P8 stay OPEN. Scoped P8(a) is unchanged.
