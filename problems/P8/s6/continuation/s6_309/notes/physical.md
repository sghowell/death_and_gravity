# The actual signed radiation kernel is retained

The marked residual is the unchanged one-real object

[rho sum_pol |M5/A_B|^2 - sum_pol |S0|^2] dPhi1,

at fixed incoming scalar COM energy E and outgoing pair-rest-frame
direction. The two-body phase-space ratio is

rho=E*sqrt(E(E-omega)-1)/
     [sqrt(E(E-omega))*sqrt(E^2-1)].

Both amplitudes use the same original Born state. M5 contains all 26
matter and 21 pure Einstein trees. S0 is evaluated on the elastic
four-massive-leg momenta, not on a nonconserved recoiled subset.
The radiative soft DRESSING current, in contrast, includes the recoiled
massive particles AND the marked null graviton, so it is conserved.

The exact public kernel evaluates the original matter graphs and
Einstein graphs, restores their distinct powers of kappa, and divides
by A_B=A_m+A_G. It contracts two transverse, trace-free tensors of
squared Frobenius norm 2, then divides each squared contraction by 2.
This is equivalent to the two unit-normalized physical polarizations.

For numerical stability the returned signed radial density is scaled
by kappa and leaves out only the common positive 1/(4*pi^2) angular-
average measure factor. Algebraic rational recoil samples permit exact
evaluation even with the original very large hierarchy. No heavy-mass,
matter-only, classical or strictly zero-gravity replacement is used.

Three independent original-source samples have negative signed density.
Their full radiative indices are positive and satisfy the inherited
state-change bound. This checks that the dressing proof really applies
to a SIGNED remainder; a positive-emission-probability shortcut would
not evaluate the same object. The uniform theorem uses the written
S307 total-variation estimate, not these samples as a proof of its bound.

The same-state virtual pole is essential. Real pole a_sigma/(2e)
pairs with -a_sigma/(2e). Using -a_elastic/(2e) instead leaves a
genuine (a_sigma-a_elastic)/(2e) pole at fixed marked state. A small
coefficient does not turn this into a finite remainder.
