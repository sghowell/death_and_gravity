# Explicit matching convention and observable

Keep the original scalar masses, couplings, source and physical metric
g=eta+2h/sqrt(kappa), signature+---. Write n=M_H^2 in the unchanged
light-mass units m_L=1. Consider only the selected local-bilocal triangle
class already computed nonlocally in S342, including the metric variation
of its outer heavy resolvent.

For the triangle's three scalar factors set f1=Phi^2, f2=Phi, f3=Phi and
L=-Box_g. A flat polynomial F(v,w,q)=sum F_abc v^a w^b q^c is lifted by
the literal local product sum F_abc*(L^a f1)*(L^b f2)*(L^c f3), integrated
with sqrt(-g). This is the complete convention; no on-shell fitting or
unstated commuting of derivatives is permitted. The factor A(L1) on f1
is retained before varying the metric.

Scale every external momentum, including the real null graviton, by a
common formal lambda while holding all internal masses fixed. The target
is the homogeneous degree6 Taylor coefficient of the full radiative
difference between the selected triangle and that local lift.
The scalar legs need not be on shell in this calculation. This is not
a physical massless limit of the original scalar.

Only after the entire generic polynomial has been computed is it
identified with the S336/S343 curvature contact T. The known coefficient
chi_triangle and the independent additional parent-theory chi are
different quantities. Computing the former does not choose the latter.
The statement concerns one real-null-TT observable, not equality of
full off-shell metric or curved-background actions.
