# What this does and does not match

Established: one known finite selected-triangle contribution to the
first parity-even local curvature contact, relative to the explicitly
fixed off-shell labeled scalar-box lift. Its coefficient, sign,
normalization, original heavy-mass bound and lower-order cancellations
are derived from the whole symbolic radiative functional.

Not established: the box contribution in the same global convention,
an aggregate coefficient of every matter class, the independent
additional parent-theory curvature coefficient, or a full
curved-background counterfunctional. Changing the local comparison
representative can redistribute known coefficients and must be stated.
This calculation does not claim an invariant separation across
unstated operator bases.

The local Taylor series is justified near zero external momenta.
Original physical timelike kinematics are above the two-light cut.
No claim here replaces that Feynman amplitude by a finite local
polynomial or bounds its truncation error. The complete nonlocal
triangle and its physical finite remainder were already included in
S342; no extra copy is added.

Internal gravity, finite-gravity quantum decoupling, exact LSZ,
complete virtual/inclusive matching, quantum unitarity, complex Regge,
same-parent bounce and UV completion remain open. Original V/G/B/P8,
the nine primitive statuses, six qualifications and scoped P8(a)
are unchanged. A computed known loop is not an arbitrary choice or
zero default for missing physical parent matching.
