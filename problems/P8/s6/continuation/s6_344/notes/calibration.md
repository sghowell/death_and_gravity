# Independent original-vector and full-kernel checks

The symbolic result is supplemented, not inferred, by three original
massive recoil states, both physical polarizations, and all three
pairings of the four identical scalar labels.

An independent4-vector implementation reconstructs every cyclic
route, expands the actual gamma polynomial and integrates it exactly.
Its degree2,4,6 differences are compared with direct256-component
linear-curvature contractions. There are54 vector-order comparisons;
all underlying original scalar mass shells and conservation are
retained in the unscaled fixtures. Nonzero curvature words prevent
a vacuous normalization check.

Separately, common momentum scaling by1/16 moves those configurations
off shell into the analytic-origin domain without changing either
internal mass. For each state/polarization/pairing, all three ACTUAL
frozen S342 line kernels are evaluated at the original heavy mass and
exact rational parameter coordinates. Their complete complex-contour
denominators and weighted TT densities are compared with independently
constructed routing polynomials, for54 line kernels total.

The contour Jacobian, scalar-line split weight and the swapped light
parameter when cyclic order changes are explicit. No numerical
quadrature, floating fit, sample-to-uniform promotion or replacement
of the physical above-threshold amplitude is used.
