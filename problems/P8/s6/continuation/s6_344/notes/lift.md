# Labeled scalar-box lift and analytic-origin expansion

For three independent scalar factors f_i, attach a separate labeled
L_i=-Box_g to each entire factor, including f1=Phi^2. A monomial
v^a w^b q^c means precisely (L^a f1)(L^b f2)(L^c f3).
Its integrated product fixes a covariant action representative without
commuting derivatives between factors or imposing scalar equations of
motion. Expanding each flat off-shell triangle coefficient in these
three virtualities therefore defines a literal local comparison.

For a real null TT wave the scalar operator insertion is
deltaL=-2*epsilon(R,R)/sqrt(kappa), with input momentum R and output R+k.
The contracted connection is2*k_mu*epsilon^(mu nu)-k^nu*tr(epsilon)=0.
This removes that contracted term only; every position in L^a remains.
The sum of all a positions is the divided difference
[(R+k)^(2a)-(R^2)^a]/[(R+k)^2-R^2], with its polynomial continuation at
k.R=0. It is not replaced by a derivative at only one endpoint.

Let U=sum c_i R_i^2, a_i=k.R_i and h_i=epsilon(R_i,R_i).
For the homogeneous flat term U^r/M^(r+1), its full TT contact numerator is

    -2 sum_i h_i sum_(q=1)^r binomial(r,q)
        U^(r-q)*c_i^q*(2*a_i)^(q-1).

Every term is polynomial even when a_i=0. The fixed volume variation is
proportional to tr(epsilon) and vanishes only after this projection.

The low-momentum expansion is justified off shell as follows. Positive
internal masses give M=sum x_i m_i^2>=m_min^2>0 on the compact real
simplex. For any fixed finite external momenta, U is bounded there.
Choose the common scale lambda so |lambda^2 U|<rho M, rho<1. The
geometric series and its finitely required derivatives are uniformly
dominated and can be integrated term by term. The outer resolvent also
has |lambda^2 v|<n for sufficiently small lambda. This proves these
analytic-origin coefficients, not convergence at the original physical
timelike invariants, which are above the two-light threshold.

The off-shell flat Taylor polynomials agree exactly. Consequently their
four external scalar-emission contributions agree at each retained order
and cancel in the comparison. An on-shell flat fit alone would not
justify that cancellation.
