# Outer resolvent, all labels and loop normalization

The selected original triangle appears with
g^2*A(v)*C_triangle(v;w,q)/4 for each of24 scalar assignments,
where A(v)=C+g^2/(n-v) acts on the entire Phi^2 factor.

The exact divided-difference product identity is

    DD(A*F)(u,v)=A(v)*DD F(u,v)+F(u)*DD A(u,v).

For the original resolvent DD A=g^2/[(n-u)(n-v)]. Therefore the
literal outer-heavy insertion is
-2*g^2*epsilon(P,P)*C_triangle(u;w,q)/[(n-u)(n-v)].
The SHIFTED triangle argument u is required. The covariant comparison
includes that same insertion; omitting it or using F(v) changes the
whole product. Other labeled scalar factors are unaffected by A.

It follows that the complete dressed difference is A(v) times the
primitive triangle difference. Since the primitive differences at
degree2 and4 are exactly zero, the first degree6 term is multiplied
only by A(0)=C+g^2/n. Derivatives of A first multiply curvature terms
at degree8 or higher; this follows from homogeneous order counting,
not from dropping an outer graph.

For the two singleton labels c,d the primitive curvature word is
Q_cd,cd. The complete24 scalar label sum is4*T, where
T=sum_(i<j) epsilon(v_ij,v_ij), v_ij=(k.p_i)p_j-(k.p_j)p_i.
The original1/4 triangle factor thus cancels, and restoring the
original loop and graviton normalizations gives

    DeltaM5_degree6 = chi_triangle*T/sqrt(kappa),
    chi_triangle = g^2*(C+g^2/n)*c(n)/(16*pi^2).

The fixed finite OS4 subtraction has the same local Phi^4 completion
in both compared functionals. Its TT contact is zero and all its
external emissions cancel in the difference. It is not retuned.

This computes an already-present selected-loop effect relative to a
specified lift. Adding this contact to the full S342 triangle would
double count. It is not a choice for the independent extra
parent-theory curvature coordinate of S336.
