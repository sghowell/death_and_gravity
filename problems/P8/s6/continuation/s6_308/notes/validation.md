on the written polynomial-degree proof, not finite sampled orders.

No forceful branch identity, numerical zero tolerance, frozen edit or
GCD adapter was used for the correction.

gates, float-free serialization and every scientific test. The fresh
run forces the entire immediate parent's packets first. Only after
those and the exact three-way source comparison pass are sources frozen.

only by its documented continuation ACKs. Raw bytes, digest, canonical
REPORT AST, manifest and frontiers are validated before raw freeze.

acceptance requirements. Their outcomes are recorded externally, never
retroactively inserted into these frozen sources. At most one FULL and
one own pair run, without resource diagnostics or blind retries.
