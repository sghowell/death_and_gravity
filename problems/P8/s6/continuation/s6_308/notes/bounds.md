# Uniform gamma-phase bound and nonuniform fixed-order control

Forrealeta, complex conjugation exchangesGamma(1-i*eta) and
Gamma(1+i*eta). All remaining factors have pure imaginary exponents
on the physical spacelike edge. Hence|Q(eta)|=|C_eta(tau)|=1 exactly.

For0<eta<=1/2 the convergent logarithm gives

|log Q|<=2*zeta(3)*eta^3/[3*(1-eta^2)]<2eta^3.

Indeedzeta(j)<=zeta(3)<3/2 forj>=3, using the decreasing integral
test,1/j<=1/3 and the geometric series ineta^2. The logarithm is
pure imaginary, so|Q-1|<=|log Q|. Multiplication byexp(i*eta*c)
does not enlarge this error, for ANY realc, even if|eta*c| is large.

On25/4<=s<=16, D=sqrt(s(s-4)) lies in[15/4,8sqrt(3)].
SinceV=D^2+2,D<14 and2/D<=8/15,

eta=(D+2/D)/(8pi*kappa)<109/(180kappa)<1/kappa.

The originalgamma comparison is therefore below2/kappa^3=2e-2400,
uniformly over positive transfer. No logarithm is expanded.
This bound concerns the complete gamma factor, not a truncation of it.

For the explicit full-Born embedding, putq=s-4 and
w=4tau/q*(1-tau/q),p=4q+16+8/q. The old boundG0>p/(2w)
and identityp*q=4V giveG0>V/(2tau). SinceA_B>A_G,
A_pole/A_B<2. Thus replacingQ byone changes only the named leading
phase model by less than4e-2400 relative to the original full Born.
It is not an estimate for the omitted physical amplitude.

By contrast, for the sequence
tau=exp[ell-2/V-r/eta] withr>0,
the one-loop phase excursion is-r. Its linear truncation has squared
magnitude1+r^2, whereas the exact selected phase has magnitudeone.
At fixed originaleta andr increasing these transfers lie inside the
stated nonforward domain and approachzero. This shows concretely why
a fixed-order loop square is not uniformly controlled by its tiny
coupling coefficient.

Complex continuation is different. Sendingln tau toln tau+2pi*i
multipliesC_eta byexp(-2pi*eta). Physical-edge unit modulus removes
neither the branch nor its monodromy. The compact-energy statement
cannot be used as a complex high-energy Regge estimate.
