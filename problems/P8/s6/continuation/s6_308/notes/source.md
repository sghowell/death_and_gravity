# Source, graph class and independent literature calibration

The original scalar-graviton stress and pure-GR projector give a
D-dependent leading pole numerator. Withspacetime dimension4+2e,
the trace subtraction is1/(1+e), so

V_e=(s-2)^2-2/(1+e)=V+2e/(1+e).

The exact original tree pole isV_e/(kappa*tau).
The impact transform has the flux factor1/(2D),D=sqrt(s(s-4)).
These normalizations are derived from the existing source; no
dilaton, massless-scalar or supergravity contribution is substituted.

For two fixed straight worldlines with linear stress couplings, the
free-graviton Gaussian exchange functional exponentiates the connected
single exchange. At ordern, the ordered integration domains partition
the product cube and generate1/n! times the nth exchange power.
This defines the leading ladder/crossed-ladder class at arbitrary order.
The rational ordered-denominator identity is also checked; its general
proof groups permutations by their last entry and uses induction.
It is not used by substituting singular i0 denominators without a
regulator: the regulated worldline construction fixes that boundary.

[Di Vecchia, Heissenberg, Russo and Veneziano,2306.16488](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.16488),
eqs3.3--3.10, provide an independent pure-GR massive-pole and flux
calibration. Their eqs2.34--2.39 illustrate the Fourier gamma phase.
The subsequent classical remainder analysis uses additional scale
hierarchies and is not imported here.

[Heissenberg,2105.04594](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.04594),
eqs2.2--2.10, explicitly separates the classical eikonal from a quantum
remainder. This distinction is essential: a leading-phase sum does not
bound that remainder, especially outside its macroscopic classical
hierarchy.

[Adamo, Cristofoli and Tourkine,2112.09113](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.09113),
eqs2.4--2.5, independently identify the equal-mass gamma phase and
dimensionless strength. Their separate cutoff scale is not adopted.
Our finite normalization is fixed by the original full-D transform and
the independent S303 phase, rather than by selecting an IR mass scale.

The known Newton shifts, unassigned physical matching, all nonlinear
graviton diagrams, recoil and real radiation remain separate original
sectors. This successor never asserts that their omission is small.
