## What does not follow

not the complete full-Born or detector-inclusive rate.

neither a forward holomorphic disk nor a high-energy Regge bound.
No macroscopic classical hierarchyGm^2>>1 is assumed; it is not
satisfied by the original weak-gravity scalar domain.

finite-e small-impact-parameter integral. The proven order is fixed

finite leading-class generating function. Full quantum construction,
