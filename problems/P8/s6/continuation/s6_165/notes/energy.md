# Complete free out-particle energy and its reference scale

Normal order the FREE out-particle Hamiltonian
against its own out-vacuum. Per unit spatial
volume its in-state expectation is

    rho_out=4N/(2pi^2) integral_0^infinity
                 p^2 omega_out |beta_p|^2 dp.

Here N=6 is two active flavors times three
colors. There are two spin/helicity states
and both particles and antiparticles, giving
the factor 4N. The twelve inert flavors have
beta=0 in this flat constant-mass diagnostic.

The mass enclosure gives omega_out<=2E.
Use (A+B)^2<=2A^2+2B^2 and the exact radial
integrals

    integral p^4/E^7 dp=1/(5m0^2),
    integral p^8/E^11 dp=1/(9m0^2).

It follows that

    rho_out<=8N/pi^2 [
        5Delta^2/(tau^4 m0^2)
        +Delta^6/(225m0^2)].

The second term is the allowance for all
higher odd transition terms, with cross
terms covered by the two-norm inequality.
It is not omitted because the first term
is adiabatically small.

Using pi^2>9, Delta<=3e197, tau=10^-100
and m0=0.99e200 gives

    first-transition energy allowance
        2.4487297214569941843e396,
    higher-transition energy allowance
        1.7630853994490358127e783,
    complete allowance <1e784.

A coarse uniform transition-amplitude bound is

    5Delta/(2tau^2 m0^3)+Delta^3/(40m0^3)
      <1e-9.

The complete energy allowance divided by
kappa=10^800 is approximately 1.7630854e-17,
strictly below 1e-16. This compares only with
the named kappa*m_Phi^4 reference scale in
m_Phi=1 units. It is NOT a relative error
against the bounce energy density, which
vanishes at H=0.

No transient renormalized local stress,
coherence, curved-space backreaction or
interacting gauge energy is bounded by
this free asymptotic particle observable.
