# The prescribed quadratic operator and its in/out states

Omit gauge interactions for this explicitly named
free/quadratic calculation. The two active opposite
SAT8 masses on the clock argument aligned in S6.164 are

    M_+/- (t)=mF +/- Delta s(t/tau),
    Delta=|y|R, tau=R/sqrt(kappa).

The numerical bound uses Delta<=3e197, R=10^300,
mF=10^200 and kappa=10^800. The twelve inert
flavors have constant masses in this flat calculation.
These are the quadratic operator's reference parameters,
not a newly computed higher-loop fermion pole dictionary.

For fixed momentum magnitude p and either helicity,
a constant spinor basis gives the two-level Hamiltonian

    H_p(t)=p sigma1+M(t) sigma3,
    omega(t)=sqrt(p^2+M(t)^2).

The other helicity has p replaced by -p and the same
transition probability. Both masses are strictly above
m0=0.99mF. A real rotation with angle arctan(p/M)
diagonalizes H. Removing the dynamical phases gives

    alpha'=-v exp(i theta) beta,
    beta'= v exp(-i theta) alpha,
    v=p M'/(2omega^2), theta'=2omega.

The column norm |alpha|^2+|beta|^2 is conserved. At
p=0 the mixing vanishes exactly. The off-diagonal
transition probabilities agree in the two directions
of a unitary two-level evolution.

The mass tails obey M(t)-M_in/out=O(|t|^-8).
In the interaction picture of each constant asymptotic
Hamiltonian, the difference generator has integrable
operator norm. Its finite-dimensional integral equation
therefore has Cauchy in/out Moller limits, for each mode.

Choose the free in-vacuum, or equivalently the
asymptotic negative-energy occupied projector. Evolution
defines translation-invariant quasifree in/out states
of the free CAR algebra. The out occupation is |beta_p|^2.
This is modewise unitarity; a single global Fock-space
unitary or finite total particle number in infinite
spatial volume is not asserted.

This does not define gauge-invariant asymptotic colored
particles of the full interacting GY14 theory. It also
does not infer a Hadamard remainder from the finite
energy estimate alone.
