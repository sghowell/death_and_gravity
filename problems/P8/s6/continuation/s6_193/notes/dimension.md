# General-d curvature identity before pole subtraction

Let H'=Hdot and use the F,G jets of angular.md. Define

    Bshape=H I+F/2,
    X=(H'+H^2)I+HF+G/2-F^2/4,
    Y=(H'+dH^2)I+(dH/2)F+G/2-F^2/2.

The old -+++ curvature convention gives

    R=trX+trY=2dH'+d(d+1)H^2+trF^2/4,
    Ricci2=(trX)^2+tr(Y^2),
    Riemann2=4tr(X^2)+2[(trBshape^2)^2-trBshape^4].

Direct non-diagonal Christoffel and Riemann fixtures independently
verify these formulas; no isotropic extrapolation supplies them.

Let a4s=(5R^2-2Ricci2+2Riemann2)/360 and

    a4V(d+1)=d*a4s+Ricci2/2-R^2/6-Riemann2/12.

At d3 this is the unchanged Proca polynomial
-R^2/8+29Ricci2/60-Riemann2/15. More generally
a4V(d+1)=a4V(4)+(d-3)a4s, with the SAME continued geometric
contractions on both sides.

After all angular contractions and radial ratios, the integrated
second-order density is I_1*(d-6)R/24 up to the exact
a^d-weighted derivative of H. Here I_1 is the n0 inverse-frequency
radial integral.

For fourth order let F4 be the complete averaged/radially reduced
tr(t^2+s^4). The exact polynomial identity is

    F4 = 2a4V(d+1) + c0 D0+c1 D1+c2 D2,

where

    D0=(a^d H^3)'/a^d,
    D1=(a^d H trF^2)'/a^d,
    D2=(a^d trF^3)'/a^d,

    c0=-4d(5d^3-96d^2+229d-126)/945,
    c1=(d^2-164d+252)/630,
    c2=-(17d-252)/3780.

Use (trF^2)'=2trFG-2trF^3 and
(trF^3)'=3trF^2G-3trF^4 to expand these derivatives.
Every independent curvature monomial is equated symbolically for
general d, including noncommuting shear invariants.

These are genuine total time derivatives for general d, so compact
metric variations annihilate them before the dimension limit.
The four-dimensional Euler density is NOT discarded as if it were
topological at noninteger dimension. Its possible finite evanescent
effect is retained in the continued curvature polynomials.
