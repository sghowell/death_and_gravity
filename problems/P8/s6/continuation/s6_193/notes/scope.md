# Scope of the fixed-prescription bridge

S6.193 closes the specific homogeneous-shear finite-subtraction
matching gap left in S6.192. It proves a compact variational identity,
a full noncommuting shear angular/curvature identity at general
dimension, and the original fixed finite dimension limit.

It does not by itself state a numerical nonlinear local-current
response norm. The known S6.189 local linear full-spatial TT Hessian
is not substituted for every higher amplitude derivative. A
quantitative local-current estimate can now be added to S6.192
without an unresolved choice of finite prescription.

All statements concern the conditional canonical Gaussian Proca
sector and the admitted homogeneous histories with the unchanged
common state. They do not establish full spatial or scalar/tensor
mixed response, the full feedback inverse, a finite-coupling
quantum background, the other interacting parent sectors, their
measure/state/loops, or the physical heavy/cutoff/threshold and
omitted-order matching. Vacuum cuts/contour/truncation and
finite-gravity IR/Regge obligations remain.

Original P8(b), V/G/B and P8 remain OPEN. Scoped P8(a) and A.20-A.23
are unchanged. A matching identity cannot be promoted to a full
solution, stability theorem or UV completion by its test count.
