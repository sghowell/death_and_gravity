# Known coefficient versus independent matching

The mixed forward bound concerns the explicitly derived minimal
one-loop representative. Add the whole known S6.290 matter-metric
endpoint and keep its independent finite curvature coordinates.

The constant R Phi^2 coordinate and a constant quartic counterterm
have zero crossed b20 at one insertion. This is not an all-order
field redefinition or a statement that higher EFT coefficients vanish.
The R H coordinate contributes a genuine heavy-exchange residue:
h=-2g c_RH-g^2 Bconst/(16pi^2), Bconst=-ln(mu)/6 at the reference scale1.
Its b20 contribution is-2h(n+2mu)/[kappa(n-2mu)^3].
At original mu=1, Bconst=0 does not imply c_RH=0.

The known bubble piece is counted once, in the S6.290 endpoint.
The finite R H piece is not included in the2001g^2/(kappa n) bound.
Additional finite cubic/heavy-residue, higher-source and higher-EFT
matching insertions require their own determination and bounds.
The zero pure-GR on-shell heavy mass shift does not determine them.

The complete flat mixed graph UV cancellation fixes neither the
curved finite matching coordinate nor an unspecified UV completion.
No finite prescription, sign, resolution or matching parameter is
chosen to obtain a positivity conclusion.

Known C/kappa and g^2/kappa loop coefficients together are below
2002g^2/(kappa n)<10^-1001 at the exact original parameters. This is
one selected part of the full source amplitude. Pure metric order,
other original coupling sectors, higher insertions, all-loop errors,
physical IR and Regge/state/domain/bounce obligations remain separate.
