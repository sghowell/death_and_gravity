representative, its full offshell numerator/master reduction and
two-body cut inventory, covariant finite H-metric completion, exact
infrared subtraction, and a direct known subthreshold forward bound.

matching, other coupling/all-loop control, a stable heavy atom or
physical width resummation, physical dressed/detector infrared
factorization and unitarity, a positive full spectral measure,
fixed-negative-transfer Regge/remainder, or original V/G/B/P8 closure.

bounds compute a local analytic coefficient; they are not fixed-transfer
high-energy bounds and are not used to apply pole-subtracted positivity.

All nine primitive frontiers,149 previous matching records and six
historical qualifications are retained. The new matching record states
only the scoped known coefficient result. The rejected S6.279 evidence
stays rejected. Scoped P8(a), the source/frame and the original

proof. Numerical integrations calibrate the whole-dimensional

rigorous quadrature error certificates or substitutes for the bounds.
