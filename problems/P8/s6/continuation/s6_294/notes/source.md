# Original source and complete mixed graph ownership

The whole original source and114 accepted parent source identities are
retained by copying, not mutating, the parent's cached check dictionary.
Eight new valence identities enumerate the connected minimal mixed classes.

There are two original H Phi Phi vertices. At metric coupling squared,
a non-onepoint graph has one internal graviton. With two linear matter
stress vertices, ten matter ends and four external Phi legs leave three
internal matter edges. Four vertices and four total edges give one loop.
The stress species assignments are Phi/Phi, H/Phi and H/H.

One metric-cubic contact plus one stress leaves two internal matter
edges and three vertices. Two metric-cubic contacts leave one H edge
and two vertices. A quadratic metric contact closes a scaleless h
tadpole. Pure-gravity/ghost vertices at this coupling can only appear
as flat onepoint insertions; use the original whole volume condition,
not a deletion of genuine soft graphs. Disconnected vacuum graphs
divide out of the normalized connected amplitude.

If a scalar edge is a bridge, the graph is an external selfenergy or a
proper H Phi Phi endpoint on a heavy exchange. If a metric edge is a
bridge, the graph is part of the whole S6.290 matter-metric endpoint.
With neither bridge, the four-vertex loop is a mixed box. The two
metric-cubic contact bubble is its own connected two-vertex class.

Thus the amplitude consists of twelve labelled mixed boxes; complete
proper heavy endpoints on both ends of each s/t/u exchange; the heavy
selfenergy; four external Phi sqrtZ factors; the two metric-cubic
contact bubble; and the entire inherited matter-metric endpoint.
The latter includes active-light and active-heavy triangles, both
metric-cubic contacts, H-metric mixing, the covariant light OS kinetic
and mass terms, and the original heavy-onepoint counterterm.

The four external factors are sqrtZ, not four Z. There is no external
H residue attached to an internal H line; its selfenergy supplies the
correct pole expansion. The pure-GR heavy mass shift vanishes on shell
in the retained whole-D calculation, but this does not fix other
mass/residue matching or make the decaying H an exact stable state.

Other source/coupling sectors and higher EFT coefficients are not
silently absorbed into g. The original g,n,C,kappa and all historical
state/domain/measure obligations remain unchanged.
