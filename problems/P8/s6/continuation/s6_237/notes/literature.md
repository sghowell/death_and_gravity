# Primary-source context and independent calculation

[Henning, Lu and Murayama, One-loop Matching and Running with Covariant Derivative Expansion](https://arxiv.org/abs/1604.01019) provides the functional context for retaining mixed heavy/light one-loop effects. This checkpoint uses the complete nonlocal quartic Hessian and all diagrams already derived for the separate model; no mixed loop is removed.

[Denner, Techniques for the calculation of electroweak radiative corrections at the one-loop level and results for W-physics at LEP200](https://arxiv.org/abs/0709.1075) gives the conventional scalar-integral and Feynman-prescription notation used in S234/S235. Our Cbar sign, ordered Dbar variables and external normalization are unchanged. The higher primitive jets and logarithmic moment identities are independently derived here.

[de Rham, Melville, Tolley and Zhou, Massive Galileon Positivity Bounds](https://arxiv.org/abs/1702.08577) supplies context for separating matching, higher operators and loop errors from a full UV statement. S231/S232 retain the actual physical dispersion premises. A complete first-loop coefficient bound is progress on their matching inputs, not proof of every exact premise.

The joint bidisk, complete Cauchy coefficient tails, product majorants and actual signed intervals are the present calculation. None of the references supplies those constants or certifies an exact S matrix, omitted loops, finite-gravity Regge theory, common-parent bounce or original P8 closure.
