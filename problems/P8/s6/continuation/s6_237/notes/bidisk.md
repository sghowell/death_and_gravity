# Joint holomorphy and the complete analytic coefficient tail

Use independent complex variables v,t with |v|<=1/4 and |t|<=1/4. Then

s=2+v-t/2, u=2-v-t/2,
|s|,|u|<=19/8<3, |t|<=1/4.

For every light parameter xi and every heavy parameter eta, each channel satisfies

L=1-channel*xi(1-xi), |L-1|<=19/32,
Re L>=13/32>0, |L|<2,
b=other_channel*eta(1-eta), |b|<2.

The real part of n z+(1-z)²L-bz² is bounded below by n z+(13/32)(1-z)²-(19/32)z². It is strictly positive on the entire parameter cube at the actual n, including z=0 and z=1. This proves the full triangle and box integrals are jointly holomorphic on a neighborhood of the closed bidisk. Their channel poles lie far outside it. No exact all-order S-matrix analyticity is inferred.

The principal logarithm is analytic because Re L>0. Its full power-series majorant gives

|Log L|<=|L-1|/(1-|L-1|)<=19/13<2.

With ln n<462, M=500 bounds |ln n-Log L|+3 and |B| everywhere on the bidisk.
