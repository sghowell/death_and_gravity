# Two auxiliary pivots and the remaining quantum domain

On the full temporal stationary branch let d=C_N,
tau=H_TT=-N U/Gamma and k=partial_N Tstar.
The complete auxiliary Hessian is

    [[d+tau k^2, -tau k],
     [-tau k,    tau   ]].

Its determinant is d*tau and its Schur complement is d.
The displayed exact inverse retains both off-diagonal k contacts.
The full-source interval bounds give -31/10<d<-3,
-2<tau<-1/2, Gamma>99/100, |Tstar|<3*10^-250 and
|partial_N Tstar|<10*10^-250.
Thus this Hessian is negative definite and det>3/2.
This is a uniformly regular nonlinear branch, not merely an
implicit-function theorem at one classical point.

cancel on this branch. The present uniform pivot verifies the

constraint/gauge factor, boundary data and time ordering remain.
No continuum determinant is separately regularized or canceled.

1/2 and2 on the reconstructed branch. Consequently the zero-lapse

domain. This does not show that the same Gaussian quantum state

positive Weyl operators. No projection or renormalization of a
