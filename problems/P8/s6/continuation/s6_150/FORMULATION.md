# S6.150: isolated fixed-contact MS conversion

Keep the GY14 model, physical Phi mass one, zero heavy-field
source, canonical unit-residue Phi convention and all actual
parameters fixed. Isolate ONLY the -sigma component of the
S6.133 first-order quartic map. Hold g=G^2, M, Y, m, the entire
I0 reference and the remaining scale/field directions fixed.

Prove the cancellation of the S6.125 fixed-contact insertion
against re-expansion of the complete one-loop scalar amplitude.
Do so before discarding epsilon dependence in products with
poles. The contact is fixed by the old potential condition;
neither it nor a second nonlocal counterterm may be fitted to b2.

The result is one matching identity through formal loop order
two. It neither transfers the full old scalar two-loop budget to
the GY14 MS model nor completes the full canonical pole/error.
Primitive statuses remain unchanged. Other matching directions,
their mixed terms, vacuum/source assembly, truncation, V/G/B and
original P8 remain open.
