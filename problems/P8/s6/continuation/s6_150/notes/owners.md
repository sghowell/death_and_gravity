# Physical mass, field and source ownership

At fixed free masses the first scalar inverse correction is
L T_light/2-g B_HL after the assigned H-source cancellation.
Its L derivative is T_light/2, independent of momentum.
The sigma insertion therefore requires exactly the local
physical-mass counterterm sigma T_light/2 with opposite sign.
It has zero slope and zero on-shell-subtracted nonlocal tail.

The scalar first slope r depends on g,M, not L. The first
fermion slope fprime depends on Y,m, not L. Thus the first
canonical field correction r-fprime is unchanged. The proper
one-loop fermion mass, kinetic and Yukawa references are also
L independent. The heavy source J1=-G T_light/2 is unchanged.
No new heavy mass/cubic shift is produced by this isolated
direction beyond the paired UV references in the bare note.

At Phi=H=0 the quartic contact has zero value, source and
quadratic Hessian. It therefore makes no order-two vacuum
determinant insertion. A quartic vertex closed into a vacuum
graph has two loop integrations; with its additional h
counterterm weight its first vacuum contribution is h^3.
The first free vacuum determinant is L independent at fixed
physical/free masses. This argument does not remove other
vacuum/source terms, including the first-source square.

The amplitude is already expressed in the same canonical
unit-residue field. There is no additional LSZ multiplication.
The identities concern an isolated direction; the cross terms
with the remaining scale, coupling and field changes require
their own complete second-order matching.
