# Bare equality before finite parts

Use h as a formal loop marker and F0=I0/(2Q) with the ENTIRE
regulated reference. The first total canonical UV references are

    c1G=L G F0, c1M=g F0,
    c1L=3L^2 F0 + the L-independent fermion quartic MS pole.

The fixed sigma_D is in addition to c1L in the old quartic
reference. S6.125's assigned second-order local references are

    c2G=sigma_D G F0, c2M=0, c2L=6 sigma_D L F0.

They equal sigma_D times the L derivative of c1. In terms of
g=G^2 this is c2g=2 sigma_D g F0. This is the LINEAR order-two
G reference; the already-owned square G1^2 is not relabeled as
2G G2. Its L re-expansion first changes that h^2 term at h^3.

Set Lstar+h sigma_D(Lstar)=L, keeping the other coordinates
fixed. Affinity gives the exact isolated coordinate inverse

    Lstar=(L-h c_D)/(1-h k_D)
         =L-h sigma_D+h^2 sigma_D partial_L sigma_D+O(h^3).

The old bare L numerator is Lstar+h sigma_D(Lstar)+
h c1L(Lstar)+h^2 c2L(Lstar). Its h^2 correction vanishes:
the first pair is exactly L, while h c1's -sigma_D partial_L
c1 cancels h^2 c2. The same identity holds for G and M.
No new noncontact G2 or M2 is needed for this isolated removal.

The first canonical field reference is L independent (owners
note). Dividing by the same Z for G and Z^2 for L preserves the
identity. This does not multiply a canonical amplitude by an
extra external residue factor.

The cancellation is a meromorphic identity before epsilon zero.
For sigma_D=sigma0+epsilon sigma1 and I0=1/epsilon+finite,
truncating only one sigma_D leaves the finite defect sigma1.
The exact map term h^2 sigma_D partial_L sigma_D also cannot be
dropped just because sigma is finite. Other second-order local
potential contacts can differ by the fixed potential convention,
but have zero b2 and are not fitted here.
