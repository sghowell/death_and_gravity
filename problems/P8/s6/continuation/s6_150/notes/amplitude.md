# Full three-channel cancellation, not a bare-contact shortcut

At fixed g,M the complete integrated scalar one-loop channel,
modulo its momentum-independent potential contact, is

    F(L)=C^2 I_R/2+C(T_A+T_B)/2+B/2,
    C=-L+g/(M-z).

Both heavy triangles and the heavy box are retained. Before
integration W_A W_B=(C+t_A)(C+t_B); its derivative is
-2C-t_A-t_B. Since the triangles and box are L independent,

    partial_L F=-C I_R-(T_A+T_B)/2.

This derivative is precisely S6.125's assigned finite-contact
insertion divided by sigma. The local UV piece obeys the same
identity: sigma partial_L[F0 sum_channels C^2] is cancelled by
-6 sigma L F0+2 sigma g F0 sum_channels 1/(M-z).
Its sign and both cubic/heavy contributions are retained.

The old canonical amplitude has, for this isolated direction,

    A0(Lstar)+h[F(Lstar)-sigma(Lstar)]
      +h^2 sigma(Lstar) partial_L F(Lstar),

summed over the three channels for F and the insertion; the
potential contact itself appears only once. A0 is the full tree
-L+g sum_channels 1/(M-z), so A0(Lstar)-h sigma(Lstar)=A0(L)
by the exact inverse equation. Re-expanding hF supplies
-h^2 sigma partial_L F and cancels the explicit insertion,
as an entire nonlocal function, through h^2. Additional fixed
potential contacts and their derivatives are still local.

Each one-loop integral is already renormalized before this
finite parameter variation. Its L dependence is polynomial at
fixed heavy propagators, so the derivative is a finite linear
combination of the same convergent/renormalized integrals.
The entire regulated local reference follows the bare identity;
no integration limit is exchanged with an unpaired pole.

A bare contact's b2 is zero, but its loop insertion need not be:
the diagnostic pure-quartic bubble gives sigma L/(960 pi^2).
This nonzero control is not the actual g=0 model; it catches
the invalid shortcut. Only the matched pair cancels here.
