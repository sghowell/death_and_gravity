# Pointwise contact cancellation and noncommuting shifts

S6.150 proves the isolated sigma conversion as a full
regulated identity, retaining both heavy triangles,
the box, local proper-reference terms and the physical
mass insertion. At fixed other coordinates,

    Lstar+h sigma_D(Lstar)=L,
    Aold=A0(Lstar)+h[F(Lstar)-sigma_D(Lstar)]
         +h^2 sigma_D(Lstar) partial_L F(Lstar).

Since the L-dependent tree contact is -L, the first
two contact terms combine exactly to -L. Expanding
hF(Lstar) gives -h^2 sigma_D partial_L F, canceling
the displayed nonlocal insertion. All assigned bare
references obey the same common-regulator identity.

This cancellation is pointwise for nearby g,M,Y and
at fixed common regulator. Composing a pointwise
remainder O(h^3) with any analytic q(h)=q0+O(h)
preserves that order. Consequently it also holds
after the subsequent field-coordinate map, without
assuming that its vector field commutes with the
isolated sigma direction. Their commutator is in
general nonzero.

The resulting direct interaction-MS first scalar
functional is Aold_scalar+sigma+ell sum C^2/(2Q).
Its sigma dependence has canceled. The second raw
scalar sum uses only the four 88/64/24/16 families;
a separate sigma insertion would undo the matched
cancellation and double count.

This argument concerns formal coefficients through
h^2 and their regulated reference products. It is
not a physical higher-order truncation estimate.
