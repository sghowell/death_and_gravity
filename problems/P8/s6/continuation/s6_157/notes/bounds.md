plus the finite MS conversion allowance

    T ell_upper | -2L+3g/(M-2) |/(2 Q_lower),

with Q_lower=144. Adding sigma changes no direct b2.

relative upper bound. The first hybrid variation is
bounded by k0_upper(4 E_s+2 E_f). It is not the
first canonical amplitude bound times an assumed
common homogeneity weight.

Let R be the S6.153 four-scalar-family bound, plus
the S6.142 both-direct-row bound, S6.143 full insertion
bound and S6.144 full mixed bound. Let F be S6.156's
complete second tree-field relative allowance. Then

    E2=R+k0_upper(4 E_s+2 E_f)+T F.

rational upper bounds. No giant floating-point
subtraction determines an inequality. At the actual
parameters, approximate values for orientation are

    first_cross/T     6.87384305853845e-213,
