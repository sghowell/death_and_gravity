# Full regulated map and the finite hybrid amplitude

Use H as a scheme label for physical Phi OS conditions,
minimal interaction poles, direct-MS fermion proper
kernels and exact Gaussian heavy-field elimination.
It is not an additional heavy field. The canonical
amplitude is

    Acan=A_H0(q_H,D)+h A_H1(q_H,D)+h^2 A_H2(q_H,D).

At fixed mu and masses the full first parameter shift
is q1,D=-k_D Ephi q with k_D=k0+epsilon k1+...,
Ephi=2L dL+2g dg+Y dY. In particular M1=0.
The old zero-reference heavy-mass shift is not added
again: the raw families have already been converted
to MS interaction references.

S6.156 matched the full regulated bare fields and
re-expressed the first interaction poles before taking
finite parts. With t=t_MS its second map is

    G2=(k0^2-t)G+k1 L G/Q,
    L2=(3k0^2-2t)L+3k1 L^2/Q,
    M2=k1 g/Q,
    g2=(3k0^2-2t)g+2k1 L g/Q.

The last equation includes G1^2 in addition to 2G G2.
The induced M2 remains even though the first heavy
mass shift is zero. These are the full bare-coordinate
identities, not S6.155's fixed-input coefficient map.
The k1 terms cannot be omitted.

After all its counterterms have been added, A_H1 is
finite in epsilon on a neighborhood of the named
parameters. Its derivative is a finite combination
of the same convergent/renormalized integrals. Hence
its order-two coordinate variation uses q1,0 only;
positive epsilon pieces multiply no remaining pole.
This fact does not justify dropping k1 in the earlier
bare-coordinate identity.

The scalar one-loop amplitude has Ephi weight four:
it is quadratic in L,g, including the full heavy
triangles and box. The fermionic box has weight two
because it is proportional to Y^2. Therefore

    q1,0 d A_H1=-k0(4 A_H1,scalar+2 A_H1,fermion).

The raw A_H2 here is hybrid, not raw-MS amputated.
Multiplying it by an additional K^-2 or substituting
a raw-MS LSZ formula on top of this expansion would
count the normalization twice. First-order canonical
matching is already complete in S6.133.
