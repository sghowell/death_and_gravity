# Complete disjoint four-point ownership

The scalar one-field exact-H action has four raw two-loop
families: 88 UV-finite refinements (S6.121), 64 OS insertions
(S6.153), 24 double bubbles (S6.152) and 16 wineglasses
(S6.151). Their interaction references are now all MS,
while the physical inner Phi OS grouping remains fixed.
The 64/24/16 reference conversions include their entire
dimensional numerator products and nonlocal heavy terms,
not just an outer zero-momentum constant. Their disjoint
sum is the S6.153 four-family enclosure.

The noncommuting expansion of the fermionic master trace
at Phi degree four is

    (1/2) Tr[D F4-D W2 D F2+D W2 D W2 D F0],

plus the Phi4 gauge row. W2 is the full nonlocal Hessian,
including both internal-heavy pieces, not just local L.
The two direct rows are bounded by S6.142, including all
self-energy, opposite and vertex words and proper fermion
mass/kinetic/Yukawa MS counterterms. The mixed row is the
complete S6.144 family with its proper fermion box and
outer MS reference. It has no additional proper Yukawa
subgraph. S6.143 owns the full W2 F0 insertion family,
its inner OS condition and its outer MS reference.

Each assigned counterterm occurrence is removed from
the separate S6.137 insertion ledger. Adding a further
copy of a proper fermion counterterm or a bosonic
subgraph counterterm would double count. Overall local
Phi4 constants have b2=0 only after the proper forests
have been performed; their insertions at lower order
are not discarded.

The heavy source is exact: eliminating H in
MH^2/2+H(J+G Phi^2/2) produces
-(J+G Phi^2/2)^2/(2M). Its J dependence is a vacuum
constant and a Phi2 mass term. Phi parity forbids an
internal light tree exchange. The remaining Phi2 mass,
source and residue references therefore supply no
additional nonlocal Phi4 tree coefficient: their
external-leg effects are owned by S6.156's physical
mass-one, unit-residue condition.

Pure gauge and ghost vacuum terms do not acquire a
Phi dependence without the already catalogued fermion
row at this loop order. Inert fermions have Y=0 and
no external Phi vertices. The four listed fermionic
families are consequently exhaustive at this order,
not an argument about higher-loop or finite-field
convergence.
