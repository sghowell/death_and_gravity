The theorem in this child is derived for its displayed action. External

## Interaction reciprocity

Baccetti, Martin-Moruno and Visser, [arXiv:1206.3814v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1206.3814),
section 2 equations (2.2)–(2.9), define the square-root elementary-polynomial
interaction and reciprocity, including endpoint terms. Equations
(2.12)–(2.16) relate its two stress tensors and conservation constraints.
Section 4, especially (4.3), (4.6)–(4.8), obtains opposite interaction-NEC
signs under the corresponding null-vector map. Those are **effective
interaction stresses**, explicitly not real matter sources. This child
places the NEC only on actual central matter; it does not impose two
interaction NECs. Its factors of two, beta signs and physical-clock weights
are derived anew from the fixed `-2` action normalization. No source NEC
statement supplies an amplitude or quantum-health conclusion.

## Cosmological Bianchi and regular clocks

Capozziello and Martin-Moruno,
[arXiv:1211.0214v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1211.0214), section 2 equations
(8)–(15), display the bimetric FLRW equations and proper-time Bianchi
relation. Section 3 equations (18)–(19) link scale-factor extrema when
both lapses remain positive and regular. The text after (7), including
footnote 1, dismisses special parameter solutions before using (15).
Accordingly this child does **not** import (15) on every algebraic branch;
it retains `J(R)(R H_u-H_i)=0` and proves the zero-set argument separately.
The paper's reconstruction in (17) permits separately chosen hidden-sector
matter and its metric ansatz permits spatial curvature. Neither is in this
child's only-central-NEC, spatially flat action class. Its curvature and
coupling normalization are not copied into P8(b).
