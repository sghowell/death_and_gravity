# Primary-source and convention audit

- Wood, Saffin and Avgoustidis, [*Black Holes in Multi-Metric Gravity II*,
  arXiv:2410.10976v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.10976), equations (1)–(8)
  give the oriented pairwise metric action. Equation (41), the discussion
  below (45), and Figure 2 explicitly use reciprocal edge contributions
  and leaf stripping on a tree. That application is to linearized Bianchi
  constraints of nonproportional black-hole backgrounds. It is clear prior
  art for the graph method, not a sourced proof of the present FLRW/NEC
  no-bounce theorem. The current lapse powers and component comparison
  are derived directly from the displayed action. No novelty claim is made.

- The same authors' [*Clockwork Cosmology*, arXiv:2304.09205](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2304.09205),
  section 3.2, equations (3.22)–(3.25) and Appendix A, derive cosmological
  edge balances and a lapse/velocity branch for pairwise chains. The
  source discusses total versus individual matter conservation and writes
  the dynamical branch using velocity quotients. Here separate matter
  actions and on-shell conservation at every vertex are explicit; the
  equations remain undivided at Hubble zeros and preserve the algebraic
  polynomial factor. No quotient branch is imported as exhaustive, and
  no scalar/vector or cutoff conclusion is transferred.

- The original HR/bimetric action and interaction-NEC distinctions are
  pinned through S6.5, including [arXiv:1111.1655](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1111.1655)
  and [arXiv:1206.3814](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1206.3814). The finite star
  extension and its actual algebraic countercontrol are pinned through
  S6.16. These are context and action/arithmetic prerequisites, not an
  assumption that an entire tree is a single dynamic branch.

The present edge action is \(-2\sqrt{|g_i|}\sum_n\beta_n e_n(S)\),
matching S6.16. The beta coefficients carry mass dimension four. For
S6.5's \(-m^4\beta_n\) convention the explicit dictionary is
\(m^4\beta_n^{\rm old}=2\beta_n^{\rm here}\). Thus
\(P=2(\beta_1+2\beta_2y+\beta_3y^2)\) and the first raw-J Bianchi
coefficient is six, not three. P8(b)'s signature/curvature/EH signs are
\(+---\), \(R_B=-6(DH+2H^2)\), \(-G R_B/2\). The cited papers use
their own conventions; neither the A/FK curvature sign nor a matter-frame
map is silently substituted. Auxiliary vertices with zero Einstein
coefficient are included only through their actual remaining equations,
and only a strictly positive-Einstein vertex is selected as the physical
target of this gate.

The reciprocal Bianchi weight \(N_i^2a_i^3\) differs from the null-stress
weight \(N_i a_i^3\). Both are verified independently. Full-parent
background constraints do not by themselves establish a quantum cutoff,
healthy parent spectrum, or a match to the original C/D matter action.
