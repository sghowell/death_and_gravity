# Source use and boundary

The independently derived tensor/vector action and background equations
are the pinned S6.7 and S6.6 inputs. Their source audits remain unchanged.
[Gumrukcuoglu et al., arXiv:1501.02790](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1501.02790)
provides a primary cross-check for those actions, not the new cone-order
or interval-monotonicity proof. The latter are derived here with the
literal composite physical clock and ruler.

The composite-metric definition is from
[de Rham, Heisenberg and Ribeiro, arXiv:1408.1678](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1408.1678).
Its generic EFT/constraint boundary is discussed in
[arXiv:1409.3834](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1409.3834), and the distinction
between an upper cutoff estimate and a safe rolling-background window
is preserved from [Matas, arXiv:1506.00666](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1506.00666).

This certificate does not import a published scalar kinetic expression
or assume its gauge is regular at a composite bounce. It needs neither
the scalar perturbation matrix nor a claim that formal high-k tensor
characteristics are low-energy front velocities. A light-only EFT and
its controlled matching have separate obligations; the all-mode
principal contract stated here is not silently substituted for them.
