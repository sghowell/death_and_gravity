# Source and convention audit

The tensor action and composite source weights are derived literally
from the frozen S6.6–S6.10 action and replayed here. They are not inferred
from a frozen eigenfrequency or a guessed free massive propagator.

[Gümrükçüoğlu, Heisenberg, Mukohyama and Tanahashi,
arXiv:1501.02790](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1501.02790), especially equations
2.4, 3.3, 3.12 and 4.5–4.6, supplies the composite-metric and TT-action
context. The source uses `-+++`; our positive-root `+---` action dictionary
was independently fixed in the pinned ancestors. Their scale ratio A
is our y and their lapse/scale ratio r is our c/y. Its perturbation
discussion does not supply a cutoff estimate for the singularly scaled
family tested here. No scalar health conclusion is transferred from it.

[Galley, arXiv:1210.2745](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1210.2745), equations 2–4
and 21–24, explains why inserting a retarded inverse into an ordinary
single-copy action does not itself furnish a causal variational
principle. Our calculation is directly the zero-data causal initial
value problem. Independent homogeneous data would add a separate
response and are not silently discarded. The locked action is a
specified competing prediction, not a purported exact retarded
effective action.

The finite-parameter tube, outward-rational bounds, pulse comparison,
physical source normalization, and finite-error theorem in this child
are repository derivations. The source pulse is external, background
conserved, TT and infinitesimal. Neither these sources nor the present
proof establishes a microscopic probe realization, a temporal cutoff,
the scalar/vector sector, a globally free internal M1 field, or an
original-frame match. There is no global novelty assertion.
