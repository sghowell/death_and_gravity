[Cosmology in bimetric theory with an effective composite coupling to

by S6.9. This child derives the scaled reconstruction, limiting flow,
time-dependent comparison and physical projection directly. The
compact-interval convergence argument is written out through finite
compact-domain derivative bounds and Gronwall; it does not rely on
sampled numerical evolution.

The older composite-coupling cutoff cautions remain open. This is neither
a new regular parent geometry at the singular parameter endpoint nor a
quantum calculation. No general novelty claim or ultraviolet exclusion
is made.
