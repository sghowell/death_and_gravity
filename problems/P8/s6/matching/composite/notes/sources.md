# Primary-source dictionary and cutoff boundary

The background equations and examples in [the proof](background.md) are
independently derived in the fixed P8 `+---` action convention. The papers
below use mostly-plus conventions and, in places, different interaction
polynomials. Only the explicit equation dictionary is transferred.
There is no global novelty claim.

## Composite matter is not separately coupled matter

[de Rham, Heisenberg and Ribeiro, *On couplings to matter in massive
(bi-)gravity*](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1408.1678), equations (1.1),
(4.8)–(4.11), gives the algebraic composite metric, its FLRW lapse/scale
and the canonical scalar Hamiltonian. This supports the two affine sums
and their chain-rule variation, not treating the induced sources as two
independently conserved matter sectors. Sections 4.3–4.5 distinguish the
minisuperspace and original decoupling-limit constraint checks from the
generic theory. Its matter-loop potential statement explicitly omits
curvature corrections in equation (2.12) and the following discussion;
it is not closure of the whole effective action.

## Cosmological branches

[Gümrükçüoğlu, Heisenberg, Mukohyama and Tanahashi, *Cosmology in
bimetric theory with an effective composite coupling to matter*](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1501.02790),
equations (3.3), (3.9)–(3.12), supplies the corresponding lapse/scale,
Friedmann/null and pressure-factor branch dictionary. Their scale ratio
`A` equals this gate's `y`; their lapse/scale ratio `r` equals `c/y`.
Their `m^2 M_g^2 J` equals this gate's dimensional polynomial `P`.
The function called `P(X_chi)` in that paper is the scalar Lagrangian,
not our interaction polynomial. Section 3.2 already discusses an
effective-metric bounce on the pressure branch. This is compatible with,
not excluded by, the old separate-matter theorem. Their scalar UV kinetic
sign results do not establish this gate's full coupled gradient stability;
the conclusion explicitly leaves scalar gradient stability unresolved.
The exact free example and local potential reconstruction here require
their own checks rather than inheriting a generic stability verdict.

## A finite EFT boundary, not exact generic ghost freedom

[de Rham, Heisenberg and Ribeiro, *Ghosts & Matter Couplings in Massive
(bi-&multi-)Gravity*](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1409.3834), particularly the
Hamiltonian analysis and EFT discussion, identifies the loss of the
generic constraint for the composite metric coupling. Absence of a BD
mode in minisuperspace or its initial perturbations does not prove generic
nonlinear ghost freedom. Conversely, a mode above an EFT cutoff need not
invalidate every low-energy prediction. Its safe-scale discussion is not
a computed cutoff for the specific rolling backgrounds constructed here.

[Matas, *Cutoff for Extensions of Massive Gravity and Bi-Gravity*](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1506.00666),
equations (1.2)–(1.3) and section 5.3, derives a mixed vector–scalar–matter
operator and the parametric upper bound
`Lambda_cutoff ≲ (alpha*beta)^(-1/5)*(m^3 M_Pl^2)^(1/5)` in its
normalized flat-vacuum setup. This is an upper bound, not a guaranteed
safe scale for our branch. The source normalizes the flat spin-2 mass
and coupling variables differently; no numerical cutoff or mass dictionary
for the present rolling solutions has been computed. Curvature,
physical frequencies, scalar background gradients, kinetic normalization
and higher operators all require a compatible hierarchy. Taking `M*tau`
large alone does not check every such condition. The source also warns
that the metric and unconstrained vielbein formulations can differ once
the matter coupling changes the symmetrization condition.

## A completion changes higher-source terms and the physical map

[Lüben and Schmidt-May, *Ghost-free completion of an effective matter
coupling in bimetric theory*](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1804.04671),
sections 4–5, constructs a trimetric origin whose small-matter-source
expansion reproduces the composite coupling at leading order. Higher
source-order terms and corrected symmetrization constraints are essential.
In particular, equation (4.16) modifies the vacuum constraint. It is
not the bare composite action used here, nor a guarantee that its
leading-order bounce survives with negligible corrections. A matching
calculation would have to keep the completed physical matter metric and
quantify the source expansion and omitted terms on the actual solution.
This is a possible further parent problem, not a completed UV result for
the present candidate.

None of these sources establishes global CD/free-M1 matching, the desired
full rolling EFT hierarchy, or positivity closure for this gate. Those
remain research obligations rather than user-intervention blockers.
