# Primary-source and proof boundary

[Schmidt-May, arXiv:1409.3146](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1409.3146),
equations 4.11–4.13 already derive the proportional GR branch with
matter. Equations 4.15 and 4.21–4.22 establish the massless
physical-source response and decoupled linear massive field; 4.20
rescales to the physical metric. Section 4.3, equations 4.23–4.35,
also develops nonlinear physical-metric variables, retaining interactions.
Equation 2.7 gives the vacuum-energy shifts of all beta coefficients.
These are prior results, not discoveries claimed by S6.12.

Our specialization has the paper's `a=b=c=alpha_s=1`,
`m_g²=m_f²=M²/2`, `mu⁴=M²m²/2`, after convention conversion.
Its `M_P²=M²/4` corresponds to our `M_eff²=2M_P²=M²/2`
with the respective stress normalizations. The shifted-beta physical
mass is likewise `m²/4`. Our separately proved symmetric off-shell
Cayley branch, conditional functional continuation and CD/M1 budgets
do not imply generic nonlinear equivalence to GR or assert novelty.

[Hassan, Schmidt-May and von Strauss, arXiv:1303.6940](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1303.6940),
section 2.1, equations 2.2–2.5, and Appendix A.2–A.4 distinguish genuine
stationary elimination with boundary/source data from eliminating a
different field's equation and then varying a substituted action. Equations
2.6–2.12 give the bimetric interaction and proportional-vacuum mass context.
Its higher-curvature expansion is not imported as a massive-eigenmode
matching theorem for our physical composite variable. Our Cayley/parity
branch and its functional assumptions are derived separately.

[Gümrükçüoğlu, Heisenberg, Mukohyama and Tanahashi,
arXiv:1501.02790](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1501.02790), equations 2.4, 3.3
and 4.5–4.6, supplies the composite metric and tensor-action context
already rederived in the pinned ancestors. Its `-+++` convention and
ratios differ from this repository. We retain the fixed `+---` action,
physical composite proper time, and literal source throughout.

[de Rham, Heisenberg and Ribeiro, arXiv:1409.3834](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1409.3834)
explains why the composite matter coupling does not establish absence of
the BD mode at arbitrary scales, and distinguishes strong coupling from
a cutoff. No cutoff or full-parent quantum-health claim is imported here.

The exact matrix/potential identities, zero-relative classical branch,
connected stationary-branch lemma and CD/M1 error budgets are repository
derivations. The functional lemma uses the implicit-function/continuation
argument stated in full in the proof; finite symbolic examples are not
misrepresented as its formalization. The old source-hashed classification
provides the target action and physical-time background, not a UV verdict.
No global novelty assertion is made.
