# P8 S6.14 — Actual auxiliary-metric tensor-cone screen

This child pins S6.13 without changing it. It studies the same algebraic
auxiliary vierbein action, now allowing the separately named beta4 endpoint
terms. The original source-model no-flat theorem and its countercontrol stand.

The physical metric is **h=u^T eta u**, not its vacuum approximation and not
either Einstein metric. Use the P8(b) signature +---, curvature
R_B=-6(Hdot+2H²), Einstein G_B00=+3H² and EH actions -G R_B/2,-F R_B/2.
This is not the opposite FK curvature convention used in the P8(a) work.
All frames are real, nonsingular and positively oriented, with the parent
symmetrization/root constraints. Lapses and spatial scales are positive.

Constants G,F>0, p_g,p_f>0 and epsilon>0 define the general positive-link
claim; B and beta4g,beta4f are arbitrary real constants. No u EH term or
additional derivative interactions are introduced. Matter acts only on h.
Its homogeneous background has density rho and pressure p; the actual null
stress is n_h=epsilon(rho+p). For the tensor-action claims, matter specifically
consists of homogeneous canonical scalars with a positive field-space metric,
and no independent linear TT anisotropic response. Background isotropy of a
more general medium alone would not supply that last hypothesis.

Two claims are kept separate:

1. The full u equations imply P_g(c_e-1)+P_f(c_f-1)=n_h/2, where
   P_g=p_g a_e/a_u, P_f=p_f a_v/a_u and c_i=a_u n_i/(n_u a_i).
   Hence n_h>0 requires at least one full Einstein tensor principal speed
   greater than one in the actual h frame. This does not exclude an
   asymmetric light-only EFT with its own integration/error contract.
2. On the exact exchange-symmetric branch G=F, p_g=p_f=q>0,
   beta4g=beta4f, e=v=r, u=diag(N,A,A,A)r, the physical common tensor itself
   has c_T=A/N=1+A n_h/(4q)>1. Its coefficients are
   G_T,h=2GN/A³>0 and F_T,h=2G/(NA)>0. The physical source couples to this
   common channel, not only to a removable relative mode. A cosmological
   common channel is not asserted to be a stationary mass pole.

The theorem is conditional on an actual regular background solution. It is
non-vacuous: a separately exhibited free-scalar expanding family solves all
e/v/u and scalar equations locally, with A>1. That family is not CD/M1, a
bounce, a global solution theorem, a cutoff-controlled example or a claim
of scalar/vector health. Its branch has no independently chosen fake lapse.

The external conserved TT probe is distinct from the background scalar.
Its literal source action is integral sqrt|h| j gamma_u. Eliminating u keeps
both the common source and a local j² contact. This is exact at quadratic
order; it does not impose source positivity or select a quantum state.

Optional coefficient-error inequalities are necessary pointwise thresholds,
not computed corrections. They assume the supplied physical-frame error
bounds and positive corrected kinetic coefficient. Finite-band/higher-order
or quantum corrections require their own source/field-map/error calculation.
No cutoff, universal UV exclusion, original CD/M1 operator match, general
trimetric exclusion, or closure of S6/P8 is certified. Negative/mixed links,
nonrolling sources, changed actions and asymmetric light-only reductions
are explicitly outside the stronger common-channel verdict.
