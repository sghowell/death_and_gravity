# Primary sources, conventions and evidence boundary

[Lüben and Schmidt-May, arXiv:1804.04671](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1804.04671),
sections 2.1–2.3, supplies the auxiliary-parent action and inverse-vierbein
source equations. Its selected model removes the physical metric's kinetic
term and keeps only the two lowest link coefficients. The beta4 endpoints
here are the separately named extension, not part of that selection.
Equations 2.13–2.17 are the source-normalization reference; the frozen S6.13
Euler dictionary is replayed. Section 5.1 warns against replacing the sourced
vierbein theory by a metric-only composite action. Its discussion of the
massless mode concerns the relevant proportional setting and is not a
general rolling-background source-decoupling theorem. No cutoff, general
novelty, all-mode health or UV result is imported from the paper.

The weighted cone identity, sourced TT completion, actual free-scalar local
solution, and positive-link common-cone corollary are independently derived
from the specified full action in this child. They do not rely on a printed
cosmological tensor formula or an inference from a flat relative mass.

fixes +---, R_B=-6(Hdot+2H²) and EH=-M²R_B/2. Thus G_B00=+3H².

formulation as well as S6.13 and the old CD/M1 witness used only in the
explicitly conditional velocity comparison. All common-mode and external
probe normalizations are fixed literally in the proof.

The certificate checks exact algebra and immutable source hashes, together
with an independent Fraction/polynomial route and a separately authored
