# Primary-source audit and evidence boundary

The [proof](no-bounce.md) derives its equations from the explicit P8
`+---` action and verifies them independently. The papers below supply
cross-checks and important hypothesis distinctions; we do not claim that
the no-bounce deduction is globally novel.

## Full cosmological equations and polynomial branches

[von Strauss et al., *Cosmological Solutions in Bimetric Gravity and their
Observational Tests*](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1111.1655), equations (2.10),
(2.12), and (2.14)--(2.18), provides the arbitrary-parameter FLRW
equations and the factored Bianchi constraint. Their `-+++` variables
translate as `Y=b`, `X=N_f` after `N_g=1`,
`M_star^2=F/G` and `m^2=mathfrak m^4/G`. Their mixed stress components
are `T^0_0=-rho,T^i_i=p`. We compare the physical equations after this
dictionary rather than importing bare action signs. Equation (2.12)
exhibits both polynomial and dynamical factors; its undivided form is
needed at an extremality. Our lapse-first calculation and pointwise
root proof do not use the quotients in equation (2.13), nor a differentiated
Friedmann equation that could lose an independent acceleration condition.

## Apparent bounce counterexamples and hidden matter

[Capozziello and Martín-Moruno, *Bounces, turnarounds and singularities in
bimetric gravity*](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1211.0214), equations (6)--(15),
allows matter in both sectors and general spatial curvature. Its
reconstruction discussion around equation (17) leaves the hidden matter
equation of state free; it does not impose our pair of matter NEC
conditions. Equations (18)--(19) give the simultaneous-extremality
kinematic relation on the dynamical branch, with the explicit local
restriction `0<N<infinity`. This is compatible with our `P!=0` case
and is not a counterexample to a regular spatially flat NEC theorem.
Their kinematic relation is not used at a polynomial root here. Our
separate root argument is read directly from the full `g` null equation.
We do not adopt broader assertions about singular lapse limits or reconstruct
an admissible matter sector merely from a desired scale factor.

## Interaction NEC is not physical matter NEC

[Baccetti, Martín-Moruno and Visser, *Null Energy Condition violations in
bimetric gravity*](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1206.3814), section 4,
equations (4.2)--(4.8), proves anti-correlation of interaction-induced
effective null stresses between the two metrics. The authors explicitly
distinguish these effective stresses from real matter stress. Our
positive-weight cancellation is the direct common-FLRW specialization,
with exact lapse and scale-ratio coefficients checked from the action.
The deduction of a nondegenerate-bounce obstruction additionally uses
regular common-flat geometry, the Bianchi case split, both Einstein
acceleration equations, and the independent physical-matter NECs.
The primary result therefore supports an identity in the proof; it is
not used as a blanket statement against bimetric cosmology, all effective
NEC violation, or a UV completion.

## What the certificate adds

The artifact packages a specified-hypothesis deduction, exact source and
normalization checks, coefficientwise independent arithmetic, exhaustive
pointwise polynomial-root handling and quantitative CD mismatch. It
neither certifies a literature novelty search nor inherits the restricted
beta1 parent's vacuum health for arbitrary interaction parameters.
No physical quantum state, higher-operator matching remainder, or
general all-bimetric exclusion has been supplied by these sources.
