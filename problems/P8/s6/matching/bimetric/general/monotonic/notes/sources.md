# Primary-source and evidence audit

The new [proof](monotonicity.md) is derived from the exact pinned
arbitrary-beta action and equations. The following source checks support
the equation and hypothesis dictionary; they are not a global novelty
search or a claim that every bimetric parent is excluded.

[von Strauss et al., *Cosmological Solutions in Bimetric Gravity and their
Observational Tests*](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1111.1655), equations (2.12)
and (2.14)--(2.17), supplies the two metric equations and factored Bianchi
constraint. The S6.4 convention audit translates the source's `-+++`
FLRW variables to the P8 `+---` action. This child keeps the same
normalization and physical matter frame. It retains both factors of the
Bianchi constraint; it does not use a divided lapse chart at a root.
The all-time normalized-Hubble identity is independently computed here,
including the derivative of the scale ratio and the arbitrary lapse.

[Baccetti, Martín-Moruno and Visser, *Null Energy Condition violations in
bimetric gravity*](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1206.3814), section 4,
especially equations (4.2)--(4.8), relates interaction-induced null stresses
in the two metrics with opposite signs. These are effective interaction
stresses, not physical matter stress. The exact positive-weight cancellation
used here is its common-FLRW specialization. Our monotonicity deduction
also needs the parent equations, separate physical matter NECs, a regular
connected interval, and the explicit polynomial-root topology argument.
Those additional steps are not replaced by the source's NEC statement.

[Capozziello and Martín-Moruno, *Bounces, turnarounds and singularities in
bimetric gravity*](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1211.0214), equations (18)--(19),
gives the dynamical-branch extremality relation with a positive finite
relative lapse. Its reconstruction discussion permits arbitrary hidden
matter and nonzero spatial curvature and does not impose this theorem's
pair of NEC hypotheses. It is not a counterexample to the present common-flat
result. We do not use its dynamical relation on interior root intervals
or inherit claims about singular-lapse limits. The closed-root-set argument
and the identically-zero-polynomial exception are proved directly here.

The exact decoupled and interacting controls delimit what is actually
monotone. Neither their existence nor any cited paper supplies full parent
perturbation/quantum health, a global completion, or general UV matching.
S6.4 remains an unchanged, narrower input; this child adds the sign-preserving
statement and endpoint-only CD error bound under the same regular-class
hypotheses.
