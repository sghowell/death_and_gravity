# Primary-source and dictionary audit

## Variable coefficients and source placement

[Wood, *A new look at multi-gravity and dimensional deconstruction*,
arXiv:2501.16442v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2501.16442), section 4.1 and
Appendix B, is the source motivating this different parent class. Equations
(4.13)--(4.15) retain interaction-dependent clock forces and modified Bianchi
constraints. Appendix B, especially (B.8), (B.11), and (B.13)--(B.19), separates
Einstein-frame canonical scalars, scalar-dependent interaction coefficients,
and matter coupled to a conformally related physical metric.

Its Einstein-frame construction requires a positive scalar kinetic
coefficient before a real canonical clock can be introduced. In four
dimensions the coefficient is proportional to
`Z/F+(3/2)(F'/F)^2`. Interaction coefficients rescale as
`beta_tilde_n=A_g^(4-n) A_f^n beta_n`. These observations do not imply that
arbitrary chosen coefficient functions arise from the paper's particular
dimensional-deconstruction model. Our local action is specified directly;
no such realization, cutoff or health result is imported.

The source uses mostly-plus conventions and an interaction coefficient
without our explicit factor two. Comparing interaction densities requires
`beta_source=2 beta_here`, in addition to the metric/curvature dictionary.
The source's ordinary matter need not live on its canonical scalar's
Einstein metric. Our fixture expressly takes the physical metric to be `g`
and both canonical fields to be minimally coupled to it. Section 1 of the
proof derives the trace exchange separately to prevent an illicit transfer
of a physical-frame theorem. No generic matter-frame equivalence is claimed.

two-TT/shift normalization, moving-weight canonical operator, exact inner
scaling and Fraction controls are computed from the stated action here.
They are not transferred from a printed cosmological kinetic formula or a
flat-vacuum Fierz--Pauli mass. No priority claim is made for variable-coupling
background reconstruction, scalar-tensor multigravity, or adiabatic analysis.

fixture. A global completion of those functions, a complete cosmological
