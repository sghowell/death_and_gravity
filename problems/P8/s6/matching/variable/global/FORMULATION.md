# Exploratory candidate G1: reciprocal scales and a reconstructed lapse

This action uses the same two positive Einstein terms, canonical clock phi,
free canonical chi and physical metric g as the
[S6.20 formulation](../FORMULATION.md). It changes the coefficient functions
and the f lapse. It does not extend S6.20's frozen conclusion by assertion.

Use physical g time `T=tau*u`, with positive M,tau and `d=1+u^2`. Set

```
a=d^2, b=2/d^2, y=b/a, h=4u/d, N_g=1,
A=(1-u^2)d^10/2=h'/y^3,
z'=(A+sqrt(A^2+2))/2, z(0)=0,
c=N_f=h/z, with its analytic value c(0)=4.
```

Primes are u derivatives, and z is a dimensionless geometric function,
not another matter scalar. In particular `H_f=-z/tau`, whereas the scalar
clock is defined below. The two scalar kinetic energies are specified by

```
n=2(y^3 z'-h')=y^3(sqrt(A^2+2)-A),
chi_T=M/(10tau*a^3), k=n-1/(100a^6),
phi=M integral_0^u sqrt(k(v)) dv.
```

The interaction coefficients are genuine functions of phi through its
inverse, with `beta_n(phi)=M^2/tau^2*b_n(u(phi/M))` and

```
b1=y^3 z'/(c-y), b2=b3=0,
b4=3z^2/2-b1/y^3,
b0=(3h^2-n/2)/2-3b1*y.
```

The intended mathematical domain is all real u. The accompanying note
proves positive k, c>y>0 and global invertibility of phi, rather than
assuming these from point checks. All lapse/scale and scalar equations
are replayed for general z jets before choosing this profile.

This is an exploratory full-background construction, not a witness for
the original single-DHOST-clock C/D ladder. In particular, its extra metric
has an independent tensor sector. Bare canonical scalar positivity and a
positive relative shift coefficient do not establish reduced scalar/vector
health, physical subluminality, or uniform controlled elimination.

The asymptotic algebraic mass loss is a guard against assuming a uniform
positive massive threshold. It does not exclude every decoupling mechanism:
small physical source overlap or another controlled reduction would require
their own estimates. No original row or UV no-completion claim is made.
