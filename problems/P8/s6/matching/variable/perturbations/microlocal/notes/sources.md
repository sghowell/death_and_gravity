# Sources, dictionaries and attribution boundary

The calculation starts from the literal source-aware scalar action and
regular phase map in the frozen local repository, not a published scalar
health formula. S6.20 pins the specified variable-beta action/background;
S6.22 independently audits its ADM action, all lapse/shift constraints and
regular finite-K reduction. The new generic symbol calculation retains
those sources and their time boundaries. It does not import exploratory
G1 or the sibling tensor-response result.

[Cusin, Khosravi and Noller, arXiv:1608.06643v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1608.06643),
section 4.2, equations (4.43)–(4.49), supplies the metric/clock Hessian
organization for its scale-free coupling model. The end of that section
explicitly leaves a complete scalar cosmological stability investigation
for later work. Its common scalar prefactor multiplying constant beta
coefficients is not this reconstructed set of independently varying
beta_n(phi). It uses -+++ and a conformal-lapse parameter: in our physical
g cosmic clock its lapse-speed parameter is c/y, not our N_f=c. No
cosmological cutoff or stability verdict is transferred. The paper also
warns that a Minkowski strong-coupling scale does not automatically give
the corresponding cosmological scale.

[De Felice, Mukohyama and Uzan, arXiv:1702.04490](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1702.04490),
equations (10)–(13), illustrates scalar-dependent beta coefficients with
conformally coupled matter. Its equation (31) applies to the particular
constant-clock de Sitter background considered on pages 4–5, with constant
metric ratio and coincident tensor cones; its two scalar light-speed
statements are explicitly in that setting. The rolling CD-scale family
here is different, keeps free chi minimally coupled to the prescribed g
metric, and has three retained scalar pairs. That condition is not used
as a health oracle. Neither the source's model nor its UV-motivated
interpretation supplies the adopted S6 controlled-matching obligations.

No generic novelty claim is made. The present contribution is the exact
source-specific time-boundary/constraint and uniform-symbol audit of the
pinned local family, with explicit failed-chart and omitted-boundary
controls. The uniform energy estimate is proved directly from the
displayed symmetrizer identity; it does not require a source theorem
about this model's quantum state or nonlinear evolution.
