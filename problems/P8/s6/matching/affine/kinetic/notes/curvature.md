## Homothetic square: an exact spectator

Write Gamma=LC(g)+C+I*A with tr(C_mu)=0. The trace of a matrix
commutator is zero, while tr(LC_mu)=partial_mu log(sqrt(abs(g))). Thus
H_mu_nu=R^a_a_mu_nu=4*(dA)_mu_nu exactly, not merely at quadratic order.
The original projective invariance makes every S6.37 term independent
of A. Adding -zeta_H*H_mu_nu*H^mu_nu/64 therefore gives, after the same
60-component elimination, exactly CD/M1 plus -zeta_H*(dA)^2/4.
For constant zeta_H>0 this is a gravitationally coupled Maxwell field;
locally its remaining gauge is A -> A+d lambda. A=0 preserves the
background, but the unresolved quotient has acquired no kinetic term.
This construction provides neither a heavy gap nor new UV control.
Field-dependent zeta_H would be a different interacting action, still
not a kinetic promotion of those 60 components.

The curl and Maxwell mechanism have the primary-source control
[Poplawski, Eqs.3-4 and Section II](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0709.3652v2).
Our factors follow the declared A=tr(kappa)/4 convention.

## Trace-free curvature trace form: incompatible physical signs

Consider only the explicitly specified invariant

    -zeta/4 tr(Rhat_mu_nu Rhat^mu_nu),

At a timelike isotropic point, the homogeneous time-derivative density
is zeta*sum_i [tr(dot(C_i)^2)-(tr(dot(C_i)))^2/4]/2 before imposing

norm of matrix entries. Its full 64-dimensional Hessian acts by
transposing the first two indices and subtracting one quarter of
their trace for spatial last index i; it is zero for last index zero.
The full projective-kernel identity is checked before the two
traceless physical sectors are substituted.

Section 6.3 and Eqs.6.26-6.34, classifies particular constant-coupling

kinematically torsion-free or metric-compatible theories from the
unrestricted metric-affine theory. Neither their constraint count nor
