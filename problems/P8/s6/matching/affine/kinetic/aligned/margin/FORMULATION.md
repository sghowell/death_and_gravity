# P8 S6.56 — Background-preserving clock-cone margin candidate

This is a **new named action**, not a revision of S6.42--S6.55.
It follows the adopted S6 permission for recorded on-tube corrections
with explicit bounds. Original P8 remains open.

## Literal change

Add to the original physical-metric Lorentzian scalar density

    Delta P=(M²/tau²) epsilon_margin (x+1)²/h²,
    h=(1+u²)³, u=phi/tau, x=g^{mu nu} partial_mu phi partial_nu phi,
    0<epsilon_margin<=1/100.

All other terms, the aligned vector, mass functions, finite matching
prescription and physical matter metric are unchanged. On the exact
clock x=-1, Delta P and its first variations vanish. Thus the old
complete classical bounce and free-matter history remain exact.
The additional lower scalar is independent of the affine connection.

## Established scope

At classical quadratic order this changes only J to
J+4 epsilon_margin/h² in the lapse-square coefficient. The
two physical scalar squared principal speeds are

    1, J/(J+4 epsilon_margin/h²).

The clock speed is strictly below one and positive at every finite
time. The matter direction stays exactly luminal. Tensor and vector
principal cones are unchanged. Both the punctured unitary chart and
the regular gamma chart are derived from the same action, including
the moving canonical boundary. The latter covers the bounce.

The full nonlinear lower-scalar addition introduces no auxiliary
velocity or spatial derivative. The joint rolling lapse/temporal
Hamiltonian Hessian becomes diag(-2[J+4 epsilon_margin/h²],-1).
S6.45's local nonlinear constraint-count argument therefore persists:
seven physical modes including free matter, not a nonlinear stability
or well-posedness assertion.

For epsilon_margin=10^-6, the fractional classical light-kinetic
increase is below 3*10^-6 at all times. The clock-cone margin exceeds
10^-7 on |u|<=1/2. The unchanged S6.55 free-vector observable bounds
still apply on this background.

## Quantum diagnostic, not a quantum verdict

The already fixed finite vector potential at mu=m0 has
B_xx=2392/(6561 h²)>0. Its Lorentzian contribution has negative
P_xx. At M*tau=10^12,m0*tau=1000 it is less than one billionth
of the new positive P_xx=2 epsilon_margin/h² in magnitude.

This comparison concerns only that local potential jet. It is not
the constrained, state-dependent quantum principal symbol. The
finite potential also has nonzero background/first variations;
they are not silently cancelled by new counterterms. Other local
operators, causal nonlocal response, quantum-corrected background,
constraints and the unprotected luminal matter direction remain
to be controlled. No full quantum cone, cutoff, V/G/B or P8
closure is asserted.
