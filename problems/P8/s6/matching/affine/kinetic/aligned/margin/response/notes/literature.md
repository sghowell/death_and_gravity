# Quantum-feedback boundary and next calculation

The current result solves the classical linearized operator
driven by the already computed vector stress on the fixed
classical history. It does not replace that stress by its
value on the response metric or reprepare the Gaussian state.

[Anderson, Molina-Paris and Mottola, section III](https://arxiv.org/html/gr-qc/0209075)
derive a causal metric response containing both a local contact
term and a retarded stress-commutator integral. Their
renormalization discussion requires treating those pieces
together. The detailed worked stability example is a scalar
field in Minkowski space, not this clock-mass Proca bounce.
This supplies a structural warning, not a numerical bound or
an imported stability theorem for the present model.

[Meda and Pinamonti](https://arxiv.org/html/2201.10288v2)
study a scalar semiclassical toy system whose retarded inverse
and stability depend on its specified parameters and background.
That result is likewise not a Proca-on-bounce feedback estimate.

The next missing component is the actual causal second
functional variation, with the same clock-mass functions,
physical metric, finite counterterms and a compatible
Hadamard preparation on perturbed histories. Its local
contact terms, full history and constraint coupling must
be retained. The scalar operator norm derived here can be
used only after that quantum-response map has a justified
norm in compatible function spaces.

Small source values alone establish neither a contraction
of that complete map nor a controlled nonlinear residual.
No counterterm, growing causal branch or quantum response
has been deleted in the present fixed-source calculation.
