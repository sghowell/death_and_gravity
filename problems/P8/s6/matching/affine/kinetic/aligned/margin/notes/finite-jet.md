# One fixed local vector-potential jet

Keep the same finite prescription and mu=m0 as S6.53--S6.55.
The finite vector potential is Euclidean
m0^4 B/(64pi²), so its Lorentzian lower-scalar term has the
opposite sign. The frozen actual mass-function calculation gives

    B_N=-22/(27h),
    B_NN=2(8019h+4784)/(6561h²).

Since the physical source variable is x=-N^-2,

    B_x=B_N/2,
    B_xx=(B_NN+3B_N)/4=2392/(6561h²)>0.

The 1/h terms cancel only if the nonlinear lapse-to-x chain
rule is kept. Thus the specified finite potential has negative
Lorentzian P_xx; its sign is not an unknown order-one constant.

For an isolated scalar lower term P(x) at fixed physical
metric, perturb phi=u+pi and expand
delta x=-2 pi_dot-pi_dot²+|grad pi|². In the convention
L2=K pi_dot²/2-G |grad pi|²/2 this gives

    K=-2P_x+4P_xx, G=-2P_x, K-G=4P_xx.

This identity explains the diagnostic. It is not the constrained
DHOST-plus-matter principal matrix.

The new tree term contributes +2epsilon/h² to P_xx.
In normalized light-action units the loop has weight
R^4/(64pi² L²), L=M*tau,R=m0*tau. Using pi²>9, its
negative P_xx divided by the added positive one is bounded by

    R^4/(576 L²) *1196/(6561epsilon).

At epsilon=10^-6,L=10^12,R=1000 this is below 10^-9.
No finite counterterm or state is changed to obtain this bound.

The retained free-vector operator, exact clock, mass/source
variations and physical readout are unchanged by the added
lower scalar. Accordingly S6.55's new Hadamard state and its
five vector-only first-variation bounds carry over unchanged.

The full quantum problem remains separate. The finite potential
has nonzero background and first variations; this candidate
does not cancel them. Its other local derivative terms and
causal state-dependent second functional variation must be
combined with corrected background and constraints. Matter's
classical cone remains saturated. The single negative P_xx
neither proves a superluminal quantum mode of the old model
nor establishes quantum causality for the new one.
