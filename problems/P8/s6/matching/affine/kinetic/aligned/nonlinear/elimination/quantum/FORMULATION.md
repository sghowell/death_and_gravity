# S6.47: vector-only local one-loop coefficient and scale bound

Original P8 remains OPEN. The S6.42 action and matter frame are
unchanged; S6.46 and its ancestry are rebuilt. No finite counterterm
or physical operator is added to a frozen action.

Calculate the retained-vector determinant at fixed light fields,
canonically normalizing W by sqrt(zeta_physical). Set m0^2=M^2/zeta_physical,
a=1/gamma_t and b=1/gamma_s. The physical timelike rest-frame calculation
retains all four components and the temporal/longitudinal constraint.
Its constant-coefficient Euclidean determinant is

    (omega^2+k^2+b*m0^2)^2*a*m0^2
        *(omega^2+(b/a)*k^2+b*m0^2).

This is a local coefficient calculation, not a claimed global vacuum
background or solution. At generic timelike light fields a,b depend
on phi,X. Zero background derivatives here means no curvature or
derivatives of the mass tensor; it does not remove the scalar X
dependence already in that tensor.

Use d=4-2epsilon_DR dimensional regularization and modified minimal
subtraction. Continue the transverse multiplicity as d-2, with one
longitudinal mode. Scaleless contact determinants vanish in this
specified prescription only. For L=log(m0^2/mu^2), the pole coefficient
and finite local potential per physical volume are

    V_pole=-m0^4*C/(64pi^2 epsilon_DR),
    V_MSbar=m0^4*B/(64pi^2),
    C=2b^2+a^(3/2)b^(1/2),
    B=2b^2[L+log(b)-1/2]+a^(3/2)b^(1/2)[L+log(a)-3/2].

On the actual clock a=b=1, C=3 and B=3L-5/2. Their nonzero first
clock N derivatives, and exact second derivatives at fixed u, show
that source alignment does not make this coefficient a spectator.
These are coefficient jets, not the full quantum lapse Jacobian or
stress tensor. Finite local counterterms and scale changes matter.

On the original closed X tube and at mu=m0, C<4 and |B|<4. Therefore
|V_MSbar|/(M^2/tau^2)<R^4/(144 Lp^2), where R=m0*tau and Lp=M*tau.
An explicit R=1000, Lp=10^12 gives normalized zeta=10^-6,
m0/M=10^-9 and this local ratio <10^-14. The denominator is a
reference classical density scale, not a lower bound on an observable
energy that could vanish at the bounce.

No full curved-background/derivative-expansion or quantum remainder,
quantum-corrected solution or constrained spectrum, interacting cutoff,
Lorentz-invariant vacuum or finite-gravity Regge bound, V/G/B or original
P8 closure is claimed. The affine complement, light/gravity loops,
measure/subtraction choices and finite matching counterterms remain
separate. No naturalness restriction or UV completion is inferred
from this vector-only, scheme-explicit local calculation.
