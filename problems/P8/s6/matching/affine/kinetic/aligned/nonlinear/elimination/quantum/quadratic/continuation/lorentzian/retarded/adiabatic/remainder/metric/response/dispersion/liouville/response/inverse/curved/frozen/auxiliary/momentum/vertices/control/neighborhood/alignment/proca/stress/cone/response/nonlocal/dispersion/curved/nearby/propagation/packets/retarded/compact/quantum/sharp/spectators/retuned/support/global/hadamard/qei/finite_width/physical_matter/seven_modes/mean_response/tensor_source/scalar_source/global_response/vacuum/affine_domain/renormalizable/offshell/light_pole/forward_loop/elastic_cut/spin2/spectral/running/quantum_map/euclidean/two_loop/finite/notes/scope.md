# Integrated finite-subsector progress, not two-loop closure

This child bounds actual integrated bare two-loop graph
contributions. It is stronger than an interior denominator
test: its positive sector majorant controls the full parameter
domain and the forward derivatives of all 88 selected graphs.

It does not evaluate their exact finite amplitudes or assert
the sign of their sum. It does not include the 104 refinements
with ultraviolet subgraphs, the counterterm insertion diagrams,
the finite conversion to the fixed parent prescription or the
complete two-loop light mass, residue and LSZ corrections.

The full two-loop coefficient therefore remains open. The
next task is to construct and bound the subtraction-dependent
part with the already fixed finite counterterms. Their
contribution cannot be inferred from the finite subset's size.

No finite number of loop calculations by itself bounds all
higher-loop errors, proves a quantum ultraviolet completion
or supplies an all-energy complex contour. Finite-gravity
IR/Regge control and Delta_grav remain unresolved.

The canonical vacuum does not automatically transport quantum
states, absolute counterterms, cosmological observables or
corrected cones to the affine construction. A common controlled
propagating bounce parent and original V/G/B remain open.
Scoped P8(a) and A.20-A.23 are unchanged. Original P8(b),
and therefore original P8, is not finished or closed.
