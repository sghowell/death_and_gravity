# Primary-source cross-check and limits

[Fuentes-Martin et al., A Guide to Functional Methods Beyond
One-Loop Order](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.12270v2),
JHEP 08 (2025) 099,
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP08(2025)099), Section 2,
equations (2.7)-(2.9), treats mixed statistics, the
counterterm insertion and the Legendre removal of
reducible terms. The Euclidean Gaussian derivation
and this model's operator/flavor expansion are
provided independently in this checkpoint.

[Fuentes-Martin, Palavric and Thomsen, Functional Matching
and Renormalization Group Equations at Two-Loop
Order](https://arxiv.org/html/2311.13630v2), Section 2.2,
explains why lower-order terms proportional to
epsilon can leave finite higher-loop matching
contributions. The explicit single- and double-pole
products here implement that accounting warning.

Only these loop-organization and regulator points
are used. Neither paper's matching theorem supplies
this candidate's unevaluated integrals, high-energy
amplitude bounds, gauge spectrum, cutoff, gravity
or bounce dictionary. No all-orders UV construction
is inferred. The finite algebraic checks and written
arbitrary-index argument are not proof-assistant
formalization or independent peer review.
