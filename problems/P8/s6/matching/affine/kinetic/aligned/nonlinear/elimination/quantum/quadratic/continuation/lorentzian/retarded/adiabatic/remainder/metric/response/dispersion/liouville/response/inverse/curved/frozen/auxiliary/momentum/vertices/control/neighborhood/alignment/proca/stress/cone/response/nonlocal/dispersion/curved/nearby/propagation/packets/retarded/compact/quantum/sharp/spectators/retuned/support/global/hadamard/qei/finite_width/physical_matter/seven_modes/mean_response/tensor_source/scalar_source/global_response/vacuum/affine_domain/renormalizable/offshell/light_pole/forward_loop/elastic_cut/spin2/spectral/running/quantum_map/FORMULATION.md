# S6.118: finite-order quantum map of the full matched vacuum

Original P8 remains OPEN.

Starting with the same local two-field polynomial vacuum of S6.110,
the fixed on-shell/counterterm prescription of S6.112-S6.114, and the
literal cubic derivative map R of S6.111, define the full transformed
action by S'[Psi,H]=S[Psi+R(Psi),H]. Do not replace this action by its
quartic derivative truncation.

The statement is perturbative, through one loop in the flat vacuum.
It includes every generated vertex needed for four-light scattering,
all transformed parent counterterms, the regulated Jacobian and
physical sources. An independent contraction of the actual
four-dimensional jet polynomial checks the composite field's
one-particle mixing. Its dimensional continuation is subtracted before
taking dimension four.

The resulting properly normalized light-particle amplitude agrees
through one loop with the full polynomial parent. Hence S6.113's
coefficient bound and S6.114's cut transfer to this full matched action.
An explicit positive residue and local inverse-propagator disc bound
supplement the source/LSZ argument.

This is not a quantum equivalence theorem for the bare S6.109
truncated target, a global differential inverse, a finite-gravity
field map, a rolling-state construction or original V/G/B closure.
Continuous functional reasoning is a written perturbative proof,
not proof-assistant formalization or a nonperturbative measure.
