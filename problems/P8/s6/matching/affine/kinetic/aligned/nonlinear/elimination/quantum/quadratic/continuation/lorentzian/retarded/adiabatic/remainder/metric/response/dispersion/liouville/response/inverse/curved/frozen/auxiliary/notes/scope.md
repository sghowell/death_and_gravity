# What the quantitative auxiliary domain does and does not provide

S6.45 proved a nonempty local nonlinear canonical-jet
neighborhood by compactness and the implicit function
theorem. S6.75 now supplies explicit sufficient radii,
a uniformly convergent actual root and a continuous
Taylor remainder for the full post-temporal Hamiltonian.
The original source, boundary primitive, full scalar
function and spatial terms are kept.

This advances an interaction-control prerequisite. It
does not supply a physical frequency cutoff. The nine
invariants contain momentum densities, their divergence,
squares of fields/gradients and spatial curvature.
Translating their polydisc into a canonical mode or
wave-packet norm requires the spatial momentum constraints
and the full geometric field-to-invariant map.

In particular, invariant polynomial degree and physical
field degree differ: several invariant coordinates are
already quadratic in fields, and the metric factors and
curvature have their own nonlinear expansions. The
coefficient bounds are not advertised as cubic or
quartic physical scattering amplitudes.

To continue toward a controlled interacting band, the
next steps are the full spatial momentum reduction with
matter and vector generators, canonical normalization
and finite-measure hard-channel estimates. Any claimed
frequency interval also needs a stated time window,
infrared/transfer domain, interaction order and omitted
operator/loop budget. The old D-only M0 hard-tree result
cannot be silently transferred to this CD-plus-matter-
plus-retained-vector parent.

This checkpoint concerns the classical tree Hamiltonian
plus the established margin. The background-cancelling
tadpole and retained Gaussian response are a separately
tracked quantum order; the latter makes the actual
coupled response nonlocal. Nothing here turns those
quantum equations into two local algebraic constraints
or removes the frozen pole diagnosed in S6.74.

The radii are conservative sufficient bounds, not
necessary or optimized scales. They neither assert
stability of the evolving bounce nor exclude it.
No finite Wilson matching, V/G/B verdict, or original
P8 closure follows. These remaining mathematics do not
constitute a user-intervention blocker.
