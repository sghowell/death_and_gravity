# What this finite-order result establishes

The complete one-loop four-point function of the **specified polynomial
vacuum model**, in the specified subtraction scheme, has a controlled
forward coefficient. The exact upper bound includes every channel,
the full nonlocal heavy propagators, mixed loop topologies, external
on-shell normalization and the allowed local counterterms. Together with
the actual full tree exchange, its b2 remains strictly positive.

This is a substantive finite-order matching estimate: small couplings
alone were not enough, because the desired tree coefficient is exceptionally
small. The proof explicitly preserves the low-momentum vertex cancellation
and the high-momentum ultraviolet subtraction. It is not merely a finite
sampling of loop momenta or an assumed order-one error.

It does NOT establish:

- A bound on all higher-loop corrections or a nonperturbative all-energy
  unitary/causal completion of the four-dimensional polynomial theory.
- A globally controlled absorptive spectral density through the unstable
  heavy resonance, a high-energy complex contour or the complete V test.
- A finite-gravity infrared/Regge remainder or the G test.
- Quantum equivalence under the derivative-dependent classical field map,
  a common propagating parent on the rolling bounce, corrected cosmological
  cones, absolute coupled sources or the B test.
- Exclusion or completion of an entire original P8 ladder row.

The domain is a specific holomorphic subthreshold disc, not the much
larger classical tree spectral window. No squared bare unstable-resonance
integral is used. No old state, fixed tadpole, counterterm choice or frozen
action is reassigned to this model.

The continuum analyticity, angular averaging, dominated differentiation
and Cauchy arguments are explicit written proofs with exact algebraic
inputs. They are source-pinned and regression-tested, not proof-assistant
formalized or peer reviewed. All ordinary scientific calculations use
unmodified SymPy. Original P8 remains OPEN.
