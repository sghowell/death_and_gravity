# Targeted primary-source boundary check

[Criado and Perez-Victoria, arXiv:1811.09413v2](https://arxiv.org/html/1811.09413v2),
sections 2-4, distinguish a perturbative field change from
discarding an equation-of-motion term. They also discuss
higher-order terms and the source/renormalization issues
in quantum matching. Those distinctions are relevant here:
we reconstruct the full pointwise current and actual map,
bound its generated higher field powers, and make no
automatic quantum-transfer claim. The model-specific R,
current and error constants are independently derived
and checked in this package.

[Henning, Lu and Murayama, arXiv:1604.01019v1](https://arxiv.org/html/1604.01019v1),
section 3.1, provides the nonlocal heavy-integration
context reviewed in S6.110. Its distinction between a
full heavy inverse and a finite local truncation is
retained in the exact remainder here.

Neither source supplies a finite-gravity or bounce-parent
result for this candidate. No theorem about full UV
completion is invoked. The written continuous-domain
arguments are source-pinned but not proof-assistant
formalized or peer reviewed.
