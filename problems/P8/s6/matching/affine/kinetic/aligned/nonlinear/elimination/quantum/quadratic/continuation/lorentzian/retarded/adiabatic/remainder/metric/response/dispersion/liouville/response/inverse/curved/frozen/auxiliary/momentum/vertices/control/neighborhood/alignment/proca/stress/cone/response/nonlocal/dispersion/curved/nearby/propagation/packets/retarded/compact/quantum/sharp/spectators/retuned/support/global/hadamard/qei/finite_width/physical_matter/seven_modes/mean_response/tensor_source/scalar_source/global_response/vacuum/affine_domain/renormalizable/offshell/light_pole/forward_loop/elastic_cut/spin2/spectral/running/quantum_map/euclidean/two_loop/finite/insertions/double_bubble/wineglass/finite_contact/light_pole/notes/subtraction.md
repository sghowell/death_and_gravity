# Complete local insertion and on-shell ledger

Use Pi_R(s)=Pi(s)-Pi(1)-(s-1)Pi'(1) at each light loop order.
All regulated local pieces are grouped before taking bounds or
removing the regulator. M and g are the fixed canonical heavy
parameters, not a stable-heavy-pole on-shell scheme.

Let I0bar=I0/(16 pi^2), the same entire regulated mass-one
zero-external bubble used in S6.113. Its inherited total
canonical interaction counterterms obey

    delta g = L g I0bar,   delta M = g I0bar/2,
    delta L = 3 L^2 I0bar/2.

The once-fixed extra finite quartic contact from S6.125 remains
present. No new finite g or M condition is introduced here.

The reduced one-loop two-point function has the form
(-L+g/M)T/2 + g B(s,M), where T is the regulated light
tadpole and B is the full light/heavy bubble. If
B2=-partial_M B, its full local parameter variation is

    (-delta L+delta g/M-g delta M/M^2)T/2
      + delta g B - g delta M B2.

The first part is p independent and vanishes under outer OS.
The remaining part is +Lg I0bar B-g^2 I0bar B2/2.
This cancels exactly the proper UV pieces of the six mixed
and three same-heavy sunset refinements. Accordingly their
proper light bubble is I_R(Q)=I(Q)-I0bar, not I(Q).

The exact squared sunset vertex divided by six partitions into
local L^2/6, total mixed weight -Lg, same-heavy g^2/2 and
different-heavy g^2. The mixed and same-heavy integrated
representations below keep these full weights. Local sunset
proper contractions merge both external vertices and leave
p-independent tadpoles; outer OS removes them. Legal
overlapping/nested forests are enumerated, not replaced by
products of incompatible cores.

For the nested family, first combine the entire actual inner
one-loop self-energy and its on-shell quadratic counterterms.
Its p-independent tadpole pieces vanish in that combination.
On a Euclidean line z=k^2+1 the inserted multiplier is exactly
the inherited Q(z)=Pi1_R(1-z)/z=alpha+R(z). The outer
one-loop expression is one half integral V_M Q(z)/(k^2+1).

The alpha part is alpha times the complete one-loop two-point
function. Outer OS makes it alpha Pi1_R(s). The R part's
outer local and zero-transfer-heavy terms are p independent;
apply OS to their common regulated integrand before its
regulator limit. The remaining two outer heavy channels have
total coefficient g and the convergent radial bound below.

The finite inner kinetic subtraction makes alpha nonzero.
Its insertion can generate an overall logarithmic divergence
even in a raw graph whose original overall degree was negative.
It must not be discarded using the raw UV inventory.
The explicit alpha Pi1_R grouping and new outer OS mass/kinetic
counterterms remove precisely this subtraction-generated local
piece. The R remainder is estimated only after the split.

The inherited delta L and once-fixed finite quartic contact
insertions into the one-loop two-point function are local tadpoles;
their outer OS projection is zero. Heavy one-point counterterms
also give local light quadratic terms, and their allocation with
the light mass counterterm does not affect Pi_R. All four
p-dependent nested mixed refinements are represented by the
actual inherited Q; none has an additional proper UV core.

New two-loop light mass and kinetic counterterms impose the
same two on-shell conditions. All interaction counterterms
are total canonical-field ones: independent bare coupling
relations absorb field factors, as in S6.113. No separate
external tree rescaling is inserted into this two-point result.
An independent complete four-point/LSZ ledger remains a
separate deliverable.
