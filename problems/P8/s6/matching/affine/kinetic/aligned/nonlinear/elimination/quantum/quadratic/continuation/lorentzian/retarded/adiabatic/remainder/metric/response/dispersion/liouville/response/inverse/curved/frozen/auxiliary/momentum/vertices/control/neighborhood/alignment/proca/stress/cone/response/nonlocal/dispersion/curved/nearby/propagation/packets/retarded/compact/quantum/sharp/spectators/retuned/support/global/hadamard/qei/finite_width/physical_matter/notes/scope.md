# Positivity, dynamics and the remaining source problem

At the center the exact full classical scalar density is

    rho=(ell+dc)^2/(2N^3)+G/(2N), N>0, G>=0.

It is nonnegative. More generally on the actual clock h>=1 and
the positive-lapse branch has a positive conformal factor, so
the exact normal density remains a sum of two nonnegative terms.

Nevertheless a pure tensor canonical momentum PT=A gives
n1=0, n2=(40/81)A^2 and rho2=-A^2/135. There is no contradiction:
the positive background rho0=1/200 is retained in the full
observable. The stationary background lapse Hessian -243/80
is nonzero, so the smooth implicit lapse branch exists locally
in A and remains positive sufficiently near A=0. That argument
does not authorize arbitrarily large A or assert a global
inhomogeneous solution of the nonlinear field equations.
The quadratic phase path keeps free second-order fields zero;
it is not a second-order dynamically evolved perturbation.

Likewise the negative scalar principal configuration and Proca
momentum coefficients refer to a constrained observable's
second variation, not the positive principal evolution
Hamiltonian. They establish no ghost, instability or UV no-go.
S6.99's scalar state is not a newly completed tensor/vector state
and is not automatically a backreacted state of the full theory.

For minimal matter the exact covariant identity is
nabla^mu T_mu,nu=(Box psi) partial_nu psi. In a coupled
perturbative expansion, the second coefficient of its left side
contains

    nabla0 T2 + (delta nabla) T1 + (delta^2 nabla) T0.

The algebraic order check in this child retains these three
terms. It is bookkeeping, NOT a proof that a proposed
renormalized source satisfies the quantum Ward identity.
Second-order mean fields and metric/clock response also enter
when evaluating stress on an actual perturbative solution.
They cannot be supplied by normal ordering rho2 in isolation.

This result supplies actual observable vertices needed by that
next calculation, including nonzero tensor/vector contributions
that linear-lapse truncation would erase. It does not supply
the full quantum state, counterterms, tadpoles, Ward completion
or self-consistent semiclassical solution. Those are research
work, not a new choice-of-assumptions blocker for the user.

The adopted vacuum V, finite-gravity G and common-parent bounce B
tests, full omitted-operator/loop/heavy-threshold control and the
original P8(b) classification remain open. Physical-energy
reconstruction is not itself a necessary-and-sufficient UV
classification certificate. Original P8 is not closed.
