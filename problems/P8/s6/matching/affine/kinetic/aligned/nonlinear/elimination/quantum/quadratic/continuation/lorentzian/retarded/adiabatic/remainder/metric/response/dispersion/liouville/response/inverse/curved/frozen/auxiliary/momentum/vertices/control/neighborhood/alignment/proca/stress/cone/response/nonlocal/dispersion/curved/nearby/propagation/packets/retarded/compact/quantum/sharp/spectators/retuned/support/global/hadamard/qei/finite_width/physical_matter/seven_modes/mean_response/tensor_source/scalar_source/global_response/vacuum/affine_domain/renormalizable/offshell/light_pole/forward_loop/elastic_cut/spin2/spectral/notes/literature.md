# Primary-source context and independent unequal-mass calculation

[Caron-Huot and Tokuda, arXiv:2406.07606v2, section 3.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.07606v2)
use transfer-channel partial-wave unitarity to study matter imprints
on Regge data. Their discussion also identifies unequal external
and internal masses as requiring a separate calculation.

This leaf derives the unequal-mass spin-two vertex cuts directly
from our actual triangles and tree vertices. It does not use the
paper's Regge ansatz or promote the integer-spin calculation into
a complex-spin continuation. No high-energy residue or trajectory
is fixed here.
