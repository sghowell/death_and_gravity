# Why this is not yet the conserved physical stress

For the background metric define only the TEST tensor

    T_test,mu,nu=partial_mu O partial_nu O
                -g_mu,nu*(partial O)^2/2.

In a local orthonormal frame its comoving energy is
[O_u^2+a^-2*|grad O|^2]/2. This is the positive differential-square
expression to which the preceding inequality applies.

The exact covariant identity is

    nabla^mu T_test,mu,nu=(Box_g O)*partial_nu O.

Native differentiation with arbitrary gradient and arbitrary symmetric
Hessian verifies it in normal coordinates. Since both sides are
tensors and every point admits such coordinates, this proves the
covariant identity; it is not just a homogeneous-field calculation.

The complete actual gamma Euler principal matrix is rebuilt as

    K^-1 G=[c^2,0; ell*(1-c^2),1].

Thus Box_g O has leading clock source
q*ell*(1-c^2)*b. At the bounce its coefficient per q*b is 8/6075,
not zero. A genuine free-background Klein--Gordon identity is
therefore unavailable: lower-order terms cannot cancel this
nonzero highest-frequency coefficient for all solutions.

This does NOT claim nonconservation of the full matter stress in
the full covariant coupled theory. That source must include the
metric/clock response, appropriate perturbative orders, and its
renormalization/constraint identities. The calculation proves that
the tensor made solely from the relational linearization and the
fixed metric cannot simply be declared that source.

A reference-state normal ordering gives well-defined smooth
expectation differences and the stated positive quadratic bounds.
It does not supply missing covariant source terms, a conserved
renormalized stress, or self-consistent semiclassical backreaction.
