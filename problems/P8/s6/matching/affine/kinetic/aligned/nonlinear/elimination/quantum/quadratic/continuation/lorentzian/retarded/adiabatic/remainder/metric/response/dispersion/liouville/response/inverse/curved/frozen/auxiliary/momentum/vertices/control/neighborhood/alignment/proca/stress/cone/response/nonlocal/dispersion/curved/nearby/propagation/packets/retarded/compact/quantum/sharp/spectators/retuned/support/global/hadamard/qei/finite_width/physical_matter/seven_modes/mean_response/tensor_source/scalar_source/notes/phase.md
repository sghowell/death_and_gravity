# Exact bridge and a positive difference of the coupled scalar state

scalar density phase. The natural exponential phase has

    Pzeta=p_v+3a^3 ell chi-18a^3 H v,
    Pchi=p_chi+3a^3 ell v.

function is a^3(3ell v chi-9H v^2). Since a^3 ell is
constant, its explicit time derivative is

    F_t=-9a^3(H'+3H^2)v^2.

real u and nonzero k. Independently the full matrices
obey M_natural B=B M_old+B'. Dropping F_t would give
the wrong generator and the wrong background variation.
