# Boundary of the quantum statement

The checkpoint fixes the actual background, quadratic action,
reduced canonical variables, relational linear observable,
source convention and compact probes used by its classical parent.
It proves a new positive scalar Gaussian linear-field state
and state-independent commutator, not a transfer of the old
ordinary-Proca state, stress or profile.

The physical matter cone does not bound the support of the
commutator in this exact reduced quadratic model. To turn this
into a common-parent exclusion still requires a specified locality
property of that parent and controlled matching of its actual
observable and response. In particular, a relational linear
observable in the reduced gravitational system is not by itself
a construction of a nonperturbative local quantum-gravity operator.

The compact probes involve arbitrarily high temporal and spatial
Fourier components. S6.92 isolates a finite spatial contribution,
but its cutoff is not an established interacting EFT validity
scale and its projected source is no longer spatially compact.
The parent's own high-band response must still be bounded;
low-band matching alone cannot rule out a cancelling high band.

No canonical interactions, omitted operators, loop corrections,
heavy thresholds, finite Wilson coefficients or V/G/B common-parent
comparison are supplied by Gaussian CCR quantization. A positive
tempered Gaussian state is not enough for renormalized stress,
energy inequalities, backreaction or realistic-field P8(a).
This is not a proof of a globally complete nearby bounce,
a universal DHOST no-go, or original P8 closure.
