# Independent primary-source normalization

[Schienbein, Staub, Steudtner and Svirina, arXiv:1809.06797v2](https://arxiv.org/html/1809.06797v2)
state the real-scalar factorial conventions and the loop normalization
in sections 2-3. Their equations (24), (64), (70) and (85)
give the one-loop quartic, cubic and scalar-mass tensor coefficients
used for our independent two-field contraction. The discussion
also warns that wave-function mixing and dimensionful parameters
need explicit bookkeeping.

Our actual subtraction-reference change is derived independently
from the already frozen full-model counterterms. The paper's MS
convention is a one-loop logarithmic normalization check, not a
claim that our full on-shell/finite-contact scheme is MS.
No two-loop coefficient or finite matching conversion is imported.
