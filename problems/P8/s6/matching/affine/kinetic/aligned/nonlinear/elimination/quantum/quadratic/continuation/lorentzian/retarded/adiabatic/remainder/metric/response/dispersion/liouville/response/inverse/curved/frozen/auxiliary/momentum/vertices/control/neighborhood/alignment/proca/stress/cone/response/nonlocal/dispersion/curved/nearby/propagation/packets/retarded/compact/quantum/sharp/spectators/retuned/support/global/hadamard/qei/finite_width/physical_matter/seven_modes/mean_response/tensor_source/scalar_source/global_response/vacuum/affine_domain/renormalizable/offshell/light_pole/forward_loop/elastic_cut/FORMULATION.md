# Actual one-loop elastic absorption below the heavy resonance

Use the same canonical mass-one light particle, polynomial interactions
and once-fixed renormalization scheme of S6.110-S6.113. M denotes the
heavy mass squared, not the gravitational Planck scale. Work in the
flat decoupled vacuum and keep gravity out of this calculation.

For real physical s in [4,6] and cosine z in [-1,1], set
t=-(s-4)(1-z)/2, u=-(s-4)(1+z)/2. Compute the elastic one-loop
discontinuity using the exact full tree amplitude at every angle.
The window lies far below M and all heavy-pair thresholds.

Prove a nonzero absorptive weight on an open interval, including the
correct identical-particle phase factor and the continuous threshold zero.
Bound the actual low-energy cut functional
I_[4,6]=(2/pi) integral_4^6 Im A_one_loop(s,0)/(s-2)^3 ds.
Here b2 is a Taylor coefficient, half the second derivative.

Combine that integral with the complete previously bounded one-loop
real coefficient. Do not infer a global dispersive equality, truncate
the heavy exchange inside the cut, or integrate a bare squared unstable
heavy pole. All higher-loop, high-energy contour and finite-gravity
hypotheses remain separate original P8 obligations.
