# A fixed local parent subtraction and its conversion boundary

The inner fermion self-energy is paired with its
complete physical mass/residue subtraction before
forming the spectral propagator insertion. The
remaining triangle and box products are absolutely
convergent, as the spectral estimates below prove.
The bubble product requires one remaining reference.

Let F denote the common regulated paired-insertion
bubble at zero external momentum, including its
1/Q normalization and the same inner on-shell
reference. F is not assigned an unregulated finite
value. Subtract F sum_z C(z)^2 with one set of total
order-two sector counterterms in the original
two-field parent:

    delta L=3 L^2 F,  delta g=2 L g F,  delta M=g F.

Indeed variation of -L+g sum_z (M-z)^(-1) gives
-delta L+delta g sum h-g delta M sum h^2
=-F sum C^2. All of these are local Phi^4, H Phi^2
or H^2 parent terms. There is no independently
adjustable higher-derivative b2 contact. For the
fundamental cubic this sector has delta G=L G F.
The lower-order square G1^2 in the full second-order
conversion remains separately owned by S6.137.

Keep a common regulator through the whole inner
pairing and outer subtraction. In particular the
regulated F owns its dimensional numerator/prefactor
terms, including finite epsilon-times-pole products.
Only the difference with the entire F C^2 reference
removed is passed to the four-dimensional spectral
and parameter representation. Its uniform convergent
majorants justify this limit. Setting epsilon to zero
in an unsubtracted pole would not define this scheme.

For the purely local L^2 bubble, replacing the old
channel-two anchor of S6.135 by the zero anchor
changes only a constant, hence not b2. For the full
nonlocal family a finite change delta F instead has

    delta b2=delta F[-4Lg/(M-2)^3+6g^2/(M-2)^4],

which is generally nonzero. It must NOT be silently
discarded. This checkpoint defines and bounds the
family in the displayed fixed local-parent scheme.
Its finite conversion to the shared S6.133 MS
interaction boundary is not computed here. Nor are
the other two-loop references or canonical shifts.
A bound in this scheme is not yet the complete
order-two matching correction in the common scheme.
