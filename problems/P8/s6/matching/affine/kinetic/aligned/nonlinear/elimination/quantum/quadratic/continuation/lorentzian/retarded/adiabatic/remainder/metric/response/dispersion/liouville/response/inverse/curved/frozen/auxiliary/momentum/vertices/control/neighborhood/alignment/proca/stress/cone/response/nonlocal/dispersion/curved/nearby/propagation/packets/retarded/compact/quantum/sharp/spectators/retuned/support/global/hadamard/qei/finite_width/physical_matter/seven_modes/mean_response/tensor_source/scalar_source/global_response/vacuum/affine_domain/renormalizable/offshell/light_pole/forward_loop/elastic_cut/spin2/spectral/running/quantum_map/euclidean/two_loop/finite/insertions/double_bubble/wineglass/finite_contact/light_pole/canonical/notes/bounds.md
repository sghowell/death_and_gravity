# Actual complete canonical coefficient

Write A=A0+ell A1+ell^2 A2+O(ell^3), in the same scalar
canonical scheme and mass-one external kinematics. At t=0,
u=4-s, define b2=[(s-2)^2]A(s,0). The exact tree amplitude,
with the full heavy inverse, has b2,0=4lambda.

The earlier bounds were deliberately stated as contributions until
the counterterm and external-field ledger was complete. The independent
marked graph, cubic-product, local reference and LSZ arguments now
supply the missing assembly. Thus their exact same positive sum E2
bounds the complete canonical scalar two-loop coefficient:

    |b2,2| <= E2 < 3 times 10^-607,
    E2/(4lambda) < 10^-7.

The inherited complete one-loop error is E1. Exact rational arithmetic
at the actual model point gives

    (E1+E2)/(4lambda) < 10^-6,
    4lambda-E1-E2 > 0.

Accordingly the scalar forward coefficient through two loops lies in
[4lambda-E1-E2, 4lambda+E1+E2] and is strictly positive.
The combined relative error's diagnostic value is about
5.14374554 times 10^-7. The exact rational inequalities, not this
decimal approximation, are the proof gates.

These are the same all-radius integrated bounds on the same
first-sheet forward disc |s-2|<=1. No radial cutoff or heavy
derivative expansion is introduced in the assembly. The light-pole
normalization uses the independent unit disc about s=1:
its factored inverse error through two loops is below 10^-18,
and its canonical pole residue is exactly one at this order.
No additional amplitude error proportional to that upper bound
is needed for an exactly imposed OS normalization.

The bounds are absolute, not a computation of the correction's
sign or exact value. Strict positivity through a finite loop order
does not bound the omitted O(ell^3) term, establish a full
absorptive integral or control an all-energy complex contour.
It therefore does not prove the original UV-admissibility verdict.
The finite-gravity amplitude is not included.
