# Complete global representative and strict center bounce

For the total leading profiles define, exactly,

    N_eta=1+n, hat_a_eta=a exp(xi),
    a_eta=hat_a_eta[(h-1+N_eta^-2)/h]^(-1/4).

Here h=t^3>=1, and the lapse is NOT spatially rescaled.
For any positive N, the frame factor lies between 1 and
sqrt(N), in the appropriate order. Thus its logarithm
has magnitude at most |log N|/2. For |n|<=1/2,
|log(1+n)|<=2|n|, so

    |log(a_eta/a)|<=|xi|+|n|.

The joint estimates give N_eta>1/2 and a_eta>1/2
for all real u (indeed a_eta/a is within exp(10^-10)
of one). These functions are smooth at every finite u.
Their lapse and scale tail bounds therefore give a
complete metric on R times R3: conserved spatial
momenta imply proper-time integrand
N/sqrt(1+P^2/a^2), bounded below by a positive constant
for each timelike geodesic. For null geodesics the
affine integrand is N a/|P|, again bounded below.
Both integrals diverge in both time directions.
The spatially constant lapse introduces no further
coordinate boundary.

Reflection of the ADDED covariance implies even n,xi,
odd trace and matter field, and zero scale velocity
at u=0. The parent's exact frame formula gives

    (log a_eta)''(0)
      =4+xi''-(3/2)[(1+n)^2-1]+n''/[2(1+n)].

For the scalar block |xi''|<40eta_S and
|n''|<200eta_S. Tensor xi'' is nonnegative and
n''>=-30eta_T. Proca xi'',n'' are nonnegative.
These are independently derived parent center jets,
not assumptions of positivity for the scalar correction.
Let L be the exact joint global lapse bound at the
largest amplitudes. Since 1/[2(1+n)]<=1 for N>=1/2,

    (log a_eta)''(0)
       >=4-3L-(3/2)L^2-240eta_S-30eta_T >3.

Its actual proper Hubble derivative divides by N_eta^2
at the center, so remains positive. Therefore the
representative has a strict local bounce and is complete
both ways. Scalar-only bounds are the special case
eta_T=eta_P=0, without any band-average sign assumption.

The exact frame map is used to construct a positive
geometric representative of a FIRST-ORDER mean profile.
This does not turn the truncated profile into a solution
of a finite-amplitude semiclassical equation. Its
nonlinear residual and an interacting quantum state on
this representative have not been computed or bounded.
