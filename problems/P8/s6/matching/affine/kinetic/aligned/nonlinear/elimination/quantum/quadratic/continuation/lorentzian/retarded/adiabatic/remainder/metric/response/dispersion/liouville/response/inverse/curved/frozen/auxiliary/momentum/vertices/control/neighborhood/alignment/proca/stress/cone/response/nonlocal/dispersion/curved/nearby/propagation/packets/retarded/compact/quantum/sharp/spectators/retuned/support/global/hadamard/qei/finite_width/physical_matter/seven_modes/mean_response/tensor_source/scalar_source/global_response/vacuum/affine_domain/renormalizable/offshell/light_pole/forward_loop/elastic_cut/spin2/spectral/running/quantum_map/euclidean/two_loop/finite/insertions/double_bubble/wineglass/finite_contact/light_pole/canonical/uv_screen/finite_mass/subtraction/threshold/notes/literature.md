# Primary normalization input and independent derivations

Stephen P. Martin, *Two-loop effective potential for a
general renormalizable theory and softly broken
supersymmetry*, hep-ph/0111209v2:
[primary paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-ph/0111209),
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.65.116003).
We use only the one-loop fermion normalization in
section 3, equation (3.3), with two Weyl components per
Dirac field, and the finite tadpole convention in
equation (2.17). We do not import a two-loop potential
or any stability claim from that paper.

The candidate field content, dimensional Ward regulator
and marginal-flow tensors are the frozen S6.128-S6.130
inputs. The determinant derivatives, complete higher-field
coefficient formula, two-point trace reduction, grouped
moment anchors, complex remainder and field-reference
map are derived here. The quartic pole plus four
external field factors reproduce the inherited fermion
contribution Q beta_L=8NYL-48NY^2.
This agreement does not equate MSbar and on-shell
coupling values. Written continuum arguments are not
proof-assistant formalized or peer reviewed.
