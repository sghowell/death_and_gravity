# What this selected increment does and does not prove

The local quartic threshold is much larger than the
old extremely small tree forward coefficient, but it
is momentum independent and contributes zero to that
second derivative. The two-derivative operator also
is constant on the equal-mass shell; it is retained
explicitly off shell. The complete higher-momentum
box term is bounded, rather than guessed from small
couplings or truncated after a few powers.

The positive formal and exactly normalized intervals
belong to the selected tree plus one-loop fermion
functional. They do not claim positivity of the box
coefficient alone, full new-model quantum unitarity,
a high-energy dispersion theorem or a bound on all
omitted primitive loop orders.

Old scalar loops and their fixed canonical subtraction
scheme are not included. Their conversion to the
prospective full MSbar boundary requires its own
local-coupling and field ledger before amplitude
budgets can be combined. No old complete two-loop
number is transferred to this new calculation.

The first two-gauge low-cut subtraction remains
unchanged. Its three-parent-loop order is not
promoted or cancelled by this one-loop increment.
No confinement, mass-gap restoration or finite-gravity
IR/Regge allowance is derived.

The bounce field/state/cutoff dictionary and its
error budgets remain separate. No entire function-
group ladder row or general parent class is excluded,
and original V/G/B and P8 remain open. Missing estimates
are ongoing research, not a request to choose an
arbitrary sign or a user-preference blocker.
