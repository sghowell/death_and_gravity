Established at the named reference boundary:

- Complete finite and affine-local subtraction of the first fermion
  self-energy, followed by an unbounded complex-half-plane insertion bound.
- A separate global spacelike envelope retaining logarithmic-over-momentum
  decay of the inserted free propagator.
- Coverage of every real loop momentum in the specified symmetric bubble
  routing on the unit forward disc, with no complex momentum shift.
- A positive selected complete scalar-fermion one-loop Euclidean inverse
  on the explicitly specified finite reference-energy window.

Not established:

- A complete two-loop forest, outer-integral bound, all new-model two-loop
  primitive amplitudes or any later-loop/truncation error budget.
- A justified Wilsonian cutoff, global quantum potential, reflection
  positivity, exact gauge spectrum or full Lorentzian pole classification.
- Sufficient high-complex-energy V contour control or a strict full V verdict.
- A finite-gravity regulated Regge remainder/Delta for G.
- A common bounce-parent field, derivative, state and cutoff dictionary for B.
- Original P8 closure or exclusion of any full old ladder row.

The next research use is an explicitly renormalized outer insertion family,
retaining the decay envelope and all local reference counterterms. The
missing estimates remain research work, not a user-choice blocker.
