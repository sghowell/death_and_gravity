# What this inverse does and does not precondition

The exact flat-vacuum block obeys
B(p;h)=S(h)*B(p;1)*S(h), S(h)=diag(1/h,1).
The spectral matrix, finite matrix, threshold matrix and
inverse endpoint constants independently satisfy this
congruence; it is not a leading-log-only statement.

For a fixed smooth positive function h(t), define an operator

P f=S(t)*B_1[ S(.)*f(.) ](t).

Its zero-past inverse is exactly

P^-1 f=S(t)^-1*Rop_1[ S(.)^-1*f(.) ](t).

Products are multiplication operators, and the inner S stays
inside the convolution. For h in [1,125/64], the C0 operator
norm is bounded by (125/64)^2 times the fixed h=1 inverse norm.
The congruence also transports the instantaneous complement.
No time-dependent coefficient has been pulled through a
convolution or differentiated without its commutator.

This is a definition of a useful causal preconditioner, not
a proof that the actual curved four-times-integrated stress
response equals P plus an integrable remainder. In particular
the S6.71 two acoustic shifts are different, the exact selected
state has nonzero initial squeezing, and the physical output
has contact and normalization terms. Replacing those kernels
by a frozen vacuum kernel would discard required data.

The next mathematical obligation is to derive the ultraviolet
normal form of that *actual* prepared response, after a fixed
number of time primitives, and bound the difference from a
suitable P as a no-loss causal remainder. The S6.70 momentum
correction bound and S6.71 exact covariance/readout map are
ingredients, but neither supplies this stress estimate.

One must then retain the tree, lower-order local and fixed
tadpole terms in the full integrated equation and specify
compatible initial conditions. Even a bounded finite-time
retarded inverse for that equation would not by itself prove
its physical quantum spectrum healthy, transfer off-clock
Hadamard/causal-cone results, or control interactions and the
cutoff. An exact uncut loop resummation is not being promoted
to an ultraviolet completion of the finite DHOST EFT.

No new state, action term, subtraction prescription or
infinite-frequency physical stability postulate is adopted
here. P8(a)'s scoped result is unchanged. Original P8(b),
including V/G/B, and original P8 remain open. The remaining
work is mathematical, not a user-intervention blocker.
