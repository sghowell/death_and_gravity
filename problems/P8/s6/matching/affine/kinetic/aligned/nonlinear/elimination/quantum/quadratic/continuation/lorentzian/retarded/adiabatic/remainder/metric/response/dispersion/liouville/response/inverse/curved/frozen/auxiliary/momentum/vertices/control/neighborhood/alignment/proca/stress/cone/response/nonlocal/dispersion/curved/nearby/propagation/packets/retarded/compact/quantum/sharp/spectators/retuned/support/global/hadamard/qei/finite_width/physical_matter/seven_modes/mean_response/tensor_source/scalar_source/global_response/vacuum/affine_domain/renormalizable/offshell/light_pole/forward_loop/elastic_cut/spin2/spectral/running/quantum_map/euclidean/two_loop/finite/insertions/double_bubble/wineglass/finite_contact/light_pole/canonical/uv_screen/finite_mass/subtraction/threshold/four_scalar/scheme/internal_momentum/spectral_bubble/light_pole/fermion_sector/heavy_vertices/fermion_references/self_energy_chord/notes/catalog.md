# Primitive words and the complete self-energy subset

The quartic background contribution from a contracted
six-vertex fermion loop has four labelled external
Phi vertices and two identical internal boson
endpoints. Fix external label one as cyclic origin.
The two endpoints occupy two of the remaining five
positions; the other external labels have 3!
orders. This gives 10 times 6=60 cyclic words,
retaining both fermion-loop orientations.

Classify by the shortest cyclic distance between
the internal endpoints. The exact counts are
24 at distance one, 24 at distance two and 12
at distance three. The first group contains
proper fermion self-energy subgraphs. The
second contains proper Yukawa-vertex subgraphs.
The last has no such divergent proper subgraph.
Only the first group is bounded here.

The self-energy words are also precisely the
six cyclic first-box orderings with each of
their four internal fermion propagators marked.
This is a literal bijection, not an additional
multiplicity to apply to the 24 words.

The determinant identity makes the normalization
explicit. For the one-loop term
Tr[(S yPhi)^4]/4, replace one S by
-S Sigma_MS S and sum the four positions.
Cyclic symmetry cancels the factor four in
the denominator. Equivalently the first
self-energy variation of -Tr log(D+yPhi)
is -Tr[(D+yPhi)^(-1) Sigma_MS].
The fourth background coefficient agrees
without an extra one half. An independent
noncommuting matrix calculation checks both
routes before the labelled-word enumeration.

The complete 24-word subset is closed under
every permutation of the four external Phi
labels. The active closed color/flavor factor
is N=6. The scalar inner coupling is Y, and
the gauge inner coupling carries a Cf with
Cf=4/3, giving N Cf=8. No extra fourteen-
flavor or bosonic symmetry factor is added.
