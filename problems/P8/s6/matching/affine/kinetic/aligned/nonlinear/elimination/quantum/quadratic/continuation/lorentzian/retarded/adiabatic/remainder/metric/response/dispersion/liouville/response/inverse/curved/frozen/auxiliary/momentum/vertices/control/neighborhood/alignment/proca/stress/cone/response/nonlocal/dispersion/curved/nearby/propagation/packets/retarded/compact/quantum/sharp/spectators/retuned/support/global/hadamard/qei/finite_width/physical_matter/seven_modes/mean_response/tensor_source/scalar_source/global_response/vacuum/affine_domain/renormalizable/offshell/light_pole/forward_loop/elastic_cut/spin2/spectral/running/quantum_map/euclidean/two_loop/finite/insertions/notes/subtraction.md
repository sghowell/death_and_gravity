# The full insertion family and its inherited local subtraction

The outer quartic bubble has summed external-label Wick weight
3/2 over three channels. There are two internal-line insertion
positions, and the one-loop self-energy has its usual contraction
factor 1/2. Their product is 3/2, independently agreeing with
the frozen tadpole-insertion family weight in S6.120.

The outer two quartic vertices have 16 full-heavy choices and
the inner quartic self-energy vertex has four, giving all 64
refinements. Its local Phi^4 tadpole and the zero-transfer
heavy-exchange tadpole are momentum-independent. The fixed
on-shell operation

    Pi(s)-Pi(1)-(s-1)Pi'(1)

annihilates those terms together with every local affine
quadratic counterterm. The two remaining mixed heavy
channels cancel the contraction factor 1/2 and give the
actual S6.112 mixed kernel with prefactor g/(16pi^2).

Local mass terms must be combined before estimating.
Their separate magnitudes are not a bound for the
on-shell-subtracted family.

For each outer channel the complete insertion integrand is

    V1 V2 (Q1+Q2)/(2 D1 D2).

With Q=alpha+R, its constant part is exactly 2alpha times
the same one-loop bubble integrand. Its decaying part
retains both R insertions. The latter is absolutely
integrable by the explicit bounds in the other notes.

Use the S6.113 regulated reference I0 throughout this
family's outer subtraction. In that reference the
large-momentum vertex is C(z)=-lambda4+g/(M-z).
The insertion's remaining ultraviolet term is exactly
2alpha C(z)^2 I0/(32pi^2) in each channel.

Linear continuation of the inherited outer subtraction
therefore uses 2alpha times its already fixed local
full-model counterterms:

    delta lambda4 = 2alpha times 3lambda4^2 F,
    delta g       = 2alpha times 2lambda4 g F,
    delta M       = 2alpha times g F,
    F=I0/(32pi^2).

Literal variation of the tree amplitude verifies cancellation
in all three channels. I0 is the entire regulated mass-one,
zero-external-momentum bubble of S6.113, not an unregulated
finite number or a silently substituted pole-only scheme.

These are total interaction counterterms in the same canonical
renormalized fields. The independent bare coupling relations
absorb the field counterterms; an additional external tree
rescaling is not added to this group. The parent's fixed
finite potential-quartic condition is retained; its contact
has zero second forward derivative. Allocating a
momentum-independent potential contact among diagram
families cannot affect the bound proved here.

Thus this precise grouped renormalized contribution is

    A_insertions = 2alpha A_one_loop,renormalized
                   + A_decaying_remainder

at the level of the forward second coefficient, independently
of that harmless constant-contact allocation. This establishes
the chosen family's subtraction, not the values of all other
two-loop counterterms or the complete two-loop pole/LSZ result.
