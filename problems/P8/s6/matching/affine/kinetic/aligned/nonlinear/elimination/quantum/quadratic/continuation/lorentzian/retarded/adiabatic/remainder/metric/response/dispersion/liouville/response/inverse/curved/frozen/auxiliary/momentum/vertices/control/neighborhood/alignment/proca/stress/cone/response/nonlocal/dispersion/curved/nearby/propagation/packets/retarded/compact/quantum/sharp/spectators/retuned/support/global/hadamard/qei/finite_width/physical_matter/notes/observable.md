# Physical normal frame, not a test-field substitution

The physical spatial metric is e^2 hat_g and its volume is
U hat_volume with U=e^3. Minimal scalar coupling gives exactly

    P_psi=physical_volume times (n_phys^mu partial_mu psi).

Consequently its physical normal velocity is (ell+dc)/U. This
identity is independent of the ADM shift. Contracting the standard
minimal scalar tensor with the physical normal twice, and with
one third of the physical spatial inverse metric, gives the rho
and p in FORMULATION.md. The spatial inverse contributes e^-2;
the kinetic term contributes U^-2=e^-6. These different powers
must not be confused with the hatted Hamiltonian density.

Taylor differentiate in N and the independent invariants, then
assign their physical perturbation orders. The background is
rho0=p0=ell^2/2, and both first variations are

    rho1=p1=ell dc1-3ell^2 n1/(2h).

Their second variations share K2 stated in the formulation.
The exact coefficient of n2 is -3ell^2/(2h), nonzero everywhere
on this finite-time clock. The density-pressure difference is
2G2/3. In contrast, the zero first lapse derivative of a stationary
Hamiltonian removes its n2 term at quadratic order. That
Hamiltonian argument does not apply to this observable.

An independent calculation inserts N=1+n1+n2 directly into the
exact powers e^-6 and e^-2 in the labelled algebra and truncates
only AFTER substitution. It agrees with both reconstructed jets.
The full force vanishes at physical degrees one and two.

Physical units are restored by the inherited action factor:
kappa/tau^4=M^2/tau^2. In particular these normalized matter
coefficients must not be used as a new stress with kappa silently
set to hbar, or combined with a covariance whose hbar/kappa factor
was dropped. No semiclassical expectation is evaluated here.
