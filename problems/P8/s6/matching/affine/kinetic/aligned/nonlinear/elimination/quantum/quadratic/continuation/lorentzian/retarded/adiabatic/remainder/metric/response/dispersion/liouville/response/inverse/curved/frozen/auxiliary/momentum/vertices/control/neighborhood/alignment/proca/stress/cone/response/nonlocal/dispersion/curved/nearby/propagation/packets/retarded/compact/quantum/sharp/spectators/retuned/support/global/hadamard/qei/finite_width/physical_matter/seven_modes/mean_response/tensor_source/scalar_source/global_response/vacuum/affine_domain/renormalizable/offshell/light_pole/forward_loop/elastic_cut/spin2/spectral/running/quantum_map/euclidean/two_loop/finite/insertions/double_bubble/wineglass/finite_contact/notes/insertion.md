# Insert the fixed contact, retaining the same local reference

A finite one-loop quartic counterterm inserted in
a one-loop four-point graph contributes at two
loops. It is the ordinary partial derivative
delta L_fin times d/dL, with g and M fixed.
Differentiate the independent canonical couplings
before inserting the actual matching relations.

For each channel define C=-L+g/(M-z), I_R the
finite light bubble, T_A,T_B the integrated
complete heavy triangles and B the integrated
heavy-box product. Up to a momentum-independent
potential contact, the full one-loop channel is

    A1=C^2 I_R/2+C(T_A+T_B)/2+B/2.

At fixed g,M the triangles and box are independent
of L. The exact variation is therefore

    dA1/dL=-C I_R-(T_A+T_B)/2.

The pointwise two-vertex product and this
integrated identity are checked separately.
Both triangle contributions are retained even
when the channel's external vertices differ.

The momentum-independent contact already inside
A1 may depend on L, but its partial derivative
still has zero second momentum derivative.
That does not make a contact inserted inside
a loop momentum independent. An explicit
pure-quartic-bubble diagnostic exhibits a
nonzero second coefficient from a generic
local insertion; it is not an alternate
parameter choice for the actual model.

The raw insertion also varies the UV reference.
Let F0=I0/(32pi^2), with the entire same
regulated I0 of S6.113. The required linear
continuation of its total local counterterms is

    delta L_2=delta L_fin times 6L F0,
    delta g_2=delta L_fin times 2g F0,
    delta M_2=0.

Their tree variation cancels delta L_fin times
d/dL of the sum of C(z)^2 F0 in all channels.
Every identity is checked against the actual
previous counterterm expressions.

These terms define the same reference operation
on this insertion, not a new fitted b2 contact.
A further fixed two-loop potential contact is
momentum independent and cannot change b2.

The total canonical-field convention is retained:
there is no extra external tree LSZ rescaling
appended to this group. A full quantitative
two-loop light-pole and source-aware normalization
calculation remains a separate obligation.
