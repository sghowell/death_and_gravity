# Independent phase space and bubble-cut normalization

Use S=1+iT and canonical positive-norm scalar states. Perturbative
unitarity of the local real two-field model gives, at one-loop order,

2 Im A_one_loop(s,0)=(1/2!) integral dPhi2 |A_tree(s,z)|^2

for the two identical light intermediate particles. Below s=6 no heavy
pair is accessible. The Phi->-Phi symmetry excludes an odd-light
Phi-H two-particle intermediate state. A single bare H pole is at s=M,
outside the interval. The effective nonlocal vertex is obtained from
this local model; no general unitarity theorem for arbitrary nonlocal
interactions is assumed.

Write E=sqrt(p^2+1), beta=p/E=sqrt(1-4/s). The radial energy delta
function contributes p^2/E^2 divided by 2p/E, namely p/(2E)=beta/2.
Keeping all factors of 2pi, dPhi2/dOmega=beta/(32pi^2).
Azimuthal integration gives dPhi2/dz=beta/(16pi). Including both
the identical-particle factor and the conversion from 2 Im to Im,

rho(s)=Im A_one_loop(s,0)
      =beta/(64pi) integral_-1^1 A0(s,z)^2 dz
      =beta/(32pi) Q(s).

An independent constant-vertex bubble check fixes the same normalization.
For Delta=1-sx(1-x), the negative-Delta parameter interval has endpoints
(1-beta)/2 and (1+beta)/2 and width beta. The discontinuity of
-log(Delta-i0) is positive imaginary pi on that interval.
Multiplying by the independently established bubble factor
C^2/(32pi^2) gives C^2 beta/(32pi), identical to the phase-space result.

All displayed identities and parameter roots are checked natively.
The finite real local counterterms have no discontinuity on this interval.
Inserting one-loop corrected vertices into the cut would be higher order;
the tree amplitude is exactly the required one-loop input.

At s=4 the angular average is finite and beta=0, so rho=0. For every
4<s<=6 the exact amplitude enclosure makes rho strictly positive.
No claim of strict absorption at the threshold itself is made.
