# Relation to the remaining causal-response problem

The exact longitudinal equation now separates a scalar-shaped
high-momentum potential from a momentum-dependent correction.
The latter has a two-source C2 bound with inverse-square momentum
decay, including the prepared acoustic-time shift.

This does not yet prove that the renormalized vector stress equals
a scalar logarithmic kernel plus a bounded no-loss remainder.
That step must vary the original physical readouts, distinguish
the two sector time coordinates away from the clock, and preserve
the fixed local subtraction and initial-state interference.
The factor f in the physical current is especially important.

The logarithmic inversion approach for scalar cosmology in
[Meda, Pinamonti and Siemssen](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.14665)
is relevant motivation: their Section 5 treats derivative loss by
isolating a retarded operator before proving a local solution.
This checkpoint establishes a vector mode reduction, not a
transfer of that solution theorem.

The next analysis can use chi and r_sigma as a regular source
chart and the exact momentum correction as part of a remainder
estimate. It must still construct the coupled matrix inverse
with the full finite complement and classical constraints.
A potential bound is neither that inverse nor a quantum
stability, physical cone, cutoff or common-parent V/G/B result.

No new off-clock ordinary-Proca Hadamard theorem is asserted:
the acoustic mode equation is an exact auxiliary representation
of the same constrained vector, not permission to copy a
different field's state or causal cone. Original P8 stays open.
