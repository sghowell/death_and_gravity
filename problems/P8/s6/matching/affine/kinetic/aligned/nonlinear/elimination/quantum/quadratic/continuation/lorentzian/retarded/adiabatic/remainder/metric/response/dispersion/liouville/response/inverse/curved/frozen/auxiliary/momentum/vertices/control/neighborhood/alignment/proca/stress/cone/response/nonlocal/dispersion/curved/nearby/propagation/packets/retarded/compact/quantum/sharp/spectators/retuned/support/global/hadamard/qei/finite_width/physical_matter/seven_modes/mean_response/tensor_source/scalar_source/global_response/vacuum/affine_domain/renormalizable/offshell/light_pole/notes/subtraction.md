# One consistent finite subtraction conversion

All light tadpole/mass terms are local in s. An arbitrary local affine
a0+b0 s vanishes under f(s)-f(1)-(s-1)f'(1), as checked literally.
Choose the on-shell mass and residue subtraction once for the **new
polynomial vacuum model**. It is not a retuning of an old cosmological
counterterm or a change of the S6.82 state.

At zero momentum the remaining positive shift is

p=Pi_R(0)=g/(16pi^2) integral_0^1 [b-log(1+b)] dx >0.

The derivative of h-log(1+h) is h/(1+h), and its value at zero is zero.
For h>=0, it lies between zero and h^2/2, strictly above zero when h>0.
The parameter gap therefore yields
0<p<g/(96pi^2 M^2)<g/(864M^2)<10^-405.

The potential's quadratic curvature equals -D_OS(0)=1-p, not the
mass-one pole position. Relative to S6.110's curvature-one potential
scheme, first choose the finite residue counterterm delta Z=-Pi'(1)
and then the corresponding finite potential shift -p Phi^2/2. The latter
does not change the quartic normalization condition or the already
subtracted one-loop nonlinear remainder:

V_stationary=(1-p)Phi^2/2 + delta Phi^4/24 + V1_R(Phi).

Here delta>0 and 0<=V1_R(Phi)<10^-815 Phi^6 were established by the
full-Hessian calculation in the parent, with strict positivity for Phi!=0.
Hence this truncated once-converted constant-field potential still has its
unique global minimum at zero and positive quadratic curvature.

This is perturbative bookkeeping at one loop: inserting a one-loop
counterterm into the one-loop Hessian would be a two-loop contribution,
which is not made or bounded here. The wave-function counterterm is treated
in the same fixed canonical renormalized field. No extra field rescaling
is silently performed while claiming unchanged interaction parameters.

The positivity statement concerns the constant-field effective potential,
not convexity of every inhomogeneous background Hessian or convergence of
the loop expansion at arbitrarily large fields.
