# Neutral background, color factors and the gauge-exchange term

Phi is a gauge singlet and H has no direct Yukawa
coupling. At A=0 the tree scalar/gauge Hessian is
block diagonal. The fermion Phi-A mixed Hessian has
one color generator inside a color-diagonal fermion
trace and vanishes. The determinant's AA block does
not vanish and must be contracted with the gauge
propagator in the master functional.

The code uses all eight literal fundamental SU(3)
generators to verify Tr(Ta)=0, Tr(Ta Tb)=delta_ab/2
and the quadratic Casimir 4/3. There are two active
Dirac flavors with Yukawas +y and -y, and twelve
inert flavors of the same mass. Odd Phi degrees
cancel; the inert flavors do not enter positive
Phi derivatives.

For scalar exchange the active color/flavor factor
is two times Tr(identity)=6. For contracted gauge
generators the factor per flavor is
sum_a Tr(Ta Ta)=4: the Phi-dependent active factor
is 8, while the degree-zero fourteen-flavor factor
is 56. Copying the scalar factor six into the
gauge-exchange term would be incorrect.

The gauge block retains its Dirac matrices, gauge
couplings, vertex factors of i, all current
orderings and the vectorlike gauge-preserving
dimensional regulator. The common trace coefficient
one half does not replace these signs or factors.
The finite color identities do not license early
four-dimensional Lorentz contractions or an
independent-diagram gauge-invariance claim.

There is no direct ghost-fermion vertex or tree Phi
dependence in the ghost block. Pure gauge/ghost
diagrams remain in the separate bosonic sector.
No gauge integral, IR allowance, exact gauge
spectrum or finite-gravity contribution is
evaluated in this ownership checkpoint.
