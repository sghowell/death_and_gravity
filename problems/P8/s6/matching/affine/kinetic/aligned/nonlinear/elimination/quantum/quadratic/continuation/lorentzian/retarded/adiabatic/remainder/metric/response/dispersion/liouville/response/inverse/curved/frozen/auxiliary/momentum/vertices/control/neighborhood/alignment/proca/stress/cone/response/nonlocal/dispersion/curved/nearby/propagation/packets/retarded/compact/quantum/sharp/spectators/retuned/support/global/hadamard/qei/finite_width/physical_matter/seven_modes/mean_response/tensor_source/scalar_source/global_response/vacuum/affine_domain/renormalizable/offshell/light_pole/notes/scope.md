# Evidence boundary and remaining work

Established: the actual one-loop light two-point function in this particular
polynomial vacuum, its full reduced-Hessian normalization, one fixed
on-shell subtraction, a nonzero analytic disc with a strict no-extra-zero
estimate, and explicit positive-curvature conversion of the fixed
constant-field potential scheme.

Not established: all-order pole or residue control; the full renormalized
four-point amplitude or its cut/contour/error bounds; exact stability of the
heavy particle (which can decay); nonlinear quantum field-map equivalence;
or the finite-gravity and rolling-bounce common-parent parts of V/G/B.

The four-point estimate must retain mixed heavy/light loops and the
momentum-dependent vertices of the exact heavy inverse. A field-independent
heavy determinant alone would miss the two-point function computed here.
A wide classical tree spectral window is not a loop analytic domain.

No original 32-row verdict, old state, frozen proof/source/report hash or
counterterm scheme is overwritten. This is a child of S6.111 solely to keep
one ordered evidence chain; its pole calculation uses S6.110's exact
polynomial model, not a quantum transfer through S6.111's derivative map.
