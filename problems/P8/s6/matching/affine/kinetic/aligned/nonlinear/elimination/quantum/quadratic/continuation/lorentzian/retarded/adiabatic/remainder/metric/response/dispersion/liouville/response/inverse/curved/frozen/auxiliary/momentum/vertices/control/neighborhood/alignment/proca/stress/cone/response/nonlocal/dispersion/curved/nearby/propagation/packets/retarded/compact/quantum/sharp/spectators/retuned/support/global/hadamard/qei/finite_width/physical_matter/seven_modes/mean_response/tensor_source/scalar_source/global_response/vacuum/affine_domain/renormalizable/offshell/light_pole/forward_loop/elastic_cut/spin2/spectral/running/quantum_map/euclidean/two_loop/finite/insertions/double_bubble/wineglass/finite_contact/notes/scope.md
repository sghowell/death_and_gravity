# A fixed missing contribution, not original completion

The finite potential contact was already fixed.
This child computes it and bounds its one-loop
insertion at two-loop order with the same
linear local reference subtraction.

It is not counted again as a raw two-loop
refinement or as one of the UV forest pieces.
Conversely, its absence from the 192-graph
raw inventory is not a reason to omit it.

The resulting known-contribution bounds remain
small relative to the tree forward coefficient.
They are not an assertion that all two-loop
pole/residue/LSZ and source-aware matching
questions are finished. Those bookkeeping
and quantitative self-energy obligations
must still be reconciled explicitly.

A negative finite quartic contact is not a
negative physical forward coefficient or
a ghost. Its sign and magnitude follow from
a fixed potential normalization. Bare UV
counterterms are not assigned finite values.

All-higher-loop and all-energy vacuum errors,
finite-gravity IR/Regge and Delta_grav,
absolute coupled renormalization, corrected
cosmological cones and a common controlled
propagating bounce parent remain open.

Scoped P8(a) and A.20-A.23 are unchanged.
Original P8(b), full V/G/B and original P8
remain OPEN.
