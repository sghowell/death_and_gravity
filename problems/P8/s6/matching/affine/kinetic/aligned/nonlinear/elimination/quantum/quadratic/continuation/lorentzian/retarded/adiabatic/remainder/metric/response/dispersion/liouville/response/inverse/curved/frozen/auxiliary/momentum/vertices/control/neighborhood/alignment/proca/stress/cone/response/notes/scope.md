The old full-rank prepared inverse and old frozen pole are therefore explicitly not invoked. Conversely, a rank-one local coefficient is not a proof of a constrained full quantum propagator. Nonlocal terms and lower-order constraints must be analyzed in the actual new equations.

The full state-dependent retarded response, its contacts and causal inverse remain to be rebuilt. Also open are the full quantum nearby scalar cone, canonical interaction estimates for the added profile, mixed loops, heavy thresholds and omitted operators. No Minkowski-vacuum completion, finite-gravity dispersion remainder or common-parent V/G/B matching is certified. Original P8 remains open.
