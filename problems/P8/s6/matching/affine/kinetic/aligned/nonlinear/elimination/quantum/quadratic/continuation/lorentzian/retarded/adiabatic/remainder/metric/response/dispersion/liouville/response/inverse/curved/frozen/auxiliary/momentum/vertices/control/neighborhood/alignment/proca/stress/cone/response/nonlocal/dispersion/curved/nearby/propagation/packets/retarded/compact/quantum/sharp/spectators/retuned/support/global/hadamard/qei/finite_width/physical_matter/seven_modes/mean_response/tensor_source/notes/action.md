# Vary the actual tensor action before choosing the clock

The action and physical metric are exactly those of S6.103.
The spatial chart leaves the physical lapse N unchanged.
Write h=(1+u^2)^3 and

    R(phi,X)=1+(X-1)/h(phi),  e=R^(-1/4).

The literal ADM coefficients give G_T=F_T=-2B=R and A1=0.
For the spatial metric a^2 exp(gamma), tr gamma=0, its volume
is exactly a^3 and its extrinsic-curvature trace is 3H.
The tensor shear and spatial-curvature terms therefore give,
for each transverse traceless polarization with E:E=2,

    L_T=R a^3 T_dot^2/(4N)-N R a q T^2/4,
    H_T=N P^2/(R a^3)+N R a q T^2/4.

The lower scalar functions and the retuned lapse potential
depend on the trace/volume and have no pure-TT quadratic
term in this chart. Terms with homogeneous lapse gradients
vanish. The stated action is only the quadratic TT action
under homogeneous background/clock variations; it is not
a covariant unconstrained graviton stress construction.

In the actual spatial chart a=e A,

    H_T,hat=N e P^2/A^3+N e^(-3) A q T^2/4.

On N=phi_dot=1, R=e=1 and this is literally the tensor
generator used by the already prepared S6.103 state.
Off this clock surface R cannot be set to one before variation.

Put x=P^2/a^6, y=q T^2/(4a^2), r=x+y, s=x-y/3 and K=r-3s.
The actual fixed-physical-metric lapse and pressure variations are

    rho_T=(1-1/h)r+3s/h=r-K/h,   p_T=s.

The fixed-A lapse variation instead gives

    F_T=(1-1/h)r+3s/(2h)=rho_T-3s/(2h).

Thus the spatial-frame pressure chain is retained, but F_T is
NOT the ordinary Proca source r-3s/(2h): their difference is
-K/h. Nor is the positive on-clock proxy r the physical lapse
source. A pure tensor-coordinate datum has rho_T=-y at h=1.
That negative quadratic source does not establish a ghost,
a negative full energy, or a UV no-go.

All normalizations remain as in the parent: covariance amplitude
eta=hbar lambda/kappa, kappa=M^2 tau^2, physical source unit
M^2/tau^2. There are exactly two tensor polarizations.
