This checkpoint uses the actual coupled global reduced scalar
state, not a free matter replacement. Read [FORMULATION.md](FORMULATION.md)
for its exact scope and the proofs under notes/.

bounds, actual leading short-sampling constants, and the precise
fixed-background stress-conservation obstruction. The report
fully rebuilds S6.99 and pins all own source/proof/test bytes.

give a covariant conserved stress source or solve original P8.
