# Complete two-point graph inventory

The heavy field is Gaussian with a field-independent quadratic kernel
and a linear G H Phi^2/2 coupling. Integrating it out is exact, leaving
the full momentum-dependent quartic vertex
V_M=-L+g(h_1+h_2+h_3), h_j=1/(M+Q_j^2), plus a
field-independent determinant. No heavy derivative expansion is used.
Heavy one-point counterterms produce only light quadratic terms.

For two external light legs and two loops, the reduced Phi4 half-edge
and Euler equations require two vertices and three internal light edges.
Enumerating multiplicities of 00, 11 and 01, requiring connectivity,
light one-particle irreducibility and degree at most four, gives exactly
three labelled skeletons. The sunset has three parallel edges and
external counts (1,1). Two nested-tadpole representatives have two
cross edges, a self-loop and external counts (2,0) or (0,2).

External assignments, the 2! vertex factor, parallel-edge factorials
and each self-loop's factor two give sunset weight 1/6 and nested
weights 1/8+1/8=1/4. Independently, for K_phi=K+L phi^2/2,
Gamma2=L/(8K_phi^2)-(L phi)^2/(12K_phi^3) has
second derivative -5L^2/(12K^3) at zero background.
The sum of positive Minkowski self-energy Wick weights is 5/12.

At each vertex, choice zero is local and choices one to three are
the three exact heavy pairings. Self-loop half-edges remain distinct.
There are 16 refinements per family, 32 total. Vertex labels and each
light/heavy edge are retained. For every nonempty internal-edge
subset, require connected 1PI topology, loop number at least one and
omega=4 loops-2 edges>=0. Boundary Phi and H half-edges are counted
from actual vertex valences. Restricted forests contain only edge-nested
or vertex-disjoint UV cores; overlapping proper cores are never paired.

The sunset partition is one local, six mixed local/heavy, three
same-heavy-pair and six different-heavy-pair cases. The local sunset
has three overlapping logarithmic Phi4 cores and overall quadratic
Phi2 degree. Mixed cases have one H Phi2 logarithmic core and
overall logarithmic Phi2 degree. Same-heavy cases have only a
logarithmic H2 core. Different-heavy cases have no UV cores.

For the chosen nested representative, choices are (outer,inner).
Outer zero/one gives eight p-independent cases; the independent
external two-forest polynomial is exactly zero. Outer two/three
with inner zero/one gives four cases whose inner light/H tadpoles
are p independent. They vanish after the fixed inner on-shell
quadratic subtraction. The final four cases have a mixed inner
self-energy and exactly its single Phi2 logarithmic core.

The UV cores in the p-independent cases are still enumerated,
including their overlap and two-loop heavy one-point cores.
Their regulated contribution is annihilated by the relevant
on-shell operation; they are not declared bare UV-finite.
Subtraction-generated terms from finite kinetic renormalization
are handled separately in the complete ledger.
