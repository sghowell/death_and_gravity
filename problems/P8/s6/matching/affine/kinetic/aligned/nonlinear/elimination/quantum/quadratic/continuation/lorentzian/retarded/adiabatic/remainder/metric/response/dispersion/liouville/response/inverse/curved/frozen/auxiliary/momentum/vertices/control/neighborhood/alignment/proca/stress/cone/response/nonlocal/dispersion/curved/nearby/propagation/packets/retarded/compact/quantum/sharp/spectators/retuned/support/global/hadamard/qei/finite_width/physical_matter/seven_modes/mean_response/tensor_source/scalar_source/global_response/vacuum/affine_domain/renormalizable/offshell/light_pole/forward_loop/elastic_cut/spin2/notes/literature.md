# Primary-source normalization and scope check

[Caron-Huot and Tokuda, arXiv:2406.07606v2, sections 2.1-2.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.07606v2)
study a minimally coupled scalar example, explicitly separating the
leading matter-loop gravitational contribution from pure gravity loops.
Their equations (5)-(8) expose the fixed-transfer pole cancellation,
the finite negative scalar-loop coefficient and its dependence on
high-energy Regge data in the dispersion relation. The equal-mass,
single-identical-field limit in pole.md checks their equation (6).
Our actual two-field triangle denominators and hierarchy bounds are
derived independently; the paper's scalar coefficient and any Regge
scale are not transferred to this different model.

The calculation here fixes no high-energy trajectory, residue
derivative or contour remainder. A small low-energy vertex correction
does not replace those missing inputs or provide Delta_grav.
