# Scope and continuation

The fixed object is the S6.81 ordinary-Proca action, S6.82 already-fixed
profile, S6.55 selected state and the retained Gaussian approximation.
Their new local, nonlocal and isolated scalar-reference checkpoints are
replayed upstream. Original source-dependent mass vertices, full-rank
loop inverses and frozen-pole findings are not assigned to this action.

Established here is a causal finite-time inverse for the actual
prepared homogeneous linear tree-plus-vector-plus-profile equations.
It includes the classical matter charge and both physical metric
forces. The inverse is not selected by deleting pole residues or
freely choosing higher-derivative initial data. The sources and
forcing are zero on an initial neighborhood; extension past the
observation interval can be cut off without changing a causal result.

The finite inverse bound is an existence statement. It has not been
numerically evaluated or shown small. It does not control
source-dependent nonlinear inverse norms, a full inhomogeneous
spectrum, arbitrary initial covariances, quantum kinetic signs or
characteristic cones. In particular causal solvability does not
imply stability or an all-frequency healthy resummed propagator.

A finite truncation of an EFT is not assumed valid at arbitrarily
high frequencies. The exact massive kernel here belongs to the
declared retained approximation. This result therefore does not
supply a Wilsonian cutoff or show that its resummed solutions lie
below one. Canonical interactions of the fixed profile, omitted
operators, mixed loops and heavy thresholds remain separate work.

The isolated flat ordinary-Proca reference is not a demonstrated
stationary healthy Minkowski vacuum of the full scalar-tensor
candidate. V/G/B matching still requires the common parent,
vacuum, decoupling/finite-gravity dispersion assumptions, strict
absorptive input and controlled error/remainder data in the
original contract. No arbitrary allowance is introduced for the
gravitational subtraction contour or its finite remainder.

objective is unchanged. This checkpoint creates no user-intervention
