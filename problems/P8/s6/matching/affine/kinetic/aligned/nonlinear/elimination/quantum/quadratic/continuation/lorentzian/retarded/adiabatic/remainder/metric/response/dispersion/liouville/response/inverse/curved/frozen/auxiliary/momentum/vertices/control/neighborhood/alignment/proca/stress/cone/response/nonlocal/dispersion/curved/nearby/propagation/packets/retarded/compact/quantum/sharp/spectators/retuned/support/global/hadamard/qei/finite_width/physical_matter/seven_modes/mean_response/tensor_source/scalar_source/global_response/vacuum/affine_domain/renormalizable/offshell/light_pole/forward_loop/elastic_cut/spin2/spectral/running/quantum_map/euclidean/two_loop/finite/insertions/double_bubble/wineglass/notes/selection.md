# Exact remaining graphs and all external assignments

Use the frozen wineglass skeleton with edges
(A,B), (A,C), (B,C), (B,C), two external legs at A
and one at each of B,C.

The parallel light bubble is logarithmically divergent
precisely when the fixed heavy-pairing choices at B,C
belong to {0,3}. The choice at A is arbitrary.
Exhaustive comparison with S6.120 checks this
criterion against all 64 refinements.

There are 16 selected graphs. Fourteen become integrable
after the proper bubble subtraction. Only A in {0,1}
with B=C=0 also has a logarithmic two-loop core.

The proper cores have local external types Phi^4,
H Phi^2 or H^2. The overall cores have types Phi^4
or H Phi^2. Their momentum subtraction is therefore
local and needs no higher-derivative four-point term.

All allowed restricted forests are retained. The
two overall cases have four forests; the other
fourteen have two. There is no extra hidden divergent
subgraph in the exact full-heavy inventory.

The labelled skeleton and external-assignment
enumeration is replayed explicitly. Summing its
original Wick weight over each crossing channel
gives weight one, hence total weight three.
The estimates apply to every assignment, not just
one conveniently chosen diagram.

These are precisely the raw refinements not included
in the 88 finite, 64 grouped insertion and 24
factorizing double-bubble refinements. Additional
counterterm diagrams are not counted by this
192-refinement partition.
