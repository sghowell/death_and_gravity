# Proof provenance

The full self-energy, fixed residue subtraction, stress triangles
and composite polynomial are the frozen calculations of
S6.112, S6.115 and S6.118. The positive integral, dominated limit,
operator bounds and nonuniformity witness are derived here.

Off-shell coordinate behavior does not by itself invalidate
on-shell equivalence; see
[Criado and Perez-Victoria, section 2](https://arxiv.org/html/1811.09413v2).
No new high-energy, reflection-positivity or gravitational
assumption is imported from that discussion.
