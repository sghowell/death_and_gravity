# What this result changes and what it does not

S6.100 left its exact finite-width reference functional unevaluated.
S6.101 supplies a uniform explicit upper bound for a specified
nonempty family of actual globally transported generalized
Hadamard reference states, for all real compact samplers in a
nonzero comoving interval. It includes low momentum, a complete
time-dependent sixth-order graph, its nonzero defect, and the
all-order initial state's nonzero remainder.

It is an inequality for the original relational quadratic test
energy. No chart-dependent field is substituted for O=chi.
No finite adiabatic truncation is promoted to a Hadamard state.
No point sampling is promoted to a continuous-domain estimate.

The bound is nonoptimal. A sharp cosmological calibration or a
controlled relative short-sampling remainder is NOT established.
Nor are the covariant physical stress, its metric/clock response,
renormalized Ward identities, self-consistent semiclassical
backreaction, or the interacting theory. The nonzero coupled
test-tensor divergence identified in S6.100 remains relevant.

Full tensor/vector state construction, omitted operators, loops,
heavy thresholds and cutoff control, and actual vacuum/dispersion/
common-parent V/G/B matching remain open. The original realistic,
null and nonminimal P8(a), UV classification P8(b), and original
P8 are NOT closed. Missing research estimates are not a request
for the user to supply arbitrary constants.

Native exact algebra, exact rational/root bounds, independently
cross-checked whole-interval ball arithmetic and report mutations
are executable evidence. The Borel, Duhamel, distributional and
Fourier arguments are written proofs, not proof-assistant
formalizations or external peer review.
