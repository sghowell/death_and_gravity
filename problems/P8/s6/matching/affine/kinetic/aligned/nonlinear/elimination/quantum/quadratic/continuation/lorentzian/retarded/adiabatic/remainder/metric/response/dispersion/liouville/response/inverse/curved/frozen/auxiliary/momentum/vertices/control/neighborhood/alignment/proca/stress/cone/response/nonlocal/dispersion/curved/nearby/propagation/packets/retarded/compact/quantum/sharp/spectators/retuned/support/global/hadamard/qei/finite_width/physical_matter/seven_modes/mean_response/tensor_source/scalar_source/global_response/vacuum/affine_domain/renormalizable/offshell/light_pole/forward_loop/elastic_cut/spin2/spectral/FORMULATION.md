# Spectral content of the already computed vertex

Use the same polynomial vacuum, light mass one, fixed on-shell residue
and minimally coupled stress form factor of S6.115. No additional
matching operator or high-energy ansatz is introduced. Write M=mu^2
and g=G^2 for its actual heavy mass squared and cubic coupling squared.

For the already computed one-loop F_R(t), derive its two discontinuities,
an exact once-subtracted representation, the inverse-second moment
F_R'(0), and a bound on the portion from transfer invariant [4,6].
Also bound the separate full light and heavy contributions.

The targets are:

1. A positive pushforward measure with sufficient inverse moments,
   without assuming its unweighted total mass is finite.
2. The explicit branch points and densities of both actual triangle
   mass assignments.
3. An independently normalized spin-two cut using the actual crossed
   scalar tree vertices and the identical-pair phase space.
4. An exact positive-series enclosure for the common angular function.
5. Quantitative full-versus-low-window slope-moment comparisons.

Every density and threshold is at this one-matter-loop order. The
interacting heavy particle is unstable; a free-heavy cut in the
truncated calculation is not an exact asymptotic spectral assertion
and is not a uniform approximation to higher-loop threshold behavior.

A form-factor dispersion relation is not the fixed-transfer scattering
dispersion relation required by G. No complex-angular-momentum
continuation, high-energy Regge trajectory, residue or contour follows
from the ordinary integer-spin projection used here.
