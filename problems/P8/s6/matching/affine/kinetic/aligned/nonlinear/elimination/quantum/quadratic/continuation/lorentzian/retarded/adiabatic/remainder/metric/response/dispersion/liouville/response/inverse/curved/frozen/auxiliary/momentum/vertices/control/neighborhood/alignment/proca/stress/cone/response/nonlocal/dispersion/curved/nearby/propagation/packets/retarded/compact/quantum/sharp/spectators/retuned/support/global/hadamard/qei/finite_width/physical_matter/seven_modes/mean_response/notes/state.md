# A fixed finite Hadamard state difference

Put q=|k|^2 and define f(q)=exp[-1/((q-1)(4-q))] for 1<q<4,
zero otherwise. The flat exponential gives a C-infinity nonnegative
function, strictly positive inside its shell. Its integral in
d^3k/(2pi)^3 is finite and nonzero. Divide by that integral to
obtain b(k). This definition fixes the band; its normalization is
not an adjustable state-dependent subtraction.

To the S6.103 anchor Proca covariance add eta b(k) I6, leaving
all other blocks unchanged. Positivity is preserved because the
addition is real positive semidefinite. The CCR is unchanged
because its transpose-antisymmetric part vanishes. Exact evolution
with the actual global Proca generator gives

    Delta W(t,s,k)=eta b(k) U_P,a(t,0,k) U_P,a(s,0,k)^T.

This is a bisolution with the temporal component reconstructed
by the actual Proca constraint. On each compact time strip,
all its time derivatives are bounded on the compact momentum
support. Arbitrary spatial derivatives only add bounded powers
of k. Dominated differentiation thus makes the spacetime kernel
smooth. Adding it preserves the reference's Hadamard wavefront
relation, and defines another positive quasifree state.

The band is homogeneous and isotropic. It has finite energy
DENSITY, not finite total energy on infinite R^3. No claim
of finite total energy is needed for this homogeneous response.
The actual generator is built from I and kk^T and is
rotation covariant, so evolution preserves this isotropy.
Spatial stress is therefore proportional to delta_ij.
The mean momentum flux is odd in k and integrates to zero;
no homogeneous shift or anisotropic stress source is omitted.

For the added covariance only, reflection about the anchor is
exact. The scale a is even and, with T=diag(I3,-I3),
T M_P T=-M_P. Uniqueness gives U_P(-u,0)=T U_P(u,0) T.
Since the anchor addition is proportional to I6 and the stress
Hessians commute with T, r(u) and s(u) are even. The prepared
reference state itself is NOT asserted to be time symmetric.

The displayed amplitude eta includes hbar/kappa. Physical
stress conversion gives (M^2/tau^2)eta=hbar lambda/tau^4.
This is a state difference with the same action, reference
prescription and scalar/tensor factors. The leading interacting
mean response is computed separately; it is not a claim that
the free product state's scalar one-point function has already
solved the interacting equations.
