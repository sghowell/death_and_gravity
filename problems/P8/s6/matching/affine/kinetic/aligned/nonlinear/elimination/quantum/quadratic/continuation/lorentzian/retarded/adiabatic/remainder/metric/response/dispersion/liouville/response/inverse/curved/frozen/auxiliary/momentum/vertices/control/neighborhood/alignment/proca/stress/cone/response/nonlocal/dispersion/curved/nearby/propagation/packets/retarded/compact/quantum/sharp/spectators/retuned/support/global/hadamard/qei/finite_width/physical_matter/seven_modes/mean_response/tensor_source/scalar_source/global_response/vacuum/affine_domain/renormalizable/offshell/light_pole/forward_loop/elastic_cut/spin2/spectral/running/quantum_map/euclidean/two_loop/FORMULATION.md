# S6.120: complete bare two-loop four-point denominators

Original P8 remains OPEN.

For the fixed canonical polynomial vacuum of S6.110-S6.119,
enumerate every connected light-1PI two-loop four-light graph
using its exact Gaussian-heavy reduced quartic action. Keep
each full heavy exchange; never expand its inverse in loop momenta.

Construct all first and second graph polynomials, independently
verify the first with the matrix-tree theorem, and certify an
explicit common lower bound on the second throughout the compact
forward disc |s-2|<=1, t=0, u=4-s. The light mass squared is one;
the result is uniform for heavy mass squared M>=16.

Enumerate local superficially divergent subgraphs and their
vertex-aware compatible forest sets. This is preparation for
renormalized integration, not its replacement. No subtraction
coefficient, ultraviolet boundary integral or complete two-loop
LSZ correction is evaluated by this child.

The target is a finite exact denominator/topology theorem.
It is not a bound on the two-loop forward coefficient, all
higher-loop errors, a high-energy contour, finite gravity, a
common bounce parent or original V/G/B closure.
