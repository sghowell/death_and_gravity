# Scope, parameter boundaries and research follow-through

The new result concerns the linear response of the retained
Gaussian vector plus the already-fixed scalar tadpole, coupled
to the literal homogeneous classical metric/free-matter
system. The metric/state preparation, physical frame, action
and subtraction prescription are unchanged. The normal-form
proof uses exact on-clock modes and physical Hamiltonian
sources. It imports no finite off-clock ordinary-Proca
Hadamard or cone theorem.

The C0 kernel and inverse constants are finite but not
numerically evaluated. The proof therefore does not replace
S6.68's explicit C10-to-C0 one-application estimate by an
explicit small no-loss feedback estimate. A strongly growing
retarded solution is consistent with the new existence and
uniqueness theorem. The infinite-frequency reference inverse
is used to solve a specified Gaussian linear equation, not
to declare an uncut resummation physically valid above the
finite EFT's cutoff.

Only zero-past prepared forcing and zero matter-charge
perturbation are included. Independent initial covariances,
arbitrary metric Cauchy jets and spatially varying sources
require additional work. A nonlinear inverse would require
uniform estimates in a neighborhood of the geometry/state;
the constants here belong to the fixed background and do
not prove that uniform statement.

## Primary-source cross-check, not a theorem transfer

[Meda's 2026 scalar-FLRW review, section 2.2](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10773-026-06339-9)
discusses the derivative loss and logarithmic inverse in the
scalar problem. The vector physical-current normal form here
is derived separately; the scalar result is not used as a
Proca or coupled-DHOST theorem.

[Galanda et al., April 2026 preprint, sections 4.4 and 5](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.01047v1)
analyze a different linearized scalar-Einstein system, retaining
pole and cut contributions in its full retarded symbol. Their
instability analysis depends on that system and its parameters.
It is not a direct verdict on this vector/DHOST clock. Equally,
the existence of the present prepared inverse supplies no
independent stability conclusion. No numerical cosmological
claim from that preprint is adopted.

## Remaining work

The homogeneous prepared no-loss linear inverse has now been
addressed in its stated scope. Still required are physical
quantum stability/cone and interaction/cutoff estimates for
the retained EFT, spatial and independent-state response,
other loops, and finite Wilson/common-parent V/G/B matching.
If nonlinear nearby semiclassical solutions are pursued,
their parameter-uniform inverse and compatible initial-data
conditions are separate obligations.

No fitted kernel samples, leading-log pole deletion, arbitrary
finite coefficient choice or reselected quantum state closes
these gaps. The scoped P8(a) result is unchanged, and original
P8 remains open. Missing estimates remain research work, not
a request for a new user choice.
