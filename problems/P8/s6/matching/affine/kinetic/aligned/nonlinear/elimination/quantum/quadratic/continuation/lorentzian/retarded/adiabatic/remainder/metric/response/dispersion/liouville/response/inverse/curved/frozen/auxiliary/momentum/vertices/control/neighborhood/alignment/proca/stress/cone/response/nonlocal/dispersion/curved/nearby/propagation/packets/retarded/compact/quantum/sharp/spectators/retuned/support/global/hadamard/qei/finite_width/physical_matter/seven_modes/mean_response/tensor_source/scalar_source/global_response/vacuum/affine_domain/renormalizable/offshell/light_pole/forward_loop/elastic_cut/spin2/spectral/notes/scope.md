# Scope, threshold order and missing gravitational data

This is the exact spectral content of one previously specified
one-matter-loop stress form factor. It adds no matching operators.
The triangle discontinuities, full slope moments and finite-window
moment bounds refer to that retained perturbative coefficient.

The heavy scalar already has a nonzero decay channel in the same
model. Its propagators in this one-loop coefficient have their
zeroth-order mass, as required at that order. Accordingly, the
heavy-pair cut is a fixed-order branch point, not a theorem about
exact stable-heavy asymptotic states. No uniform higher-loop or
width-resummed approximation is asserted near that threshold.

Positive vertex spectral weight is not a full-amplitude optical
positivity condition. Its once-subtracted dispersion identity
does not control the fixed-negative-transfer gravitational
scattering contour. Ordinary spin-two partial waves do not
supply complex-spin analyticity or a Regge tower.

In particular, none of the following is established:

- an all-orders spectral density or scattering amplitude;
- all-channel gravity, massless cuts or graviton-loop errors;
- a numerical Regge residue, trajectory, contour or Delta_grav;
- a UV identification of the heavy scalar with the Regge scale;
- quantum equivalence of the prior derivative field map;
- a common controlled finite-gravity rolling-bounce parent.

The phase-space and continuous spectral proofs are written
arguments checked through exact identities and rational bounds,
not proof-assistant formalized or peer reviewed. Original P8
and its full V/G/B obligations remain OPEN.
