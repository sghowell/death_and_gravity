# Quantitative classical progress and remaining P8 obligations

S6.92 makes S6.91's compact-probe existence result quantitative.
It specifies smooth nonnegative normalized source bumps and
a smooth compact detector supported near the actual clock
front. Exact geometry, normalization and L2 bounds give a
positive classical pairing at least 3D/4. A finite comoving
spatial-frequency contribution remains at least D/2.

The widths and cutoff are finite symbolic expressions that
retain the full low-frequency exponential remainder bound.
They are extremely conservative, not optimized or evaluated
as decimal approximations. No realistic source bandwidth,
amplitude, apparatus, noise or detector-resolution claim is made.
An arbitrarily small source rescaling preserves the linear
sign but does not prove a nonlinear apparatus or measurable
quantum effect.

The result gives explicit *conditional* low-band and parent-
tail error gates. It does not verify them. In particular,
the parent high-frequency tail cannot be replaced by the
classical high-frequency estimate, and temporal-frequency
control is still missing.

No interacting canonical cutoff, omitted-operator/loop/threshold
budget, finite Wilson matching, healthy full-candidate vacuum,
new-background quantum state/profile/response, common-parent
V/G/B, globally complete nearby bounce, UV completion/exclusion
or original P8 closure is established. The old quantum and
hard-tree examples are not transferred to these probes.

This checkpoint isolates an exact classical comparison target.
The next substantive UV step is to establish or refute the
actual common-parent error inequalities, with their domain
and canonical maps, rather than regard the existence of a
finite mathematical cutoff as an EFT validity theorem.
