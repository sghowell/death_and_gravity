# Full quotient beyond a timelike scalar rest frame

The native matrix reconstruction keeps an arbitrary covector
v_mu in the literal p R_Gamma, double-dual c G_Gamma v v
and epsilon F4 Galileon. The affine scalar Hessian is
generally nonsymmetric. No torsion-free or metric-compatible
connection assumption is introduced.

The resulting 64-by-64 Hessian is differentiated from the
quadratic connection polynomial. Four projective columns
are exact kernel directions. The same trace-gauge embedding
as S6.37 gives the complete 60-dimensional quotient.

An independent timelike reconstruction agrees entry by
entry with the older literal matrix. Separate spacelike,
nonzero null and zero-gradient matrices reproduce the full
determinant. The timelike/spacelike rational coefficients
are generic algebraic calibrations, not samples of the
new family at a specified (u,X). The null and zero cases
use the actual n=1024,u=0 removable values p=1/2,c=0,F4=512.
A nonzero null gradient is not the constant-field vacuum.

These finite checks supplement, not replace, the domain
argument. For a proper Lorentz transformation L, the
64-component distortion representation has determinant
(det L)^16(det L)^-16(det L)^-16=1. Its projective covector
subrepresentation also has determinant one. Therefore
the induced 60-dimensional quotient representation is
unimodular and the Hessian determinant is unchanged.
Every timelike gradient can be put in the checked rest frame,
so the determinant identity holds on the open timelike set.

The new p,c,F4 are real analytic in u and in every gradient
component, including X=0. The gradient domain specified by
-1/(4n)<X<6/5 is star-shaped about zero, since scaling a
gradient by s multiplies X by s^2. Together with real u
it is connected. The real-analytic determinant identity
therefore extends from the open timelike subset to the
whole domain. Its strictly nonzero factor 2R-1 gives
rank 60 on the quotient at spacelike, null and zero gradients
as well. No rest-frame formula is divided by sqrt(X) at zero.

The analytic source and the invertible quotient determine
an analytic auxiliary solution locally. Substitution in
the complete action, with the displayed covariant boundary,
gives the new reduced scalar-tensor action. Stationary
variation therefore yields the same classical metric,
scalar and physical matter equations, modulo projective
gauge. This is not a quantitative bound on every boosted
coordinate inverse, nor a propagating connection spectrum.
