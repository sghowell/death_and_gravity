# Result boundary and original obligations

Established: the complete first two-gauge discontinuity
of the separately named GY14-unbroken candidate has
a nonzero boundary mismatch at an interior point of
the old massive scalar disc at three parent loops,
including the entire finite-mass one-loop transition
momentum remainder. The relative correction at that
point is strictly below 5 times 10^-194.

This upgrades a leading local-operator screen to a
finite-mass first-cut screen. It does not transfer the
old scalar two-loop coefficient to a new field content.
It does not construct a complete new-model three-loop
amplitude or bound all subsequent loop orders.

The perturbative massless gauge description is not
a nonperturbative spectrum. The tiny formal infrared
transmutation scale is not a proven confinement gap.
No whole candidate, parent mechanism or ladder row is
excluded. In particular, this work does not prove that
a modified light-cut subtraction or different specified
dispersion observable cannot meet the original contract.

V still needs justified dispersion applicability and
the error control required for a strict necessary test.
G still needs its stated IR/Regge contour, order of
limits and derived gravitational allowance.
B still needs the common-parent field, derivative,
state, spectrum, cutoff and remainder dictionary on
the vacuum and bounce domains. A small vacuum cut
does not supply any of these missing statements.

The adopted conditional finite-EFT/necessary-positivity
contract is not silently strengthened into construction
of a full all-orders UV theory. No global nonlinear
stability theorem is added either. Scoped P8(a) and
the later A.20-A.23 results remain unchanged.
Original V/G/B and original P8 remain OPEN.

Exact symbolic identities and finite test inventories
support the written analytic arguments. The report
does not label them proof-assistant FORMALIZED,
nonperturbative or independently peer reviewed.
