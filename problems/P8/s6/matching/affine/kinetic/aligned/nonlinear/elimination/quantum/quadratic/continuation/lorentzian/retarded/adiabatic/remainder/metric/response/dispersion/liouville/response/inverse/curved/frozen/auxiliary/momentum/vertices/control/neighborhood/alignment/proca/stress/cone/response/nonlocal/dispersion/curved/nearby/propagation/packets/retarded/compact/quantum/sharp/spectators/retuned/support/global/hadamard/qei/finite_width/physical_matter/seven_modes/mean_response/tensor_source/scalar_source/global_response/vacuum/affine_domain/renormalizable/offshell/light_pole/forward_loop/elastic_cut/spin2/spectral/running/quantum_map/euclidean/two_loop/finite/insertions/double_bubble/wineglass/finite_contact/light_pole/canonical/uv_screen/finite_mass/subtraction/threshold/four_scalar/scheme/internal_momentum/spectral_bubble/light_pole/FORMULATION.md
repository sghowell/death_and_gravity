# P8 S6.136 — First spectral covariance insertion in the scalar quadratic sector

S6.135 supplies the complete first fermion propagator insertion as a
spectral integral. Differentiate the full scalar one-loop quadratic
half-trace once in its light covariance. This includes all local
tadpole terms and the nonlocal H-Phi bubble with exactly one inserted
light line. The four-point bubble's factor of two does not apply.

Use the fixed physical mass-one scalar, unit residue, H one-point
reference and the same leading interaction boundary. Pair the whole
inner on-shell fermion reference first. Subtract the whole outer
mass and slope anchors before spectral integration. Unregulated
local tadpoles and mass constants are not assigned finite estimates.

Bound the mixed heavy/spectral bubble on Re(s)<=4. Its spectral
derivative integrals converge, fixing a positive finite outer slope
and a uniform quadratic remainder. Extend the explicitly selected
complete one-loop quadratic functional by this covariance-insertion
sector only, with its displayed local mass and field references.

This selected functional is not the complete two-loop inverse.
Other primitive graphs, lower-order finite interaction conversions,
later-loop errors and the old pure-scalar budget in a different
scheme are not silently included. The reference-energy window is
not a derived cutoff; the heavy mass is not an exact stable-particle
claim. Original V/G/B and P8 remain OPEN, and no old scientific
source/proof/test/report bytes are modified.
