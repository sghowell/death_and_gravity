# What transfers, and what the new action removes

At fixed light fields the vector action has unchanged quadratic
operator A=M+K, where M is the complete variable mass matrix and K
the same Maxwell operator. The old source completion is

L_old=L_light+(W-S)^T*M*(W-S)/2+W^T*K*W/2,
L_new=L_light+W^T*A*W/2.

The old stationary mean is A^-1*M*S. Its source-dependent on-shell
light term is S^T*(M-M*A^-1*M)*S/2; the new mean and this term vanish.
Keep this operator order: M and K need not commute. The exact
two-component symbolic identity checks the algebraic completion
step; the existing curved-space Gaussian theorem, not that toy
matrix calculation, supplies the determinant and covariance result.

The vector Hessian is identical at every fixed light configuration,
so its Gaussian determinant and covariance evolution with the same
initial covariance are unchanged. The initial state is not reselected.
On the original clock S0=S1=0. The changed coherent mean therefore
starts at light degree two, and the changed source functional starts
at light degree four. The established Gaussian background stress,
light-quadratic response and fixed tadpole transfer at the same
parameters. Higher source-dependent light response does not.
Mixed light-vector and graviton loops are not a fixed-light Gaussian
determinant and do not acquire bounds from this argument. Nor is a
new off-clock Hadamard existence theorem supplied.

In particular deleting S cannot cure the previously calculated
quadratic frozen quantum pole at the same L=10^24. Conversely its
old numerical frequency bracket cannot be copied to L=10^400.
Neither diagnostic is an actual time-dependent instability theorem.

For the S6.79 constraint-compatible homogeneous light family at phi=0,
the old and new Hamiltonians with j=0 are exactly identical, not just
equal at the center N=1. The same positive matter root P(N) and
fixed-phase lapse Hessian remain valid. The physical lapse is N and
the physical spatial metric is N times the hat metric at that slice.

Let q be the hat squared momentum, p=-2*q*b and j=-P_sigma. The new
central quadratic correction obtained from the full Hamiltonian is

Delta H = -N^(3/2)*delta*q*b*P_sigma
          +(3/4)*N^(3/2)*delta^2*P_sigma^2.

This cancels precisely the old B_(sigma,b)=N^(3/2)*delta*q mixing
and replaces the vector kinetic limit kappa/sqrt(N) by
gamma_t/sqrt(N). Direct reconstruction checks the complete old
six-channel blocks plus this literal correction. B_new=0 on the
central slice, so the negative -q^2 mixing square in the b coordinate
potential is absent. All remaining Euler entries, retaining finite
background time jets, are at most O(q); their determinant is O(q^3),
not the former negative O(q^4). This follows also from exact new
vector parity: around zero W the vector-light bilinear vanishes on
every homogeneous light background, so time derivatives do not
restore that removed cross block.

This removes one precise mechanism, not every possible failure.
The isolated vector mass ratio gamma_t/gamma_s is unchanged: at
N=1 it equals one and its N derivative is -8/81. At the certified
left-side datum N=1-10^-28 it is greater than one. No newly proved
physical cone or UV exclusion follows merely from quoting that
ratio. The complete applicability, frequency, interaction and
matching-error conditions remain to be established.
