# Preserve the boundary between tree matching and P8 closure

This result identifies the actual canonical vacuum quartic
off shell, modulo a displayed local field redefinition and
boundary current, with explicit higher-field and heavy-inverse
errors. It is stronger than checking a finite list of
on-shell four-point coefficients.

It still does not equate the full polynomial, exponential,
analytic affine or old kinetic-parent actions. The target
contains the actual vacuum quadratic and quartic, not every
higher finite-M coefficient. The pointwise derivative and
common Schwartz-domain bounds are not a map of a whole
noncompact rolling cosmology. No common propagating heavy
parent of that cosmology has been established.

The map is derivative-dependent. Its formal order-by-order
inverse must not be called a globally invertible map of
nonlinear solutions or phase spaces. Nonzero higher-field
terms are generated and bounded, not ignored. Their effects
outside the specified domain have no certificate here.

At the quantum level field/source transformations,
Jacobian terms, renormalization and mixed loops require
consistent treatment. The classical field redefinition
does not automatically transfer the S6.110 potential
subtractions, give quantum on-shell pole data or prove
a full b2 error bound. The large tree spectral window
still contains light-particle loop thresholds.

The matching has no finite-gravity infrared prescription
or Regge-contour remainder, no controlled corrected cone,
and no absolute cosmological source. Old fixed counterterms,
states and classical clock actions remain unchanged.
Full V/G/B, P8(b) classification and original P8 remain OPEN.
