# Light LSZ and the even-vacuum connected amplitude

The inherited pole theorem concerns the chosen canonical Phi field:
Pi_j,R(1)=Pi_j,R'(1)=0 for j=1,2, and the actual unit-disc
bound excludes extra zeros of its inverse propagator through two
loops. The physical light mass is one and its canonical residue
is exactly one at that order. This is not a statement about the
bare-field normalization factor.

Let Phi_B=sqrt(Z) Phi_R, Z=1+ell z1+ell^2 z2.
For an amputated four-point amplitude,
A_B=Z^-2 A_R, while the bare pole residue is Z times the
canonical residue. Thus the four-leg bare LSZ factor is Z^2.
For A_R=A0+ell A1+ell^2 A2,

    A_B,1=A1-2z1 A0,
    A_B,2=A2-2z1 A1+(3z1^2-2z2)A0,
    (Z^2)_1=2z1, (Z^2)_2=z1^2+2z2.

Multiplication and truncation through order two recover A_R
exactly, including the z1^2 cross terms. Applying an additional
Z^2 factor to an already canonical amplitude would be an error.

The total canonical interaction coefficients obey
L_B Z^2=L_total and G_B Z=G_total; the heavy field has its
unchanged canonical normalization. Squaring the latter gives
g_B Z^2=G_total^2, including the first-order cubic square in
the second-order total g coefficient. These field identities are
checked independently from the graph-product ledger. No new heavy
kinetic or heavy on-shell condition is introduced.

The perturbative vacuum branch is Phi->-Phi symmetric. The literal
potential and every selected local counterterm are even in Phi.
Gaussian integration of H preserves this symmetry and keeps the
full nonlocal light quartic interaction. Odd light 1PI vertices
therefore vanish in this symmetric perturbative construction.

The functional connected four-point identity consists of a light
1PI four-point kernel plus three channels of two light three-point
kernels joined by a light propagator, with external two-point
chains. The three-point terms vanish here and the external chains
are precisely those removed by amputation and LSZ.
Heavy-reducible exchanges do NOT vanish; they already belong to
the full Gaussian-heavy light quartic kernel and were retained in
the graph enumeration.

An independent scalar Legendre-series diagnostic gives
K^4 W4=3 Gamma3^2/K-Gamma4. It checks the three-channel
functional derivative coefficient and its even-vacuum reduction,
not the full momentum-space amplitude. The latter relies on the
full-label graph proof and the functional identity just stated.

There is no internal light tree exchange on which a new second-order
light quadratic counterterm could act. Its remaining effect is in the
external pole/residue calculation already included. The tree's heavy
mass counterterms are separately retained in the local/product ledger.

These statements are about canonical Phi, not the derivative-coordinate
Psi of S6.118. That coordinate has composite source and residue
corrections; its two-loop source-aware map is not inferred here.
