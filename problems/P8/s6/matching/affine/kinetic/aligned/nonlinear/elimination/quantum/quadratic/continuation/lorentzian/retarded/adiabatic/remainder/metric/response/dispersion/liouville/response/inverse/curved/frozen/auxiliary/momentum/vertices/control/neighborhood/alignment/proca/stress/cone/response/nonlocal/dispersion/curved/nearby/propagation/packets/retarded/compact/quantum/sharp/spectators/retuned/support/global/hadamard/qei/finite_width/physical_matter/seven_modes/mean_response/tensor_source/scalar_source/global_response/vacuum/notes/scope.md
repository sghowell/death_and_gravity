# Verification and remaining original P8 obligations

Native verification independently reconstructs the old
reduced coefficients, Ia completion, full vacuum Taylor
jets, labelled amplitude and exact exchange remainder.
It retains the actual branch domains, signs, canonical
normalization and the even-order scaling. Whole-domain
inequalities follow from the written coefficient and
analytic arguments, not floating-point sampling.

The new functions are classical candidate data. The frozen
S6.103 reference, the S6.104-S6.107 relative calculations,
their actions and their counterterm prescriptions are not
changed. Finite clock jets transfer the stated classical
background, free generators and leading source kernels;
the old absolute S6.82 cancellation is not reassigned to
this vacuum, this action or the newer reference state.
No complete extended affine lower dictionary is constructed:
the old q integral and its integration constants cannot be
assumed to extend regularly merely from the reduced R bound.

The smooth branch agrees on an open tube but is not analytic
across its joins. The analytic branch agrees through a finite
jet order and has a quantified full-tube approximation.
Its co-scaled canonical limit retains a fixed nonzero
DHOST interaction. This removes two overly strong candidate
obstructions, not the remaining original problem.

V still requires the actual controlled interacting vacuum
amplitude, cuts/absorptive weight and error bounds.
G still requires the specified finite-gravity, fixed-negative-
transfer Regge/IR-pole and contour analysis before the
forward limit, not a guessed order-one gravitational error.
B still requires one actual healthy common parent on a
domain connecting the vacuum to the physical bounce, with
states, observables, branch/canonical maps, heavy thresholds,
omitted operators and loop errors controlled on that domain.

The heavy model here is a vacuum tree amplitude regression,
not that common parent. Its positive two-derivative Einstein
extension fails the strict flat-bounce NEC test. This failure
is limited to that explicit minimal parent. It is not an
exhaustive CD/M1 or scalar-tensor no-go theorem.

The source-pinned written arguments are not proof-assistant
formalized or peer reviewed. Every report field has a
mutation control and unsupported exact-input domains are
rejected. A passing implementation certificate is not a
substitute for any of the unproved V/G/B hypotheses.
Original P8(b), and therefore original P8, remains open.
