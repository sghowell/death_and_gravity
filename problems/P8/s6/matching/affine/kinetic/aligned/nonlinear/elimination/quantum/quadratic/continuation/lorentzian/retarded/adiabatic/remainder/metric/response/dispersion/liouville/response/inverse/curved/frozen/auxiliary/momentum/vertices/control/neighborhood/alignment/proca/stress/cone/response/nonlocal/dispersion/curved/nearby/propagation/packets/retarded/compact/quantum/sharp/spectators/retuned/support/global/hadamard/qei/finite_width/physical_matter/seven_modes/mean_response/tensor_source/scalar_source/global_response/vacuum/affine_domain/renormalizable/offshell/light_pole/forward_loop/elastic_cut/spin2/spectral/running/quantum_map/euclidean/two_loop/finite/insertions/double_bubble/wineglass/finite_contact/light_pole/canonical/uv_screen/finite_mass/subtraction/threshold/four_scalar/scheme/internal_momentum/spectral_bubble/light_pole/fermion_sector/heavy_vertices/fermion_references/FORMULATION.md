# P8 S6.139 — Proper fermion local-reference matching

The remaining two-loop fermion families require
proper fermion self-energy and Yukawa subtractions,
not only the closed-loop scalar self-energy
pairing already treated.

Derive the one-loop scalar and gauge contributions
to the proper fermion inverse at zero external
momentum and its kinetic coefficient. Retain the
dimensional numerator until its finite products
with poles have been extracted. Derive the zero-
momentum scalar Yukawa vertex by differentiating
the background mass at fixed renormalization scale.

Check the resulting proper counterterms against
the independent Yukawa beta function, including
all external-field factors. Relate the shared
MSbar inputs to zero-momentum canonical fermion
references without resetting the complete
physical Phi normalization. Provide conservative
exact rational bounds at the actual parameters.

This gives local proper-reference data, not
completed two-loop insertions, primitive
integrals, the outstanding outer-reference
conversion, an exact fermion spectrum or P8
closure. Prior scientific bytes remain frozen.
