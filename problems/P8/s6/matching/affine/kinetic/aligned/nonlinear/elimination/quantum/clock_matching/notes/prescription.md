# Explicit scalar-coefficient continuation of the clock-mass counterterms

This checkpoint defines a named local subtraction prescription, the
scalar-coefficient continuation. It is not a claim of scheme-independent
finite terms or a derivation of unknown UV Wilson coefficients. The
renormalized classical functions are held at the frozen S6.42 values;
the prescription specifies the vector-only local subtraction with no
additional finite terms beyond those generated by its stated dimensional
continuation. Other matching operators and loops are not supplied.

Keep the original timelike clock tube, physical metric and unit normal
n. In D+1 dimensions let P_munu=g_munu+n_mu*n_nu be the spatial
projector and define a spatial average by P^munu X_munu/D. The
actual scalar mass functions a_m,b_m depend on the clock variables,
not on an unnormalized D-dimensional spatial trace. Write
delta_a=a_m-1, delta_b=b_m-1. Both vanish identically along the
clock, but their lapse derivatives are alpha=4/(9h), beta=28/(81h).

## Counterterm density and its dimensional continuation

Use the full frozen zero-derivative coefficient

    C4(a_m,b_m)=2*b_m^2+a_m^(3/2)*b_m^(1/2).

Its definition is independent of D. This is required to preserve the
S6.47 finite potential and lapse jets. On the positive mass tube it
is a smooth local scalar. Let a4V_4 denote the four-dimensional Proca
heat coefficient evaluated as a covariant invariant in D+1 dimensions,
and use the four-dimensional minimal-scalar a4 coefficient similarly.
Their boxR terms are retained in the local density; compactly supported
Euler variations vanish for that divergence. No noncompact infinite-time
flux is assumed to vanish.

Define the following covariant coefficient tensors:

    C2_munu=R*g_munu/12-5*Ricci_munu/6,
    Z4_munu=Tsc4_munu+g_munu*boxR/6.

Tsc4 is the scalar pole stress tensor obtained by varying twice the
fixed four-dimensional scalar a4 coefficient in D+1 dimensions. The
normalization is the same common 1/(64pi^2 epsilon_DR) convention
as S6.52; the respective m^4,m^2,m^0 powers are kept explicit.
Set

    ga2=-2*C2_nn, gb2=6*C2_spatial_average,
    ga4=-Z4_nn/2, gb4=3*Z4_spatial_average/2.

The Lorentzian pole-counterterm density, before its common normalization,
is defined to be

    m^4*C4(a_m,b_m)+m^2*R+2*a4V_4
      -m^2*(delta_a*ga2+delta_b*gb2)
      -(delta_a*ga4+delta_b*gb4).

Three times the spatial average, rather than a D-dimensional spatial
trace, is an explicit part of this continuation. Both choices are
covariant on the clock tube and agree in four dimensions, but they can
differ by finite evanescent terms. The present choice keeps the frozen
flat scalar-coefficient prescription. No claim is made that it is the
unique extension. Terms quadratic and higher in the curvature-coupled
mass deviations are not supplied by this first-variation construction.

## First variation before restricting to the clock

For any smooth local geometric coefficient G with finitely many metric
derivatives, the product rule gives on this background

    delta[(a_m-1)G]=G_clock*delta a_m.

This statement applies before integration by parts: the other term
contains a_m-1=0 and vanishes pointwise. With compactly supported
variations it remains true of the Euler variation. The same holds
for b_m. All derivatives of the background mass deviations vanish
along the clock. Consequently the mass terms contribute
alpha*ga+beta*gb to lapse energy. Setting the mass deviations to
zero before variation would incorrectly erase this term.

In a homogeneous physical spatial variation at fixed clock and lapse,
X and both scalar mass functions are unchanged. The first spatial
variation of these added mass terms is therefore zero. The exact jet
controls use time-dependent alpha,beta, the differentiated volume
factor a_s^D and metric jets through order four, rather than treating
the geometric coefficients as lapse-independent constants. This is
an independent algebraic control of the general product-rule proof.

On FLRW the second-order coefficients use

    R_D=D[2H'+(D+1)H^2],
    Ricci_nn=-D(H'+H^2), Ricci_spatial_average=H'+D H^2.

For the fourth-order scalar tensor, the code varies its invariant
action directly. Its pressure also satisfies its D-dimensional local
Ward identity. Its scalar Laplacian is boxR_D=-R_D''-D H R_D'.
These are local covariant counterterm tensors, not renormalized scalar
expectation values with finite trace/EOM anomalies discarded.

## Finite term and checks

Set D=3-2epsilon_DR only after varying this counterterm density.
If P_CT(D) is its lapse pole coefficient, subtract
-2 partial_D P_CT(D)|_3 from the radial finite coefficient derived
in S6.52. The resulting finite local energy is thereby matched in
this explicit prescription. Pressure has only the ordinary-Proca
counterterm contribution at first order on the clock.

All three actual lapse poles agree at D=3. The zero-order finite
energy is unchanged from S6.47. Switching off alpha,beta reproduces
the independently checked ordinary-Proca finite result. The extra
second-order evanescent lapse coefficient is

    alpha[H^2+(8/3)H']+beta[3H^2-2H'].

At mu=m its matched energy coefficient is therefore

    -(10+alpha-3beta)H^2-(2/3)beta H'.

The full fourth-order expression is retained in the source and report.
This calculation does not replace a dimensionally regulated scalar
identity by a finite renormalized one; such a substitution can miss
finite anomalies and would not establish this prescription.

## Scope

The result concerns the retained Gaussian vector's on-clock first
homogeneous metric variations on the compact rolling interval. The
mass component exchanges with the clock equation and is not required
to obey an ordinary separately conserved Proca stress identity. No
bound on the scalar equation, derivatives of the nonlocal state term,
quadratic/higher quantum variations, full corrected bounce/constraints
or cones follows from energy/pressure values alone.

All-order Hadamard admissibility, all-time state control, other field
and higher loops, interacting cutoff, a common UV/vacuum extension,
finite-gravity Regge bounds and V/G/B remain open. The timelike
projector prescription is not asserted to extend smoothly across X=0.
No frozen source, action or finite potential coefficient is changed.
