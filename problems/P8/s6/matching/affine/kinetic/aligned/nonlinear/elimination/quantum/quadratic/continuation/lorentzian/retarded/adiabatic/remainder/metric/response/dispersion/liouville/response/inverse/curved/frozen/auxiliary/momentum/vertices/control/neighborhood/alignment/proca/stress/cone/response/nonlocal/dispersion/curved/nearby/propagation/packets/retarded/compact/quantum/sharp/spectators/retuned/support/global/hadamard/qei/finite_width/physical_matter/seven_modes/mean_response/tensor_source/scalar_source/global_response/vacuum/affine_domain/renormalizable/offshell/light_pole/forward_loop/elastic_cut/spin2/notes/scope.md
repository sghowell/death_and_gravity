# Exact scope and unresolved obligations

This leaf supplies the one-loop on-shell spin-two matter form factor
of the actual polynomial vacuum after one specified minimal Einstein
extension. It uses the same already-fixed on-shell light mass and
residue scheme, both mixed internal stress insertions, and a literal
canonical-stress projection. The result includes a strict slope bound,
a complex-transfer remainder, and a controlled negative-transfer limit
of the t-channel graviton vertex coefficient.

It does not supply:

- the complete order-M_Pl^-2 amplitude in every exchange channel;
- a bound on graviton loops, massless cuts or higher-loop matter effects;
- a derived Regge residue, trajectory, contour or Delta_grav;
- arbitrary higher-curvature matching coefficients;
- quantum equivalence of the earlier derivative field map;
- a common finite-gravity parent for the regular affine vacuum and the
  complete rolling bounce, with its state and canonical maps.

The selected hierarchy is not an error estimate for omitted diagrams.
The heavy spin-zero mass is not a demonstrated Reggeization scale.
The minimally coupled positive-kinetic scalar parent still obeys the
flat-FLRW no-bounce obstruction already recorded for that parent.
This does not exclude other parents or an entire original ladder row.

Residuals and native replays check the stated algebra, input rejection
and immutable certificate structure. Continuous estimates, covariant
loop reduction and diagram completeness at this restricted order are
written arguments, not proof-assistant formalized or peer reviewed.
Full V/G/B and original P8 remain OPEN.
