formulation, with actual couplings and explicit absolute error bounds

themselves become a fully normalized two-loop amplitude here. Their

independently. Nor is the source-aware derivative coordinate map
extended to two loops by the canonical two-point result.

corrected cosmological characteristic cones and a common controlled
