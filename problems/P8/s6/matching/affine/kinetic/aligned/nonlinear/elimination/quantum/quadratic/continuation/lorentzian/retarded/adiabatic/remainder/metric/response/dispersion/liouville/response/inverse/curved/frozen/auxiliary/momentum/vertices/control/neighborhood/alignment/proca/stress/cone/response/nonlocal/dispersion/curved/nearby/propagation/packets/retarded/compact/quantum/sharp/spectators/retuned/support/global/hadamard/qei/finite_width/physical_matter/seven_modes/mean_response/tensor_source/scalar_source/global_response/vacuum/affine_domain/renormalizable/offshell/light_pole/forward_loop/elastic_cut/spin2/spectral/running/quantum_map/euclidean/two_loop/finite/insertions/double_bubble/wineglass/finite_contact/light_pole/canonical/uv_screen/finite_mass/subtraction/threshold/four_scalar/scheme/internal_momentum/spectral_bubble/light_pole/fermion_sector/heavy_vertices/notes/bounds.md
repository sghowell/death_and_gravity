# Spectral integration, exact logarithm enclosure and Cauchy bound

The complete paired insertion has the frozen density
w(v)=C v (1-T/v)^(3/2)/(v-1)^2 with
T=4mF^2>=16 and C=2NY/Q, N=6. In particular
0<=w(v)<=4C/v. Its total measure is infinite;
only the weighted, properly subtracted expressions
below are used.

The parameter estimates and v-4>=v/2 give

    integral_T^infinity w |J2| <=12 C/T,
    integral_T^infinity w |J3| <=8 C[log(4T)+1]/T,
    integral_T^infinity w |J4| <=16 C/T.

The primitives are -1/v and
-[log(4v)+1]/v; both vanish at infinity. Thus all
three spectral integrals have explicit integrable
majorants uniform in the forward disc. Together
with the strict parameter gap these prove
holomorphy and justify exchanges of integrals
and the finite regulator limit after subtraction.

There are three channels, with one, four and four
bubble, triangle and box products. The two
insertion positions carry one half each. With
V=L+g/(M-3), the total absolute amplitude obeys

    E = C/(Q T) [36 V^2
                 +96 g V (log(4T)+1)+192 g^2].

The same E bounds b2 by Cauchy's coefficient
formula on |s-2|=1. This does not claim a bound
on the entire quadratic Taylor remainder up to
that same boundary, nor a positive sign for the
full family. The t-channel triangle/box dependence
is included in the common three-channel bound.

For exact rational inputs find the least integer
n>=0 such that 4T<=2^n, using integer comparisons.
Since log(2)=integral_0^1 dx/(1+x)<1,
log(4T)+1<n+1 for the present T>=16. No floating
logarithm enters the enclosure. For the actual
mF=10^200, n=1333, hence n+1=1334.

Using the inherited rational upper bound on Y
and Q>=144 gives E approximately
3.9737348142794 times 10^-623, below 10^-622.
Relative to 4 lambda it is approximately
9.9343370356986 times 10^-24, below 10^-22.
These are upper bounds, not quadrature values
of the physical two-loop amplitude.
