# Provenance and evidence boundary

The local forest prescription follows the already adopted
[Herzog review, section 3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1711.06121):
only disjoint or nested UV cores enter a restricted forest,
and the local subtraction operation must specify its scheme.

The 32 actual graphs, their independent denominator squares,
counterterm coefficients, complete integral estimates and
rational pole bound are derived here. They are not quoted
from that source or inferred from a general renormalizability
statement. No massless QCD or R-star conclusion is imported.

S6.110 fixes the actual polynomial model; S6.112 fixes the
light OS kernel; S6.113 fixes total canonical interaction
counterterms and the entire I0 reference; S6.122 fixes the
inserted Q=alpha+R split; S6.125 retains the once-fixed
quartic potential condition. Read-only parent replay and
source hashes enforce this provenance.

The finite combinatorial and algebraic computations are native
exact checks. Continuous analytic continuation, domination,
subtraction and complex-pole arguments are written proofs,
not proof-assistant formalization, peer review or a proof of
the original P8 UV-admissibility contract.
