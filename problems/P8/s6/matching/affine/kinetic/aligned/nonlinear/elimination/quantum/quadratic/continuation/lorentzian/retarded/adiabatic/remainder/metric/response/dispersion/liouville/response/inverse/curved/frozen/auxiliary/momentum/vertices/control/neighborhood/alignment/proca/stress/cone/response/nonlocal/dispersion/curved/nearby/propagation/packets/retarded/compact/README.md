# Quantitative compact classical response

This checkpoint specifies compact local classical probes with
a positive retarded pairing outside the physical matter cone.
It gives exact symbolic widths, a rational signal lower bound
and a positive finite spatial-frequency contribution. It also
separates two still-unproved common-parent error requirements.

Read [the formulation](FORMULATION.md), then:

1. [Smooth normalized compact probes](notes/probes.md).
2. [Positive signal and complete remainder](notes/signal.md).
3. [Finite spatial-frequency contribution](notes/band.md).
4. [Conditional matching and parent-tail gates](notes/matching.md).
5. [Remaining original-P8 scope](notes/scope.md).

The p8_proca_compact_response verifier natively rebuilds its
parent and compares quantitative-compact-classical-response.json
read-only. Exact checks do not convert the extremely narrow
mathematical probes into an interacting EFT or quantum experiment.
Original P8 remains open.
