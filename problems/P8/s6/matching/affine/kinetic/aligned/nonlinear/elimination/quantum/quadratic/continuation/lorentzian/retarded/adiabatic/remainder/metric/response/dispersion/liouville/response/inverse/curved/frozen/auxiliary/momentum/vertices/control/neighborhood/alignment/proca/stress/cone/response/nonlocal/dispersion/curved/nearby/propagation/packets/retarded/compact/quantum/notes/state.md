# Explicit positive Gaussian linear-field state

Let hbar>0 and kappa>0 be fixed constants. At the earliest time
t_*=-T/2 choose the even smooth weight mu(k)=sqrt(1+|k-vector|^2).
This is an arbitrary auxiliary covariance weight, not the actual
dispersion, an instantaneous Hamiltonian ground state, or a
claim about adiabatic order. It is regular at zero momentum.

For the density phase (q_1,q_2,pi_1,pi_2), define the ordered
Fourier Cauchy covariance
C0=hbar/(2 kappa) [[mu^-1 I,i I],[-i I,mu I]].
Its Gram factor is B=sqrt(hbar/(2 kappa))
[mu^-1/2 I; -i mu^1/2 I]. Native identities check C0=B B^dagger,
C0=C0^dagger and C0-C0^T=i hbar Omega/kappa.
Thus C0 is positive semidefinite and has exactly the required
antisymmetric part, with both frequency weights retained.

Here is an explicit state construction rather than an appeal to a
Klein-Gordon existence theorem for a different principal operator.
Use unitary spatial Fourier transform and the one-particle space
L2(R^3,d^3k;C^2). For a real Schwartz density-phase Cauchy test F,
put K F=B^dagger Fhat. The weight and its inverse grow at most
polynomially, so K F belongs to this Hilbert space and depends
continuously on F. Its inner product satisfies
2 Im <KF,KG>=(hbar/kappa) integral F^T Omega G.
The reality and evenness in momentum are used here.

On the ordinary symmetric bosonic Fock space, define
Phi(F)=a(KF)+a^dagger(KF), taking a anti-linear and a^dagger linear.
The algebraic finite-particle domain generated by these vectors
is invariant. The vacuum gives C0 as its two-point function;
the creation/annihilation CCR give the stipulated canonical
commutator. It is a normalized positive state because for every
polynomial A, its expectation of A^* A is norm(A vacuum)^2.
Odd correlations vanish and even correlations obey Wick pairings.

Define the evolved field by applying this same initial field
to U(t,t_*)^T Fhat, with the Fourier interpretation understood.
The estimates in growth.md prove these are Schwartz Cauchy tests,
and also permit integrating smooth compact spacetime tests.
Consequently all the asserted smeared operators and their
Gaussian correlations are tempered and well-defined. Wick
pairing of distinct argument slots defines n-point distributions;
this is not a claim about coincident-point Wick polynomials.

The two-time covariance is U_t C0 U_s^T and remains positive by
congruence on every finite collection of smeared tests.
Its antisymmetric part is i hbar U_t Omega U_s^T/kappa.
Symplectic composition gives U_t Omega U_s^T=U(t,s) Omega.
Thus the commutator does not depend on the symmetric Gaussian
covariance, and is the same in every state of this CCR algebra.

This constructs fields using one initial representation. It does
not assert that every symplectic time-evolution map is implemented
by a unitary on the infinite-volume Fock space. Nor does positivity
imply the Hadamard singularity required for a renormalized stress
tensor. The distinction between a smeared-field algebra and its
extension to local stress observables is explained in
[Khavkine and Moretti, sections 2-3](https://arxiv.org/html/1412.5945).
Their Klein-Gordon existence assumptions are not imported here.
