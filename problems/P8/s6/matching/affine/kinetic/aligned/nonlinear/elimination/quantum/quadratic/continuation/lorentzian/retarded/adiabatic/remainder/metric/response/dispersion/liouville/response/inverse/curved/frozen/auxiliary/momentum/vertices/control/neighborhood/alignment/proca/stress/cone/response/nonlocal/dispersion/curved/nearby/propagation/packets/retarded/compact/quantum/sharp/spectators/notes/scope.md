# Scope and remaining constructive route

The general theorem assumes a finite second-order Hermitian physical
principal pencil with positive definite full kinetic form and an explicit
embedding of the old kinetic-canonical light sector. It includes mixed
time-space couplings and arbitrary finite heavy/cross blocks. Positivity
of a proposed parent is an assumption to be checked, not a consequence of
the calculation. The ordinary-Proca application uses a separate actual
constraint and quadratic-decoupling calculation.

A frequency-dependent effective Schur complement need not have the same
light restriction as a bare parent. Higher-derivative, nonlocal and
infinite-tower systems, loop-induced changes of light coefficients and
nonpositive or unreduced constrained extensions are not classified.
This screening result cannot be promoted to a universal UV no-go.

The perturbative gravitational-shockwave example of
[Hollowood and Shore](https://arxiv.org/html/1512.04952v2), sections 1 and
5.1, is a useful scope check: its loop-induced frequency dependence lies
outside an unchanged finite classical principal block. It is not a
matching construction for this bounce.

The necessary repair norm is a target for a SEPARATELY NAMED changed
light action, not a permission to silently edit the frozen witness.
Such a candidate would need its literal covariant operator, actual
background and constraints, canonical principal forms, interaction/error
budget, physical matter frame and vacuum/matching analysis. An algebraic
matrix repair alone supplies none of them. Corrections that vanish with
their first variation on the original clock trajectory could be screened
constructively, but their Hessian, nearby flow and remaining sectors
would have to be derived anew.

Neither the compact response in S6.94 nor its finite spatial cutoff is
converted here into a joint temporal/spatial interacting EFT statement.
The actual common-parent low-band error and that parent's own high-band
tail remain unproved. Hadamard stress, semiclassical backreaction, V/G/B,
realistic-field P8(a) and original P8 remain open. No user intervention
is needed merely to continue these mathematical investigations.
