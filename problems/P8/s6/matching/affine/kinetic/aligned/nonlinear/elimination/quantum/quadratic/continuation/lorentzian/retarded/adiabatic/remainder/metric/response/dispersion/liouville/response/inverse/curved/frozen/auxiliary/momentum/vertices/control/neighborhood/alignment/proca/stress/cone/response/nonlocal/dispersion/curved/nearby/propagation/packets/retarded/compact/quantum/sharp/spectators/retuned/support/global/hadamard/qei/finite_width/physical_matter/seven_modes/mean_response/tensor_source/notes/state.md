# A fixed positive smooth difference of the actual tensor state

Keep the S6.103 reference, its scalar/vector factors, the
physical background, action and all counterterms unchanged.
Use the already specified normalized radial bump

    b(k)=c exp[-1/((q-1)(4-q))],  1<q=|k|^2<4,

zero elsewhere, with integral b(k) d^3k/(2pi)^3=1.
At u=0 add eta b(k) I2 to EACH of the two TT canonical
polarization covariances, with 0<=eta<=10^-20.
This is a positive real symmetric addition: it preserves
the antisymmetric CCR and Gaussian positivity.

No global polarization frame is chosen. In the Cartesian
TT variables its diagonal coordinate/momentum blocks are

    2 eta b Pi_TT,  eta b Pi_TT/2,

and its mixed block vanishes. These expressions agree with
E:E=2 in every orthonormal two-polarization frame and are
smooth because the bump stays away from k=0.

Evolve both legs with the unchanged actual tensor generator.
On any compact time interval, smooth ODE dependence and
compact frequency support bound every time/spatial derivative.
Thus the difference is smooth, positive and a bisolution.
Adding it to the parent Hadamard two-point function preserves
the parent's wavefront condition and its other five modes.

The addition is rotation invariant; parity cancels the flux
and angular averaging makes the mean spatial pressure isotropic.
It has finite homogeneous energy DENSITY, not finite total
energy on infinite R3. It is the added covariance, not the
prepared reference, that is invariant under time reflection.
The generator is even in u and anticommutes with diag(1,-1);
the anchor I2 is invariant under that reversal.

The exact proxy stress obeys r'+3H(r+s)=0 and -r/3<=s<=r.
For u>=0, H>=0 therefore makes r a^2 nonincreasing.
Reflection extends the estimate to both tails:

    r(u)<=r(0)/(1+u^2)^4 <=4 eta/(1+u^2)^4.

Here the two-polarization anchor traces are
r(0)/eta=(q+4)/2 and s(0)/eta=(12-q)/6 before band integration.
This positive proxy supplies estimates; the action-derived
physical source is still rho_T=r-(r-3s)/h.
