# Restricted control, not a higher-loop or UV theorem

The same canonical parent admits an all-real-Euclidean-momentum
bound for its computed one-loop relative self-energy. Repetitions
of that one kernel are controlled on the stated form/source
domains. The two-line comparison requires an already absolutely
integrable test kernel; the generic unsubtracted local quartic
loop fails that requirement.

It remains necessary to compute or bound primitive higher-loop
vertices and self-energies, their counterterms, and convergent
derivatives of the full scattering amplitude. A tiny insertion
norm does not remove those obligations. Euclidean positivity
also does not supply a timelike high-energy or Regge contour.

The derivative-coordinate witness is normalization-specific,
not a physical instability. Higher-loop vacuum estimates should
be developed in the canonical parent variables and transported
on shell using the proven source/LSZ statement, rather than by
assuming a uniform global off-shell coordinate bound.

None of this transports the state, absolute counterterms,
canonical cosmological observables or corrected cones to a
common propagating bounce parent. Finite-gravity control and
original V/G/B remain open. The scoped P8(a) result is unchanged;
original P8(b) and original P8 are not finished or closed.
