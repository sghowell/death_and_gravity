## Exact low-order coefficients, bounded analytic remainder

Write ell=ell0+e, N=N0+n. The actual degree-three time/phase jet is
generated by

    J=N[p^2/(4a0)-f0+ell^2/(2U0)]
      -N b1 u p/(2a0)
      +N[b1^2/(2a0)-f2+ell^2 (U^-1)_uu/2] u^2/2.

Here the coefficient functions retain exact N dependence. Taylor
truncation is by total degree in n,u,p,e, not by separate degrees.
The generator contains some terms of degree four which are discarded
when extracting a jet. The actual I1 and I3 from acceleration.md are
inserted only to compute these finite derivatives. The full analytic
Hamiltonian, not J, is used in the remainder estimates and evolution.

Independently differentiating the original coefficients with
I=I1*u+I3*u^3/6 and I_phi=I1+I3*u^2/2 reproduces H at u=0,
H_up and H_uu at p=0. Parity checks all otherwise missing odd-time
jets through degree three, and H_upp(0)=H_uup(0)=0.
These identities hold as functions of independent N and ell, so
further lapse and density derivatives may be taken before imposing
ell0^2=P2(N0). Thus J gives the required actual jets.

The exact central coefficients are enclosed after those derivatives.
For even powers of ell0 use P2(N0); for an odd remaining power use
ell0<11/100. Put r=sqrt(N0) solely for real coefficient enclosures.
The family satisfies |r-1|<10^-6. Every remaining expression is
rational in r and bounded by the original rational coefficient
enclosure algorithm on that larger symmetric r interval. This
does not replace the complex analytic lapse coordinate by a
multivalued square root.
