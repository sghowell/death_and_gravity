# What this finishes and what remains

This checkpoint finishes the missing free tensor/vector
state factors identified in S6.102. Together with the
unchanged scalar factor it supplies a positive global
quasifree state for all seven physical quadratic modes,
with the actual constrained Proca covariance, tensor
normalization, density CCR and signed wavefront cones.
The preparation is an explicit selection of global
Cauchy data on the original physical geometry.

It does not renormalize S6.102's pointwise stress vertices.
For that task the diagonal singularity, the physical
metric and clock responses, second-order mean fields,
counterterms and the complete Ward identity must be
handled consistently. Positivity of a free state does
not turn an indefinite physical stress second variation
into S6.101's positive test energy. Nor does normal
ordering that variation alone solve the mean equations.

No unique preferred state for all purposes is asserted.
The tensor/vector prescription is fixed here, while the
previously chosen scalar family's member is retained
literally. This product need not equal a prior tadpole
reference. No frozen tadpole values or subtraction
conventions are reinterpreted.

The result is not a nonlinear quantum-gravity state,
a covariant gauge-fixed/BRST interacting construction,
or a local observable algebra for raw TT gauge components.
It does not bound interaction loops, heavy thresholds
or omitted EFT operators. Vacuum V, finite-gravity G
and common-parent bounce B matching remain research
work, as does the original P8(b) UV classification.
No new user choice is required merely to begin those
remaining calculations.

The external corrected propagation theorem and the
written energy, infrared and microlocal arguments are
part of the pinned proof, not identities proved by
a symbolic residual or a proof assistant. Native
algebra checks finite exact inputs and the stated
canonical identities; no numerical sampling is
advertised as an all-frequency analytic proof.
