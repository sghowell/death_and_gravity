# Primary-source context

Criado and Perez-Victoria,
[Field redefinitions in effective theories at higher orders](https://arxiv.org/html/1811.09413v2),
sections 2 and 4, distinguish source-aware perturbative equivalence
from off-shell effective-action equality and truncated matching.
Their dimensional-regularization Jacobian and light-only heavy-
integration discussion guide the scope here. The actual derivative
contraction, finite subtraction term, generated vertices and
quantitative bounds above are independently calculated for this model.
