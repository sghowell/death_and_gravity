# Fixed local references, finite contacts and the regulator

All local counterterms use the same inherited canonical scheme.
The first-order UV parameters obey
L1_UV=3L^2 Ibar/2, G1=LG Ibar/2, M1=G^2 Ibar/2.
The once-fixed finite potential contact sigma contributes only to
L1=L1_UV+sigma. In the full reduced vertex,

    delta V1_UV = -L1_UV+2G G1 sum h-G^2 M1 sum h^2
                = -Ibar sum(-L+G^2 h)^2/2.

This identity fixes the signs and all local Phi4, H Phi2 and H2
subtractions used in the independent marked-core census.

The source-pinned second-order LINEAR reference contributions are:

- insertion family: twice alpha times the inherited first-order UV
  parameter variation, after the actual inner light OS grouping;
- double bubble: the shared-core recursive reference with
  delta L=3L^3 Ibar^2/4, delta g=L^2 g Ibar^2/2,
  delta M=L g Ibar^2/4;
- wineglass: delta L=-3L^3 J0, delta g=-L^2 g J0,
  delta M=0, with J0 the fixed inner-subtracted zero-external
  recursive reference;
- finite contact: sigma times the L derivative of the inherited
  first-order UV reference.

Each formula is imported from the actual ancestor module and checked
as a literal local tree variation. In converting to a fundamental
cubic vertex, divide each linear delta g by 2G. Add the disjoint
first-order products separately, exactly once, as proved above.

The insertion alpha term includes the local divergence generated
by the finite inner kinetic subtraction. Raw superficial degree
alone would miss that subtraction-generated term. The fixed OS
Q=alpha+R grouping and its outer reference retain it.

The finite one-loop potential contact is not set to zero merely
because its tree b2 vanishes. Its complete one-loop insertion,
including both heavy triangles and the linear reference variation,
is the separately integrated S6.125 contribution.

A new second-order Phi4 contact sigma2 retains the same constant-field
quartic condition. In this massive perturbative vacuum, the fully
renormalized zero-external four-point function is finite and analytic
near zero external momenta; its remaining constant Taylor coefficient
can be fixed by that local contact. No new derivative Phi4
counterterm is required by the actual logarithmic UV types.
The numerical value of sigma2 is unnecessary for b2 because its
second momentum derivative vanishes identically. This does not
establish a globally controlled two-loop effective potential.

No additional finite G2 or M2 freedom is used to fit the forward
coefficient: their recursive references have already been specified.
The heavy field retains its canonical zero-external reference, not
a stable-heavy-particle on-shell scheme.

Crucially, Ibar, J0, alpha and the local parameters denote their full
regulator-dependent quantities until the forest sums cancel.
Evanescent terms are not discarded inside a product with a divergent
reference. The displayed convergent kernels and numerical bounds
are their limits after the common-regulator cancellations. I0 and
J0 are never separately assigned finite numerical values.
