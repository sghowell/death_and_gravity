# Leading matched-operator massless cut

Work to leading order in the local matched interaction
C Phi^2 F^a_munu F^{a munu}, with the canonical unbroken
gauge kinetic term. Two scalar derivatives and the two
linearized field strengths give the transverse amplitude
8 C delta_ab T, where

    T=(k.l)(epsilon1.epsilon2)
       -(k.epsilon2)(l.epsilon1).

An overall vertex sign does not affect the cut. With
k=(E,0,0,E), l=(E,0,0,-E), s=4E^2, the two parallel
linear polarizations each have squared amplitude 16 C^2 s^2;
the two orthogonal choices vanish. Thus the polarization
sum is 32 C^2 s^2. The code also contracts the covariant
tensor with two metrics and checks both Ward identities,
giving the same sum without the explicit polarization basis.
SU(3) supplies dA=8 colors.

Massless two-body phase space integrates to 1/(8pi).
The identical-particle factor is 1/2!, and the optical
relation is 2 Im A = sum of cuts. Therefore

    Im A_s = dA C^2 s^2/pi.

For physical scalar scattering this is a channel contribution
at s>=4. Its local-operator loop analytically continues to
the massless branch point at s=0. No positive physical
massive-scalar cross section below s=4 is being asserted.

Modulo local counterterms, the corresponding forward logs are

    -K [s^2 log(-s/nu^2) + (4-s)^2 log(-(4-s)/nu^2)],
    K=dA C^2/pi^2.

The t-channel t^2 log(-t) term vanishes in the forward limit.
For 0<s<4 the s and u cuts overlap. On the upper physical
boundary use log(-s)=log(s)-i pi and log(-u)=log(u)+i pi;
the lower boundary reverses both signs. Their difference is

    A_plus - A_minus = 16 i pi K (s-2).

It vanishes at s=2, but its first derivative is nonzero.
At s=3/2, strictly inside the old |s-2|<=1 disc, it is
-8 i pi K. This proves failure of a center-only cut test.

The second derivative of this leading mismatch is zero.
A local branch of each logarithm can also be chosen.
We do NOT claim either is mathematically impossible.
Neither supplies the massive-gap dispersion representation:
the physical s/u cuts no longer leave its real subtraction
interval, and its original low-cut inventory is incomplete.
A new IR subtraction/regulator and appropriate hypotheses
would have to be analyzed explicitly.

The full parent diagram has two one-fermion-loop matched
vertices and one gauge loop, hence three loops. It is not
a three-loop calculation in the old scalar-only theory.
Finite-mass derivative corrections, other graphs and
nonperturbative confinement remain outside this result.
