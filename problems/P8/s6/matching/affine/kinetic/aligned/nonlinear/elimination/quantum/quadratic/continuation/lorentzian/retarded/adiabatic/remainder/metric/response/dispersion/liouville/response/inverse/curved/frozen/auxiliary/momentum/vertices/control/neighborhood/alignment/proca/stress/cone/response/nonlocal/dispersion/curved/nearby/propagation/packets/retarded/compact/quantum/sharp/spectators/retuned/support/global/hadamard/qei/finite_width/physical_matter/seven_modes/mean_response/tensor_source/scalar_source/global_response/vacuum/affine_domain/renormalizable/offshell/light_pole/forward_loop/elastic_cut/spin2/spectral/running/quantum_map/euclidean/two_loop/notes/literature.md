# Primary-source context and independent calculation

[Bogner and Weinzierl, sections 2-5](https://arxiv.org/html/1002.3458v3)
give the parametric representation, spanning-tree/two-forest
polynomials and matrix-tree construction, and distinguish
ultraviolet boundaries from interior Landau singularities.
Those constructions provide the general framework. The
192 actual refinements, exact positivity witnesses and common
compact gap are independently computed here.

[Herzog, section 3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1711.06121)
states the local R operation and restricted forests, with
nested counterterm operators applied inside out. Here only
the actual graph inventory and compatible sets are computed;
no subtraction integral or counterterm value is imported.

The full canonical quartic action, actual parameters, fixed
light on-shell prescription and one-loop comparison limitations
are frozen in S6.110-S6.119. No new all-energy or gravity
assumption is supplied by these sources.
