# S6.122: grouped on-shell self-energy insertions

Original P8 remains OPEN.

Group all 64 bare tadpole-insertion refinements of S6.120
with the already fixed one-loop on-shell quadratic
counterterms. Retain the actual full mixed self-energy;
do not separately bound the much larger local mass pieces.

Split its relative multiplier into its fixed asymptotic
constant and a decaying remainder. For the outer loop,
continue the S6.113 regulated reference subtraction linearly
for this insertion family. Keep its allowed local full-model
counterterms and the unchanged finite potential contact.

Prove an absolutely integrable complex forward-disc
majorant for the remainder and bound the constant term
using the complete previously renormalized one-loop result.
The heavy inverse is never expanded inside either loop.

This is a specific renormalized diagram family, not the
complete two-loop amplitude, its full pole/LSZ normalization
or an all-higher-loop error. Original V/G/B remains open.
