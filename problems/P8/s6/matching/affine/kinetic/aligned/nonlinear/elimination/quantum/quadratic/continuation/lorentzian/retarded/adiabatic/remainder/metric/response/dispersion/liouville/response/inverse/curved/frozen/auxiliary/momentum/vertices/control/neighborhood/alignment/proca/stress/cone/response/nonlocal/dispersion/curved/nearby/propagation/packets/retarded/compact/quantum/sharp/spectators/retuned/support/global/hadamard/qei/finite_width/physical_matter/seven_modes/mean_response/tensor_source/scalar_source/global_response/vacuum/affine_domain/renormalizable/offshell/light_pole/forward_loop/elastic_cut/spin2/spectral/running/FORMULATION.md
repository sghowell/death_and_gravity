# One-loop reference transport in the same polynomial model

Take the polynomial vacuum and the once-fixed subtraction scheme
of S6.110-S6.114. Write L=lambda4, g=G^2 and M=mu_H^2.
The historical parent key bare_polynomial_quartic specifies the
renormalized displayed tree coefficient in that scheme, not an
unregulated bare coupling.

Introduce a positive subtraction reference nu. Replace only the
common zero-external-momentum reference bubble of mass one by
one of mass nu, and derive its exact finite difference. Anchor
the original finite potential contact at nu=1 and transport it;
do not impose a new finite potential condition at every nu.

Derive the beta coefficients from that change and independently
from the full two-real-scalar coupling tensors. The physical
light pole mass and residue remain on shell; the diagnostic
light mass UV coefficient is not used to redefine them as MS
running parameters. Heavy parameters remain scheme coordinates,
not an exact stable-heavy pole.

Solve the explicitly one-loop beta equations and bound their
solution on ell=ln(nu)/(16pi^2) in [0,9]. This includes
1<=nu<=10^400, the previously selected Planck hierarchy.

The requested outputs are full-channel compensation through
one loop, preservation of the positive tree-potential margin,
and explicit finite reference-running bounds. A reference at
the Planck scale is not a claim that gravitational diagrams,
higher loops or a physical amplitude there have been controlled.
