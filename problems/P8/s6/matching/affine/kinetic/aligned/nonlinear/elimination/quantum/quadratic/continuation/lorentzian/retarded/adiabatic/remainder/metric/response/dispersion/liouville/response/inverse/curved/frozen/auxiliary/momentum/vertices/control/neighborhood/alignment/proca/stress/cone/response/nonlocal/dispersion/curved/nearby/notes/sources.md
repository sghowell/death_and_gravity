# Sources and reuse boundary

The literal homogeneous Hamiltonian and primitive are from the frozen
S6.75 action, with the S6.81 source-free constant-physical-mass update.
S6.83 supplies the correctly differentiated central phase family,
hat-Hubble time jet and exact central physical scalar cone. The new
third primitive derivative, lapse acceleration, recentered complex
domain and actual local evolution are derived here.

The old real-time Hamiltonian bound is not reused as a joint complex-
time bound: complex-domain.md constructs a fresh smaller-domain
bound. Only the already-proved primitive and coefficient estimates
on their actual domains are reused. Fixed-center contraction,
Cauchy's derivative formula and Picard iteration are applied with
the explicit radii, factorials and row norm given in the proofs.

For the separate interpretation of a central superluminal cone,
[de Rham and Tolley, Causality in Curved Spacetimes: The Speed of Light
and Gravity](https://arxiv.org/html/2007.01847v2), especially sections
3.2--3.3 and 4.2--4.4, distinguishes low-energy speeds from a
resolvable causal obstruction. The observable, field frame,
decoupling limit and EFT errors need justification. This paper is
not used to certify the local ODE solution or to declare this
candidate UV-admissible or excluded.
