# Methodological cross-check

[De Felice, Mukohyama and Takahashi, arXiv:2110.03194](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.03194),
especially section IV, motivates a specific caution: degeneracy checked
only in a scalar-clock gauge need not settle spatial auxiliary constraints.
An elliptic lapse equation can require boundary data in their studied
class. This checkpoint does not import their model's verdict. It computes
the present action's full lapse-gradient cancellation, Maxwell Legendre
map and local two-auxiliary Jacobian directly. The all64 affine elimination
and source identity come from the frozen in-repository derivation, not
from an external classification of a different metric-affine basis.
