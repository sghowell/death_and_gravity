nu^2=m^2+k_com^2/A^2. Keep m=1000 and L=M*tau=10^400.

integral |Bcal|*nu^6 d^3k_com/(2pi)^3. It consists of low,
middle and high preparation bands plus the all-momentum
oscillatory-evolution contribution; the certificate records all

replayed. The cutoff between proof bands is not a physical

    finite local term = sum_n U_(n,j) m^(4-2n)/(576 L^2).

polarization envelopes. U_(n,j) bounds the actual new matched
