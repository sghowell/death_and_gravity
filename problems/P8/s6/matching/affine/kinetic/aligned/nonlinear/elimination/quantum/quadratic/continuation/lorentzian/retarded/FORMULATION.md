Date: 2026-09-08. Original P8 remains OPEN.

Hadamard Gaussian vector state and the S6.60 fixed scalar profiles.

    a_mass=1+alpha(u)n, b_mass=1+beta(u)n,
    alpha=4/[9(1+u^2)^3], beta=28/[81(1+u^2)^3].

6. Insert the unchanged clock profiles, retain initial preparation,
   explain the background causal support and the finite-kernel boundary,

## Prepared-source domain and scope

For the zero initial-covariance response, restrict test sources to
smooth compact support strictly after u0=-1/2, vanishing on an
initial neighborhood. The old state and equations then agree there.
This is a restricted perturbative source preparation, not a new
compatible family for arbitrary metric or Cauchy-data variations.

The mass-response formula is a bare distributional kernel with an
explicit contact term. It is not yet the finite dimensionally matched
kernel. The diagonal extension, common normalization, evanescent
counterterm and finite matching must still be combined. A retarded
support statement around the unchanged Proca background does not
prove the physical cone of a finite off-clock perturbation.

No integrated feedback bound, full metric response, quantum stability,
interaction cutoff, common UV parent, V/G/B or original P8 closure is
established. The selected background cancellation is unchanged.
