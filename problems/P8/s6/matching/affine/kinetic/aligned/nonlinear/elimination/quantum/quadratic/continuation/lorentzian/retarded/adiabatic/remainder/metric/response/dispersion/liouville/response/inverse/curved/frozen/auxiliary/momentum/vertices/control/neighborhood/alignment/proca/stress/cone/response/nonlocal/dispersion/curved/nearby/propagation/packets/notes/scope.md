# Limits and research boundary

The action, nearby classical background, physical matter metric
and linearized equations are the unchanged ones from S6.88/S6.89.
This checkpoint passes from their ray separation to exact finite-band
classical wavepackets in a specified linear relational matter
observable. The preparation has controlled nonzero spatial tails.

It does not establish a compactly supported retarded source,
an operational quantum detector, a quantum commutator, or a
nonlinear solution close to the packet. The very conservative
carrier 10^72 is a finite mathematical frequency in the fixed
comoving normalization, not an asserted physically admitted
energy below a cutoff. No S6.78 hard-tree band is transferred
to this new frequency or background domain.

The distinction between wavelength resolution and EFT validity
is central to [Carrillo Gonzalez et al., Causal Effective Field
Theories](https://arxiv.org/html/2207.03491v2), especially sections
3 and4.1. Our calculation supplies a separate classical error
proof for a coupled gravitational background; it does not
import that paper's scalar EFT hierarchy or source assumptions.

Canonical interactions on this actual domain, omitted operators,
mixed loops, heavy thresholds, an admitted interacting band and
finite Wilson matching remain research tasks. So do compact
relational-source preparation, quantum response on the new
backgrounds, global/nonlinear control and common-parent V/G/B.

No UV exclusion or completion follows from this checkpoint,
and the original P8 problem remains open. The explicit written
ODE, analytic, Fourier and localization arguments are anchored
by exact native identities and rational bounds, not
proof-assistant formalization.
