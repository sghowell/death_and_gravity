# Targeted primary-source check

Reviewed 2026-09-09 while developing this separate vacuum model.
No published model is claimed to be its common bounce parent.

[Henning, Lu and Murayama, arXiv:1604.01019v1](https://arxiv.org/html/1604.01019v1),
sections 2.3 and 3.1, especially equations (46), (51), (53)-(61),
derive the resolved full-Hessian determinant and explain how the
nonlocal stationary light action retains mixed heavy/light loops.
Their discussion also separates exact nonlocal integration from
a finite local expansion used in matching. The present model's
finite-regulator square, full Hessian and determinant are
independently reconstructed, rather than supplied by citation.

[Ellis, Quevillon, You and Zhang, arXiv:1604.02445v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1604.02445),
sections 2-3, include light as well as heavy fluctuations to
recover mixed contributions. This reinforces why a
field-independent heavy determinant alone is not a reason
to discard such loops. No tabulated Wilson coefficient is
borrowed for our different massive model.

[de Rham et al., arXiv:1702.08577](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1702.08577),
section 6, gives a different weakly coupled massive example
and illustrates that the relevant derivative interactions
can first be generated at loop order. Its Galileon arguments,
couplings and loop estimates are not transplanted here.
Our derivative quartic and scalar potential do not justify
a Galileon nonrenormalization assumption.

All model-specific stability, power-counting, Gaussian,
kernel and integral inequalities in this package are given
in the adjoining proofs and exact checks. The references
provide methodological context, not an interacting V/G/B
certificate. Written arguments are not proof-assistant
formalized or peer reviewed.
