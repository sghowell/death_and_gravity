# What this family does and does not complete

This child proves the integrated bound for the
24 subtraction-dependent factorizing double-bubble
refinements with the stated graph-level local
reference subtractions. It does not recount the
eight already finite factorizing refinements.

The three bounded groups represent 176 bare
refinements. Their combined absolute error bound
does not determine a signed two-loop coefficient.

The other 16 bare wineglass refinements, their
recursive local subtractions and integrated
bounds remain open. Lower-order fixed finite
potential counterterms inserted into one-loop
amplitudes must also be accounted for. Complete
two-loop local counterterm, light mass/residue/LSZ
and source-aware field-map calculations have not
been completed by this graph count.

The reference prescription is fixed before
bounding. No adjustable higher-derivative four-point
counterterm is introduced to tune b2.

All-higher-loop truncation errors, an all-energy
complex contour, finite-gravity IR/Regge and
Delta_grav, quantum-state transfer, coupled
cosmological renormalization and a common controlled
bounce parent remain open. These are original
V/G/B obligations, not consequences of a small
partial two-loop correction.

Scoped P8(a) and A.20-A.23 are unchanged.
Original P8(b) and original P8 remain OPEN.
