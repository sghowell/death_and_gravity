# Scope and controls

The theorem is a finite reference-state difference inequality for
actual reduced scalar differential squares on subclock worldlines.
The exact right side is a convergent functional of a chosen actual
generalized Hadamard reference state. It is not a numerical bound
at an arbitrary finite sampling width.

The explicit leading short-sampling constants are additional
state-independent asymptotic information. The 0.084--0.085 percent
test-energy enhancement is a correction to THIS reference-bound
coefficient. It is not evidence that a putative stronger inequality
is false, because no optimality or saturation theorem is proved.

Known-answer controls recover the decoupled free scalar coefficients,
keep both contributions when the speeds coincide, reject invalid
velocities and coefficient domains, and retain the nonzero clock
residue and the fixed-background stress divergence. The interluminal
example tests the actual pullback hypothesis and is not exaggerated
into a worldline nonexistence theorem.

None of these statements completes the covariant physical-stress,
null/nonminimal realistic-field, cosmological-strength or singularity
parts of original P8(a). Nor do they establish interacting, omitted-
operator, loop, threshold, vacuum/dispersion/common-parent V/G/B
control or the original UV classification in P8(b). The scoped photon
realization and earlier linear operator-ladder classification remain
complete and unchanged.

The remaining finite-width and covariant-source estimates are
research work. They are not an unspecified parameter for the user
to choose. Exact tests support the pinned mode, wavefront, pullback,
positive-type and scaling arguments; those written distribution
proofs are not proof-assistant formalization.
