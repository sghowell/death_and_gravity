# Restricted result and remaining families

This checkpoint establishes the positive spectral representation of one
formal fermion propagator insertion, with the whole fixed inner reference
subtraction. It bounds the finite outer scalar bubble with two local
quartic vertices, both possible inserted light lines, all three channels,
and one overall local quartic subtraction.

The resulting six-contribution family's forward second coefficient is
strictly positive and explicitly bounded. A local finite quartic
conversion cannot tune that coefficient within this literal family.

It does not include heavy-exchange vertices, primitive fermion-box
corrections, other two-loop topologies, other lower-order coupling/field
conversion terms, or a complete new-model two-loop budget. It does not
supply later-loop or truncation errors, a cutoff, global potential,
normalized exact spectral measure, reflection positivity, V high-energy
contours, finite-gravity Regge/IR Delta for G, or common bounce-parent B.

Original P8 remains OPEN. Failure of an omitted bound or a fixed-order
approximation is not exclusion of a complete old ladder row. The next
families and matching estimates remain research, not a user-choice blocker.
