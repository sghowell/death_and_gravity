# Actual fixed-contact and insertion bounds

Write ell=ln(4M). From the fixed negative sign,
the full radial integral and 0<|C0|<L,

    |delta L_fin| <12Lg ell/(16pi^2 M)
                  <4ell L^2/(16pi^2),

using the actual g/M<L/3. At ell<600 and pi>3,
the exact rational majorant is

    D_contact=(50/3)L^2 <10^-408.

It is also below 10^-6 of the actual positive
completed-square quartic margin. This last
comparison is a finite-contact diagnostic,
not a statement about divergent bare parameters
or the full quantum potential.

For the integrated quartic variation use the
previous full complex-disc bounds

    |C|<2L, |I_R|<2/(16pi^2),
    |T_A|,|T_B|<8g ell/(16pi^2 M).

Summing all three original channels gives

    |dA1/dL| <(12+8ell)L/(16pi^2).

The same strict analytic margins permit Cauchy's
radius-one bound on the second forward coefficient.
Multiplying by the fixed contact magnitude gives

    E_contact=(50/3)L^2 times (4812/144)L
              =(10025/18)L^3
              <7 times 10^-612,
    E_contact/(4lambda)<2 times 10^-12.

The diagnostic contact value is approximately
-1.00469 times 10^-409 and the insertion error
upper bound approximately 6.67799 times 10^-612.
Only the exact formulas and rational comparisons
are used in the proof.

Adding this contribution to the four disjoint
raw-graph groups gives an upper bound below
3 times 10^-607 and below 10^-7 of tree.
Adding the independently bounded one-loop
contribution still gives a known-contribution
upper bound below 10^-6 of tree.

These are bounds on the contributions explicitly
computed and grouped in these certificates.
They do not silently supply missing full
pole/residue/LSZ or source-aware matching, and
they do not estimate every higher perturbative
order or an all-energy contour.
