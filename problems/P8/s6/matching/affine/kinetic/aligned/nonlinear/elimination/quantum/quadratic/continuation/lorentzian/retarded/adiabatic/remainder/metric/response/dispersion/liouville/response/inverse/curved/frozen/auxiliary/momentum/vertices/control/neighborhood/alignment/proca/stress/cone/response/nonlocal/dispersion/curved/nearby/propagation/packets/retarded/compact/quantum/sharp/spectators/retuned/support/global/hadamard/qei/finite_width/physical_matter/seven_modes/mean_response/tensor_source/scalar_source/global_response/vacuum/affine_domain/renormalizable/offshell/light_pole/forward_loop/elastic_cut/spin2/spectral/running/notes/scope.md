# Running coordinates are not a UV-completion verdict

This leaf establishes one-loop subtraction-reference compensation
of the actual full three-channel tree amplitude, a closed solution
of its three retained beta equations, and quantitative enclosures
for that truncated flow.

It does not bound the omitted beta coefficients or prove the
existence of an exact quantum flow through arbitrarily high scales.
The formal singularity of a one-loop beta solution cannot by itself
be identified with a true UV obstruction.

The finite reference window reaches the previously selected Planck
hierarchy only as a range of subtraction coordinates. No amplitude
at Planckian momentum, graviton loop, curvature matching coefficient,
massless cut or high-energy Regge contour has been controlled.

The light mass stays physical and on shell; the computed light
mass UV tensor is a diagnostic, not a replacement running-mass
prescription. The heavy scalar remains unstable in the interacting
model. Its running mass parameter is not an exact stable pole.

The finite potential contact is fixed once at the initial reference.
A new finite potential condition at each reference would be a
different scheme and is explicitly excluded. The running tree
quartic margin is not presented as a physical scale-dependent
potential.

Higher-loop vacuum errors, full finite-gravity G, quantum
derivative-field-map equivalence and a controlled common B
parent remain unresolved. No reference-state or counterterm
cancellation from the old rolling construction is transferred.

Continuous reference integrals, flow inequalities and perturbative
order arguments are written proofs, checked by exact identities
and rational enclosures, not proof-assistant formalized or peer
reviewed. Original P8 remains OPEN.
