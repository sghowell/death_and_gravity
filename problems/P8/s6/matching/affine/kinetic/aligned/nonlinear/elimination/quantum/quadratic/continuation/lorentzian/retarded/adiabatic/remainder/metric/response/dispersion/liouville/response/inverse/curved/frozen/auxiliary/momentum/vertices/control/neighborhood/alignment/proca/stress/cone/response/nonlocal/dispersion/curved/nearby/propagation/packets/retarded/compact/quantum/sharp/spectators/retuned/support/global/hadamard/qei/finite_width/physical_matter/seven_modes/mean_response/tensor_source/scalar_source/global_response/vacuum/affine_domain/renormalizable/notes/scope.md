# What the quantum preparation does not establish

The exact tree amplitude has positive b2=4lambda. A full
interacting vacuum verdict V additionally requires renormalized
pole/residue data, absorptive weights, crossing/analyticity and
specified pole/cut subtractions, with quantitative loop and
truncation errors. These have not been supplied by the
constant-background effective potential.

The hierarchy is delicate: at our exact calibration
lambda4^2/(4lambda)>10^180. This ratio is not a computed
loop coefficient. It shows why the mere smallness of lambda4
cannot certify a correction relative to b2. Correlated
heavy-exchange diagrams and physical matching must be kept.
Their cancellations cannot be presumed from tree matching.

The S6.108 complex-channel radius 10^144 is a TREE bound
inside the heavy exchange pole. Light two-particle cuts
already start at s=4 (x=s-2=2), well inside that region.
Consequently the same polydisc cannot be declared a holomorphic
loop-amplitude domain. H also decays to two light particles.
No positive dispersive integral through a bare squared H pole
is used. A width estimate is not the required resummed error
bound or a full all-order spectral construction.

Full four-point equality does not prove that this polynomial
model and the exponential kinetic model have identical
higher-point amplitudes. Neither is identified with the
regular auxiliary affine action of S6.109. That action
still has no proven common propagating heavy completion.

Minimal Einstein coupling of canonical positive-kinetic
scalars obeys the classical NEC and cannot support a flat
classical bounce. This excludes that specified route only;
it is not a no-go for every nonminimal or quantum parent.
There is no finite-gravity IR prescription, Regge contour or
derived gravitational remainder bound here.

No old action, quantum state, fixed counterterm, retuning
profile or absolute-source cancellation is transferred.
The seven-mode relative response is unchanged and remains
distinct from absolute coupled renormalization and a
finite-amplitude co-evolved semiclassical solution.

Formal perturbative renormalizability and finite-regulator
Gaussian identities are not all-energy UV completion.
