## Two distinct controls still needed

First, an actual nonstationary stability or growth statement
requires estimates for the curved selected-state remainder,
not just its qualitative membership in an integrable
Volterra-kernel class. Such estimates must retain the
prepared endpoints, real state and finite contacts. Freezing
the background and observing a root does not supply them.

Second, the admitted S6 model is a finite EFT. To decide
whether this formal high-frequency feature lies within its
physical predictive domain requires an interacting cutoff
and quantitative omitted-operator/loop budget. The comparison
s/L in (10^-12,10^-11) only says the bracket is below the
Planck frequency. It is not a proof that the EFT is valid
there. No fixed interaction cutoff has yet been established
for this retained seven-degree-of-freedom parent at L=10^24.

These controls are mathematically different. A root outside
an independently justified EFT domain would not exclude
the low-energy candidate; a root inside it, with a valid
nonstationary error control, would require a genuine
stability response. Neither case is assumed here.
