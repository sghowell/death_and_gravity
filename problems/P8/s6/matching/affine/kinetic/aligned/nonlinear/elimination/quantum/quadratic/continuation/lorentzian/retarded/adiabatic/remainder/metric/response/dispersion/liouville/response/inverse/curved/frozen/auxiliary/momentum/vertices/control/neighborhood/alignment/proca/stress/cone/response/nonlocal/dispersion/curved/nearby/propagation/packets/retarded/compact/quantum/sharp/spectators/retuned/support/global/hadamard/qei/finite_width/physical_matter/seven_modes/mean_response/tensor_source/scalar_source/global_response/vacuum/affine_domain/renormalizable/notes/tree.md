# Literal polynomial realization and global classical vacuum

Write x_i=z_i-2 for z_i=s,t,w, with s+t+w=4. The earlier exact
amplitude is (g^2/4) sum_i x_i^2/(D-x_i), g^2=8lambda D.
The identity

    x^2/(D-x)=D^2/(D-x)-D-x

and sum_i x_i=-2 give precisely

    A_tree=-lambda4+G^2 sum_i 1/(mu^2-z_i).

No mass-dependent constant is discarded. Independent native
checks compare the full rational amplitudes, the single-channel
division and the actual forward derivative at s=2,t=0.
Thus the S6.108 exact geometric tree remainder and positive
b2=4lambda hold for this new model. This is an on-shell
four-point statement, not an equality of the full actions
or all higher-point amplitudes.

Completing the literal potential square gives

    V=mu^2[H+G Phi^2/(2mu^2)]^2/2+Phi^2/2+delta Phi^4/24,
    delta=4G^2(D-1)/[D^2(D+2)].

For D>1 every term is nonnegative; V=0 only at Phi=H=0.
The H stationarity equation sets H=-G Phi^2/(2mu^2);
the remaining equation is Phi(1+delta Phi^2/6)=0.
It too has only the real solution Phi=0. Both equations
are checked from the actual potential, not inferred solely
from a Hessian at the origin.

At the fixed calibration G^2<10^-7, lambda4<10^-204,
and mu^2>4. Canonical kinetic terms give the tree masses
and positive residues. The tree width formula

    Gamma(H->Phi Phi)/mu
       =G^2 sqrt(1-4/mu^2)/(32 pi mu^2)
       <G^2/(96mu^2)<10^-207

uses pi>3. A tiny width does not make H an exactly stable
asymptotic state or provide an all-order spectral density.
Loop-renormalized pole data are separate obligations.

Minimal Einstein coupling gives
-2 M_Pl^2 Hubble_dot=Phi_dot^2+H_dot^2>=0 on flat FLRW.
Consequently this particular minimal scalar parent cannot
produce a classical contraction-to-expansion bounce.
No conclusion is drawn about other constrained, nonminimal,
higher-derivative or quantum gravitational parents.
