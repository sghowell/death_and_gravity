# Claim boundary and next falsifiable tests

This checkpoint screens a separately named vacuum proposal.
It does not alter the old scalar model, smooth/analytic
DHOST extensions, physical matter frame, state, bounce,
counterterms or previous certificates.

The explicit positive one-loop marginal ray is useful
evidence for further model building, but has not fixed
relevant-parameter running, potential stability, threshold
matching or nonperturbative dynamics. The new leading
Phi^2 F^2 operator shows why an unbroken gauge extension
cannot simply inherit the old massive-scalar dispersion
argument.

The result is limited to the displayed matching order.
No finite-mass momentum remainder is silently supplied by
the constant-field logarithm bound. The leading cut is
not a new full-model forward coefficient. In particular,
its center mismatch and second-derivative mismatch vanish
for stated algebraic reasons; that does not remove the
overlapping physical cuts or prove a new positivity bound.

This does not exclude the full GY14-unbroken proposal,
all gauge-Yukawa parents, any DHOST row, or original P8.
Possible continuations are a quantitative finite-mass
threshold/IR-subtraction calculation, or a separately
named gauge-Higgs parent whose extra scalar/vector
spectrum and matching errors are checked. Simply inserting
a Proca mass would not provide a justified gauge UV theory.
A Higgs proposal must include its full potential and new
beta functions, not retain the unbroken candidate's ray.

The minimal acceptance target remains the adopted V/G/B
finite-EFT positivity and common-parent matching contract.
A complete all-orders UV construction is not a new
mandatory deliverable. The perturbative remainder needed
for any strict verdict, justified finite-gravity IR/Regge
allowance and controlled connection to the physical bounce
still require work. Original P8 is OPEN.
