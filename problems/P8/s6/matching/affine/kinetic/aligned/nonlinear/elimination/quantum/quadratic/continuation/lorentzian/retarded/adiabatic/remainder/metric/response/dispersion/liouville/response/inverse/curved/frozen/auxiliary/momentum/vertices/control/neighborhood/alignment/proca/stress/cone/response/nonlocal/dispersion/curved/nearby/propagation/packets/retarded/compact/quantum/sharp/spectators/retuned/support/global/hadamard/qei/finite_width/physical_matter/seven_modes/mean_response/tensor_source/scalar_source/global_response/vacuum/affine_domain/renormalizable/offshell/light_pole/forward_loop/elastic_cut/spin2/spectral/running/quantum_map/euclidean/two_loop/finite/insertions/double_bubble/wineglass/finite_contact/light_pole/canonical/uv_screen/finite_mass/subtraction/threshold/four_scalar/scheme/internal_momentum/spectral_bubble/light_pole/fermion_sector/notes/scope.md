# A complete primitive catalog with an incomplete quantitative frontier

The catalog has nine primitive rows through four
external Phi derivatives. One entire paired
quadratic covariance-insertion row is bounded by
S6.136. One quartic row has only its local-quartic
subset bounded by S6.135. The seven other primitive
rows are explicitly UNEVALUATED. The row order,
identities and status values are validated exactly.

Separate remaining tasks are the other proper
counterterm insertions, second-order local reference
coefficients, finite MS interaction conversion and
canonical-field conversion. Their coefficients
are not supplied by a topology catalog.

The purpose of the ledger is to make the remaining
calculation complete and avoid double counting.
It neither supplies a complete two-loop amplitude
or pole/error budget nor turns its inherited subset
estimates into one. Later-loop and truncation
errors, justified V contours, finite-gravity G,
common-parent B and original P8 remain OPEN.

Pure bosonic two-loop work stays separate and is
not silently transferred across the changed
interaction reference scheme. Finite gravitational
fermion diagrams are also outside this flat
matter-sector catalog.
