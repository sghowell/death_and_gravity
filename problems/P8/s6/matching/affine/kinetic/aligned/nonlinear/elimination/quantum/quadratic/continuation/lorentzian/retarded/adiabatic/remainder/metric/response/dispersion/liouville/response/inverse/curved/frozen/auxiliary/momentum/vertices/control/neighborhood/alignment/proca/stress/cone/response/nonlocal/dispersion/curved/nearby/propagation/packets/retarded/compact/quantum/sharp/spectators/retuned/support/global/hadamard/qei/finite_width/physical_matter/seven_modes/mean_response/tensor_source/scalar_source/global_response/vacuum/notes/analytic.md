# A distinct analytic finite-jet extension

No real-analytic coefficient A3 on a connected open domain
joining this exact retuned tube to X=0 can agree with it on
an open tube: the identity h X A3=1 there would extend to
X=0 by the real-analytic identity theorem and give 0=1.
This obstruction is to exact equality for this fixed witness,
not to smooth extensions, finite-jet matching, every CD row
or every candidate. A coefficient pole is not by itself a
universal Hessian/no-vacuum argument.

Instead choose every even integer n>=1024 and set

    gamma=1024/10^800, lambda=10^-600, kappa_n=n/gamma,
    S_n=(1-X^2)^n, R_n=1+(1-S_n)(X-1)/h,
    F0=-28/25,
    Fv_n=X/2-u^2/2+(lambda kappa_n-n F0)X^2
         +(-n/3-F0)u^4,
    F_n=Ftree+exp(-u^4) S_n(Fv_n-Ftree).

Use F2=-R_n/2 and the recomputed A3,A4,A5 of the smooth
construction; K=A1=A2=0. This is a separately named
classical reduced action. The explicit Fv_n coefficients
cancel a switch-induced derivative quartic shift and set a
positive canonical quartic potential; they are not quantum
subtractions or a reassignment of old counterterms.

The domain is all real u and -1/(4n)<X<6/5. Here R_n>0.
For negative X use 1-S_n<=nX^2 and 1-X<5/4 to obtain
R_n>=1-5/(64n). For 0<=X<=1/(2n) get R_n>=1-1/(4n).
For 1/(2n)<=X<=1 get R_n>=X; for 1<=X<6/5 get R_n>=1.
Hence R_n>=1/(2n). This floor is per order, not a positive
n-independent lower bound over the whole enlarged domain.

Since 1-S_n=nX^2+O(X^4), R_X=-2nX/h+O(X^2).
The apparent X poles are removable for all u:
A3(0)=-2n/h, A4(0)=2n/h, A5(0)=0. The extension is real
analytic on the stated connected domain and F2 never vanishes.
R(0)=1 and R_X(0)=0 for every u. Near the flat vacuum
u=X=0, the covariant quadratic action has a positive
unit-residue scalar of dimensionless mass one, ordinary
positive GR tensors, the free chi and the unchanged massive
Proca modes. Clock gauge is not used at X=0.

S_n has a zero of order n at X=1. R-Rold has order n+1,
and R_X-Rold_X, A3-A3old, A4-A4old, A5-A5old and F-Ftree
have order at least n. Consequently every covariant X jet
through n-1 agrees at X=1 for all u, including all mixed
u derivatives. This preserves the original classical clock
background and the finite jets needed for the previously
derived free and leading relative-source equations. It does
not establish equality of the full open-tube action, all
quantum vertices or fixed absolute renormalized profiles.
