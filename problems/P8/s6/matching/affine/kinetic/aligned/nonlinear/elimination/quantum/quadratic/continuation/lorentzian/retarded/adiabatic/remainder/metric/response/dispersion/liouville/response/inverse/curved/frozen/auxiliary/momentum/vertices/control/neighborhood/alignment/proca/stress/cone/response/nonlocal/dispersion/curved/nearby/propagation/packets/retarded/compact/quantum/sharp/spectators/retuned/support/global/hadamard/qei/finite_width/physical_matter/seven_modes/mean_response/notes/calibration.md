# Whole-interval bounds and an independent center jet

The fixed shell has 1<=q<=4. At u=0 and unit added
covariance I6, the physical stress traces are

    r0(q)=(3m^2+3+q+2q/m^2)/2,
    s0(q)=(m^2-1+q+2q/(3m^2))/2,

with m=1000. Therefore r0<=1500004 eta after the
normalized band integral. Positivity gives |s|<=r and
|r'|<=6|H|r. On |u|<=1/100, |H|<=1/25 and the
energy exponent is at most 3/1250. Using
exp(x)<=1/(1-x) for 0<=x<1 gives r<2000000 eta.
Thus |s| has the same bound and |F|<=5000000 eta.

Let t=1+u^2. The positive numerator coefficients of J
give J>=1215/[800(10001/10000)^18]>3/2 on the interval.
Also |alpha|<1/10, ell<=1/10, |beta|<=1/20.
These bound the absolute mean generator entries by

    [[1/6000, 1/2+1/900],
     [3/100+1/40000, 3/25+1/6000]].

Both row sums are below one. The source infinity norm
is below 2100000 eta. Variation of constants from zero
anchor data bounds the mean phase by
2100000 eta/99<22000 eta throughout the interval.
Constraint and matter reconstruction then give

    |n|<1700000 eta, |B|<900000 eta,
    |delta rho_m|<=27000 eta,
    |delta psi'|<100000 eta, |delta psi|<=1000 eta.

No time or momentum grid replaces these inequalities.

For an independent center stress jet, apply the exact
quadratic observable derivative

    D K=aH K_a+H' K_H+M_P^T K+K M_P

twice to the physical density and pressure Hessians,
then set a=1, H=0, H'=4. The band stress first
derivatives vanish; r''=-12(r+s) agrees independently
with conservation. The pressure second derivative
retains its large but finite m^4 term.

Differentiate the actual mean constraint, retaining
both its changing J and alpha and the derivative of
3/(2h). Finally retain both physical-lapse factors
when converting H' to a proper-time derivative.
The exact per-mode linear response is

    delta Hdot_proper(q)/eta
      =[13500000000 q^2
        +20250117847952899 q
        +20249909098086153000000]/41006250000.

All coefficients are positive. Its maximum on [1,4]
is below 5*10^11. At eta<=10^-20 the proper
acceleration correction is therefore below 5*10^-9
in tau^-2 units. This statement concerns the leading
mean coefficient; it is not a bounded higher-order
semiclassical remainder.
