# Low degrees: independent trace and complete tensor classification

Expand minus the active fermion log determinant.
Its fourth term is N y^4 Tr(S Phi)^4/4. Four functional
derivatives give twenty-four labelled placements; cyclic
trace symmetry divides them into six boxes. Both fermion
orientations are present once. The active flavor/color
factor is N=2Nc=6, not the two-gauge trace normalization.

At zero external momentum the numerator trace is independently

    tr(mF-i gamma.q)^4=4(mF^4-6mF^2 q^2+q^4)
                     =4[D^2-8mF^2 D+8mF^4],
    D=q^2+mF^2.

Keep the logarithmically divergent integral of D^-2
in the common dimensional regulator. At nu=mF it is
Ibar/Q. The convergent integrals of D^-3 and D^-4
are 1/(2QmF^2) and 1/(6QmF^4), as checked by their
radial integrals. The full vertex therefore is
24NY^2[Ibar-8/3]/Q. Its finite value exactly matches
S6.131's fourth determinant derivative. It is not an
independent adjustable four-point contact.

For a constant Phi background, the same independent
two-point trace from S6.131 applies to each mass
mF +/- y Phi. At arbitrary reference scale the small-s
bubble starts with -log(m_j^2/nu^2)/Q and has derivative
1/(6Qm_j^2). Differentiating the complete tadpole/bubble
kernel gives the kinetic increment

    delta Z(Phi)=-(2NcY/Q) sum_+/- [log(m_j^2/nu^2)+2/3].

The coefficient of Phi^2 is 2NY^2/(QmF^2). Hence the
two-derivative action term is c Phi^2(partial Phi)^2,
c=NY^2/(QmF^2). Its four-vertex is
-4c sum_(i<j) p_i.p_j =2c sum_i p_i^2 by conservation.
Equivalently, the vertex with momenta p,-p,0,0 is
the second background derivative of the two-point
kernel and gives 4c p^2.

Completeness matters: the ten quadratic Gram variables
are four diagonal squares and six pairwise products.
All twenty-four external permutations leave exactly
two orbit sums invariant. Their linear constraint
matrix has rank eight and the two displayed invariant
vectors span its nullspace. Momentum conservation
relates the sums, leaving one operator coefficient.
The background derivative fixes that coefficient,
not just a special kinematic slice of an undetermined
tensor. On shell sum_i p_i,E^2=-4, so this term is
constant in the Mandelstam invariants.

No odd-degree Lorentz scalar can be built in this
parity-even scalar trace: the momentum tensors contract
in pairs. In particular degrees one and three vanish.
The complete local divergence is handled in the
regulator before norm estimates of the remaining
convergent momentum degrees.
