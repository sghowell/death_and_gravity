# Four actual mean sources, including scale and matter velocity

Differentiate H2 with the natural field phase held fixed.
The actual finite state-difference sources are expectations of

    F_N=A^(-3) partial_N H2,
    F_xi=(3A^3)^(-1) partial_p H2,
    F_p=A^(-3)[-A partial_A H2/3+l partial_l H2],
    F_psi=A^(-3) partial_l H2.

The trace source retains BOTH the canonical volume and
matter-charge chains. Direct differentiation using the
independent homogeneous canonical momenta P_B=3A^3 p
and Q=A^3 l checks all three Hamilton equations.

On the original background the leading mean difference is

    n=(alpha dp-3ell beta xi+F_N)/(2J),
    xi'=-dp/2+alpha n/3+F_xi,
    dp'=-3H dp+ell beta n-3ell^2 xi+F_p,
    delta psi'=beta n-3ell xi+F_psi.

The classical two-component matrix is exactly S6.104's.
The four source kernels are not an ordinary minimally
coupled scalar or Proca stress. In particular F_xi and
F_psi cannot be dropped. Matter charge remains conserved;
a nonzero F_psi changes the mean FIELD VELOCITY, not Q.

Initial xi,dp,delta psi vanish. The lapse is fixed by the
constraint and is generally nonzero. Common vacuum and
counterterm sources cancel only in this LEADING DIFFERENCE
of the two mean equations. They are not declared zero.
The quantum state has not been evolved on a new mean metric.

All coefficients and compact-band sources are smooth at
every finite real u, and J never vanishes. The linear forced
mean system therefore has a unique solution on all finite
times. This continuation fact does not bound its infinite
tails or prove a globally positive physical lapse.

The reported physical scalar matter observable separately
retains its intrinsic fluctuation terms. It is not identified
with F_N or replaced by -3ell^2(xi+n/(2h)) alone.
A simple homogeneous-fluid conservation identity for that
coordinate-averaged observable is not asserted: inhomogeneous
connection/lapse/shift correlations must also be retained.
