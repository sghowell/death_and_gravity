# S6.45: local nonlinear auxiliary-constraint theorem

Original P8 remains OPEN. This checkpoint uses the unchanged literal
source-aligned S6.42 action, original physical matter metric and free
chi, with positive curl coefficient. S6.44 and its frozen ancestry are
rebuilt. No new physical operator or off-tube extension is introduced.

Domain: the existing timelike clock tube x in [-11/10,-9/10], and
the compact rolling interval u in [-1/2,1/2]. The theorem concerns a
nonempty local open canonical/spatial-jet neighborhood of that rolling
solution, not every configuration whose scalar X lies in the tube.
The existence of this neighborhood is proved; a numerical field/jet
radius is not supplied. The scalar-clock chart requires timelike dphi.

Keep the original scalar F, all boundary terms, all six spatial metric
velocities, the four retained vector components, lapse, shift and chi.
Use the exact invertible spatial chart and perform the Legendre map
before eliminating the normal temporal vector. The all64 affine
complement and projective gauge removal are inherited from S6.41.

The complete scalar ADM action has neither lapse velocities nor lapse
spatial gradients after two specified boundary transformations. The
trace/temporal Legendre map has effective gamma_t >9/10 on the original
closed tube. On the rolling solution the actual two-auxiliary Hamiltonian
Jacobian, divided by sqrt(h_hat), is diag(-2J,-1), with J>1/40 throughout
the declared compact interval. There is no division by Theta.

Its local continuation gives four second-class auxiliary constraints,
besides the six first-class spatial-diffeomorphism/shift constraints.
The full local count is seven physical modes including chi (two tensor,
one clock scalar, three vector, one chi), or six without chi. The count
does not infer a new lapse boundary datum from a constraint still written
in velocity variables. Spatial boundary terms use periodic data or the
usual compactly supported variations/falloff conditions.

This is not a nonlinear energy, stability or causal-cone theorem on
other backgrounds; not global well-posedness, an X=0 vacuum count,
a quantified higher-order/quantum remainder, an interacting cutoff,
V/G/B UV admissibility, or original P8 closure. Exact symbolic and
independent controls accompany the written proof, not proof-assistant
formalization or external peer review.
