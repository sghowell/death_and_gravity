# Scope after removing the known first light cut

Established for the same prospective GY14-unbroken
candidate: an explicit finite-cut subtraction up
to invariant six has precisely the known first
two-gauge boundary mismatch. Removing it glues
that channel locally across the old scalar
disc at this perturbative order.

Its coefficient is controlled using the entire
finite-mass one-loop momentum remainder on a
larger complex domain. At mF=10^200 it lies in
(-4K,-3K), with a finite-mass correction below
10^-190 K and |b2_low|<4*10^-1620.

This is not a full matched-amplitude coefficient
or positivity verdict. The regular contribution,
new-model canonical pole and all higher-loop
errors remain uncomputed. No old scalar amplitude
bound is silently transferred to new fermions
and gauge particles. The fixed subtraction is an
observable definition, not a retuning of the action.

The nonperturbative gauge spectrum and all later
massless cuts are not resolved. No all-orders
mass gap or global dispersion relation is
deduced from local first-cut removal.

V retains its actual canonical spectrum, amplitude,
error and complex high-energy applicability
obligations. G retains the prescribed regulator,
negative-transfer Regge contour, combined pole
limit and derived allowance Delta. B retains
the common-parent field, derivative, state,
heavy-spectrum, cutoff and remainder dictionary
on the vacuum and bounce domains.

None is supplied by a small finite subtraction.
Conversely, this repair prevents the earlier
overlap screen from being misreported as an
exclusion of every modified dispersion route.
No entire candidate or ladder row is excluded.

The adopted finite-EFT and necessary-positivity
contract is not replaced by a demand for a full
all-orders UV theory or global nonlinear
stability theorem. Scoped P8(a) and A.20-A.23
are unchanged. Original V/G/B and P8 remain OPEN.
