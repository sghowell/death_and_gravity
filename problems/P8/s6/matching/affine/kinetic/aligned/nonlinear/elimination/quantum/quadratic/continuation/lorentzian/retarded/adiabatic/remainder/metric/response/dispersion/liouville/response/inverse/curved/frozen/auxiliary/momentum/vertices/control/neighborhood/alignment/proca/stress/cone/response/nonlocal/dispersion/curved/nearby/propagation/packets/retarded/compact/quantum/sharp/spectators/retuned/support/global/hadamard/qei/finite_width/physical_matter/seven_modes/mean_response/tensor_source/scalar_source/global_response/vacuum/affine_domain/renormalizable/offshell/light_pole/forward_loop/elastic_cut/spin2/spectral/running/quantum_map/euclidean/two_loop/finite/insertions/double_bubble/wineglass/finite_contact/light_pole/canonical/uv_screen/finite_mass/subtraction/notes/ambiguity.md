# What removing the cut does not determine

Write the local first-channel decomposition as

    A(s)=F_low(s)+H(s),

where the known low-cut discontinuity has been placed
in F_low. The cut-removal lemma says H is holomorphic
locally, under the stated perturbative channel domain.
It does not compute H''(2)/2.

The exact diagnostic c(s-2)^2 is crossing even and has
no discontinuity. Adding it preserves all specified
low-cut data while changing the center second
coefficient by c. This diagnoses insufficiency of
the low-cut data alone. It is NOT a permitted free
counterterm in the fixed renormalizable GY14 action.
That action has a determined regular matching
contribution which must still be calculated or bounded.

An added polynomial also changes high-energy behavior.
A justified global growth bound and the complete
spectral data could constrain or fix this freedom.
Neither is supplied by the finite-cut subtraction.
One must not silently use those missing inputs while
claiming the cut alone has established positivity.

The strict negative sign of b2_low is not a negative
cross section or a UV inconsistency. Below the scalar
threshold the cut is a continued object, and the
center is inside its support before subtraction.
The coefficient is defined by its controlled local
boundary germ, not a convergent positive moment
integrated naively through the center pole.

The result repairs one local analytic obstruction at
the first nonzero gauge-cut order. It does not erase
higher-loop gauge cuts, prove confinement, certify
a full new-model light pole or transfer the old
scalar two-loop error budget to the new field content.
