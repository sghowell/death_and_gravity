# Exact included sector and what remains open

Included: the full scalar quadratic half-trace with its light
covariance differentiated once by the entire first fermion
insertion. This retains both local tadpole terms, the fixed
H one-point cancellation, and the one nonlocal mixed bubble
after the inner and outer on-shell reference subtractions.

The finite family slope and its uniform complex quadratic
remainder are bounded. Adding precisely this sector to the
complete one-loop quadratic functional, with its displayed
mass and field references, preserves the stated local pole
and finite-window Euclidean estimates.

Excluded: other enlarged-model two-loop quadratic primitives,
other lower-order finite interaction/field conversion terms,
the full two-loop inverse and error budget, later-loop and
truncation errors, global potential or exact stable-heavy/gauge
spectrum, reflection positivity, a derived cutoff, justified
high-energy V contours, finite-gravity Regge/IR Delta for G,
and a common bounce-parent dictionary for B.

No old pure-scalar two-loop error is silently imported into
the new reference scheme. Original P8 remains OPEN.
The missing estimates remain research work and are not a
new user-choice blocker or exclusion of a complete old row.
