# Primary-source context

[Panzer, section 2.2, lemma 2.8 and proposition 2.9](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1908.09820v3)
explains dominating spanning-tree monomials in ordered Hepp
sectors and the resulting convergence exponents. This supplies
context for the sector method. The actual anisotropic boxes,
heavy-mass cancellation, logarithmic polynomial and coefficient
sum are independently derived and computed here; no numerical
period correlation or conjecture from that paper is used.

[Bogner and Weinzierl, section 2](https://arxiv.org/html/1002.3458v3)
provides the standard parametric framework. The actual
two-loop normalization, first-sheet path and absolutely
integrable majorants are stated explicitly in these notes.

All graph definitions, masses, couplings and compact denominator
bounds are the frozen S6.120 data. No extra renormalization
prescription or high-energy assumption is imported.
