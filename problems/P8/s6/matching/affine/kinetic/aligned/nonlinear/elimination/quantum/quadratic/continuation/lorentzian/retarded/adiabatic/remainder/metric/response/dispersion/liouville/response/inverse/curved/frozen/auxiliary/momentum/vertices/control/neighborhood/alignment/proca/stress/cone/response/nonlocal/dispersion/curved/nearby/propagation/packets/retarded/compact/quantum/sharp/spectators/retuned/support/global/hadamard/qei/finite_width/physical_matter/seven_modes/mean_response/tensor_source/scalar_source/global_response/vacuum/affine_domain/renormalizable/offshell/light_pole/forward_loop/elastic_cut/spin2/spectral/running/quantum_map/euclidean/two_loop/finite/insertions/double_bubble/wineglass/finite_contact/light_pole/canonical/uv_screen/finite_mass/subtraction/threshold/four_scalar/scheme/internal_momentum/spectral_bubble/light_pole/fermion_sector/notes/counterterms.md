# Disjoint forests, regulator products and second-order conversion

The raw mixed master term is
(1/2)Tr[D_B F_raw''], F_raw=-Tr log D_F.
A complete order-two calculation also needs

    (1/2)Tr[D_B (S_B^(1))''],
    -Tr[D_F^-1 D_F^(1)],
    S_B^(2).

The first term inserts one-loop bosonic counterterms;
the second is the exact variation of the fermion
determinant by its one-loop kinetic, mass and
background-coupling counterterms. The last contains
the required second-order local references. This
accounts for proper subgraphs involving a boson
and fermion as well as the paired two-fermion
subgraphs treated in earlier checkpoints.

If F_raw'' plus an assigned bosonic counterterm is
grouped into a renormalized subgraph such as F0,R,
that same counterterm occurrence is removed from
the separate insertion ledger. Including both
would double count. Primitive family completeness
does not mean these coefficients have been found.

Dimensional factors must remain through the full
forest and finite-reference conversion. Algebraically,

    [a0+epsilon a1+epsilon^2 a2][p1/epsilon+f0]
       has finite part a0 f0+a1 p1.

A full two-loop double pole also retains a2 p2.
Neither contribution is reproduced by setting
epsilon=0 in the numerator first. The finite
four-dimensional projections in S6.135 and S6.136
were taken only after removing, respectively, the
overall local quartic constant and the whole
outer affine reference. Other sectors, especially
nonlocal heavy prefactors, cannot assume those
finite products vanish.

Second-order canonical conversion also retains

    A2-2 z1 A1+(3 z1^2-2 z2) A0

when the field factor is 1+h z1+h^2 z2. A fundamental
cubic G+h G1+h^2 G2 has squared increment
G1^2+2G G2 at order two. Neither term is optional.
These identities specify required bookkeeping,
not the numerical two-loop MS conversion.
