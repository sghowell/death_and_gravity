# Actual massive contact, heavy exchange and exact remainder

Use all incoming momenta, p_i^2=m^2, s+t+w=4m^2 and
d_12=(s-2m^2)/2, d_13=(t-2m^2)/2, d_14=(w-2m^2)/2.
Independent sums over all 24 labelled fields give

    A_(lambda Y^2)=2lambda sum_z(z-2m^2)^2,
    A_(L3-L4)=-(3/2)stw,
    A_(g0 Phi^4)=24g0.

The minus signs from six Fourier derivatives are kept in
each L3/L4 contraction. Crossing is checked independently.
For the actual co-scaled family m=1, g6=-2gamma and
g0=-gamma/3, so

    A_light=2lambda sum_z(z-2)^2+3gamma stw-8gamma
           =2lambda sum_z x_z^2+gamma sum_z x_z^3,
    x_z=z-2.

The constant -8gamma is required by the massive on-shell
identity; it is not dropped as if this were massless kinematics.
The forward crossing coefficient is exactly b2=4lambda>0.
This scalar contact is not the full finite-M amplitude with
its gravity exchanges.

An explicit two-scalar vacuum model is

    L_H=(dH)^2/2+exp(gH)(dPhi)^2/2
        -mu^2 H^2/2-Phi^2/2.

Its field metric is positive for all finite real H and its
potential is nonnegative. Around zero it has positive
canonical light/heavy residues and masses. The H Phi Phi
vertex is the derivative coupling (g/2)H(dPhi)^2.
At tree four-light-particle order the higher terms in the
exponential do not contribute. With

    D=mu^2-2=2lambda/gamma, g^2=8lambda D,

the exact exchange is

    A_H=(g^2/4) sum_z x_z^2/(D-x_z).

It is crossing symmetric; its s-channel residue is
g^2 D^2/4>0 in the mu^2-s denominator convention.
Its first two geometric-series terms equal the entire
A_light on shell. The match is of the four-point amplitude,
not an unproved off-shell field redefinition or full action.

The exact remaining terms are

    R_H=(g^2/(4D^3)) sum_z x_z^4/(1-x_z/D).

On the complex on-shell channel polydisc |x_z|<=r<D,

    |R_H|<=3g^2 r^4/[4D^3(1-r/D)].

For lambda=10^-600, gamma=1024/10^800 and r=10^144,
the radius/gap ratio is below 10^-53 and this exact bound
is below 10^-400. The heavy mass lies above the two-light
threshold and far above this channel scale; g^2 mu^2<1.
These are verified tree parameters, not a claimed all-order
cutoff or loop estimate. The exact exchange itself has
b2=4lambda; the omitted series has no forward quadratic term.

Because mu>2, the interacting heavy excitation can decay.
A positive narrow-width tree pole is leading perturbative
spectral evidence, not the fully dressed absorptive density,
its cuts or a completed dispersion proof. The exponential
two-derivative model is itself an EFT, not a UV completion.

Moreover, minimally coupling this positive-field-metric
model to ordinary Einstein gravity gives nonnegative
homogeneous rho+p: exp(gH)dot(Phi)^2+dot(H)^2, with additional
healthy free sectors nonnegative. Flat-FLRW Einstein evolution
then has dot(Hubble)=-(rho+p)/(2M^2)<=0. That specific minimal
vacuum parent cannot supply the strict flat bounce. This
explains the missing B matching; it does not rule out all
nonminimal or constrained common parents.
