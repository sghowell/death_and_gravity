# What the sharper bounds do and do not establish

This result changes only estimates and the newly named probes.
It uses the same actual nearby classical background, exact
quadratic scalar Hamiltonian, linear relational observable and
source convention. No parent report, old literal probe, Proca
state or background quantum profile is overwritten.

The small all-frequency transfer estimate and rational compact
width remove a very conservative exponential from the proof.
The finite spatial cutoff below 10^94 is still a mathematical
cutoff, not a Wilsonian validity scale or a quantum detector
implementation. The compact tests have nonzero arbitrarily
high spatial and temporal Fourier tails. Spatial projection
loses compact support, and temporal frequency is not truncated.

A causal common-parent comparison still needs its actual
low-band matching error and its OWN high-band response bound.
For the new pair, errors of at most D/8 each would contradict
vanishing full response on the original spacelike compact
supports, because the quadratic retained response is >=D/2.
Neither actual parent error is proved here. This algebraic
budget cannot be populated with an arbitrary assigned number.

Canonical interactions, omitted operators, loops, thresholds,
finite Wilson matching, the physical gravity limit and V/G/B
remain independent open work. There is no transfer from an
earlier hard-tree band or Planck/time normalization to these
probes without the actual canonical/error calculation.

The CCR statement is for the specified reduced quadratic
system. It does not construct a Hadamard state, renormalized
stress or a nearby self-consistent semiclassical solution.
It does not establish a local nonperturbative gravitational
observable or a universal quantum-gravity causal theorem.
Original P8, including its realistic-field P8(a) questions,
is not closed.
