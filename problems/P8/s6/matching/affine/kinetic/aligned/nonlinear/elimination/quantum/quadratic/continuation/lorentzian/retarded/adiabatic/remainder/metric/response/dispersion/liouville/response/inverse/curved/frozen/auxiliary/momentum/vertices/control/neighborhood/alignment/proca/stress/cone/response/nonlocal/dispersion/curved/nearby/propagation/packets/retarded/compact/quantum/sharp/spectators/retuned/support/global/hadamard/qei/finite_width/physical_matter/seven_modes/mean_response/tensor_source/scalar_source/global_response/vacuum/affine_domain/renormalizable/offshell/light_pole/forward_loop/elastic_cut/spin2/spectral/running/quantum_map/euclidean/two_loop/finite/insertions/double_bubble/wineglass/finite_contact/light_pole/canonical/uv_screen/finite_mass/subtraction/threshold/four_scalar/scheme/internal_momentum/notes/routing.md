# One specified outer-bubble routing at every real loop momentum

On the external forward disc |s-2|<=1 take E=sqrt(s)/2=a+i b. In the
symmetric scalar bubble route the two internal light lines through
(q0 plus/minus i E, q_spatial), with q real. Their continued invariants
s_line obey the pointwise identity

    Re(s_line)=-(q0 minus/plus b)^2-|q_spatial|^2+a^2.

Since a^2=(|s|+Re(s))/8<=3/4, both lines satisfy Re(s_line)<=3/4<1,
at all real q. The result includes |q| arbitrarily larger than the
fermion mass. Changing the square-root sign merely exchanges the two
lines. The crossed u=4-s lies in the same external disc; the zero-transfer
channel has E=0 and an even stronger real-part bound.

Completing a square in a real-part inequality is not a change of integration
contour. No complex translation of q is used. The generic routing interface
reports when its disc bound exceeds a chosen half-plane instead of returning
a false pass for a valid but uncovered domain.

This statement is deliberately restricted to this symmetric bubble
backbone. Lines shifted by individual external legs or by other loop
momenta require their own routing estimates. They are not all certified by
the number 3/4. The more general half-plane formula remains available once
the appropriate real-part bound and mass gap have been proved.

Replacing the entire outer integrand by a constant insertion majorant can
destroy integrability. This checkpoint does not make that replacement and
does not claim a complete two-loop forest or amplitude.
