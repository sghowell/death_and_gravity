# Scope and source comparison

This checkpoint realizes and controls one S6.83 superluminal central
datum along its actual S6.88 classical nearby bounce. It does not
claim every nearby central datum is superluminal: the original
clock has c_clock^2=749375/749377<1 at its center. Nor does it
prove a complete global nearby bounce.

No S6.82 quantum profile, selected quantum state, cancellation,
response or inverse is transferred to these different backgrounds.
The ordinary source-free classical action is unchanged. The
high-momentum principal chart is explicitly not asserted regular
at every finite q. Positive principal kinetic/gradient matrices
are not a proof of all-wavelength or nonlinear stability.

A useful comparison is Carrillo Gonzalez, de Rham, Pozsgay and
Tolley, [Causal Effective Field Theories](https://arxiv.org/html/2207.03491v2),
section 3 and appendix F. Their homogeneous scalar analysis uses
spatial displacement and controlled finite-frequency approximations;
its source and gravity assumptions are not established here.
Our classical ray bound does not import their EFT resolvability
conditions or a gravitational UV verdict.

The next research tasks include finite-frequency wavepacket and
observable errors on this actual domain, canonical interactions
and the admitted interacting band, omitted operators and loops,
heavy thresholds and finite Wilson matching, and the common-parent
vacuum/dispersion/gravity/background contract V/G/B. Missing
estimates are research tasks, not an external intervention blocker.

The original P8 question remains open. No universal UV no-go,
UV completion, quantum stability, or original closure follows.
The analytic arguments are explicit written proofs anchored by
exact native computations, not proof-assistant formalization.
