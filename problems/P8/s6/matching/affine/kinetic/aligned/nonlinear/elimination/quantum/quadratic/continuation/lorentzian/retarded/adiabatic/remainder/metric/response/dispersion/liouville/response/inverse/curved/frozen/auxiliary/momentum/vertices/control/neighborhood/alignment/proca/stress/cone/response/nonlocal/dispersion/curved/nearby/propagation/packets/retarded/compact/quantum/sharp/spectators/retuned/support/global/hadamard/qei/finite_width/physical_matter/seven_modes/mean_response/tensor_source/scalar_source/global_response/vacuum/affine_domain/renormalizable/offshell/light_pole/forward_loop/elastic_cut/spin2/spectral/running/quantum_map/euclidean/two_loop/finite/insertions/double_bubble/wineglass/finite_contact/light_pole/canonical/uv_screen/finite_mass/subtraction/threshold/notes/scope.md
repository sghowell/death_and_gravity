# Nonclosure controls

This checkpoint controls a complete one-loop fermion
increment, not the complete quantum candidate. Its
selected positive potential adds that determinant to
the scalar tree potential after explicitly chosen
vacuum-energy, mass and field references. Old scalar
loops, mixed higher-loop graphs and gauge corrections
are not silently included.

The finite-mass light-cut and its subtraction in
S6.129-S6.130 remain unchanged. Constant field factors
on their first three-loop term would change later
loop orders, not retroactively rewrite those reports.
The old scalar two-loop bound cannot be transferred
to the new candidate without new matching and estimates.

Small high-dimension gauge thresholds do not bound
relevant mass or vacuum-energy corrections: both are
large here. That is not an exclusion under the adopted
contract, which imposes no naturalness prior. Fixing
local references is not freedom to tune an uncomputed
regular four-point amplitude coefficient.

The fermion gap domain is |y Phi_reference/mF|<1.
No actual bounce clock map has yet placed the bounce
in it. Do not infer either bounce health or bounce
failure from the reference field radius alone.
The bounce state and renormalized sources are unchanged.

An on-shell pole and positive selected potential are
local necessary inputs, not proof of high-energy
boundedness, strict dispersion positivity, finite-
gravity IR/Regge control or a common parent construction.
No entire ladder row, general gauge parent or original
P8 problem is excluded or closed by this calculation.
Missing estimates remain research, not a user-choice blocker.
