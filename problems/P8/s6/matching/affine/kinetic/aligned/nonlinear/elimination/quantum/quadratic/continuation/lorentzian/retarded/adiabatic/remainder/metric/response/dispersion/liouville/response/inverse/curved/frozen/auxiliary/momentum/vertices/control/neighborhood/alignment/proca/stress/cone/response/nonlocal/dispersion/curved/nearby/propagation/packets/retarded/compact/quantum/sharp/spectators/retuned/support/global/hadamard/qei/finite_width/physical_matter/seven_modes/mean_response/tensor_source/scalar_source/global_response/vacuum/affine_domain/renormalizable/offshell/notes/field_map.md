# Correct sign and retained higher field powers

For L0=(dphi)^2/2-phi^2/2, a cubic variation R gives

    delta L0=-E R+div[R grad(phi)].

This variation is checked independently, including its sign.
Consequently the actual substitution Phi=Psi+R(Psi) cancels
the E R term in the quartic pointwise identity. R has field
degree three and maximum derivative order four. The map is
tangent to the identity and admits a local inverse order by
order as a FORMAL field-degree series. No globally invertible
differential operator, phase-space bijection or exact finite
truncation equivalence is claimed.

The exact difference after substitution is the divergence
of j(Psi)+R(Psi)grad(Psi), plus

    [ (dR)^2-R^2 ]/2
    + L4_trunc[Psi+R(Psi)]-L4_trunc[Psi].

The free remainder has field degree six. The quartic
substitution has field degrees six, eight, ten and twelve.
The source-norm proof bounds all of these terms, rather
than merely labeling them higher order and removing them.

The untruncated heavy inverse also has an exact geometric
remainder. For a real eigenvalue z of K,

    (D+z)^-1-sum_(j=0)^3 (-z)^j/D^(j+1)
       = z^4/[D^5(1+z/D)].

For |z|<=r<D, functional calculus gives its operator-norm
ceiling r^4/[D^5(1-r/D)]. Multiplication by G^2/8 and
Cauchy-Schwarz give the stated quartic action bound.
The source must be J=(Psi+R(Psi))^2. A bound on the original
Psi Fourier support alone does not permit leaving the
source support unchanged after a nonlinear map.

There is no heavy pole on the chosen spectral window.
Retarded, advanced or Feynman prescriptions agree there
as real multipliers. This is a restricted classical
stationary solution, not a restriction on loop momenta.
