# Exact selection and no double counting

Use the representative S6.120 double bubble with light edges

    (A,C), (A,C), (B,C), (B,C),

two external legs at A and B and none at C. The fixed
half-edge ordering defines the heavy-pairing labels.

Choices zero and one at C leave the two loop integrations
independent: zero is the local vertex and one is the
heavy exchange carrying the external pair momentum P.
At A or B, choices zero and one have no loop-momentum
heavy denominator, while choices two and three give
genuinely finite triangles.

Exhaustive comparison against every frozen UV-subgraph
inventory checks that a double bubble is subtraction
dependent exactly when C is zero or one and A,B are not
both in {2,3}. There are 24 such refinements.

Of the 32 factorizing refinements, the other eight are
already finite and part of S6.121. The 32 refinements
with C in {2,3} are also already in S6.121. None is
counted again here.

Among the selected graphs, 16 have one divergent
bubble core, four have two vertex-disjoint cores,
and four have two shared-vertex cores contained in
a larger logarithmically divergent core. Their
restricted forests have respectively two, four
and six members.

Every divergent core is logarithmic and its external
field type is Phi^4, H Phi^2 or H^2. External heavy
bridges are not absorbed into a core's local
counterterm. The 16 other subtraction-dependent raw
refinements are precisely the wineglass choices
B,C in {0,3}, with A arbitrary.
