# Independent physical-signature derivation

The physical orthonormal metric is eta=diag(-1,1,1,1).
For the mixed mass eigenvalue deviations Yt,Ys,

    Y_00=-Yt, Y_ij=Ys delta_ij.

The background frame satisfies nabla_e0 ei=0,
nabla_ei e0=H ei and nabla_ei ej=+H delta_ij e0.
Consequently the connection contribution to a spatial derivative
of each covariant time or matching spatial index has the same
minus sign. Ordered derivative indices are transformed too.

Every paired Einstein label contributes its inverse-metric sign
eta(index). The curvature components are

    R_0i0i=-(H'+H^2), R_ijij=+H^2,
    R_00=-D(H'+H^2), R_ii=H'+D H^2,
    R=2D H'+D(D+1)H^2.

Ricci contraction and all six covariant mass-tensor commutators
are checked directly. The exact transverse partition multiplicities
from S6.62 evaluate the two-derivative covariant basis in arbitrary D.

Independently, the full auxiliary coordinate curvature coefficient
is evaluated with g00=-N^2. Each term with lapse-metric power p
gets the factor (-1)^p. The relative absolute volume is unchanged.
The Lorentzian four-derivative loop density is +a4sc, expanded to
quadratic mass order. This derivation does not use a Euclidean
mode or state continuation.

Both complete results equal minus the locally continued Euclidean
residue for arbitrary symbolic D, before specializing the background.
The equality is not just a flat or integer-dimensional check.

## Local continuation and signs

Continue H^(j) by (-i)^(j+1) and mass-eigenvalue time jets by
(-i)^j, keeping spatial momentum fixed. This describes local
covariant counterterms. It is not an assertion that a global
Euclidean version of the quantum state exists.

The overall loop-density sign is also checked independently:
the continued frozen Feynman polynomial has P2=k^2-omega^2,
PA=beta*k^2-alpha*omega^2 and PA2=beta^2*k^2-alpha^2*omega^2.
The Lorentzian pole is +F/2 in the present 1/(32 pi^2 epsilon)
normalization, at orders zero, two and four.

The bare-action counterterm has the opposite sign to the loop
residue. This is distinguished from a same-sign pole functional
that is subtracted from a raw readout, as in the earlier finite
matching bookkeeping. No finite Wilson coefficient is inferred.
