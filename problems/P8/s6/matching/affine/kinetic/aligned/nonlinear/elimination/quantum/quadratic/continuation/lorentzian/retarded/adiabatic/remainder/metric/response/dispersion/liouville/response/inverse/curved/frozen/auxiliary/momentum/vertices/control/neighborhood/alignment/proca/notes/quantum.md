# Changed quantum insertions despite the same clock modes

The old and new vector Hamiltonian matrices agree on
the complete original clock history, and the same
initial selected covariance is retained. The fixed
background mode equation and its established on-clock
Hadamard property therefore persist. No off-clock
covariance prescription is silently added.

However the old mass functions have physical lapse
jets alpha=4/(9h), beta=28/(81h); the new jets all
vanish. Direct differentiation of the actual temporal-
eliminated mode Hamiltonian before evaluating N=1
gives the energy change, in canonical mode variables,

Delta rho_T matrix = diag(-m^2*beta,0)/a^3,
Delta rho_L matrix = diag(-(q+m^2)*beta,alpha*z)/a^3,
z=q/(q+m^2).

The new energy matrices are both diag(q+m^2,1)/a^3.
The pressure readout matrices are exactly unchanged.
The observable is half the matrix contraction with
the mode covariance, with two transverse and one
longitudinal polarizations. Nonzero matrices establish
a changed functional readout, not the sign or numerical
value of the full integrated finite stress.

The first and second lapse jets of the Hamiltonian
matrix also change. The energy/pressure contact
matrices are calculated with alpha=beta=alpha2=beta2=0.
Thus identical on-clock mode functions do not justify
copying the old Gaussian metric response or pole
diagnostic. Metric variations contain both state
variation and the explicit changed readout contacts.

The same stated covariant dimensional prescription at
mu=m can be evaluated at constant mass. The exact
finite local energy coefficients multiplying
m^(4-2n)/(64*pi^2*L^2), n=0,1,2, are

-5/2,
-10*H^2,
2*(6*H^2*H'+2*H*H''-(H')^2).

They are derived from the original dimensionally
continued counterterms by setting the mass derivative
insertions to zero, not by retaining the old nonminimal
energy coefficients. The matched local pressures agree
with the old ordinary pressures. Each displayed
energy-pressure pair satisfies rho'+3H(rho+p)=0.
This supplies the correct local pieces, not a new bound
on the full momentum integral or its functional response.

The previous fixed scalar tadpole profiles remain
fixed coefficients in this comparison. Their exact
cancellation of the new energy source is unverified.
If the new finite Proca stress is constructed, unchanged
pressure implies that the required replacement profile
differs by (rho_new-rho_old)*(x+1)/2 on the clock tube,
with the same fixed cutoff. It must be computed once
from the new action, history and unchanged state, then
held fixed under variation. Its quantitative derivative
bounds, changes to the light quadratic response and
any resulting semiclassical claim remain further work.
