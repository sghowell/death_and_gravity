# All three momentum constraints, including matter and vector density

Keep the S6.45 canonical variables after its algebraic
temporal-vector elimination. The lapse and temporal primary
momenta vanish on the reduced constraint surface.
Their possible spatial-generator extensions therefore
do not change the remaining physical constraint.

In local spatial units a(t0)=1, let pi^{ij} be the
contravariant metric momentum density. Its weight-one
divergence is

Div(pi)^i=partial_j pi^{ij}+Gamma^i_jk*pi^{jk}.

The connection trace terms cancel; this is not the
divergence of an ordinary weight-zero tensor.
The actual lower-index momentum constraint is

C_i=-2g_ij Div(pi)^j+P_chi*partial_i chi
    +Pi^j F_ij-W_i*partial_j Pi^j.

P_chi and Pi are independent canonical momentum densities,
not their uneliminated velocity expressions. In particular
the last vector term is required by the one-form Lie
derivative generator proved in S6.45.
Raising the constraint and dividing by -2 gives the
residual used here, with the full nonlinear inverse g.

The original mass/source alignment does not remove this
vector generator. It eliminates the first-order light
source in the vector action, not its stress or spatial
momentum at higher order.

## Spatial gauge and canonical longitudinal decomposition

Use the same linear small-field metric parametrization

g_ij=(1+2v)delta_ij+gamma_ij,
partial_j gamma_ij=0, gamma_ii=0.

The total contravariant density at a local evaluation point
is decomposed as

pi=[-H*(1+v)+p/6]I+Pi_TT+H*gamma+L Z,
(LZ)_ij=partial_i Z_j+partial_j Z_i
       -(2/3)delta_ij partial_k Z_k.

Flat tracefreeness and integration by parts make LZ
orthogonal to both the scalar metric velocity and the
TT metric velocity. This remains true when Z depends
on matter and vector fields and momenta. Consequently
no extra Darboux term is generated by solving this
constraint: p,Pi_TT,P_chi,Pi retain their declared
canonical roles.

Let P_chi=P0+pi_chi. The background P0=a^3 ell=1/10
is constant in global clock coordinates. In constant
locally rescaled spatial coordinates, its numerical
value at the evaluation point is ell(t0).
The scalar fluctuation chi has no background spatial
gradient. The linear raised constraint source is

partial_i(p/6-P0*chi/2).

Thus

Z1=-(1/8)grad Delta^-1*(p-3P0*chi).

There is no linear vector contribution because the
background spatial vector and its momentum vanish.
The exact matter shift is tested; setting P0 to zero
would be a different model.

## Recursion and its nonzero-wavevector norm

On the new degree-n correction the operator is

E=Delta I+(1/3)grad div,
E(k)=-|k|^2 I-k k^T/3,
E^-1(k)=-(I-k k^T/(4|k|^2))/|k|^2.

Its determinant is -(4/3)|k|^6. At each order insert all
previous corrections, take the new residual source and
apply -E^-1. Every additional metric coefficient
multiplying the new correction has positive field degree,
so it first affects higher order. The matter/vector
current does not depend on the unknown gravitational
longitudinal momentum. Induction therefore kills all
three constraints at every constructed degree.

For real k!=0, the Euclidean inverse norm is at most
1/|k|^2. If L(k) is the tensor York lift, its exact
squared composed norm matrix is

(L E^-1)^dagger*(L E^-1)
 = 2I/|k|^2-k k^T/(2|k|^4).

The deficit from 2I/|k|^2 is positive rank one. Thus
the full tensor lift inverse has norm at most
sqrt(2)/|k|, and in particular at most 2/|k|.
These are nonzero-wavevector bounds, not full nonlinear
spatial-solution or zero-transfer estimates.

For a four-leg Hamiltonian only Z1,Z2,Z3 are needed.
A possible Z4 contracts with the isotropic background
first momentum variation and vanishes by flat trace.
Every remaining occurrence has field degree at least
five. The matter and vector background momenta do not
alter that gravitational trace argument. The explicit
moving gravitational boundary has the same property.

## Formal Fourier domain and exact fixtures

The existing labelled Fourier-jet algebra is used only
as general finite algebra; no D-only Hamiltonian or
D-only cutoff bound is imported. Nilpotent labels
extract one copy of each external leg. Repeated species
carry different labels, so factorial multiplicities
are retained.

Two-, three- and four-leg mixed fixtures use exact
symbolic u and a positive wave scale, with rational
nonexceptional directions. They include scalar
coordinates/momenta, nonzero rational TT tensors and
their dual momenta, matter and all vector components.
TT frames are made from nonvanishing rational tangents;
a diagonal seed that can project to zero is not divided
by its zero norm.

For each degree, every coefficient of all three
constraint residuals is checked. Omitting the vector
momentum-divergence term or the rolling matter source
gives explicit nonzero defects. The written induction,
not a finite set of directions alone, establishes the
generic finite recursion.

Only proper-subset wavevectors are inverted. An actual
nonzero homogeneous source is rejected by the solver.
This is not proof that homogeneous backreaction vanishes
or that a closed gravitating torus can drop its global
constraints. It is the declared finite hard-transfer
vertex domain on which the expansion is constructed.
