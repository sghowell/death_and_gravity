# Source inputs and limits

[Bajc, Lugo and Sannino, arXiv:2012.08428v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2012.08428v2),
Appendix A (A.7), (A.20)-(A.22), and Appendix B (B.1),
(B.2), (B.5), (B.10), supply the one-loop renormalization
inputs and an independent normalization check. Our L is
six times their singlet quartic. We evaluate the explicit
opposite-sign Dirac matrix and scalar Hessian ourselves.
We do not import their thermal conclusions or call a
one-loop ray a full P8 UV completion.

[Gillioz et al., arXiv:1206.7120v2](https://arxiv.org/html/1206.7120v2),
Section 3.1, equations (8)-(10), supplies the leading
heavy-fermion gauge-threshold convention. Its zero-momentum
low-energy theorem is not a uniform finite-mass form-factor
bound. The pair determinant, field remainder, polarization
contractions and crossed-cut calculation are derived here.

The adopted S6 contract
distinguishes necessary positivity with controlled matching
from construction of a full UV theory. For an unambiguous
repository path see problems/P8/s6/FORMULATION.md. No
flat-space positivity inequality is applied pointwise to
the bounce, and no numerical Regge correction is presumed.
