# Euler currents and physical stress are different readouts

Use physical lapse N=1+n and physical log-scale perturbation v. Factor out the unperturbed a³ density. For each source jet row the quadratic action has coordinate Euler derivative

    E = sum_j (-D_u-3H)^j partial L2 / partial source_j.

Consequently a weighted adjoint uses D_u+3H, not the flat-time adjoint. Both physical coordinate Euler channels are explicitly checked against their weighted formal adjoints through derivative order four, including all off-diagonal terms. The analogous clock-chart checks include the fixed local-profile contribution.

To obtain the actual physical stress variation one must also vary the volume/lapse factors converting Euler currents into stress. At the background,

    delta rho_local = -E_N - 3 v rho_local,
    delta p_local = E_v/3 - (n+3v) p_local.

Here rho_local,p_local are the newly matched S6.81 local coefficients, not the old mass-profile energy. Each constant spatial-rescaling coefficient vanishes exactly after this normalization; without these contacts it need not vanish. Self-adjointness belongs to the coordinate Euler Hessian, not to these differently normalized physical stress components.

For n=0 the finite constant density has rho=-5/2 and p=5/2 in the common m^4/(64 pi²) normalization. Its physical stress response vanishes. These facts are not claims about the nonlocal, state-dependent response.
