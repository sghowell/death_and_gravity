# Primary-source and convention cross-check

[Ruf and Steinwachs, arXiv:1806.00485v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.00485),
sections V-VI and equations (38)-(42), provide the relevant Euclidean
massive-vector and general-mass-tensor framework and the three-mode
Proca interpretation. Its appendix prints epsilon=d/2-2, opposite to
epsilon_DR here. We do not assert a sign-normalized match between
its printed pole formula and this calculation. Our pole sign is fixed
independently by the explicit Gamma integral and a cutoff logarithm
control, with the Euclidean action convention stated in full.

The anisotropic determinant, finite subtraction, actual clock jets and
continuous bound are calculated directly. No general curved-mass-tensor,
in-in or UV result is imported as an error bound for the rolling parent.
