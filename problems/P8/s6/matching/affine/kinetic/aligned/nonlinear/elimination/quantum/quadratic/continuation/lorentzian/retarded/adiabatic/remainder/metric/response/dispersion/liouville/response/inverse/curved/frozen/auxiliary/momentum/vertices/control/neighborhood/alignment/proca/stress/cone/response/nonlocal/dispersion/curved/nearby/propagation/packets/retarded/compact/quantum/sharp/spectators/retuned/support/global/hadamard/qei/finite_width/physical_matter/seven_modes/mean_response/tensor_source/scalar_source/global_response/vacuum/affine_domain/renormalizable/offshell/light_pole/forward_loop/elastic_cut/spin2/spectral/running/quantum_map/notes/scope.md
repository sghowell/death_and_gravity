# What transfers and what does not

The defined action is the complete polynomial parent after the
actual local light-field substitution. Its reduced quartic
derivative jet matches the earlier target by S6.111, but its
full heavy kernel, generated higher-field terms and transformed
counterterms remain present. S6.111's small Schwartz-class
stationary-action error is not a loop-momentum error estimate.

Through one loop and with proper stable-light LSZ, the same
physical scattering amplitude transfers S6.113's complete b2
bound and S6.114's computed cut to this full matched action.
The finite-order partial improved coefficient remains positive
with the same stated margin. This conclusion is not obtained
by assigning the same result to a bare truncated target.

The composite-field normalization is newly and explicitly fixed
at MSbar scale one. The physical parent mass, residue and finite
potential contact remain those already specified. Counterterms
are not retuned to force the desired amplitude. No off-shell
equality of ordinary effective actions is claimed.

This flat-vacuum formal result does not control a global
differential inverse, a nonperturbative quantum Jacobian, higher-loop
remainders or an all-energy UV completion. It does not covariantize
the derivative map at finite Planck mass or transport stress
improvements, curvature counterterms, rolling states or canonical
cosmological variables. S6.115's graviton form factor is not
transferred by this flat-space argument.

The finite-gravity IR/Regge contour, absolute coupled
renormalization, corrected cosmological cones and a controlled
common propagating bounce parent remain open. Original P8(a)'s
scoped result is unchanged. Original P8(b), full V/G/B and
original P8 are not finished or closed.
