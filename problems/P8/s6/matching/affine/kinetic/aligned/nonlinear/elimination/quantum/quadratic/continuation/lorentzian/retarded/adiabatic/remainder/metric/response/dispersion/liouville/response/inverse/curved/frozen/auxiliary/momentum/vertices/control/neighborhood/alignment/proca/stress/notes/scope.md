# Scope and remaining work

Established for this declared new candidate:

- the actual constant-mass energy readouts and differentiated
  subtraction, not transferred nonminimal coefficients;
- full-continuum, continuous C5 stress estimates at fixed m=1000
  and L=10^400 in the original fixed state;
- explicit fixed scalar-profile replacement, with 21 global
  mixed-derivative bounds through total order five for both
  the new profile and its change;
- exact retained-Gaussian one-point stress and clock-source
  cancellation, compatible with ordinary Proca conservation.

S6.81's old-profile comparison remains unchanged. Its literal
classical affine update, positive constant vector mass, constrained
physical vector cone and separately proved hard-tree bounds are
parent results. The new scalar coefficient correction is bounded
here in local profile/contact norms only: no transfer of a full
interacting or higher-vertex bound for this additional correction
is asserted. Its effect on the quantum-corrected light quadratic
operator is not replaced by its small coordinate coefficient.

In particular, time derivatives of stress on one background are
not functional derivatives with respect to metric variations.
The new lapse operator jets in S6.81 change the Gaussian response.
The old frozen pole diagnostic, the old prepared inverse and the
old tadpole-response kernels are not copied to this new candidate.
They must be rederived with the new mass jets and new fixed
profiles. Mixed loops and omitted operators are not controlled here.

No full scalar-neighborhood characteristic theorem, nonlinear
spatial well-posedness, all-frequency quantum stability, actual
global quantum bounce, interacting Wilsonian cutoff, finite Wilson
matching, common-parent V/G/B proof, UV completion/exclusion, or
closure of original P8 is claimed. The huge named hierarchy is a
conservative mathematical parameter choice, not a measured or
optimal scale and not a substitute for those missing arguments.
