# Common holomorphic subthreshold domain

Use all-incoming forward external legs p1,p2,-p1,-p2 with physical mass
one and signature +---. On |s-2|<=1 put u=4-s, t=0.
One holomorphic choice has Minkowski components
p1=(sqrt(s)/2, i sqrt(1-s/4),0,0), with p2 opposite spatially.
The square-root branches are analytic on a neighborhood of the closed disc.
After Wick continuation, the Euclidean bilinear square is p_i^2=-1,
while the **Hermitian** norm satisfies
||p_i||^2=(|s|+|u|)/4<=3/2. These are different products.

With a=x(1-x), ell=1-as and 0<=x<=1, Re ell>=1/4 and |ell|<=7/4.
Combining the two light denominators and shifting the loop variable gives
(y+ell)^2. The shifted heavy external vectors are
r1=(1-x)p1-xp2 and r2=(1-x)p2-xp1. Their bilinear squares equal -ell,
and convexity gives Hermitian norms at most sqrt(3/2). Thus each remaining
heavy denominator is A+2q.r_i, A=y+M-ell, y=q^2>=0.

The continuation is justified for the complete diagrams, not by blindly
inserting complex external momenta into the original unshifted real
Euclidean contour. For a box, let alpha0,alpha1 be the light parameters
at routes 0,P and alpha2,alpha3 the heavy parameters at routes p1,p2.
Let L=alpha0+alpha1, H=alpha2+alpha3, L+H=1. The full parameter polynomial is

Delta=L^2+MH-s alpha0 alpha1-u alpha2 alpha3.

Since Re s, Re u<=3 and pair products are bounded by L^2/4,H^2/4,

Re Delta >= L^2/4+MH-3H^2/4
 = 1/4+(M-1)H+H(1-H)/2 >=1/4+(M-1)H>0.

Triangles and bubbles are parameter faces of the same inequality.
For t-channel diagrams the light-pair transfer vanishes; heavy-route
differences carry s or u, so the same bound is conservative. Repeated
heavy routes give still simpler denominators. External factors h(s),
h(u),h(0) are analytic because M>24.

These positive parameter bounds define the continuation of the regulated
Euclidean diagrams and justify the light-pair shift and the real shifted
radial representation used below. All finite subtracted pieces have
integrable uniform majorants. They are holomorphic on a neighborhood
of the closed disc, so differentiation and the Cauchy bound are valid.

No statement about an unresummed unstable heavy resonance, high-energy
contour or an all-orders cut-free region is made. The light cut starts at
s=4 outside this disc and is not removed from the physical amplitude.
