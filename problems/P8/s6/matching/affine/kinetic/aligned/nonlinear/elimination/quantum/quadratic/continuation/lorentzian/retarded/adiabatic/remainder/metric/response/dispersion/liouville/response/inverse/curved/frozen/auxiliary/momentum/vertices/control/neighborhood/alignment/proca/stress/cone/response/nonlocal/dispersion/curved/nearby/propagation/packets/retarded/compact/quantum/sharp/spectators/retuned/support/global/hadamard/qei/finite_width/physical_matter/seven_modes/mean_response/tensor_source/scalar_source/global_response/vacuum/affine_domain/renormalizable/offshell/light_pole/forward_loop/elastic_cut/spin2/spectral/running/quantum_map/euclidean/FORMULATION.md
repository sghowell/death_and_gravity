derive a uniform bound over every real Euclidean momentum for the
computed one-loop light self-energy relative to the free inverse.
Identify its sharp supremum with the already fixed light-residue
counterterm magnitude and independently with the unsubtracted
zero-transfer stress-triangle sum.

Use that bound to control only repetitions of this known kernel,
including exact one- and two-line insertion tails. State the source
and integrability domains explicitly. These tails are not a bound
on all higher-loop graphs or on the renormalized forward coefficient.

For the actual S6.118 composite prescription, compute a bounded
heavy-momentum window and an explicit larger-momentum witness
against uniform smallness of this derivative coordinate. This is
not a physical ghost, loss of on-shell equivalence or a P8 no-go.

iterations. It does not establish reflection positivity, a timelike
high-energy contour, finite-gravity control, a common rolling parent
or original V/G/B closure.
