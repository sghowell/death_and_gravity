# Complex forward routing and invariant descent

The four outgoing Euclidean momenta are

    (-iE,0,0,-p), (-iE,0,0,p),
    ( iE,0,0, p), ( iE,0,0,-p).

Their sum is zero. E^2=s/4 and p^2=s/4-1 impose
unit external mass, forward t=0 and u=4-s.
All six cyclic box routes are enumerated and closed
explicitly. No scalar momentum is complex conjugated
after analytic continuation.

On |s-2|<=5, |s| and |s-4| are at most seven.
Thus |E|,|p|<=sqrt(7)/2<3/2. Each external component
one-norm is below three; each partial sum of at most
three externals is below nine, hence below eighteen.
For mF>=36 every Neumann ratio is strictly below
18/mF<=1/2. The norm integral controls a holomorphic
series initially derived from Euclidean external
momenta in a common domain. No arbitrary shift of an
unsubtracted divergent complex loop contour is used.

Each of the twenty-four permutations of external labels
maps the six cyclic orders into the same set, after
cyclically restoring the first label. Independent
changes E->-E and p->-p are explicitly such permutations.
The full analytic series is therefore even separately
in E and p and descends to a holomorphic function of
E^2,p^2, hence s. This proves continuation through
E=0 and p=0, not merely a choice of square-root branches.

The only divergent degree-zero term has already been
subtracted as the entire local regulated reference.
Degree two is finite and constant on the mass shell;
the remainder is holomorphic on a neighborhood of
the closed unit disc needed for the coefficient bound.
This is a property of this one-loop increment, not
all-loop gauge-theory analyticity or a high-energy theorem.
