# Fixed finite-potential contact insertion

The quartic potential condition fixes a definite
negative finite contact. Its insertion into the
one-loop amplitude is a two-loop contribution.

- [Same full-Hessian potential condition](notes/potential.md)
- [Two exact radial integrals](notes/radial.md)
- [Insertion and inherited local subtraction](notes/insertion.md)
- [Actual integrated bounds](notes/bounds.md)
- [Scope](notes/scope.md)

The insertion's absolute bound is below 2 times
10^-12 of the positive tree forward coefficient.
It is added to the four disjoint raw-graph groups;
the known one-plus-two-loop contributions remain
below 10^-6 of tree.

Complete pole/residue/LSZ and source-aware matching,
all-higher-loop and original V/G/B requirements
remain open. Original P8 is not closed.
