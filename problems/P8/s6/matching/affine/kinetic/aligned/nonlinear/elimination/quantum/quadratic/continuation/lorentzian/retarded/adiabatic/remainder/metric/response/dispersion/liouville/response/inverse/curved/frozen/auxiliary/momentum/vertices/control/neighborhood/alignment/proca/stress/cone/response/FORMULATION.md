# P8 S6.84: finite local ordinary Proca response

This is a new response calculation for the S6.81 source-free, constant-physical-mass Proca action with the explicitly fixed S6.82 scalar profile. It is not a replay of the old lapse-dependent-mass response. The physical metric, actual clock H=4u/(1+u²), Gaussian state and covariant subtraction scheme remain those of the preceding checkpoints. The interval is I=[-1/2,1/2], m tau=1000, and the named hierarchy is M tau=10^400.

The task is the finite local part of the homogeneous metric Hessian, including dimensional pole cancellation and evanescent finite terms, physical readout normalization, nonlinear clock-chart pullback and the local part of the already chosen fixed profile. The state-dependent nonlocal part is outside this certificate.

Prove the coefficient identities in both source charts, the weighted formal self-adjointness of the coordinate Euler Hessians, and continuous C4-to-C0 component bounds. Retain the rank-deficient fourth-derivative coefficient without interpreting a finite derivative truncation as extra physical particles.

The new results do not establish a full causal inverse, quantum stability, the full nearby quantum scalar cone, interacting cutoff, omitted-operator bounds, common-parent V/G/B matching, UV completion/exclusion or original P8 closure.
