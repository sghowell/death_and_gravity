# Actual ordinary Proca sources and conservation

At fixed comoving momentum let z=q/(q+m²), omega²=q+m², H=4u/(1+u²) and lambda=-Hz. The new minimal physical canonical factors are g_T²=a/N and g_L²=a³m²q/[N(q+m²)]; both coordinate frequencies are N²(q+m²), with q varying as a^-2. Derivatives of these literal expressions independently verify every first source and readout contact.

The frequency and moving-normalization variations are

    r=delta log omega=n-z v,
    rg_T=(v-n)/2,
    rg_L=((1+2z)v-n)/2.
    delta d=D_u rg, delta lambda=D_u r,
    delta U=D_u(delta d)+2d delta d.

Here D_u also differentiates z along fixed comoving momentum. All four mass-jet families vanish before the generic dimensional expressions are specialized. No parent function or model is replaced.

Write a mode stress readout as [A |p|²+B omega² |f|²]/(2a³), p=f'-d f. Energy has A=B=1 in both sectors. Pressure has (A,B)=(1/3,(2z-1)/3) for T and ((1+2z)/3,-1/3) for L. Their source variations include the physical output normalizations, not only the Hamiltonian vertices.

An independent covariance derivation checks conservation. With X=(|f|²,Re(f p*),|p|²),

    X'=M X,
    M=[[2d,2,0],[-omega²,0,1],[0,-2omega²,-2d]],
    delta X'=M delta X+delta M X.

Both coefficients of the unvaried and varied covariance vectors vanish in the linearized physical Ward identity

    (delta rho)' + 3H(delta rho+delta p) + 3v'(rho+p)=0.

The unvaried identity and the actual new adiabatic energy baselines are checked separately. The same varied Ward identity is also checked independently at each of the three subtraction orders, in both polarizations. This identity concerns the minimally coupled vector stress. The fixed scalar profile has its own clock Euler variation and is not silently declared separately source-free.
