# The constrained vector characteristic cone

The new physical vector action is exactly
-zeta*F(W)_(mu nu)*F(W)^(mu nu)/4-W_mu*W^mu/2.
With A=sqrt(zeta)*W, its fixed mass is m^2=1/zeta=10^6.
The mass is positive and constant, not a background
normal/spatial mass ratio asserted to be Lorentz invariant.

In a physical orthonormal cotangent frame with eta=(-+++),
put s=xi^T*eta*xi. The exact massive plane-wave operator is

P=(s+m^2)*I-xi*(xi^T*eta).

Its divergence is (xi^T*eta)*P=m^2*(xi^T*eta), so the
field equation imposes the divergence constraint. Exact
matrix multiplication gives

P^-1=(I+xi*(xi^T*eta)/m^2)/(s+m^2),
det P=m^2*(s+m^2)^3.

There are three massive physical polarizations. Dropping
the constraint and declaring four positive oscillators
would be incorrect. In particular the unconstrained
massless principal part has zero determinant for all xi.

On an admitted smooth globally hyperbolic physical metric,
the covariant equation (delta_g*d+m^2)A=0 implies
delta_g*A=0 and is equivalent to
(delta_g*d+d*delta_g+m^2)A=0 with that constraint. The
associated wave system has physical-metric null
characteristics; its causal Green operators supply the
vector finite-propagation statement. This does not give
the full coupled scalar cone or nonlinear stability.

The external theorem used for this last step is the
constrained Klein-Gordon reduction and causal Green
construction in Moretti, Murro and Volpe, section 3.2,
equations (3.2)--(3.7),
[arXiv:2210.09278v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2210.09278v3).
Its assumptions include a smooth globally hyperbolic
metric and constant positive mass. The associated wave
operator is normally hyperbolic; the Proca operator
itself is not. The source's adjacent terminology does
not justify dropping that distinction. No arbitrary
off-clock state is identified with the original selected
state merely by invoking a general existence theorem.

The actual S6.79 nearby light family also permits a direct
canonical check. Write t=sqrt(N), q=|k_hat|^2. Starting
with the source-free six-channel matrix, the mass update
gives

A_(sigma sigma)=1/t+10^6*t/q,
C_(sigma sigma)=t^3*q,
B_(sigma,b)=0.

Both vector coefficients are positive. Their central
Hamiltonian product is t^4*(q/t^2+10^6), with physical
lapse t^2 and physical squared momentum q/t^2. The
same actual physical metric appears in the covariant
argument. Finite background time jets remain in the
Euler equations; the product alone is not promoted to
a full time-dependent or frozen semiclassical spectrum.
