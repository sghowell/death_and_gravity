substituting u -> i*u in that already chosen function instead
gives (1-u^2)^2. Their difference is -4u^2; at u=1/2 they are
25/16 and 9/16. That substitution changes the history and is
not the operation performed here.

jets before the original Lorentzian background is inserted.

give the same result. No global complex-time history, Euclidean
vacuum prescription or analytic continuation of the S6.55

The new Y^2 derivative counterterms still vanish to first order
on the selected clock. Thus the S6.60 fixed profiles and their
selected background cancellation remain intact. The zero-order
potential and previously frozen linear matching are not changed.

A physical finite perturbation calculation still needs the
dimensionally regulated bare/contact/retarded kernel, compatible
varied-state Cauchy data, finite matching information and
quantitative integrated bounds. Higher-derivative local poles
are not to be resummed into exact new modes of the finite EFT.
