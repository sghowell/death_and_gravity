# Explicit selected functional and its order bookkeeping

Let h mark formal loop order, r and fp be the two complete
one-loop slopes, and r_spec the new family slope. The selected
raw quadratic functional includes the full one-loop kernels
and this covariance-insertion sector only. Its local references are

    m_ref^2=1+h[Pi_scalar(1)-f_fermion(1)]+h^2 Pi_spec(1),
    kappa=1+h(r-fp)+h^2 r_spec.

The whole regulated mass anchor Pi_spec(1) is cancelled by its
fixed on-shell reference. No separate finite MS value or
numerical size is claimed for that unsubtracted anchor.
Free internal light lines retain mass one.

After constant canonical normalization the selected inverse is

    Gamma(s)=1-s+
       {h[f_fermion,R(s)-Pi_scalar,R(s)]-h^2 Pi_spec,R(s)}/kappa.

This identity is checked before setting h=1. Expanding it to
order two also retains -(r-fp)(f_fermion,R-Pi_scalar,R);
the exact normalization is not mislabeled as an all-loop result.

At h=1 the kinetic lower endpoint is still 1-fp_upper, because
both scalar slopes are nonnegative. Add B_spec divided by that
endpoint to the full one-loop remainder bound. It remains below
2 times 10^-405 on the radius-two disc. Factoring the inverse
there fixes the mass-one unit-residue pole, excludes other poles
on the unit disc, and gives positive local Phi curvature.

On the inherited finite reference-energy range the Euclidean
inverse defect is bounded by

    [r_upper+r_spec,upper+C_upper ell_upper]/(1-fp_upper)<10^-202.

All three signs are retained. This range is not a cutoff and
this selected functional is not the full two-loop inverse.
Other primitive diagrams, finite interaction conversions,
higher-loop and truncation errors remain to be bounded.
