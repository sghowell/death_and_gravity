for the actual coupled scalar compact-band addition.

it completes the declared seven-mode leading finite
relative response calculation on an explicit nonzero
amplitude box. The original state, retuned action,
physical spatial frame and common counterterms are fixed.

The compensation is only an analysis-variable change.
Weighted noncanonical phase estimates keep the actual
CCR, and the physical trace and intrinsic matter terms
are restored. The global result is not obtained by
extending a compact-time estimate without a tail proof.

state DIFFERENCE; they have not been computed for the
new S6.103 reference or set to zero. In particular the
old S6.82 cancellation belongs to a different Proca
reference and is not transferred without a new bridge.

omitted-operator/heavy-cutoff estimate or renormalized
principal-symbol/self-energy control is established.

a uniform corrected-cone bound.

is inferred from the absolute t^-4 bound. No assertion
about every unbounded Gaussian field configuration,
an independently conserved scalar fluid or finite total
