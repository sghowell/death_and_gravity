# Primary inputs and original application

G. 't Hooft and M. Veltman, Regularization and
renormalization of gauge fields, Nuclear Physics B44
(1972) 189-213, sections 2, 3 and 5:
https://www.staff.science.uu.nl/~hooft101/gthpub/regularization_renormalization.pdf
https://doi.org/10.1016/0550-3213(72)90279-9

The input used here is dimensional regularization with
translation invariance and nonanomalous Ward identities,
and its ordinary four-dimensional limit for convergent
terms. The present vectorlike Dirac loop contains no
gamma5 anomaly. These properties are not a theorem of
the local symbolic verifier.

R. E. Cutkosky, Singularities and Discontinuities of
Feynman Amplitudes, Journal of Mathematical Physics 1
(1960) 429-433, section II, particularly II.C:
https://kamecke.userpage.physik.fu-berlin.de/ps/cutkosky.pdf
https://doi.org/10.1063/1.1703676

The input is the cut discontinuity rule and continuation
of its physical unitarity relation in external momenta.
Its application below the scalar pair threshold here
uses the separately proved uniform heavy-loop domain
and the compact angular integral; physical positivity
is not assumed there.

The leading determinant coefficient and optical
normalization are inherited from unchanged S6.128,
whose own literature note identifies their inputs.
No field-series remainder is imported as a momentum
remainder. The explicit Clifford representation,
complete Ward tensor matrix, six routing domain,
all-degree norm majorant and strict rational error
calibration are derived in this child. They are
written arguments with exact checks, not quotations
or claims of nonperturbative construction.
