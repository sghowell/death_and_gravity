# Six placements, proper local references and the overall contact

The scalar bubble with two local quartic vertices has symmetry factor 1/2.
Differentiate its two propagators D1 D2 with respect to a formal insertion:

    d/dh [(D1+h P1)(D2+h P2)] at zero = P1 D2+D1 P2.

The two terms are equal after real q reflection in the symmetric channel
routing, even with the spectral mass on opposite lines. Thus the factor
two cancels 1/2. There are three channels and two positions per channel:
six named contributions. The loop normalization is the same Q=16pi^2
as in the frozen complete scalar one-loop calculation.

Each P is the entire renormalized first fermion insertion. It includes
the inner diagram and its complete affine ultraviolet and finite on-shell
mass/residue counterterms. Bounding an isolated huge mass counterterm
instead would not be this calculation. No extra external LSZ factor or
exact kinetic-factor resummation is introduced.

After inner subtraction, the remaining overall divergence is a
momentum-independent Phi^4 term. The subtraction at z=2 in the finite
parameter representation is one way to fix that constant. Translating
this literal quartic-vertex family to another prescribed finite quartic
reference adds a local constant only; its forward second derivative
vanishes. No independent b2 or higher-derivative counterterm is fitted.

This is not the complete conversion of the full two-loop amplitude.
In particular, heavy-exchange factors can make other counterterm
variations depend on momentum, and the one-loop interaction/field
conversion inside other lower-order graphs is separate work.
The present family uses leading reference L,Y and mass-one free scalar
lines at formal order two; shifting those vertices inside this family
would first change its order-three coefficient.
