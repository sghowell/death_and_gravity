# What the classical retarded-source theorem does and does not close

S6.91 strengthens S6.90: the exact linear classical response
to a local relational source is not supported inside the
physical matter cone, and some smooth compact source and
detector tests with matter-spacelike separated supports
have a nonzero retarded pairing. This is proved for the
unchanged source-free ordinary-Proca classical action on
the actual nearby bounce, not a new chosen principal PDE.

The retarded solution still vanishes before its source.
Time-directed retarded evolution is not the same as support
inside the physical matter cone. There is no contradiction
with the earlier homogeneous prepared Volterra inverse,
which also concerns a different background/state/profile
scope.

The front proof uses all arbitrarily large classical
frequencies. It does not show that the chosen action is
valid at those frequencies. A finite EFT can receive
higher-derivative, heavy-threshold and quantum corrections
that change its front. The compact-probe existence
argument does not supply an EFT-admitted frequency band
or a bound on the response error after UV matching.
The distinction between resolvable low-energy propagation
and uncontrolled fronts is discussed in
[Causal Effective Field Theories](https://arxiv.org/html/2207.03491v2);
this report supplies its own classical calculation, not
a transfer of that paper's model-dependent bounds.

No full-candidate healthy vacuum, finite Wilson matching,
interacting canonical cutoff, omitted-operator/loop error,
common-parent V/G/B comparison, quantum state/response on
the nearby backgrounds, global complete bounce, nonlinear
probe solution or original P8 closure is established.
In particular, failure of this action treated as an exact
all-frequency matter-causal theory is not a universal
no-go theorem and is not by itself a finite-EFT UV
exclusion.

The next closure-relevant comparison must control a
nonzero compact-probe response, or another suitable
causality observable, within a single parent-admitted
low-energy domain and its error budget. This theorem
identifies the exact classical target; it does not
assume that missing matching estimate.
