# One positive seven-mode addition and its actual mean

Use independent nonnegative amplitudes eta_S,eta_T,eta_P.
The added natural scalar block is eta_S b I4, each TT
polarization has eta_T b I2, and the Cartesian Proca
block has eta_P b I6. These are exactly the previous
three additions, now taken together on the same reference.
Cross covariances remain zero. Their direct sum is
positive and symmetric; the CCR of the reference is
unchanged. Actual free evolution preserves positivity,
and each smooth compact-band difference remains smooth
on compact spacetime sets. Hence the full added state
retains the reference's generalized Hadamard condition.
This is finite homogeneous density, not finite total R3 energy.

The quadratic decomposition also holds BEFORE homogeneous
N,A,p,l restriction. Isotropic scalar contractions with
a TT perturbation vanish by trace and transversality;
contraction of longitudinal York shear with TT vanishes
as well. Cartesian projector identities check these in
every nonzero momentum direction. An ordinary Proca field
on its zero background begins quadratically in the vector;
metric/scalar corrections to those terms start cubically.
The earlier ordinary-Proca replacement has no surviving
affine trace-Gauss mixing. Thus homogeneous variation of
the quadratic Hamiltonian preserves the block decomposition.
This statement concerns this action/order, not its cubic
vertices or an interacting factorization theorem.

The four source expectations therefore add linearly.
By uniqueness of the common zero-anchor linear mean ODE,
the physical (xi,delta_p,n,delta psi) is the sum of the
three previously derived physical mean solutions.
Restore the scalar trace before that sum. The code checks
linearity of the constraint, both mean equations, field
equation and the induced matter observable independently.

The joint bounds are

    |n| <=10^64 eta_S+1500 eta_T+550000000 eta_P,
    |log(a_joint/a)|
         <=2*10^64 eta_S+8000 eta_T+3000000000 eta_P,
    |delta psi|<=10^64 eta_S+1000 eta_T+400000000 eta_P.

The exact frame is evaluated on the TOTAL n and xi. It
is not the sum of separately evaluated nonlinear frame
maps. The displayed bound follows from |xi|+|n| and
triangle inequalities. On eta_S<=10^-80,
eta_T,eta_P<=10^-20, both joint geometric bounds
are strictly below 10^-10 for every real time.

The complete physical scalar-matter observable obeys

    |delta rho_m|, |delta p_m|
       <=10^64 eta_S/t^4
          +(216 eta_T+81000000 eta_P)/t^12.

Only the coupled scalar block has intrinsic matter
fluctuations at this order. Exponential TT volume is one
and its linear lapse vanishes; the zero-background Proca
has no intrinsic scalar-matter term. Their induced mean
terms are retained and counted once through the same sum.

These are statements about a joint FREE state and its
LEADING finite relative source/mean response. They do not
construct a co-evolved interacting state or make the
common unknown absolute source vanish.
