# Primary-source check and evidence boundary

[Howard Haber, Physics 222 Solution Set 4,
Spring 2020](https://scipp.ucsc.edu/~haber/ph222/qftiiisol4_20.pdf),
pages 12-13, equations (62)-(70), supplies a direct
Feynman-gauge self-energy calculation. Its open-line
Casimir, dimensional Dirac numerator and finite
MSbar terms independently check our gauge
normalization. We use the off-shell expression,
not the later pole-mass or supersymmetric
conclusions. Checked 10 September 2026.

The scalar sign, parameter moments, fixed-scale
background derivative and finite-reference bounds
are derived here. The Yukawa UV result is checked
against the immutable S6.128 matrix calculation.
These written and executable checks are not
independent peer review or proof-assistant
formalization.
