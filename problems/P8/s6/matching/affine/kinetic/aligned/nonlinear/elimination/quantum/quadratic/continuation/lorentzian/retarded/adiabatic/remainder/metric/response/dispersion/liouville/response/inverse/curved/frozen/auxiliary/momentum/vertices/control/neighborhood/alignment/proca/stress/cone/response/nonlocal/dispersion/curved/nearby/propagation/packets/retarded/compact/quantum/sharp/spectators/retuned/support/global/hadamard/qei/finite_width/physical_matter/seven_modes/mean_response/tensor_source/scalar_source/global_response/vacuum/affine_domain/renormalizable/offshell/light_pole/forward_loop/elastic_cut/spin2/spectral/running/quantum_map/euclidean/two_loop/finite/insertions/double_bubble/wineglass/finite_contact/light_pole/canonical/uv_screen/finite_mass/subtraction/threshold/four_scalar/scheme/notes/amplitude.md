# Complete first-loop ownership and the two amplitude representations

The old scalar term is the complete S6.113 correction,
including all contact, H exchange, mixed light-heavy,
tadpole and fixed local-reference terms. Its scheme
was specified in that certificate, not assumed to be
MS. S6.132 supplies the complete one-loop fermion
four-point increment and its first field-reference
dictionary. The present bare-coupling conversion
places both in the same canonical representation.

At one fermion loop the four external Phi legs
attach directly to the fourth determinant vertex.
The second determinant vertex can only correct
external light legs of a four-Phi scalar tree:
Phi parity forbids an internal light tree exchange.
No direct H Yukawa exists. Joining a scalar tree
across two points of a fermion cycle, inserting
a gauge exchange, or putting a one-loop counterterm
inside a one-loop graph adds another loop.
Phi is a gauge singlet, so no pure one-loop gauge
graph is missing. Higher determinant vertices need
another scalar loop to supply just four external
Phi legs. Inert flavors contribute no such vertex.

One valid representation is the old scalar correction
plus the new fermion box, added to the tree evaluated
at the converted canonical-star parameters. They
already have unit light LSZ residue.

Expanding that tree through formal order h gives
the equivalent expression at the leading MS numbers:

    A_new=A0+h[A_scalar,old^(1)+A_fermion^(1)
              +ell sum C(z)^2/(2Q)+sigma
              +2(fp-r)A0]+O(h^2).

There is no further field-residue multiple. The fixed
potential contact is undone by +sigma exactly as
required by the new interaction reference. It has
zero forward second derivative and cannot be tuned
to establish the coefficient bound.

For D=M-2, the forward tree second coefficient is
2g/D^3=4lambda. Direct differentiation gives

    [b2] sum C(z)^2=-4Lg/D^3+6g^2/D^4.

Relative to the tree this is -2L+3g/D. On the actual
tree parameter relation it becomes g(4-3D)/D^2,
which is negative. This known finite reference
contribution is retained together with the positive
fermion slope and opposite-sign scalar slope.
Neither its sign nor the selected fermion-only
coefficient is mistaken for the sign of the complete
one-loop correction.

The inherited scalar and new fermion functions are
analytic on the required finite disc at this loop
order. Parameter changes inside those one-loop
functions and nonlinear terms in a tree-parameter
expansion start at two loops. No old pure-scalar
two-loop number bounds them in the enlarged model.
