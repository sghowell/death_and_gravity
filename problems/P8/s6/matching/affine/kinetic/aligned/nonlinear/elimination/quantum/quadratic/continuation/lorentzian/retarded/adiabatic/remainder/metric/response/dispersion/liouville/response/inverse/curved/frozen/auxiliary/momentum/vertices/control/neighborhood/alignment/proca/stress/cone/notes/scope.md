# Scope of the new scalar-cone checkpoint

Established: the actual scalar/matter central principal matrices,
including full spatial reduction, moving canonical terms and
Hamiltonian background time jets; a continuous positive and
subluminal central interval; and a nearby ghost/gradient-positive
but superluminal same-action clock datum with an exact cone
crossing bracket.

This calculation is for the classical S6.81 action. The new
S6.82 fixed profile is part of the retained Gaussian one-point
construction, not silently included in a classical tree cone
or used as a proxy for its functional metric response. A full
quantum principal or nonlocal response calculation must combine
the new operator jets and fixed local contacts consistently.

No nonlinear or all-spatial neighborhood result, uniform nearby
time interval, actual complete nearby bounce, full quantum
stability, interacting cutoff, larger-background tree estimate,
finite-frequency UV exclusion, finite Wilson matching, common
parent V/G/B proof, UV completion or original P8 closure follows.
The optional larger field domain would need its own interaction
and error estimates; the smaller directly certified central cone
domain is the result here.
