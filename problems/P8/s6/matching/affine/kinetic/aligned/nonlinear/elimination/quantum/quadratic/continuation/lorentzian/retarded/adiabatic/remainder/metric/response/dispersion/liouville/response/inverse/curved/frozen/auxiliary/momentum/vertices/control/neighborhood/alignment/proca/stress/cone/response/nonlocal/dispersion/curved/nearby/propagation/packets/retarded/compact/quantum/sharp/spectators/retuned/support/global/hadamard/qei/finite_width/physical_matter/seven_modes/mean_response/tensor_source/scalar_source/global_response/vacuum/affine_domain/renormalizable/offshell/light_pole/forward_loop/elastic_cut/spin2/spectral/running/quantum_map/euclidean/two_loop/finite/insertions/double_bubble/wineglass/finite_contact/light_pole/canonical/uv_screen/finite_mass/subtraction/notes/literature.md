This is context for dispersion constructions when
massless cuts obstruct the usual real subtraction
interval. Its Regge and forward-limit assumptions
are not declared proved for GY14-unbroken.

The finite-cut Cauchy integral, paired local boundary
germs, explicit divided-difference/Cauchy error,
rational logarithm enclosure and regular-part
insufficiency diagnostic are derived here.
The cutting and regulator inputs remain those
identified in unchanged S6.129; they are not
replaced by a new positivity theorem.

The present result is exact algebra with written
complex-analysis and perturbative field-theory
arguments. It is not proof-assistant formalization,
a nonperturbative spectrum theorem or a numerical
claim about the complete high-energy amplitude.
