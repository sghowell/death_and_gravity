# Primary source and independent verification boundary

K. Aoki and K. Shimada, *Galileon and generalized Galileon with projective
invariance in a metric-affine formalism*, arXiv:1806.02589v2, 2018:
[versioned primary paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1806.02589v2).

The conventions are its Eqs.2.1-2.3 and projective transformation 2.16;
the Galileon contractions are 3.16-3.17, and the action and affine
Einstein contraction are 4.1-4.2. Equations 4.5 and 4.9-4.12 are a
comparison for the principal coefficients, not the construction of the
literal connection Hessian or its inverse. The connection remains
unrestricted: neither torsionlessness nor metric compatibility is imposed.

Equation 4.8 prints the bracket f1-3*f1_X. The literal action check gives
f1-3*X*f1_X. This repository records the discrepancy and its nonzero
negative controls explicitly; it does not assert a publisher-issued
erratum. The pure f1(phi,X) R_Gamma control and full 64-component solve
both test the correction, including nonunit negative X.

Appendix B's particular sourced solution does not on its own establish
the rank of every unsourced connection component. The full quotient
calculation here finds the additional factor 8p^2-1, its three-dimensional
exceptional kernel, and a bounded inverse away from it. These are
independent finite-dimensional calculations for the specified CD lift.

The original target coefficients, sign conventions and matter action
are pinned from this project's CD/M1 witness and its earlier derivations.
They are not replaced by a new metric convention or a selected background
jet. The coefficient ODE and the explicit CD-specific inverse are derived
in this checkpoint. General dynamical metric-affine spectrum claims from
other papers or connection charts are not imported as a UV certificate.
