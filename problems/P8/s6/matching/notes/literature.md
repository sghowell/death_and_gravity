# Parent matching: source comparison

Checked 2026-09-04. These are method/regression comparisons, not a literature
priority claim. The P8 convention is (+---), X=(partial phi)^2 with no half;
all matching and background identities are derived in the new local code.

[Aoki, Mukohyama and Namba, 2107.01755v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2107.01755v2),
sections III--IV and V.C--V.E, study scalar parent reductions and distinguish
the stationary mass matrix from physical mixed-mode spectra. Their negative
P_XX branches can be UV-stable in the heavy sector while retaining IR
instabilities. Their parent NEC argument and discussion of conformally
induced matter couplings motivate checking physical-frame background
equations as well as coefficient signs. In particular, their nonminimal
discussion assumes small matter backreaction; it is not a ready-made M1
witness. We do not transfer a pointwise P_XX positivity theorem to DHOST.
Our two-field negative control is derived directly, and the new error bounds
apply specifically to the pinned D bounce and stated matching ansatzes.

[Mukohyama and Namba, 2010.09184v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2010.09184v2),
sections II--IV, construct a curved-field-space partial completion of P(X)
and compare its cosmological background and perturbations order by order.
Their analysis illustrates why heavy-field elimination needs more than a
mass label: corrections and degenerate limits affect its validity. We use
the same separation of parent solutions from reduced approximations, without
importing their coefficient conventions or identifying a partial scalar
completion with a complete gravitational UV theory.

The frame follow-up compares with [Ben Achour, Langlois and Noui,
1602.08398v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1602.08398v1), sections IV--V and VII.B.
Their (5.9)--(5.10) agree with the necessary C_X and B_X conditions derived
here from the independently checked ADM kinetic and tensor identities;
their (7.23) identifies the same loss of invertibility. Their generic local
class-Ia/Horndeski relation is not an everywhere-invertible map of a given
cosmological solution. In particular, an Einstein-Hilbert seed is a strict
subcase of the Horndeski seed; neither can be inferred just from tensor
normalization. No transformation equation is used here solely as a source
transcription.

[Creminelli et al., 1610.04207](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1610.04207), section 3
and appendix A, discuss tensor-frame completeness and the singular map that
would remove the beyond-Horndeski operator along their healthy cosmologies.
This is relevant context, not a ready-made formula for our D lapse-mixing
class. The new calculation derives its own Jacobian, locates its two zeros,
and contrasts that failed map with the already-regular S5 spatial map.
The elementary Einstein-NEC concavity lemma and finite-slice tensor error
bounds are separately proved for the stated parent/geometry assumptions.
None of these known mechanisms is claimed as newly discovered.

The S6.1 source review still governs vacuum/gravitational dispersion questions.
No cited source supplies an applicable numerical Regge remainder for P8, and
none is assumed in this checkpoint.
