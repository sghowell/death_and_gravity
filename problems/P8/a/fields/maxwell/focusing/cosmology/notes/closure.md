# Relation to the literal P8(a) objective

The original repository statement asks for Penrose–Hawking-type
incompleteness theorems using QEIs of realistic quantum fields, with
constants strong enough for cosmological scales. It does not require
an observed-universe fit or a new exact SEE solution before a conditional
singularity theorem can count. Nor does it explicitly require interacting
QED, every field or every spacetime. Those should not be introduced as
new mandatory goals after the fact.

The A.16–A.18 chain now addresses three distinct parts of that objective:

1. A.16 proves an actual free physical photon all-Hadamard local-strip
   QSEI, including the reference anomaly, proper-time sampler derivatives
   and explicit real finite beta_M. It is not a field-independent energy
   axiom or a scalar bound relabeled as electromagnetism.
2. A.17 derives a timelike incompleteness implication in a stipulated
   global spatially flat FLRW spacetime. The energy input for the pure
   Maxwell source is that QSEI. A quantified geometric contraction history
   and conditional future derivative bounds are explicit hypotheses;
   no independent initial SEC or future pointwise SEC is assumed.
3. A.18 proves a strictly positive sourced margin on an explicit C3
   neighborhood of radiation/matter-era geometric histories, for all
   p in [1/2,2/3]. Its requirement delta(1+|beta_M|)<=10^-8 is only a
   Planck-to-proper-time separation for fixed finite beta_M, and its
   independent sigma<=5 permits a stated cosmological-constant/source
   scale. A uniform-p family of smooth future-complete geometries satisfies
   the same history and final future bounds; this rules out a merely
   geometric exclusion being mislabeled as a quantum theorem. The controls
   are not actual allowed quantum SEE solutions. The constants are
   therefore strong enough for cosmological
   durations under the displayed geometric and source hypotheses, not
   merely on the earlier scalar microscopic continuation slab.

This is a proved **free-photon/global-flat-FLRW specialization** of the
literal realistic-field and cosmological-strength objective. The
classical reference geometry need not itself be an exact quantum solution
for the theorem to be valid. If an actual quantum solution satisfies the
hypotheses and has a normal reaching tau, the theorem excludes it. A
separate quantum witness is therefore not a missing proof step in this
conditional implication.

Important boundaries are retained. The past C3 tube is a geometric
hypothesis, and its existence as a geometry does not verify a Maxwell
source match. It does not determine the conditional future caps. A
tempting tighter future envelope was explicitly rejected because its
Taylor bound alone forbade a long extension; the certified theorem uses
the independently consistent, more permissive envelope instead. This
particular tube happens to satisfy initial timelike convergence; A.17's
larger theorem, not this calibration, removes a compulsory initial SEC
premise. The use of a derivative-controlled FLRW class is not a theorem
for arbitrary anisotropic or inhomogeneous spacetimes, null geodesics,
or all realistic/interacting fields. No optimality, curvature blow-up,
maximal inextendibility, observed model, or fundamental EFT validity is
claimed. Those are meaningful extensions if the intended scope demands
them, not concealed prerequisites of the narrower theorem already proved.

For the pure-Maxwell specialization the quantum energy premise is only
the derived photon QSEI. If ordinary matter is additionally admitted,
its nonnegative effective energy density is a separate classical
assumption and must be stated. Such a mixed-source corollary must not be
advertised without qualification as using only quantum energy premises.

Root-level closure of P8(a), and especially P8(b)/P8 as a whole, is not
assigned by this child. The integrated project can evaluate the literal
objective against this precisely delimited theorem without either
silently broadening its conclusion or continually adding new goals.
