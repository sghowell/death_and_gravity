## 2. All-p power-law history and contracting proper clock

Let p in [1/2,2/3] and fix tau>0. The reference expansion history is
a_ref(T)=a_*(T/T_*)^p with T_*=p tau/2. Reversing the normal parameter by

Thus x=s/tau is the dimensionless proper clock, not conformal time.

    -d_x^j[tau H_ref(tau x)]=j!p/(p/2-x)^(j+1)>0.           (3)
