# Scope, source dependence and what this theorem does not say

This child proves a null affine endpoint implication for an actual
free nonminimal conformal scalar in a stated global FLRW class.
It uses the physical double-null QEI of A.22, not a field-independent
null energy axiom or an unproved null-line restriction. The source
and curvature conventions, finite scalar reference and one-sided
Wick qualification are carried through every step.

The central new geometric step is explicit: the actual SEE makes
the TOTAL null stress spatially homogeneous, so finite-plane
integration supplies a Ricci bound for the FLRW index form without
requiring the quantum state to be homogeneous. A one-sided field
cap is used on a finite two-dimensional rectangle; it is not
silently weakened to a cap only along a null segment. Such a
weakening would encounter the pinned A.21 obstruction.

The theorem has separate actual past and conditional future
geometric/state hypotheses. It proves neither the future caps from
the past nor a momentum cutoff from a small Wick square. No
independent pointwise NEC or SEC is added. For a pure scalar source,
the energy premise is the derived state-qualified scalar QEI. An
additional source's null lower bound is a separate premise and
must not be omitted when claiming that mixed-source corollary.

The complete geometry in control.md rules out a purely geometric
endpoint claim. It is not a small-source SEE solution. The actual
thermal SEE example in thermal.md verifies the required past, not
the conditional future. Those two controls have different logical
roles and cannot be conflated.

The original P8(a) objective concerns realistic-field QEIs with
cosmological-scale constants. This is a concrete massless conformal
scalar null specialization with explicit dimensionless budgets;
it is not a claim about arbitrary mass, coupling, interacting fields
or general non-FLRW spacetimes. Those broader conclusions do not
follow from this proof, but an observed-universe fit or an optimal
constant is not inserted as an extra prerequisite for the precise
conditional theorem already stated. Integrated root closure remains
a separate assessment, especially because P8(b)'s UV matching remains
open.

The QEI antecedent and its primary-source context are in the fully
replayed A.22 report. The affine transport, finite-plane reduction,
outgoing Jacobi identity, constants and complete geometry control
are written out here. The native and Fraction checks certify their
displayed algebra and constants, not distributional QFT or global
Lorentzian geometry in a proof assistant. They are not independent
peer review. No maximal inextendibility, curvature blowup or
fundamental EFT endpoint control is assigned.
