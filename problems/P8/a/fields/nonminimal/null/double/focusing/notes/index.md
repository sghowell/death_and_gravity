2tau. The whole closed sampled interval then lies in the actual
smooth positive global FLRW geometry. Use an initial round sphere

    eta'=a^-2,
    R_comoving=tau+eta,
    b=a(tau+eta)>0,
    b'=a'(tau+eta)+1/a,
    b''=a''(tau+eta),                                    (9)

where eta(0)=0 and primes denote affine lambda derivatives. Positivity
of b holds on every existing finite future segment, because a>0

    theta0=2b'(0)/b(0)=2(H0+1/tau)<=-2/tau.

    h'^2+(b''/b)h^2
