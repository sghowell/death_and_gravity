# Primary-source context and independent derivation

[Fliss, Freivogel and Kontou, arXiv:2111.05772](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2111.05772)
studies double-null smearing and boost-dependent bounds for null
stress in quantum field theory. This supplies context, not a
novelty claim for double-null smearing or an imported numerical
constant. Here the physical four-dimensional vacuum measure,
both derivative kernels, half-plane integration and normalization
are derived explicitly in plane.md and replayed natively.

[Fliss, Freivogel, Kontou and Santos, arXiv:2309.10848](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2309.10848)
distinguishes physical from effective nonminimal stress and gives
state-dependent null inequalities. Its Section IV.A-B and Theorem
IV.1 were checked for scope. We do not apply that general theorem
verbatim: the positive-squares rearrangement here restricts xi to
[0,1/2] and yields a nonnegative Wick weight permitting a one-sided
upper cap. No all-coupling extension or momentum cutoff is inferred.

The scalar conformal stress and reference prescription are imported
only from the fully replayed local A.20 certificate through A.21.
Their primary-source convention audit remains there. The affine
contraction, sampling measures, derivative operators and reference
null polynomial in this branch are freshly reconstructed. Native
algebra verifies formulas; the analytic proofs are written and
source-pinned, not independently peer reviewed or formalized.
