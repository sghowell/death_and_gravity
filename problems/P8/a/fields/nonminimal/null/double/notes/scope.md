# Scope and controls

The result concerns the physical null contraction of a free real
massless scalar. On flat space the positive-squares proof covers
0<=xi<=1/2; only xi=1/6 is transported to conformally flat FLRW.
The field is not an interacting model and the Wick upper cap is
an explicit condition on the relative state expectation throughout
the two-dimensional sample. It is neither an absolute field cap
nor a condition bounding every physical momentum or total energy.

The local four-dimensional stress is smeared AFTER taking its
renormalized expectation. No transverse smear of Phi is squared.
The plane two-point pullback is justified, in contrast to a direct
vacuum pullback onto a null line. The full nonminimal improvement,
all spatial Fourier frequencies, the mixed derivative cross term,
physical four-dimensional mode measure and boost scaling are kept.

The selected compact profile has zero value and first traces,
not arbitrarily assigned derivative norms. Its full polynomial
Gram is reconstructed twice, including an independent Fraction
calculation without SymPy. Its H0^2 limiting argument is explicit.
The rational boost constant is not rounded or claimed optimal.

A.21 is a pinned negative control: a one-sided cap on a NULL
SEGMENT cannot prevent arbitrarily negative compact null energy.
The extra sampling width here is an essential hypothesis, and
its shrinking limit diverges. This proof does not assert that
transverse-only smearing on a null hypersurface would suffice.

A two-dimensional bound is not automatically a focusing condition
on each individual null geodesic. Missing steps include a justified
reduction to the appropriate affine index form and controlled
curvature/state hypotheses on the actual geometry. Equations
(3)-(4) supply concrete physical inputs, not that missing theorem.
Nor do they supply a self-consistent semiclassical solution or
an original P8(b) vacuum/gravity/common-parent UV classification.

The Python checks certify exact displayed identities, coefficients,
positive-domain controls, source bytes and read-only report replay.
They do not formalize positive-type distribution theory, Sobolev
density or conformal quantum field theory in a proof assistant,
and are not independent peer review. All those written arguments
are pinned as part of the source manifest. Original P8 stays open.
