The usual all-Hadamard absence of four-dimensional null-line
lower bounds for the minimally coupled massless scalar is an
established result; see Theorem II.1 of
[Fewster and Roman](https://arxiv.org/pdf/gr-qc/0209036).
The present calculation independently constructs a smooth
single-mode squeezed family and proves a stronger qualification

cap on the same segment does not remove the obstruction.
It retains nonminimal improvement for every 0<=xi<=1/4.

The publisher also lists a
[2009 erratum](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.80.069903).
Its full text was not accessible during this check.

is imported: the mass-shell measure, normalized continuum
mode, exact occupation covariance and all stress/energy
constants here are derived independently in the pinned sources.
The literature citation is a baseline result, not proof of this
specific capped-family refinement or a claim of novelty.

The following distinctions are essential.

- The restriction is of the smooth vacuum-subtracted stress and
  Wick-square expectations, not of a singular vacuum covariance.
- Each state is an actual normalized Hadamard Fock state with
  finite energy, but there is NO common upper bound on the
  energy, physical momentum or absolute Wick square.
- The one-sided cap holds on the whole sampled null segment,
  not every spacetime point or every affine time.
- Complete-line ANEC is positive for this family. No general
  ANEC theorem or self-consistent gravitational conclusion
  follows from this computation.
- The field is fixed-background Minkowski. This is not an
  admitted SEE geometry/source pair or a bounce UV test.
- Neither transverse/double smearing, an energy or momentum
  cutoff, a two-sided state bound, entropy information nor
  an alternative null focusing hypothesis is excluded here.
