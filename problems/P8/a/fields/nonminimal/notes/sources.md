Theorem 4.3, supplies a primary-source cross-check of the flat
state-dependent inequality for real compact samplers and
0<=xi<=1/4. This branch independently recomputes the massless
coefficient and boundary identity. Its compact-Cauchy coherent
counterexample is derived here, not imported as their example.

[Herzog and Huang, arXiv:1301.5002](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1301.5002),
Eq. (23) and the scalar a4=1/360 discussion, fix the zero-type-D
conformally flat reference input. The explicit FK sign map and
finite beta_S coordinate are stated in anomaly.md.
