# Primary-source audit and the exact evidence boundary

The pinned mathematical starting point is A.11,
`certificates/smooth-preparation.json`, SHA256
`8dba8215c5a28e2a6379c09e4d452838449065c396e10888aa733b186799456f`.
Its actual-map and full-state proof remain unchanged. Its replay includes
A.10's causal logarithmic inverse, including the positive pole. A.13
derives the additional Einstein-resummed poles directly; it does not
borrow a source's Fourier contour as a replacement for the retarded one.

* [Meda--Pinamonti--Siemssen, arXiv:2007.14665](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2007.14665),
  Section 5.1 and Eqs. (60)--(64), identifies the retarded logarithmic
  derivative and a local inverse strategy. Proposition 5.8 and Theorem
  5.9 are local fixed-point statements with compatible state/metric data;
  Remark 5.2 distinguishes their C3 mild result from a pointwise smooth
  trace equation. Remark 5.3 gives local continuous dependence, not the
  long-time quantitative estimate needed here. Section 6 leaves global
  maximal-solution analysis open. No massive-field theorem or unrestricted
  smoothness conclusion is imported into this massless model.
* [Flanagan--Wald, arXiv:gr-qc/9602052](https://arxiv.org/pdf/gr-qc/9602052),
  Section IV and Appendix A, separates exact solutions of the linearized
  semiclassical equations from order-reduced perturbative prescriptions.
  Appendix A studies the poles and exponentially growing pieces of the
  linear response; these are not automatically represented by an ordinary
  real-frequency transform. This motivates checking the complete causal
  analytic structure, not asserting a result for our rolling metric.
* [Hu--Roura--Verdaguer, arXiv:gr-qc/0402029](https://arxiv.org/pdf/gr-qc/0402029),
  Appendix E, distinguishes a retarded prescription with growing poles,
  late-time boundary prescriptions, and order reduction. In particular
  the alternative contour that removes late-time growth is not the same
  retarded response. We use that distinction, not their stochastic-gravity
  state or tensor-sector numerical constants.

The full trace/sign dictionary is checked against A.11: FK curvature,
G_ab=-kappa T_ab, physical epsilon=1, fixed lambda and gamma=0. The source
papers' conventions are not substituted into this dictionary. The pole
count, positive Volterra series, residue/cut signs, norm inequalities and
forcing moment in this subtree are independent derivations. They establish
an exact frozen linear operator theorem, not the stability or instability
of any actual nonlinear semiclassical trajectory.
