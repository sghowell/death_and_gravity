# P8-A.11: conserved preparation and a smooth local actual SEE solution

This is a **short-slab existence theorem**, not continuation over A.8's
whole target, a transfer of A.9, or completion of original P8(a).

## Frozen physical data

Keep the massless minimally coupled scalar, the original positive
zero-mean homogeneous isotropic quasifree vacuum in the radiation past,
and the raw physical prescription

    lambda = 2 sqrt(2) A eta_star^2, gamma = 0.

Keep zero cosmological constant, no additional explicit curvature-squared
gravitational source, and the ordinary radiation normalization

    rho_rad = 3 A^2/(kappa a_phys^4).

Use the FK conventions of A.8: `R=6 a_phys''/a_phys^3`, and the physical
Einstein equation has density `3 H^2=kappa rho` and trace
`R=kappa Theta`. The quantum state's radiation integration constant is
the original zero, not a parameter adjusted in this construction.

Fix the physical, not bookkeeping, amplitude

    delta = 10^-14, kappa hbar = 2880 pi^2 delta A^2 eta_star^4.

Set `x=eta/eta_star-1`, `a=a_phys/(A eta_star)`, `u=eta_star^2 U`,
`h=a'/a`, and use dimensionless primes below. The original last free
slice is `x=0`. The pinned A.8 history, denoted by bars, is unchanged
through its plateau point `y=5/2`, at conformal time `x0`. No new vacuum
is assigned at `x0`.

## The permitted joint preparation

Let `s=x-x0`, `L=10^-10`, and choose a smooth real cutoff `chi` with
`0<=chi<=1`, `chi=1` on `[0,L/4]`, and `chi=0` on `[L/2,L]`.
For example `chi(s)=1-sigma(4s/L-1)` using the original smooth step.
Let `cbar(x)` be the fixed weighted actual density defect on the old
smooth A.8 history:

    cbar = pi^2 eta_star^4 a_phys_bar^4/hbar
           * [3 Hbar^2/kappa - rho_rad_bar - rho_quantum_bar].

Prescribe `c(x0+s)=chi(s)cbar(x0+s)` in the new slab and `c=cbar`
in the unchanged past. On the unknown geometry define

    rho_ext = hbar/(pi^2 A^4 eta_star^8) * c/a^4,
    p_ext   = hbar/(pi^2 A^4 eta_star^8) * [c/(3a^4)-c'/(3h a^4)].

This source is exactly conserved on that geometry. It vanishes,
including its pressure, in the final half-slab. It is an auxiliary
preparation stress, not an extra physical source in the final equation.
It need not obey an energy condition and is **not globally compact in
time**: the unchanged off-shell radiation past itself requires a
nonzero preparation stress. Its possibly large switch derivatives are
not silently bounded by its density amplitude.

## Conclusion and quantitative neighborhood

There is a unique fixed point in the closed ball

    X(s)=u'(x0+s)-ubar'(x0+s), X(0)=0, ||X|| <= 10^-6,

for the full conserved-source integrated-trace map in
[the construction proof](notes/construction.md). Its potential and
metric are smooth, agree exactly with the old history through an
initial neighborhood of the slab, and obey

    2 <= a <= 3, 1/3 <= h <= 1/2,
    ||u-ubar|| <= L ||X||,
    ||X|| <= (63*10^-9)/(1-3/25) < 72*10^-9.

The original vacuum is transported on this new smooth metric. It is
therefore an actual positive Hadamard state; it is not the old final
covariance frozen while changing metric jets. The fixed classical
radiation and actual quantum stress solve the **full unforced SEE** on
the open final subslab `x0+L/2 < x < x0+L`.

The intermediate forced solution solves both the energy and trace
equations. The source construction supplies the exact initial energy
constraint; conservation propagates it without changing radiation.
The theorem's uniqueness is within the stated map, data, cutoff and
ball. It is not uniqueness among every possible preparation or branch.

## Explicit non-conclusions

No claim is made of a solution on the whole `2<y<3` window, controlled
large-time response, a small bound for every higher metric derivative,
state fluctuations, or a full physical validity regime for the switch.
A.9 remains a theorem on its original fixed metric. Transferring its
two-point reference estimate and proving the needed focusing hypotheses
on an exact solution are later obligations. Massive, interacting and
realistic field content is still outside this free-field testbed.

The certificate checks exact algebra, rational estimates, source pins,
and independent finite controls. The all-frequency estimates, Banach
argument, smoothness induction and Hadamard propagation are written
analytic proofs, not formalized theorems generated by the test suite.
