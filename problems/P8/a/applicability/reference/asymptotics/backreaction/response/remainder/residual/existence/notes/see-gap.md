# What remains before an actual SEE solution can be claimed

A.8 certifies a finite residual of the **actual** quantum source on a
fixed smooth prepared geometry. A.9 certifies an all-sampler QSEI on
that same geometry. A.10 bounds changes of one nonlinear mode functional
and provides a causal logarithmic inverse. None of these statements
implies that a nearby exact semiclassical geometry exists.

## The still-missing full map

In the massless minimal convention, `U=-a''/a`, so the new uniform norm
of `U'` is suitable for an integrated-trace variable. But the complete
equation also contains the local geometric terms, anomaly, fixed finite
prescription, inverse powers of the metric, auxiliary trace evolution,
and gravitational scales. All of them must enter the actual functional
map and its self-map and Lipschitz inequalities. The small coefficient
for `R'[U]-R'[V]` is not a bound for their sum.

In particular the positive pole of the isolated logarithmic inverse is
retained in A.10. Its compact-interval norm has an exponentially growing
lower contribution as the duration increases. A short-interval
contraction cannot be extrapolated to the full target interval without
an enclosure or a separate stability/branch-selection argument. A small
source residual alone is not such an argument.

## Actual compatible state and initial data

The initial Einstein energy constraint must be satisfied exactly with
the chosen ordinary radiation source and actual state. If the old metric
is frozen up to a future starting slice, a smooth continuation fixes all
its initial jets and the transported Cauchy covariance there. Merely
declaring a different initial energy value cannot correct its mismatch.

Changing initial metric jets or classical radiation normalization is a
different construction and must be stated and controlled. In particular,
altering metric jets while treating a Cauchy covariance as unchanged is
not a proof that it is still compatible with a Hadamard state. A joint
metric/state preparation or an explicitly compatible initial hierarchy
is needed; it cannot be replaced by zeroing abstract state moments.

The present `C1` mode functional does have a well-defined nonlinear
continuation on nonsmooth potential balls. That useful analytic fact
does not manufacture a smooth spacetime or promote a finite-regularity
state to a Hadamard state. Even a fixed point for `U in C1` would only
give `a in C3` before a further regularity argument.

## Later application is another gate

After a suitable smooth exact-solution construction, its full
two-point function, geometry and reference stress would need estimates
on the relevant geodesic and sampling domains. A.9's coefficient on its
fixed metric does not automatically transfer to that solution.
The focusing theorem also has initial geometric and domain hypotheses;
realistic massive/interacting field content is not covered by the
single free massless minimal scalar testbed.

These are mathematical research obligations. A.10 neither solves them
nor identifies a missing permission or user choice. Original P8(a) and
P8 therefore remain open.
