# Primary-source and lineage audit

Checked 2026-09-06. The full conserved-source map, short-slab constants
and flat-start smoothness proof are derived here, not imported from a
massive-model or abstract state-moment theorem.

- [Meda--Pinamonti--Siemssen, 2007.14665](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2007.14665),
  Proposition 5.3 and Remark 5.2: causal inversion of the retarded
  logarithm and the distinction between a `C3` mild metric and a
  sufficiently regular pointwise solution. The pinned A.10 inverse
  includes the positive pole. This new construction supplies its own
  common-past mode estimates, compatible preparation and smoothness
  argument; it does not simply specialize that paper's massive result.
- [Gottschalk--Siemssen, 1809.03812v3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1809.03812v3),
  Section 5.6 and Theorem 5.11: smooth preparation and the energy
  constraint are separate obligations. Here an exactly conserved
  weighted-density source enforces both throughout the switch. Its
  actual quantum state is transported from the original past, not
  identified with an arbitrary solution of an abstract moment system.
- [Pinned A.10 formulation](../../FORMULATION.md): the actual
  all-frequency nonlinear functional, shared-history Lipschitz bound
  and causal pole-plus-cut inverse. The present theorem adds the
  gravitational, local-geometric, source and auxiliary terms.
- [Pinned A.8 formulation](../../../FORMULATION.md): actual stress,
  named prescription, prepared Hadamard history and density-defect
  constants used to bound the external source. No old certificate,
  formulation or source file is changed.

The two primary papers motivate and delimit the construction. Exact
identities and rational replay do not by themselves prove Banach's
theorem, the all-order smoothness induction, or Hadamard propagation;
those analytic steps are stated and justified in the written proofs.
