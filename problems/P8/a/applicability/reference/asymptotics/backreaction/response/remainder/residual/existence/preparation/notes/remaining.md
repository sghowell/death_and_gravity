# Remaining P8(a) obligations after this local construction

A.11 changes the local applicability status: a smooth actual-state
unforced SEE solution now exists on a specified final short slab,
with fixed ordinary radiation and a jointly conserved preparation.
This does **not** imply the following later steps.

1. Continue or otherwise construct an exact solution on a domain long
   enough for the intended focusing argument. The positive pole of the
   logarithmic inverse remains; repeatedly asserting local existence
   does not bound accumulated response or higher jets.
2. Bound the actual two-point reference functional, proper-time
   geometry and relevant stress on that exact solution. A.9's
   all-sampler QSEI is still on its old fixed metric, not automatically
   on the new one. A small `C1` potential change alone is not the
   previously proved sampling-domain estimate.
3. Verify the geometric initial conditions, sampling domains and
   quantitative focusing/incompleteness conclusion, not merely a
   pointwise stress sign in one reference state.
4. Extend beyond the present free massless minimal field where the
   original realistic-field problem requires it. Neither an arbitrary
   interacting field theorem nor full semiclassical fluctuation control
   follows from this testbed.

The auxiliary source is zero in the physical target slab, but no claim
is made that the entire preparation is an autonomous solution of the
source-free cosmological model. Its high pressure derivatives and the
very short dimensionless slab are stated costs of the construction.

These are research tasks, not missing user authorization or a reason
to relabel original P8 as closed. Nor does P8(a) logically require
global shadowing of this particular A.8 candidate if a different exact
solution/focusing route can meet the original theorem's requirements.
