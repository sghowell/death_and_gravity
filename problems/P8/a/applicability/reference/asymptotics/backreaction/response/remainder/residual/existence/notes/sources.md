# Source and applicability audit

The immediate immutable input is A.9, certificate SHA256
`514b4ee50201a13c45dd02ca7bd4879dd58c95453740a0466c5c9c215b04cf20`.
Its replay includes A.8's actual stress and A.7's prepared-state mode
functional. A.10 reads the first potential-derivative bound from the
replayed A.7 certificate and does not alter any ancestor artifact.

[Meda, Pinamonti and Siemssen, *Existence and uniqueness of solutions of
the semiclassical Einstein equation in cosmological models*](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2007.14665)
motivates the integrated-trace norm and logarithmic inverse; relevant
locations are Proposition 3.2, Proposition 5.3 and Lemma 5.7.
Theorem 5.9 requires its stated initial-state compatibility and frequency
positivity assumptions. Remark 5.2 expressly identifies the resulting
regularity as `C3` and the solution as mild. We do not use that theorem
as an already-proved smooth massless shadowing result for A.8.

The positive spectral representation, dyadic norm majorant, actual
massless two-potential estimates and rational constants are derived and
checked in this subtree. The source's qualitative contraction constants
are not assigned arbitrary numerical values. The mode note separately
records a test of one displayed auxiliary estimate and supplies direct
replacement inequalities; it does not purport to refute the paper's
existence theorem or make a global literature-novelty claim.

The source-based gaps and the precise non-claims are recorded in
[the remaining-obligations note](see-gap.md). In particular a local
operator inverse is not an all-duration stability estimate and an
actual-mode continuity lemma is not a complete SEE solution.
