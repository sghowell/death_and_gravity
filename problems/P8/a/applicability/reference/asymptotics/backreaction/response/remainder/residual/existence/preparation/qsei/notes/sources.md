  not substituted into a geometric proper-time theorem.
* [Fewster--Kontou, A semiclassical singularity theorem,
  arXiv:2108.12668](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2108.12668), Section 5,
  Eqs. (46)--(47), gives the normal-geodesic index form and sufficient
  focal-point test. Theorem 5.1 has further domain and initial-curvature
  assumptions. We check why its underlying test does not fire on the
  present comoving slab; we do not assert a theorem about other surfaces
  or about an unknown extended solution.

Both primary PDFs were checked directly for the stated formulas. The
new two-frequency scattering estimates, actual-SEE reference credit and
finite-window index obstruction are derived in this subtree. In FK
conventions the physical EED is `(rho+3p)/2`, the Einstein equation is
`G_ab=-kappa T_ab`, and `R_UU=-kappa E_total`. There is no change to the
finite stress prescription, ordinary radiation integration constant, or
the quantum state's original history. The certificate verifies exact
algebra/arithmetic and source hashes; the functional analytic arguments
are written proofs, not a formalized PDE or global causal theorem.
