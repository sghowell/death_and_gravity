# P2 S2: route M3 at the barrier top — written proof of the window certificates

Status: S2, 2026-10-17. This is the proof behind `src/p2/m3.py` and
`src/p2/m3_contour.py`, and behind the certificates `certificates/m3/*.json`.
Pre-registration: `notes/s2-preregistration.md`.
Replay: `python -m p2.m3 verify` (about 1 min). Conventions: FORMULATION v0.3.
Point: P_B. Here β = (½+δ)κ₋, ω_l = (l+½)Ω_P and x = r* (the tortoise
coordinate). The FORMULATION Jost normalization makes x real on (r₊, r_c).

## Theorem (certified for l ∈ {20, 50, 100})

  W_l(ω) ≠ 0 for every ω in 𝒲_l = [ω_l − 8γ₀, ω_l + 6γ₀] × [−β, 0].

The certified coercivity margins, with μ_HO = 2(l+½)Ω_Pγ₀:

| l | μ_cert | μ_cert/μ_HO | float λ at low corner/μ_HO | min c | cells |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 20 | 0.32454 | 0.528 | 0.661 | 0.119 | 1.35 M (deep bisection) |
| 50 | 0.85991 | 0.568 | 0.681 | 0.120 | 20 k |
| 100 | 1.6854 | 0.560 | 0.679 | 0.121 | 18 k |

- The window's top lies above the P2-2 threshold a_l: a_l/κ₋ = 106.64, 255.51
  and 503.43, against window tops 110.94, 258.50 and 504.44. The a_100
  certificate was computed in S2 (`certificates/m3/lg_PB_l100.json`).
- Hence, at these l, the whole set {Re ω ≥ ω_l − 8γ₀, −β ≤ Im ω ≤ 0} is free of
  QNMs: P2-2 covers Re ω ≥ a_l, and the theorem covers the window.

## 1. The rotated contour

Let x_P = r*(r_P) = −2.43797 and let the contour be

  x(s) = x_P + e^{iπ/4}s,  s ∈ ℝ.

Write r(x) for the inverse of r* on the exterior. It solves dr/dx = F(r), where
F = Δ/r² = −(Λ/3)∏(r − r_i)/r².

**Lemma 1 (no singularity between the real axis and the contour).** r(x) extends
analytically, by vertical continuation from the real axis, to the closed sectors
Σ₊ = {Re x ≥ x_P, 0 ≤ Im x ≤ Re x − x_P} and Σ₋ = {Re x ≤ x_P, Re x − x_P ≤ Im x ≤ 0},
with r(x) ∉ {0, ∞}. Hence V_l(x) = V_l(r(x)) is analytic on Σ₊ ∪ Σ₋.

*Proof.*
- Along any continuation, Re r*(r(x)) = Re x, because Re r* = Σ_i A_i log|r − r_i|
  is single-valued.
- Σ_i A_i = 0 exactly (the residues of r²/Δ = O(r⁻²)). So a continuation can fail
  only where r → 0, i.e. at Re x = Re r*(0) = Σ A_i log|r_i| = −2.29664, or where
  r → ∞, i.e. at Re x = 0. It cannot approach a root r_i at finite Re x.
- The ODE dr/dx = F(r) is analytic on ℂ∖{0}, so a continuation exists for as long
  as r stays in a compact subset of ℂ∖{0}.
- Σ₋ has Re x ≤ x_P < −2.29664 (certified margin 0.141), so Σ₋ is free.
- In Σ₊, only the two vertical segments at Re x = −2.29664 (height 0.1413) and at
  Re x = 0 (height 2.4380) can carry a singularity. Validated integration along
  both keeps |r| ∈ [1.96, 2.01] and [2.41, 2.49] respectively
  (`m3_PB_geometry.json`).
- The vertically continued function R(X, Y) satisfies ∂_X R = F(R) as well: the
  difference G = ∂_X R − F(R) solves a linear ODE in Y with G(X, 0) = 0. It
  therefore satisfies the Cauchy–Riemann equations and is analytic. ∎

**Lemma 2 (the contour image).**
- Validated a-priori-box Taylor steps of dr/ds = e^{iπ/4}F(r) enclose r(s) on
  s ∈ [−64, 32]. They use step 1/16, order 20, and a wrapping-free propagation of
  the initial ball through the exact scalar flow derivative F(r(t))/F(r₀).
- The identity Re r*(r(s)) = x_P + s/√2 is checked to hold within the balls at
  every 8th step.
- For s ≤ −64: |r(s) − r₊| ≤ 0.00956. For s ≥ 32: |r(s) − r_c| ≤ 0.351.
  Proof: the identity gives |r − r_h| = exp((Re x − Σ_{i≠h}A_i log|r − r_i|)/A_h).
  Bounding the sum on a disc around r_h (radius 0.06 resp. 0.6) and running a
  first-exit continuity argument gives the result. ∎

## 2. From a QNM to a decaying solution on the contour

**Lemma 3.** Suppose ω ∈ 𝒲_l has W_l(ω) = 0. Then u(s) := Φ(x(s)) is a nonzero
solution of

  −u″ + Q u = 0,  Q(s) = e^{iπ/2}(V_l(r(s)) − ω²),

and u and u′ decay exponentially as s → ±∞. Here Φ is the common Jost solution.

*Proof.*
- W_l(ω) = 0 means R₊ ∝ R_c (FORMULATION §1.2), i.e. Φ₊ := rR₊ ∝ Φ_c := rR_c on
  the real exterior. No identification with Volterra solutions is needed here.
- Φ″ = (V − ω²)Φ continues analytically into Σ₋ and Σ₊ (Lemma 1). On the contour,
  d²/ds² = e^{iπ/2} d²/dx². Take u = Φ₊∘x for s ≤ 0 and u = λΦ_c∘x for s ≥ 0;
  the two agree at s = 0, where x = x_P is real.
- **The disc around r₊.** Let X₋ := Re x(−64) = x_P − 64/√2 = −47.69. Every vertical
  segment from the real axis to the contour with Re x = X ≤ X₋ stays in the disc
  D₊ = {|r − r₊| ≤ 0.00956}, by the first-exit argument of Lemma 2 with the
  identity Re r*(r) = X.
  - D₊ lies inside the convergence disc of the Frobenius factor g₊, of radius
    r₊ − r₋ = 0.0970 > 0.00956.
  - R̃₊(r) := Σ_{i≠+} A_i log((r − r_i)/(r₊ − r_i)) is analytic and bounded on D₊
    (principal logs, arguments within 0.1 of 1).
- **The x-representation.** On the real exterior near r₊,
  x = A₊ log(r − r₊) + c₊ + R̃₊(r), with A₊ = 1/(2κ₊) and the real constant
  c₊ = Σ_{i≠+} A_i log|r₊ − r_i| (`Point.c_plus`). So on the real half-line
  Re x ≤ X₋, where r − r₊ > 0,
    (r − r₊)^{−iω/(2κ₊)} = e^{−iωA₊ log(r − r₊)} = e^{−iω(x − c₊ − R̃₊(r))},
  and the Jost solution there is
    Φ₊(x) = c·r(x)·g₊(r(x))·exp(−iω(x − c₊ − R̃₊(r(x)))).      (∗)
  - Let Ω₋ := Σ₋ ∩ {Re x ≤ X₋}; it contains the contour for s ≤ −64. On a
    neighbourhood of Ω₋, r(x) is analytic and single-valued in x (Lemma 1), with
    values in or near D₊. So the right side of (∗) is analytic there, as a
    function of x.
  - The continued Φ₊ (the ODE has analytic coefficients on Σ₋) agrees with (∗)
    on the real half-line. Ω₋ is connected, so they agree on all of Ω₋ (identity
    theorem).
  - (∗) must be read as a function of x, not of r. The contour image spirals
    into r₊: arg(r(s) − r₊) = 2κ₊ Im x(s) + O(0.1), which tends to −∞, and the
    continuous argument is already −3.17 at s = −64. No single branch of
    log(r − r₊), or of (r − r₊)^{−iω/(2κ₊)}, exists along the contour; x keeps
    track of the winding. Near r₊, r(x) = r₊ + ψ(e^{2κ₊(x − c₊)}), where ψ
    inverts r ↦ (r − r₊)e^{2κ₊R̃₊(r)}.
- **Decay.** Hence |u(s)| ≤ C|e^{−iωx(s)}| = C e^{Im(ω x_P) + s·Im(ω e^{iπ/4})}.
  The same bound holds for u′ = e^{iπ/4}Φ′. Differentiating (∗) with
  dr/dx = F(r) gives Φ′ = e^{−iω(x − c₊ − R̃₊(r))}·h(r), with h analytic and
  bounded on D₊.
- **The window.** Im(ω e^{iπ/4}) = (Re ω + Im ω)/√2 ≥ (a₁ − β)/√2 > 0 is certified
  at every ω of each window (a₁ + b₁ > 0). At l = 20 this gives decay at least
  like e^{−61κ₋|s|}, and faster at l = 50 and 100.
- **The cosmological end** is the same, with X₊ := Re x(32) = x_P + 32/√2 = 20.19
  and the disc D_c = {|r − r_c| ≤ 0.351}. D_c lies inside the convergence disc of
  g_c, of radius r_c − r₊ = 4.77 > 0.351. ∎

## 3. Variable-phase coercivity

Take any real C¹ phase φ(s) and set ε₁ = η|φ′| (η > 0). Integrate by parts; the
boundary terms vanish by Lemma 3. Then use Young's inequality,
|φ′||u′||u| ≤ ε₁|u′|² + φ′²/(4ε₁)|u|². For every u that decays with u′:

  J(u) := Re ∫ e^{−iφ}(−u″ + Qu)ū ds ≥ ∫ c|u′|² + q|u|²,
  c = cos φ − η|φ′|,   q = Re(e^{−iφ}Q) − |φ′|/(4η).

(The middle step: ∫ −u″e^{−iφ}ū = ∫ e^{−iφ}|u′|² − i∫φ′e^{−iφ}u′ū.)

Design: φ = φ_∞ s⁴/(s₁⁴ + s⁴) with φ_∞ = −1.4375, s₁ = 5 and η = 1/2.
- Near the barrier top φ ≈ 0. There q ≈ ½|V″|s² − 2|Im ω| Re ω: the inverted
  oscillator, rotated by π/4 into a harmonic one.
- In the tails φ → φ_∞. There q ≈ |sin φ_∞| Re ω² > 0.

## 4. Ground-state bound

**Lemma 4.** Let c ≥ 0. Let g = e^{−G} > 0, with G′ continuous and piecewise
linear, and G′ = 0 outside a compact set. If E := q + c′G′ − cG′² + cG″ ≥ μ almost
everywhere, then ∫ c|u′|² + q|u|² ≥ μ‖u‖² for every decaying u as in Lemma 3.

*Proof.*
- Write u = gv. Then ∫c|u′|² = ∫cg²|v′|² − ∫(cg′)′g|v|² + [c g g′|v|²].
- c g g′ is continuous and piecewise C¹, so it is absolutely continuous. Since
  g′/g = −G′ vanishes outside a compact set, the boundary term is 0.
- −(cg′)′/g = c′G′ − cG′² + cG″, which gives ∫c|u′|² + q|u|² ≥ ∫E g²|v|² ≥ μ‖u‖². ∎

**Proof of the theorem.**
- Lemma 3 gives a nonzero u with J(u) = 0.
- §3 and Lemma 4 give J(u) ≥ μ‖u‖² with μ = μ_cert > 0.
- This is a contradiction, so W_l(ω) ≠ 0. ∎

## 5. What the computer checks (m3.py)

- **E ≥ μ_cert on s ∈ [−64, 32].** On each cell (cells are split at contour
  steps and at s = 0), use the mean-value form
  E(s) ≥ E(s_m) − sup_cell|E′|·w/2.
  - E(s_m) is evaluated at the point s_m, with r(s_m) from the Taylor model.
  - E′ is evaluated over the cell box, with r enclosed on the cell and
    dQ/ds = e^{3iπ/4}V′(r)F(r).
  - Exact derivatives of φ come from a truncated series.
  - Both are evaluated for all ω² in the endpoint-exact interval hull of
    {ω² : ω ∈ 𝒲_l}.
    - E is affine in ω², so for each s the interval evaluation over the hull
      rectangle is exact.
    - The rectangle is, however, larger than the image {ω² : ω ∈ 𝒲_l}. In floats
      this costs about 0.02–0.03 μ_HO (S2 review, W1).
  - Cells whose bound is below 0.9·μ_t are bisected, up to depth 10.
- **Tails.** For s ≤ −64 and s ≥ 32, G′ = 0 and E = q. The check is
  q ≥ −|V|_disc + min Re(−e^{−iφ}iω²) − |φ′(S)|/(4η), with φ between φ(S) and φ_∞.
  It uses two facts about φ = φ_∞s⁴/(s₁⁴ + s⁴):
  - |φ′(s)| ≤ |φ′(S)| for |s| ≥ |S|. Here φ′ ∝ s³/(s₁⁴ + s⁴)², and |φ′| is
    decreasing for |s| > (3/5)^{1/4}s₁ = 4.40, while |S| = 64 and 32.
  - φ(s) lies between φ(S) and φ_∞, since φ is monotone in |s|.
- **c ≥ 0.** c is certified ≥ 0.119 on every cell, and > 0 on the tails.
- **Choice of G.** G′ solves the Riccati equation cG″ = μ_t − q − c′G′ + cG′²
  (float, μ_t = 0.95·λ_float), from the maximum of the float ground state outward
  until G′ returns to 0; then G′ = 0. This is design only: any positive g is
  admissible, and the certificate is the inequality itself.

## 6. Controls and limits

- **A2 (a weak control).** The box has half-width γ₀/50 around the l-th PS
  fundamental mode (a true QNM; float, S1 solver).
  - Branch (a) runs the A1 checker with the window's ground state. It returns
    μ = −0.068, −0.097 and −0.285, i.e. −0.110, −0.064 and −0.095 μ_HO, and
    does not certify.
  - Branch (b), designing a ground state at the box, never reaches the checker:
    the float margin there is ≤ 0, so the Riccati sweep overflows
    (m3.py, `design_ground_state`). It counts as "not certified" only by
    convention.
  - So A2 catches only gross errors. Its ground state was designed 1.1κ₋ above
    the mode, and a checker that overestimated the margin by less than about
    0.06–0.11 μ_HO would still return "not certified" on these boxes.
- **A2′ (near-mode control; S2 review §5, now part of the pipeline).** The same
  checker runs with a ground state designed at Im ω = −0.88γ₀, where the float
  margin is still about +0.1 μ_HO. Box IN contains the mode; STRAD has
  Im ∈ [−1.05, −0.95]γ₀; OUT has Im ∈ [−0.90, −0.86]γ₀ and is mode-free.
  - IN and STRAD (both containing the QNM) do not certify at any l: μ/μ_HO is
    −0.102 and −0.131 (l = 20), −0.113 and −0.143 (l = 50), −0.142 and −0.172
    (l = 100).
  - OUT certifies at l = 20 and 50 (+0.017, +0.007 μ_HO). So the checker's verdict
    switches within about 0.1γ₀ of a true QNM, and A2's failure is not an artefact
    of a poor design.
  - At l = 100 OUT does not certify (−0.022 μ_HO): the spline loss of s2-report §3
    (0.07 μ_HO at l = 100) exceeds what is left of the float margin.
  - The records are in `m3_PB_l{l}.json` ("A2prime_near_mode"); the mode location
    comes from the float S1 solver, so A2′ is a control, not a certificate.
- **The float margin is essentially sharp.**
  - At the PS mode, λ_float/μ_HO = −0.020, −0.004 and −0.001 for l = 20, 50, 100.
  - Between the PS mode and the real axis, λ_float/μ_HO is close to
    1 − |Im ω|/γ₀ (S2 design study).
  - So the method measures the distance to the PS fundamental (n = 0) layer at
    Im ω ≈ −γ₀, and loses only O(l⁻²) there. (The first overtones lie near
    −3γ₀, about −5.05κ₋.)
- **Not established here.**
  - Uniformity in l. The certificates are at three discrete l. S4 must run the
    same inequality in the scaled variable σ = s·(l+½)^{1/2}, with closing boxes at
    h = 1/(l+½) → 0, where E ≥ μ reduces to the harmonic-oscillator ground-state
    bound with margin 1 − β/γ₀ = 0.673 minus design losses.
  - The sub-barrier regime (Re ω < ω_l − 8γ₀) and the low-frequency regime.
  - Route M2 (Riccati tube with an M1-quality centre) was not implemented. M3
    reached the targets first, at much lower cost (`s2-preregistration.md` §2).
