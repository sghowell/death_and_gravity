## Result and strict scope

For every finite N>=1, the selected complete four-scalar/N-graviton
tree obeys
|M_N|/A0<=3*n^2*N!*(2e16)^N/[kappa^(N/2)*product_i(w_i)].
Here n is the unchanged heavy mass squared, kappa=1e800,
A0=Am+AG is the positive unexpanded real Born normalization,
and the leaf TT tensors have unit Frobenius norm.

The original recoil domain is retained: incoming COM energy
5/4<=E<=2, positive emitted energies totaling at most 1/8,
positive associated elastic transfers, and no exact internal poles.
The estimate is uniform in angular and nested-collinear approaches,
arbitrary energy hierarchies and nonforward hard-angle limits.

Contracting maximal COMPLETE pure-soft branches leaves a unique
contact, heavy-path or hard-graviton-path core. The proof keeps
that combinatorial ownership, pairs hard vertices with their
propagators, and assigns each light scalar energy charge once.
Nonnegative core generating functions compose with the S318
weighted-current majorant. An exact cap/radius barrier supplies
the all-finite coefficient envelope.

Independent core inventories through N5 and the original complete
5116-tree calibration check the construction. At the original
parameters, the coefficient before product_i(1/w_i) is below
1e-755 for N3 and 1e-1138 for N4. The n^2 factor is incurred
once for the tuned matter interference, not once per emission.

This is a bare tree bound. Squaring its explicit soft poles still
gives logarithmically divergent fixed-multiplicity energy integrals.
The factorial upper envelope also does not prove convergence of
the physical multiplicity sum. Infrared overlap subtraction,
real-virtual completion, hard/evanescent matching, quantum state,
absolute complex Regge control and common-parent bounce matching
remain separate. Original V/G/B/P8 remain OPEN.
