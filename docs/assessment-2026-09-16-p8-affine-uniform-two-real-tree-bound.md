## Result and strict scope

The complete original two-graviton tree contains 434 graphs. This
checkpoint bounds the entire amplitude, not only its collinear pair
class or leading-soft approximation. With the same positive,
unexpanded Born pieces A_m and A_G, the result is

|M_(4Phi+2h)|/(A_m+A_G)<2e-395/(a*b).

It holds for positive emitted energies a,b with a+b<=1/8, incoming
COM energy 5/4<=E<=2, physical spatial TT polarizations of unit
Frobenius norm and the stated recoil map with nonzero elastic t,u.
The estimate is uniform in relative collinear and hard forward
neighborhoods, including simultaneous approaches. It retains the
explicit soft-energy poles and does not assign a unique helicity
value at an excluded exactly-collinear propagator point.

The 387-tree complementary class contains 33 contact, 177 heavy
and 177 Einstein graphs. The retained 47-tree pair class is bounded
by S311. The full sum is the physical Ward-invariant tree; its two
computational classes are not separately called detector observables.

## General estimates and independent checks

The mixed hard-cut proof assigns every subset of the future radiation
to one outgoing massive cluster and its complement to the other.
A Kallen identity and recoil inequalities give
-K^2>(tau+W^2)/300000, with W=a+b. Timelike cuts lie in [45/8,16],
heavy inverses are below 2/n and light-scalar gaps exceed 3*w_subset/8.

All Einstein vertices and successive hard propagators are paired,
so the relative estimate does not accumulate extra forward poles.
Canonical matter/metric vertex bounds and complete finite graph
inventories yield the complementary-sector bound below 1e-395/(a*b).
Positive A_m,A_G fractions combine matter and gravity without an
extra factor two. S311 adds its unchanged pair bound
9*(a+b)/(sqrt(kappa)*a*b), giving the stated complete result.

Independent exact-rational checks use the original parameters at
interior, near-collinear, extreme hard-forward and simultaneous
angular-boundary states. Six configurations also test every radiation
assignment, both mixed channels, massive/light gaps and cluster
identities. These checks supplement the uniform written proof.

Squaring against radial phase space a da b db leaves logarithmic
soft divergences. A tiny prefactor does not produce an infrared-finite
rate. This checkpoint supplies neither overlap subtraction nor its
virtual partner, all-N probabilities, finite hard/evanescent matching,
an interacting state, unitarity, absolute complex Regge or the
original common-parent bounce. No unknown matching constant is chosen.
