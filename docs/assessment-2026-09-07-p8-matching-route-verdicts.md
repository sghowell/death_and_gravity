# P8: two bounded matching-route exclusions

The ordinary two-metric investigation now supports two precise negative
route verdicts. They concern a controlled retained-order coefficient match
and a delta-convergent causal response on a specified input class. Neither
is an all-order bimetric exclusion or completion of the adopted
[S6 V/G/B contract](../problems/P8/s6/FORMULATION.md).

This assessment states short consequences of the linked source-pinned
results. It is not a new numerical calculation, certificate, or checkpoint.
No parent action, matter frame, earlier verdict, or source hash is changed.

## 1. Retained-order matching with a small coefficient remainder

### Hypotheses and verdict

Use the general class of
[S6.31](../problems/P8/s6/matching/variable/reduction/general/FORMULATION.md):
two positive constant Einstein coefficients \(M_G^2,M_F^2\), a canonical
clock and free canonical chi coupled only to physical g, and interaction
`-2 sqrt|g| sum beta_n(phi) e_n(sqrt(g^-1 f))`. All five beta functions
are smooth. There are no extra derivative interactions or implicit metric
or matter redefinitions. The hidden metric's own algebraic equation has a
positive smooth proportional root \(f_0=r(\phi)^2g\), with

\[
\beta_1+3r\beta_2+3r^2\beta_3+r^3\beta_4=0,
\qquad P=\beta_1+2r\beta_2+r^2\beta_3\ne0.
\]

Keep the declared covariant scalar-tensor normal form and its separate
higher-operator complement. An invertible scalar-only clock change is
allowed; physical g and free chi are not reassigned. Suppose a proposed
match decomposes the final coefficients into the formal action through
four total derivatives plus controlled remainders Delta in this same form.
An unproved projection of higher operators into those coefficients does
not establish this hypothesis.

At the target's center \(\theta=0,X_\theta=1\), let \(M_{\rm CD}^2>0\)
denote the target tensor normalization, and define

\[
\Xi=A_1-2F_{2X},\qquad I=X\Xi/G_T,\qquad
\mathcal R=\frac{|\Delta A_1|+2|\Delta F_{2X}|}{M_{\rm CD}^2}.
\]

If the final coefficients satisfy

\[
|G_T/M_{\rm CD}^2-1|\le\epsilon_T<1,
\qquad |I-1|\le\epsilon_I<1,
\]

then necessarily

\[
\mathcal R\ge(1-\epsilon_T)(1-\epsilon_I). \tag{1}
\]

Thus a matching ansatz supplying a remainder ceiling strictly below (1)
is excluded. Exact coefficient matching requires \(\mathcal R\ge1\).
This is a specified C1-in-X coefficient norm, not a norm on every possible
effective action or physical response.

### Proof and the specialized normalization

S6.31 derives the complete traceful potential inverse and retained action.
After all coefficient derivatives and integrations by parts,

\[
A_{1,\rm ret}=2F_{2X,\rm ret},\qquad A_{3,\rm ret}=0,
\qquad \Xi_{\rm ret}=0.
\]

The separate curvature-square coefficient
\(\kappa=M_F^4r^3/(4P)\) is nonzero. A scalar-only clock change multiplies
Xi by the squared clock Jacobian and preserves its zero. Since the final
GT is positive, the matching tolerances imply
\(\Xi_{\rm eff}=I_{\rm eff}G_{T,\rm eff}
\ge(1-\epsilon_T)(1-\epsilon_I)M_{\rm CD}^2\) at X=1.
But \(\Xi_{\rm eff}=\Delta A_1-2\Delta F_{2X}\), proving (1).
The nonzero fourth-order physical tensor symbol also proves that the
retained action itself is not exactly quadratic DHOST modulo boundaries
and scalar-only clock changes. Ordinary beta2/beta3 tuning does not remove
either retained-order obstruction.

The calibration \(M_*^2=5M^2\) is **not a general-class identity**. It is
the favorable leading center normalization granted specifically in
[S6.30](../problems/P8/s6/matching/variable/reduction/FORMULATION.md), for
unchanged VARIABLE with \(M_G=M_F=M\), \(r(0)=2\), and its actual clock map.
Taking \(M_{\rm CD}^2=M_*^2\) for that comparison, the retained center has
\(F_{2X}=A_1=0\), whereas CD has
\(F_{2X}=-M_*^2/2,A_1=0\). Exact repair therefore needs
\(\Delta F_{2X}=-M_*^2/2,\Delta A_1=0\). Granting this tensor fit does
not retune or establish agreement of the fixed free-chi trajectory.
GT here is the declared scalar-tensor coefficient, not the full kinetic
response after derivatives of the separate curvature operator are included.

### What this does not exclude

Equation (1) excludes a **small-remainder route**, not an unbounded
higher-order repair. S6.31 exhibits changes of the parent potential that
preserve all retained data but change the degree-six action. They do not
claim to preserve VARIABLE's rolling solution.
[S6.32](../problems/P8/s6/matching/variable/reduction/sixth/FORMULATION.md)
also proves that, on its literal off-shell tensor probe, the unchanged
parent's sixth-order contribution tends to \(-2M^2\), while the previous
order tends to \(+2M^2\); their combined center coefficient is O(delta).
Neither the isolated omitted term nor this cancellation is a bound on
the complete remainder. Formal derivative grading alone is not a proved
hierarchy on the target's X-near-one tube. A metric redefinition which
moves the curvature-square term also moves matter/source contacts; it
cannot silently restore the original free-M1 interpretation.

## 2. A delta-convergent zero-data response with smaller uniform error

### Hypotheses and endpoint theorem

This part concerns **only unchanged VARIABLE**, not every S6.31 potential.
Use [S6.33's exact own-f branch](../problems/P8/s6/matching/variable/reduction/exact/FORMULATION.md)
on the off-shell physical probe \(g=\eta,\theta=T\), with
\(u=T/\tau,L=1/100,a=L/2,0<\delta=c-2\le10^{-6}\).
M and tau are fixed. The isotropic hidden lapse is the exact N, not the
rolling solution's lapse c. No literal delta-zero action is added.

Admit this probe and one fixed smooth input q in the comparison domain:
\(0\le q\le\eta\), support strictly inside
\((-3L/4,-5L/8)\), and \(\int q\,du\ge\eta L/16\), with fixed
amplitude \(\eta>0\). Select the actual own-f solution with
\(Q(-L)=Q_u(-L)=0\). The physical metric is prescribed, not claimed to
solve its remaining field equation. The input is not a matter stress.

The source-frozen
[S6.36 statement](../problems/P8/s6/matching/variable/reduction/exact/scattering/forcing/FORMULATION.md)
and [proof](../problems/P8/s6/matching/variable/reduction/exact/scattering/forcing/notes/proof.md)
give a bounded original-Q family and two parameter sequences tending to
zero whose limiting lower separation exceeds
\(\gamma\eta\), where \(\gamma=237/42560\). Consequently, for **any**
approximation \(\widehat Q_\delta(+a)\) having a finite limit,

\[
\limsup_{\delta\downarrow0}|Q_\delta(+a)-\widehat Q_\delta(+a)|
>\frac{\gamma\eta}{2}
=\frac{237\eta}{85120}>\frac{\eta}{400}. \tag{2}
\]

Proof: apply the triangle inequality to the separated pair. The
approximation's paired difference tends to zero; the two errors together
must cover the strictly greater than gamma-eta separation. Their limsup
is at most twice the limsup error in (2). Both finite propagation errors
in S6.36 have already been retained; no vanishing comparison error or
exact reference subsequential limit is assumed.

Thus a delta-convergent description with a uniformly smaller endpoint
error is excluded on this experiment. The conclusion does not require
the approximation to be local: a convergent nonlocal description is
subject to the same floor. Delta-dependent phase-retaining descriptions,
other states and smaller input domains are not excluded by (2).

### Direct original-g equation readout and normalization

This conclusion need not rest only on a hidden-field output. The exact
quadratic action, per unit spatial volume in the norm-two anisotropy
convention, is

\[
S_T=\frac{M^2}{4\tau}\int du\,
[q_u^2+kQ_u^2-w_\delta(Q-q)^2],
\quad k=b^3/N,\quad w_\delta=2B_1b,
\quad B_1=\tau^2\beta_1/M^2.
\]

Vary q and Q independently first. Define the physical-time equation
readout by \(E_g(T)=-2\,\delta S_T/\delta q(T)\). It is exactly

\[
E_g=\frac{M^2}{\tau^2}
[q_{uu}+w_\delta(q-Q)]. \tag{3}
\]

Only now insert the zero-data retarded own-f solution. This is not a
retarded kernel substituted into a single-copy action and asserted to
be variational. At observation q and every derivative vanish, so
\(\tau^2E_g/M^2=-w_\delta Q\).

At the fixed nonzero endpoint +a, punctured coefficient analyticity gives
\(w_\delta\to w_0>0\). The coefficients are even in u. The stronger
uniform bounds in S6.36's proof, valid at -a and hence +a, give

\[
k>\frac{6859}{2100},\qquad
\Omega^2=\frac{w_\delta}{k}>\frac{15947134}{801},\qquad
w_0\ge\frac{6859}{2100}\frac{15947134}{801}>\frac1{L^2}.
\]

These fixed margins justify strictness after taking the limit. Since Q
is bounded, replacing \(w_\delta\) by \(w_0\) changes the readout by o(1).
Therefore its oscillation is \(w_0\operatorname{osc}Q\). Any convergent
approximation to the normalized original-g readout has limsup error

\[
>\frac{w_0\gamma\eta}{2}
>\frac{237\eta}{85120L^2}. \tag{4}
\]

Physical E_g errors multiply (4) by \(M^2/\tau^2\). The **literal
variational derivative** \(\delta S_T/\delta q(T)=-E_g/2\) has half
this magnitude. This factor of two follows from the displayed action,
not a change of source normalization. Equation (3) is an off-shell metric
Euler readout, not a conserved matter-source or undriven-g response claim.

## 3. What can be closed, and what remains research

The two route verdicts are explicit: a retained-order match with remainder
below (1), and a delta-convergent response with error below (2) or (4),
are rejected under their stated hypotheses. No further refinement of the
same tensor probe is required to establish these conditional exclusions.
They do not exhaust the admissible S6 matching constructions.

Smooth compact pulses are not a certified low-frequency EFT band. The
fixed off-shell probe need not belong to a proposed narrower matching
domain; admitting it was an explicit hypothesis above. Small eta alone
does not prove a uniform nonlinear remainder. Different inverse/state
prescriptions, finite-band restrictions, resummations, nonsimple or
nonproportional roots and extra operators remain separate investigations,
not implicitly excluded or accepted.

The adopted gates remain distinct. [S6.28](../problems/P8/s6/matching/variable/vacuum/FORMULATION.md)
already rules out the stipulated proportional constant-clock Minkowski
vacuum within unchanged VARIABLE's specified local field interval. This
fails that local algebraic V prerequisite, not a positivity-amplitude test;
a smooth off-interval extension is permitted as a different candidate.
No applicable finite-gravity Regge/IR remainder has been supplied for G.
The full common-parent physical-domain/state/operator match required by B
is not established or universally excluded here. A physical history from
the vacuum to the bounce is not an added requirement.

The useful pivot is a **leading physical-frame CD dictionary**, before
another background reconstruction or transfer refinement. Test one
explicitly enlarged interaction mechanism capable of escaping the
proportional-root Xi-zero relation. A bounded next success condition is
an explicit covariant leading map for the required F2_X, A1 and A3 on the
open clock tube, with free chi unchanged, regular constraints and either
an identified expansion parameter or a controlled exact algebraic inverse;
an algebraic obstruction rejects that
mechanism before further probe work. This is a research recommendation,
not a claim that such a parent exists or a new prerequisite appended to S6.

The scoped linear P8(b) classification and completed scoped photon
objective are unchanged. Original P8 remains open.
