## Result and strict scope

The limiting additional-soft insertion is
Z(sigma;y)=deltaDelta(sigma)+deltaa(sigma)*ln(y).
On the same full fixed-Born D4 leading-cloud probability space, compare
the full-state signed density conditioned by R<=x with the retained-state
density conditioned by R_eta<=x. Each uses its own actual remaining
energy, x-R or x-R_eta, and its exact conditioning normalization.

For 0<=a<=1 and 0<eta<=x<=1/8, their L1 difference is at most
63000*a*eta*(1+ln(1/eta))^2/kappa. At the original parameters this is
strictly below 51000*eta*(1+ln(1/eta))^2/kappa^2.
The proof includes both event-normalization terms, the same-event
remaining logarithm and the singular excluded-event layer. It uses
distinguished emissions before conditioning and the finite-cutoff
renewal density bound, without expanding the small cut probability.

This closes the specific limiting-log cutoff estimate left open in
S331. It is total variation of signed densities on a common space, not
TV convergence of finite/infinite emission-configuration laws; those
configuration laws are singular for a>0. No physical mark is evaluated
outside its own allowed cut. The zero-index and zero-count cases are
handled separately.

The proof is written analysis supported by exact algebra and numerical
calibrations, not kernel formalization. Joint quantitative regulator
control, outer hard dimensional/evanescent matching, finite radiative
hard terms, interacting detector probability and original P8 closure
are not established here.
