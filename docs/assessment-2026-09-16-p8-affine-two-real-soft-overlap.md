## Why the estimate is sufficient for this subtraction

The derivative bound is proved in a complex energy neighborhood,
not inferred from a bounded real amplitude. Recoil square roots and
all relevant denominators remain analytic; the 47 paired graphs are
combined before their angular bound. Grouped external orderings
recover the complete elastic limit. Two-variable Cauchy then supplies
the stated derivative estimate.

Integrating it over the energy rectangle gives the entropy-shaped
I(a,b). This handles hierarchical soft limits without claiming that
the unscaled remainder is pointwise bounded. Exact sharing integrals
control both interference and the squared remainder. The difference
is defined before removing a common lower cutoff; the two divergent
unsubtracted integrals are not separately assigned finite values.

Six exact original-parameter configurations check complete amplitudes,
both faces, shells, conservation and Ward identities. Removing the
marked null leg from a reduced current leaves a nonzero Ward defect.
Independent high-precision sharing integrals supplement the exact
positive-series derivation, not the physical amplitude proof.
