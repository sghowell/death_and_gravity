## An exact own-f background now exists on a uniform window

[S6.33](../problems/P8/s6/matching/variable/reduction/exact/FORMULATION.md)
solves the hidden metric's exact isotropic equations on the same off-shell
physical probe g=eta with the original prescribed clock. The physical
metric, canonical clock action and free-chi coupling are not reassigned.
This is not a solution of the remaining physical-metric/clock equations.

For every 0<c-2<=1/100 and abs(T)<=tau/100, the branch through b(0)=2
has a strictly positive lapse. An undivided Euler identity and a nonzero
implicit-function Jacobian prove local uniqueness without initially
assuming even metric functions. A desingularized contraction proves the
uniform continuation. Both a pure-Fraction engine and independently
written Arb arithmetic enclose the entire parameter box, not a grid.

The exact center lapse is c^2/2, not the original rolling solution's c.
The center metric component is c^4/4, and its defect from the first
retained own-f metric on this probe is (c^2-4)^2/4. These values converge
as c approaches 2, but they do not by themselves bound all derivatives
or the complete higher-operator action. The desingularized metric has a
joint analytic extension; the literal parent action is undefined at c=2.

## The tensor equation still needs a state prescription

The literal quadratic tensor action is now known on this exact own-f
background. A separate four-coordinate curvature derivation reproduces
it, including the Einstein boundary and both tensor equations.

When the physical metric perturbation q is prescribed, the hidden mode
satisfies D_T(Kf D_T Q)+2beta1 b(Q-q)=0, with Kf=M^2 b^3/N. Its inner
coefficient is 16/(1+8x^2), where x=T/(tau sqrt(c-2)). If q is varied
too, the relative mode instead has coefficient 80/(1+8x^2). The two
operators answer different questions and cannot be interchanged.

The regular background does not select homogeneous tensor data or a
retarded, advanced or Feynman inverse. Convergence of the coefficient
equations implies convergence of solutions only with convergent inner-
coordinate data and inputs. It is not a fixed physical-window theorem.

## A specified causal inverse has now been tested

[S6.34](../problems/P8/s6/matching/variable/reduction/exact/retarded/FORMULATION.md)
selects zero initial hidden data and a retarded own-f response to a
prescribed smooth metric pulse. Positive finite-parameter coefficient
bounds give a Green kernel with an explicit positive-flux margin.
For every admitted pulse, the hidden endpoint response is at least
63eta/1024 even though the pulse and all its derivatives have vanished
near observation. Its inner-time velocity is at least 21eta/80.

The zero history and pulse have the same entire final metric germ and
the same zero initial hidden state. No exact map of the final germ alone
can reproduce both outputs. The original physical-metric equation has
a separately normalized nonzero readout too; its literal variational
derivative differs by the explicitly recorded factor of two.

The pulse duration is proportional to tau sqrt(c-2). This is not a fixed
physical low-frequency EFT exclusion. A prescribed metric history is
also not automatically a conserved free-chi source or an undriven
solution of the physical-g equations. The retarded equation is used
directly, not inserted into a single-copy action and silently declared
to be a variational effective action.

The original CD/source dictionary, a controlled physical-domain inverse,
