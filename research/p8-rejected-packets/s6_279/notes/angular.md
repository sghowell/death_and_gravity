# Complete phase-sensitive angular cut and exact forward evaluation

Let a,b be the unit initial scalar axes on the two sides of the cut,
n the unit graviton direction, x=a.n,y=b.n,z=a.b,
A=z-xy and B=n.(a cross b).
The common transverse-frame product is A+iB, up to a conjugation
convention that leaves its real fourth power unchanged. Summing both
opposite helicities gives2Re(A+iB)^4, not2|A+iB|^4.
The same-helicity pair supplies its separate mass-dependent term.

Thus, with e^2=s/4,p^2=(s-4m^2)/4, the WHOLE helicity sewing is

    2[m^8+p^8(A^4-6A^2B^2+B^4)]
      /[kappa^2(e^2-p^2x^2)(e^2-p^2y^2)].

The Gram identity B^2=1-z^2-x^2-y^2+2zxy gives the explicit rational
kernel in the report. Average it over the entire n sphere and divide
by32pi. This normalization contains both the1/2! for identical gravitons
and the1/2 in2 Im A. No further external-state factor is applied.
Both left/right exchange and identical external-axis parity are exact.

At forward, z1 and y=x. Set beta^2=1-4m^2/s, with0<=beta<1.
The full result is Im A=s^2 F(beta)/(256pi kappa^2), where

    F=4-31beta^2/3+118beta^4/15-beta^6
      +(beta^7-6beta^3+8beta-3/beta)atanh(beta).

This is NOT a velocity expansion. Divide
[(1-r)^4+r^4(1-x^2)^4]/(1-rx^2)^2 by its denominator exactly.
The polynomial quotient is integrated directly; the two remaining
moments are atanh(beta)/beta and
1/[2(1-beta^2)]+atanh(beta)/(2beta), with exact differentiated primitives.

The removable beta0 limit is1, giving m^4/(16pi kappa^2).
The beta1 limit is8/15, giving s^2/(480pi kappa^2) as the high-energy
diagnostic. No uniform massless limit of the all-spin expansion is assumed.

The result is a physical s-cut. The massless intermediate threshold is
at0, but0<s<4m^2 is an unphysical external region. No continuation to
that region or complete crossing-symmetric loop amplitude is inferred
from the physical optical statement.
